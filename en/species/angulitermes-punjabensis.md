## Summary

***Angulitermes punjabensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1974; the type locality is Pakistan: Punjab: Choa Saiden Shah. Distribution: Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *punjabensis*: Named after the locality Punjab
