## Summary

***Anenteotermes ateuchestes*** is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Gambia: 11 m. from Bathurst on Yundum Road. Distribution: Cameroon, Gambia, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ateuchestes*: From Greek ateuchēs (unarmed), cognate with genus name Anenteotermes
