## Summary

***Neotermes platyfrons*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krecek & Scheffrahn in 2001; the type locality is Dominican Republic: La Altagracia Province, 2.6 km SW of Punta Cana. Distribution: Dominican Republic.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *platyfrons*: From platy + frons
