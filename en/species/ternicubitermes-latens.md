## Summary

***Ternicubitermes latens*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1966; the type locality is Tanzania: Ugunda, Kakoma. Distribution: Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *latens*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
