## Summary

***Fulleritermes coatoni*** is a species of the genus *Fulleritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1965; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Sibasa. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Fulleritermes*: Named after the entomologist C. Fuller; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *coatoni*: Named after W.G.H. Coaton
