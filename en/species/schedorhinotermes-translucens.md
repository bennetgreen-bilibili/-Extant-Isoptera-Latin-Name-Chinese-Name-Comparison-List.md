## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes translucens*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Solomon Islands, Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Papua New Guinea. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *translucens*: From the descriptive term 'transluc' (translucent)
