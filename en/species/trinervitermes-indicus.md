## Summary

***Trinervitermes indicus*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is India: Uttar Pradesh, Jhansi Division, Kalyanpur. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *indicus*: After India
