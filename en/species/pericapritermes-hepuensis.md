## Summary

***Pericapritermes hepuensis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Yang in 1990; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province. Distribution: China (Guangxi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *hepuensis*: Named after the locality Hepu
