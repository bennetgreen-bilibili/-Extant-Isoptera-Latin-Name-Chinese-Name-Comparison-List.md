## Summary

***Microtermes congoensis congoensis*** is a subspecies of *Microtermes congoensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Microtermes*. The subspecies was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Microtermes congoensis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *congoensis*: After Congo
