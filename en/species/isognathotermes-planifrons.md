## Summary

***Isognathotermes planifrons*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: tropical Africa. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *planifrons*: Latin planus (flat) + frons (front), flat-fronted
