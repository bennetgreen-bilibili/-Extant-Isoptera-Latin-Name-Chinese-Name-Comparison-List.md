## Summary

***Glyptotermes longipennis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Colombia: La Guajira: San Antonio \[ 11.4853N 72.7067W \]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longipennis*: Latin longus (long) + penna (wing), long-winged
