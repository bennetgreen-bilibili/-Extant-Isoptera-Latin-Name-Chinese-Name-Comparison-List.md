## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes sinensis*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Mengla. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *sinensis*: Latin Sinensis (of China)
