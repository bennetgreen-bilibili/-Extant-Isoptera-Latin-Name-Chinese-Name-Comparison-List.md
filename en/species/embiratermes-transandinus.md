## Summary

***Embiratermes transandinus*** is a species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Araujo in 1977; the type locality is Ecuador: Los Rios: Centro Científico Rio Palenque \[0.5921S 79.3611W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Embiratermes*: From Tupi Embirá (a tree) + -termes.

Specific epithet *transandinus*: From transand + inus
