## Summary

***Glyptotermes franciae*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1958; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna: Mt. Makiling. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *franciae*: Named after Francia (or France)
