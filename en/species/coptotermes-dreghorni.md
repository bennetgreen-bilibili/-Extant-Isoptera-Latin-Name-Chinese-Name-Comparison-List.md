## Summary

***Coptotermes dreghorni*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Timber Reserve # 263, near Ravenshoe. Distribution: Queensland, Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dreghorni*: Named after Dreghorn
