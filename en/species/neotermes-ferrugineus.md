## Summary

***Neotermes ferrugineus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Huon-Golf; Kela; Samoa-Hafen. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ferrugineus*: Latin ferrugineus (rusty)
