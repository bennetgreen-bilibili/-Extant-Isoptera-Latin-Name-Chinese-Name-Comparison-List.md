## Summary

***Glyptotermes zhaoi*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1984; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Jian'ou. Distribution: China (Fujian).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zhaoi*: Named after the Chinese entomologist Xiufu Zhao (1919–2004).
