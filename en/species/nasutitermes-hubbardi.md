## Summary

***Nasutitermes hubbardi*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Banks in 1919; the type locality is Cuba: Caboda; Jamaica: Mandeville. Distribution: Cuba, Dominican Republic, Jamaica, Haiti.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *hubbardi*: Named after Henry G. Hubbard
