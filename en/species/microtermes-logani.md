## Summary

***Microtermes logani*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Bacchus in 1997; the type locality is Malawi: Zomba. Distribution: Malawi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *logani*: Named after Logan
