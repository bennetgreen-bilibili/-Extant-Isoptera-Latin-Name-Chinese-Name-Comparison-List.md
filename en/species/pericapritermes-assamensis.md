## Summary

***Pericapritermes assamensis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Mathur & Thapa in 1965; the type locality is India: Assam: Goalpara Division: Central Range, Polo Block. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *assamensis*: Named after the locality Assam
