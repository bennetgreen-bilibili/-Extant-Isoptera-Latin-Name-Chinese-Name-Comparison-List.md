## Summary

***Microtermes pusillus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: West Africa, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *pusillus*: From the descriptive term 'pusill' (tiny)
