## Summary

***Odontotermes badius badius*** is a subspecies of *Odontotermes badius*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Odontotermes*. The subspecies was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Durban. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Odontotermes badius* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *badius*: Latin badius (bay, chestnut-brown)
