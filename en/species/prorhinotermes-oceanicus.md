## Summary

***Prorhinotermes oceanicus*** is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Costa Rica: Cocos Island. Distribution: Costa Rica, Cocos Island, Costa Rica. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prorhinotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *oceanicus*: Latin oceanus (ocean): oceanic
