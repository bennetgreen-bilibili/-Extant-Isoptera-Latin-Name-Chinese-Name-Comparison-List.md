## Summary

***Constrictotermes cacaoensis*** is a species of the genus *Constrictotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ensaf, Nel & Betsch in 2002; the type locality is French Guiana: Montagne Guadelope \[4.539N 52.44W\]. Distribution: French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Constrictotermes*: From Latin constrictus (constricted) + termes.

Specific epithet *cacaoensis*: Named after the locality Cacao
