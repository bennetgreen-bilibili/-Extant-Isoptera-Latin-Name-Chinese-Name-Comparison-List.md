## Summary

***Stolotermes inopinus*** is a species of the genus *Stolotermes*, in the family STOLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gay in 1969; the type locality is New Zealand: Wellington, 2 mi E of Eastbourne. Distribution: New Zealand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stolotermes*: From Latin stolo (shoot, stolon) + termes.

Specific epithet *inopinus*: Latin inopinus (unexpected)
