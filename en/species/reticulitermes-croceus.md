## Summary

***Reticulitermes croceus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1982; the type locality is China: Hunan Province: Huitong. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *croceus*: Latin croceus (saffron-yellow)
