## Summary

***Aganotermes oryctes*** is the only extant species of the genus *Aganotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Letaba. Distribution: South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

Genus name *Aganotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *oryctes*: Greek oryktēs (digger)
