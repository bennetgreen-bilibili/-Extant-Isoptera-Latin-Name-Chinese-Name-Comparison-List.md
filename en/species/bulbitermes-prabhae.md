## Summary

***Bulbitermes prabhae*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 1965; the type locality is Myanmar: Maymyo, 1/4 mile from Botanical Garden. Distribution: Vietnam, Myanmar, Thailand. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *prabhae*: Named after Prabha
