## Summary

***Ampoulitermes zacharia*** is a species of the genus *Ampoulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Joseph & Mathew in 2024; the type locality is India: Kerala: Idukki (Mamalakandam). Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ampoulitermes*: From Latin ampulla (flask) + termes.

Specific epithet *zacharia*: Named after Zacharia
