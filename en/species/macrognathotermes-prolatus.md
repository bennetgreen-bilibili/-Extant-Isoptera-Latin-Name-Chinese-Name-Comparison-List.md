## Summary

***Macrognathotermes prolatus*** is a species of the genus *Macrognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Miller in 1991; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory, Groote Eylandt. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrognathotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *prolatus*: From prol + atus
