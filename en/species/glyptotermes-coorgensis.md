## Summary

***Glyptotermes coorgensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Karanataka: Coorg: Sidapur. Distribution: India. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *coorgensis*: Named after the locality Coorg
