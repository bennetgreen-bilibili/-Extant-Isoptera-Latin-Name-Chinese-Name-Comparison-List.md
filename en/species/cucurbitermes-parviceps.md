## Summary

***Cucurbitermes parviceps*** is a species of the genus *Cucurbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Xu & Dong in 1994; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Kaihua. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cucurbitermes*: From Latin cucurbita (gourd) + termes.

Specific epithet *parviceps*: Latin parvus (small) + -ceps (head), small-headed
