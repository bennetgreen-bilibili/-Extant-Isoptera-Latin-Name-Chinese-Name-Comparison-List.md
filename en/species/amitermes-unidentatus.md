## Summary

***Amitermes unidentatus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1897; the type locality is Tanzania: Bawi. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, South Africa, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia. This site records 4 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *unidentatus*: From unident + atus
