## Summary

***Nitiditermes niokoloensis*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Roy-Noël in 1969; the type locality is Senegal: Niokolo-Koba region, near Niokolo-Koba on trail of Bangaré. Distribution: Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *niokoloensis*: Named after Niokolo-Koba, Senegal
