## Summary

***Neotermes erythraeus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1918; the type locality is Eritrea: Nefasit. Distribution: Eritrea, Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *erythraeus*: From erythra + eus
