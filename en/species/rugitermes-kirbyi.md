## Summary

***Rugitermes kirbyi*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Costa Rica: Cartago. Distribution: Costa Rica, Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *kirbyi*: Named after the entomologist W. F. Kirby
