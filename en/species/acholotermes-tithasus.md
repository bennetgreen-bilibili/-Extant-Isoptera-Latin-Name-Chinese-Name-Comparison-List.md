## Summary

***Acholotermes tithasus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Acholotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Yangambi. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acholotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + cholē (bile) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tithasus*: From Greek τιθασός (tithasos) 'tamed, domesticated' (LSJ), echoing the genus name Acholotermes (acholos 'meek') and the congeneric epithets imbellis ('unwarlike') and epius ('gentle'), alluding to the soldierless, mild-natured condition. Latinized as tithasus.
