## Summary

***Euhamitermes guizhouensis*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Gong in 1985; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Ceheng. Distribution: China, Guizhou.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *guizhouensis*: After Guizhou (China)
