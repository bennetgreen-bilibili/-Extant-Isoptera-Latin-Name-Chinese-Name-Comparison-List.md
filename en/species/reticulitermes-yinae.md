## Summary

***Reticulitermes yinae*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Zhu, Huang & Li in 1986; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Simao. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *yinae*: Named after the Chinese entomologist Wenying Yin (1922–2023).
