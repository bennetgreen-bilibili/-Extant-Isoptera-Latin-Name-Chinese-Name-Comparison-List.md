## Summary

***Glyptotermes posticus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Virgin Islands (USA): St. Thomas. Distribution: Cuba, Haiti, Jamaica, U.S. Virgin Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *posticus*: Latin posticus (posterior, hind)
