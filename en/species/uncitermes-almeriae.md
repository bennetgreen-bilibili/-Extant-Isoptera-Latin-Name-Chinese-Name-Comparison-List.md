## Summary

***Uncitermes almeriae*** is a species of the genus *Uncitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Carrijo in 2016; the type locality is Ecuador: Orellana: Yasuni National Park \[0.6717S 76.3979W\]. Distribution: Peru, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Uncitermes*: From Latin uncus (hook) + termes.

Specific epithet *almeriae*: Named after Almeria
