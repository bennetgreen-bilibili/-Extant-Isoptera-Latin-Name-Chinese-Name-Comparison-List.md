## Summary

***Neotermes buxensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is India: North Bengal: Buxa Forest Division. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *buxensis*: Named after the locality Bux
