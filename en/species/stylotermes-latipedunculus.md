## Summary

***Stylotermes latipedunculus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Yu & Ping in 1964; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Nan-xi. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latipedunculus*: From latipedunc + ulus
