## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes hutsoni*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1926; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Pelmadulla. Distribution: South Asia, Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *hutsoni*: Named after Hutson
