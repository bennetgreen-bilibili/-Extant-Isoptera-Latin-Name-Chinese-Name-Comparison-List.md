## Summary

***Neotermes kalimpongensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti in 1975; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Darjeeling District: Kalimpong: Bong Bosti. Distribution: India, West Bengal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kalimpongensis*: Named after the locality Kalimpong
