## Summary

***Odontotermes feae*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1896; the type locality is Myanmar: Carin Chebà, 900–1100 m. Distribution: South Asia, Southeast Asia, India, Thailand, Vietnam, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *feae*: Named after Leonardo Fea
