## Summary

***Roisinitermes ebogoensis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Roisinitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2018; the type locality is Cameroon: Mbalmayo Forest Reserve: Ebogo \[ 3.382N 11.463E \]. Distribution: Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Roisinitermes*: Named after a person (Roisin); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ebogoensis*: Named after the locality Ebogo
