## Summary

***Odontotermes guptai*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1961; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Nagaur District, near Gudha Village, near Sambhar Lake. Distribution: South Asia, Pakistan, India, Bangladesh. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *guptai*: Named after Gupta
