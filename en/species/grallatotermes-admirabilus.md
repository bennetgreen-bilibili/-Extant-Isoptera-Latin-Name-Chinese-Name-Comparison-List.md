## Summary

***Grallatotermes admirabilus*** is a species of the genus *Grallatotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1930; the type locality is Philippines: Negros, above Dumaguete, Horns of Negros; Suab River on Cotabato Coast. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Grallatotermes*: From Latin grallator (stilt-walker) + termes.

Specific epithet *admirabilus*: Latin admirabilis (admirable)
