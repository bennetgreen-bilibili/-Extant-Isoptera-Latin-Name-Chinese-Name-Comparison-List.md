## Summary

***Cryptotermes kirbyi*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Moszkowski in 1955; the type locality is Madagascar: 21 km N of Ambovombe. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *kirbyi*: Named after the entomologist W. F. Kirby
