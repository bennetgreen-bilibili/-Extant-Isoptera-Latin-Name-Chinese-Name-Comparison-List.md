## Summary

***Hospitalitermes javanicus*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Akbar in 1986; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: 19 km ex Palabuhan, Ratu-Lengkong. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *javanicus*: Named after the locality Javan
