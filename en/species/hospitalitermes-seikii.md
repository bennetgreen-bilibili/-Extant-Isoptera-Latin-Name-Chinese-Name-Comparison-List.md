## Summary

***Hospitalitermes seikii*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Syaukani in 2010; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra, Padang, Hutan Pendidikan dan Penelitian Biologi (0°57’0"S 100°21’11"E). Distribution: Singapore, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Indonesia, Cambodia, Vietnam, Brunei, China, India, Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *seikii*: Named after a person; probably honors the Japanese entomologist Seiki Yamane (cf. Lacessitermes yamanei in the same paper), but the etymology statement was not seen; uncertain.
