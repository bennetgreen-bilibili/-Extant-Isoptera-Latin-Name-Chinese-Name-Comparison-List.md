## Summary

***Noditermes festivus*** is a species of the genus *Noditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1958; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Parc National de l’ Upemba: "Kenia affl. dr. Lusinga et s. affl. dr. Lufwa". Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Noditermes*: From Latin nodus (knot, node) + termes.

Specific epithet *festivus*: Latin festivus (festive)
