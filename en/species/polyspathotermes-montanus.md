## Summary

***Polyspathotermes montanus*** is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1966; the type locality is Malawi: Nyika plateau, 22 m along access road from Rumpi-Chisenga road. Distribution: Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *montanus*: Latin montanus (mountain-dwelling)
