## Summary

***Cryptotermes juliani*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Dominican Republic: Peravia Province: Las Avispas. Distribution: Dominican Republic.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *juliani*: Named after Julian
