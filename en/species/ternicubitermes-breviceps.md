## Summary

***Ternicubitermes breviceps*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Zimbabwe: Harare (Salisbury). Distribution: Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *breviceps*: From brevi + ceps
