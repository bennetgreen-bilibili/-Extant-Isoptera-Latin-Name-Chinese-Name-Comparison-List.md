## Summary

***Nasutitermes takasagoensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Nawa in 1911; the type locality is Japan: Loo Choo (Ryukyu) Islands: Ishigakijima. Distribution: Japan, Guam, Taiwan, China, Christmas Island. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *takasagoensis*: Named after Takasago (Taiwan)
