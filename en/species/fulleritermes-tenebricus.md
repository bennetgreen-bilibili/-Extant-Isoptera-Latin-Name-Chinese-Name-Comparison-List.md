## Summary

***Fulleritermes tenebricus*** is a species of the genus *Fulleritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kakoulima; Nigeria: Olokemeji. Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Senegal, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gambia, Guinea, Nigeria, Sudan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Fulleritermes*: Named after the entomologist C. Fuller; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tenebricus*: From Latin tenebricus, dark/gloomy
