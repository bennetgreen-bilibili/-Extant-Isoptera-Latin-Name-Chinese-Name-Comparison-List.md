## Summary

***Subulitermes denisae*** is a species of the genus *Subulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roisin in 1995; the type locality is Panama: Parque National Soberanía, near Limbo Camp, Pipeline Road. Distribution: Panama, Costa Rica, Nicaragua.

## Etymology

Genus name *Subulitermes*: From Latin subula (awl) + termes.

Specific epithet *denisae*: Named after Denisa
