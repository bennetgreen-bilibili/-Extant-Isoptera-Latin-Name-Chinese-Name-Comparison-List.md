## Summary

***Procubitermes niapuensis*** is a species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Niapu. Distribution: Cameroon, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procubitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *niapuensis*: Named after Niapu, DR Congo
