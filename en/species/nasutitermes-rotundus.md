## Summary

***Nasutitermes rotundus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Light & Wilson in 1936; the type locality is Philippines: Mindanao: Cotabato Coast. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *rotundus*: Latin rotundus (round)
