## Summary

***Reticulitermes nanjiangensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chen & Ping in 1984; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Najiang County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *nanjiangensis*: Named after the locality Nanjiang
