## Summary

***Syntermes territus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1924; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Colombia, French Guiana, Brazil, Guyana, Suriname, Panama, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *territus*: Latin territus (frightened)
