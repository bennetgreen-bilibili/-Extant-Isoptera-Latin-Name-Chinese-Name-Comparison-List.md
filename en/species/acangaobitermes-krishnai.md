## Summary

***Acangaobitermes krishnai*** is the only extant species of the genus *Acangaobitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Rocha, Cancello & Cuezzo in 2011; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Parque Nacional das Emas \[18.1S 52.91W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acangaobitermes*: From the place name Acangaobí; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *krishnai*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna
