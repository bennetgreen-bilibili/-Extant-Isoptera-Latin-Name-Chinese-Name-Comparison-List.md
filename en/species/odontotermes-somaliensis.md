## Summary

***Odontotermes somaliensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1927; the type locality is Somalia: Villaggio Duca degli Abruzzi. Distribution: East Africa, Somalia, Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *somaliensis*: Named after Somalia
