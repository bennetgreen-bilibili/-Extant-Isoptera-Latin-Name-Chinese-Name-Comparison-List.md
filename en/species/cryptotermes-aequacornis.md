## Summary

***Cryptotermes aequacornis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: San Juan-Laventille: near La Vache Bay \[ 10.746N 61.488W \]. Distribution: Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *aequacornis*: From aequa + cornis
