## Summary

***Coptotermes sinabangensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1923; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Simeulue, Sinabang. Distribution: Sumatra, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sinabangensis*: Named after Sinabang (island, Indonesia)
