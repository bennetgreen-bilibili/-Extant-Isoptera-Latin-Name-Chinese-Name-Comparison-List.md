## Summary

***Spatulitermes coolingi*** is the only extant species of the genus *Spatulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Coaton in 1971; the type locality is Namibia: 64.4 km ex Rundu-Grootfontein. Distribution: southern Africa, Namibia, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Spatulitermes*: From Latin spatula (spatula) + termes.

Specific epithet *coolingi*: Named after Cooling
