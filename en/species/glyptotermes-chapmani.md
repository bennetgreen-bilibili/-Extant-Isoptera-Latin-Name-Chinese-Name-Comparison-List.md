## Summary

***Glyptotermes chapmani*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1930; the type locality is Philippines: Leyte, near Quiot. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chapmani*: Named after Chapman
