## Summary

***Eremotermes sanyuktae*** is a species of the genus *Eremotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Thakur in 1989; the type locality is India: Gujarat: Surat district: Magdall port area. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eremotermes*: From Greek erēmos (desert, solitary) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sanyuktae*: Named after a person "Sanyukta" (Indian female name)
