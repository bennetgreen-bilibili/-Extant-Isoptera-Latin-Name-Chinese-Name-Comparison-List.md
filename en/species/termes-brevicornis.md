## Summary

***Termes brevicornis*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *brevicornis*: Latin brevis (short) + cornu (horn/antenna), short-horned
