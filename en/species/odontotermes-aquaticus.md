## Summary

***Odontotermes aquaticus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1897; the type locality is Cameroon: River Mungo. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Togo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aquaticus*: Latin aquaticus (aquatic)
