## Summary

***Angulitermes asirensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani & Bose in 1991; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Rahifa: Asir. Distribution: Saudi Arabia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *asirensis*: Named after the locality Asir
