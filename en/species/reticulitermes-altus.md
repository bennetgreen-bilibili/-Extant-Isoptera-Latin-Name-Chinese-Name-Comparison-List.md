## Summary

***Reticulitermes altus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao, Pan, Ma & Shi in 1982; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Dechang. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *altus*: Latin altus (high)
