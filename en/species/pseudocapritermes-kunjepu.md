## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes kunjepu*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Mathew in 2020; the type locality is India: Kerala: Kottayam, Pinnakkanadu \[ 9.63N 76.76E \]. Distribution: India, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *kunjepu*: Named after Kunjepu
