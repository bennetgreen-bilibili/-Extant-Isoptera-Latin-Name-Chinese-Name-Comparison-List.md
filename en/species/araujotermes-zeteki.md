## Summary

***Araujotermes zeteki*** is a species of the genus *Araujotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Panama: Summit. Distribution: Colombia, Brazil, Panama, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Araujotermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist R. L. Araujo; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zeteki*: Named after J. Zetek
