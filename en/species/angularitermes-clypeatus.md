## Summary

***Angularitermes clypeatus*** is a species of the genus *Angularitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angularitermes*: From Latin angularis (angular) + termes.

Specific epithet *clypeatus*: Latin clypeatus (shielded)
