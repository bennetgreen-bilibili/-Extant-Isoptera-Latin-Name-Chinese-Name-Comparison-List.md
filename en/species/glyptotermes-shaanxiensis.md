## Summary

***Glyptotermes shaanxiensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Huang & Zhang in 1986; the type locality is China: Shaanxi: Baihe. Distribution: China (Shaanxi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *shaanxiensis*: Named after the locality Shaanx
