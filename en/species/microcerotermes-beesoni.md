## Summary

***Microcerotermes beesoni*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1933; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Haldwani: Chakrata Range. Distribution: South Asia, India, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Pakistan. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *beesoni*: Named after the forest entomologist C. F. C. Beeson
