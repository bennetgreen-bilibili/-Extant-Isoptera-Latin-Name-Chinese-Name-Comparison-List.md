## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes eleanorae*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1970; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: Little Andaman Island: 19 km south of Toikoibuea, Bedeabdulu. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *eleanorae*: Named after Eleanor
