## Summary

***Protocapritermes krisiformis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Protocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: New South Wales: Sutherland, near Sydney. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Protocapritermes*: From Greek prōtos (first) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *krisiformis*: Named after Krishna + Latin -formis (shaped)
