## Summary

***Neotermes magnoculus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Huachi \[ 15.6667S 67.25W \]. Distribution: Bolivia, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *magnoculus*: From magn + oculus
