## Summary

***Astratotermes hilarus*** is a species of the genus *Astratotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg. Distribution: Central African Congo Basin, East Africa, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astratotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hilarus*: Latin hilarus (cheerful)
