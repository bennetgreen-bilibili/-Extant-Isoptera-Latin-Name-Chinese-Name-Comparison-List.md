## Summary

***Stylotermes sui*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1993; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Shixing County: Xianrendong of Chebaling. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sui*: Named after a person "Sui/Su" (Chinese surname)
