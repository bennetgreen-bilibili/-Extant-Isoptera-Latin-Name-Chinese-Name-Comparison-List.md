## Summary

***Hospitalitermes irianensis*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Maiti in 1966; the type locality is Indonesia: Irian Jaya (West Irian): Idenburg River: Meervlakte. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *irianensis*: Named after the locality Irian
