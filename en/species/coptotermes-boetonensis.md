## Summary

***Coptotermes boetonensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Sulawesi: Boeton Island. Distribution: Java, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *boetonensis*: Named after the locality Boeton
