## Summary

***Glyptotermes paratuberculatus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Lamag District: Pin. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paratuberculatus*: From paratubercul + atus
