## Summary

***Procryptotermes corniceps*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1923; the type locality is Puerto Rico: Boqueron-Salinas. Distribution: Caribbean.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *corniceps*: From corni + ceps
