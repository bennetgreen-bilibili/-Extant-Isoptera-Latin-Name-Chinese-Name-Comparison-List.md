## Summary

***Glyptotermes euryceps*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao, Zhu & Gong in 1981; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Chengdu. Distribution: China (Sichuan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *euryceps*: From eury + ceps
