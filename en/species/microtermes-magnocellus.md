## Summary

***Microtermes magnocellus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1915; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga. Distribution: Ethiopia, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *magnocellus*: From magn + ocellus
