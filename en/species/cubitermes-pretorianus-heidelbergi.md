## Summary

***Cubitermes pretorianus heidelbergi*** is a subspecies of *Cubitermes pretorianus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), genus *Cubitermes*. The subspecies was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Heidelberg. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Cubitermes pretorianus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *heidelbergi*: Named after Heidelberg (Germany)
