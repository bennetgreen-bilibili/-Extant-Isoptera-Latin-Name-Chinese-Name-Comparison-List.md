## Summary

***Cortaritermes intermedius*** is a species of the genus *Cortaritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Banks in 1919; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: Sangre Grande: Cumuto \[10.5865N 61.2078W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, French Guiana, Uruguay, Venezuela, Bolivia, Paraguay, Trinidad, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cortaritermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *intermedius*: Latin intermedius (intermediate)
