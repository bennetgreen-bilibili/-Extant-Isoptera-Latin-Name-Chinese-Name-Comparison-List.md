## Summary

***Cryptotermes sumatrensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1930; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Fort de Kock. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *sumatrensis*: Named after the locality Sumatr
