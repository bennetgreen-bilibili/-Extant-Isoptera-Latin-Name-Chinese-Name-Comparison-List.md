## Summary

***Odontotermes apollo*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1905; the type locality is Kenya: Eburru \[taken from type specimen label\]. Distribution: East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *apollo*: From a- (without) + pollo
