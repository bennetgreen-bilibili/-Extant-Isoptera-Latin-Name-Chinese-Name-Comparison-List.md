## Summary

***Microcerotermes nanulus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1925; the type locality is Benin: Agouagou. Distribution: Benin, Côte d'Ivoire.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nanulus*: From nan + ulus
