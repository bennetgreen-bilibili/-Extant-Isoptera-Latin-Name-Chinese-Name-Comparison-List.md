## Summary

***Neotermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Lamag District: Sukau. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
