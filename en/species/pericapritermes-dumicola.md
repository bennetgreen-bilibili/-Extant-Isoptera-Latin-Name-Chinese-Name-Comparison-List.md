## Summary

***Pericapritermes dumicola*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga District: Mwakijembe. Distribution: Indonesia, Kenya, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *dumicola*: Latin dumus (thicket) + -cola (dweller): thicket-dwelling
