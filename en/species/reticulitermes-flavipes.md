## Summary

***Reticulitermes flavipes*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kollar in 1837; the type locality is Austria: Schönbrunn. Distribution: eastern North America, eastern United States, southeastern Canada, introduced to Europe through trade, Guatemala, Austria, Bahamas, Chile, France, Germany, Uruguay. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *flavipes*: From flavi + pes
