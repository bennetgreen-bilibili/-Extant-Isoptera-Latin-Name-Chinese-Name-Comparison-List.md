## Summary

***Pseudacanthotermes curticeps*** is a species of the genus *Pseudacanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Tshela. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudacanthotermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *curticeps*: Latin curtus (short) + -ceps (head), short-headed
