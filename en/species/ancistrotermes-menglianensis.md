## Summary

***Ancistrotermes menglianensis*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Wang in 1991; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Autonomous County of Menglian: Dai-Lahu-Va. Distribution: Yunnan (Menglian 1020m).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *menglianensis*: Named after the locality Menglian
