## Summary

***Angulitermes rathorai*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Kumar & Thakur in 2010; the type locality is India: Haryana. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *rathorai*: Named after a person "Rathore"
