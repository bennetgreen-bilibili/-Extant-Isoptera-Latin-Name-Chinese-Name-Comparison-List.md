## Summary

***Pericapritermes desaegeri*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1963; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Parc National Garamba. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *desaegeri*: Named after Henri De Saeger
