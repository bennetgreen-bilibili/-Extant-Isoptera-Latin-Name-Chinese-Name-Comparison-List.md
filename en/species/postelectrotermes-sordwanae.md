## Summary

***Postelectrotermes sordwanae*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1949; the type locality is South Africa: North Zululand: Jesser Point, Sordwana Beach. Distribution: West Africa, Ivory Coast, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *sordwanae*: Named after Sodwana (South Africa)
