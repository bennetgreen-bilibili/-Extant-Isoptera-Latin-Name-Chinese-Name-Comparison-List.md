## Summary

***Amitermes lilloi*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2020; the type locality is Argentina: Province of Tucumán, Tapia \[ 26.594S 65.28W \]. Distribution: southeastern South America.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lilloi*: Named after Lillo (Argentine naturalist Miguel Lillo)
