## Summary

***Pericapritermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya. Distribution: India, Sri Lanka. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
