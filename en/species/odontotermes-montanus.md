## Summary

***Odontotermes montanus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1960; the type locality is Kenya: on the Kikuyu Escarpment, Nairobi-Naivasha road. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *montanus*: Latin montanus (mountain-dwelling)
