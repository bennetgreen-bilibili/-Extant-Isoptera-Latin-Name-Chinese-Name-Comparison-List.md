## Summary

***Anoplotermes ater*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Brasil: Rio de Janeiro: Nova Friburgo \[ 22.27S 42.53W \]; Colombia: ?. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Paraguay, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *ater*: Latin ater (dark)
