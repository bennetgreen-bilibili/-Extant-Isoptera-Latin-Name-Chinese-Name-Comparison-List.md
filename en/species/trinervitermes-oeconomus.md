## Summary

***Trinervitermes oeconomus*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Trägårdh in 1904; the type locality is Sudan: Kaka. Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Burkina Faso, Benin, Central Africa, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Ghana, Mali, Nigeria, Senegal, Sudan, Uganda. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *oeconomus*: Greek oikonomos (economical)
