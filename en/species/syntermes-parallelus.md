## Summary

***Syntermes parallelus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1923; the type locality is Guyana: Upper Demerara-Berbice: Canister Falls \[4.8N 58.4333W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Venezuela, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parallelus*: From the descriptive term 'parallel' (parallel)
