## Summary

***Isognathotermes fungifaber*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1896; the type locality is Cameroon: Bonge; Ekundu; Itoki; Kitta; N’dian. Distribution: tropical Africa. This site records 4 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fungifaber*: Latin fungus (fungus) + faber (maker)
