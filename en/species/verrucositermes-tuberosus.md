## Summary

***Verrucositermes tuberosus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Verrucositermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1960; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kalina Point at the Congo River, Kinshasa. Distribution: Cameroon, Congo (Kinshasa), Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Verrucositermes*: From Latin verrucosus (warty) + termes.

Specific epithet *tuberosus*: From tuber + osus
