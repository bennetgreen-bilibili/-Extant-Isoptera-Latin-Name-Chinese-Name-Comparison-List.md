## Summary

***Cryptotermes mobydicki*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2025; the type locality is French Guiana: Petit Saut \[ 5.07N 53.032W \]. Distribution: French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *mobydicki*: Named after Moby Dick, the whale in Melville's Moby-Dick
