## Summary

***Euhamitermes urbanii*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1977; the type locality is Bhutan: Changra. Distribution: Oriental region.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *urbanii*: Named after Urban
