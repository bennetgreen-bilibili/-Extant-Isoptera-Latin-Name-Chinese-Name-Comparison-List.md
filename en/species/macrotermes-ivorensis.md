## Summary

***Macrotermes ivorensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé & Noirot in 1951; the type locality is Ivory Coast: Adiopodoumé. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Guinea, Nigeria, Senegal, Sierra Leone.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *ivorensis*: Named after Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire)
