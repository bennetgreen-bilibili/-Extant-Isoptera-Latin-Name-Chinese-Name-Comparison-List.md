## Summary

***Microcerotermes edentatus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai). Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Nigeria, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *edentatus*: Latin e- (without) + dentatus (toothed), toothless
