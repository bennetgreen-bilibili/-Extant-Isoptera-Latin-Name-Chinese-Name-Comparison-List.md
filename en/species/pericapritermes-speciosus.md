## Summary

***Pericapritermes speciosus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *speciosus*: Latin speciosus (beautiful, showy)
