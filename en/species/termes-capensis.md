## Summary

***Termes capensis*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is South Africa: Cape Province: Stellenbosch. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *capensis*: From Latin capensis, of the Cape (of Good Hope)
