## Summary

***Macrotermes orthognathus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1985; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Xingyi County. Distribution: Guizhou, China, Xingyi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *orthognathus*: Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw), straight-jawed
