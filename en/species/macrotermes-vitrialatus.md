## Summary

***Macrotermes vitrialatus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: "an Bord" (at the border). Distribution: Old World tropics, Central Africa, East Africa, southern Africa, Angola, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, South Africa, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 5 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *vitrialatus*: Latin vitrum (glass) + latus (side/wing), glass-winged
