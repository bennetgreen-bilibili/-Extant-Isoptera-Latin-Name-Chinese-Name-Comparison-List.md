## Summary

***Procubitermes ueleensis*** is a species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Moto (Haut Uelé). Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uele region, Gabon, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procubitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *ueleensis*: Named after the locality Uele
