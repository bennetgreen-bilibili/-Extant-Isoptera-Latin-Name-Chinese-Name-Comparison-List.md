## Summary

***Ruptitermes xanthochiton*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Paraguay, French Guiana, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *xanthochiton*: Greek xanthos (yellow) + chitōn (shell), yellow-shelled
