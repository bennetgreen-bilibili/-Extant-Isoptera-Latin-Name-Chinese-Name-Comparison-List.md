## Summary

***Neocapritermes utiariti*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna & Araujo in 1968; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Utiariti (waterfall) \[ 13.027S 58.283W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *utiariti*: Named after the locality Utiariti (Mato Grosso, Brazil)
