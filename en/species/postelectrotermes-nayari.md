## Summary

***Postelectrotermes nayari*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Verma in 1971; the type locality is India: Kerala State: Trivandrum: Chackai. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *nayari*: Named after Nayar
