## Summary

***Foraminitermes valens*** is a species of the genus *Foraminitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Foraminitermes*: From Latin foramen (opening) + termes.

Specific epithet *valens*: Latin valens (strong)
