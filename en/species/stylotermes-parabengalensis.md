## Summary

***Stylotermes parabengalensis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti in 1975; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Jalpaiguri District, near Nagrakata railway station, alt. 270 m, 85 km NE of Jalpaiguri. Distribution: China, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parabengalensis*: Greek para (near) + Bengal: near Bengal
