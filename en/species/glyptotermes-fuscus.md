## Summary

***Glyptotermes fuscus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1912; the type locality is Taiwan: Nanrenshan, Pingtung Co. \[ 22.0817N 120.835E \]. Distribution: Japan, China (Taiwan). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fuscus*: Latin fuscus (dark, dusky)
