## Summary

***Amitermes cryptodon*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1930; the type locality is Mexico: Colima State: Madrid. Distribution: Central America and Caribbean, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cryptodon*: Greek kryptos (hidden) + odous (tooth), hidden-toothed
