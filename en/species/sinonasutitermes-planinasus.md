## Summary

***Sinonasutitermes planinasus*** is a species of the genus *Sinonasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1991; the type locality is China: Hainan Province: Wanning County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinonasutitermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + nasutus (nasute) + termes.

Specific epithet *planinasus*: Latin planus (flat) + nasus (nose), flat-nosed
