## Summary

***Syntermes dirus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Burmeister in 1839; the type locality is Brasil: "Rio" (probably Rio de Janeiro) \[22.9S 43.23W\]. Distribution: Brazil. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dirus*: Latin dirus (dreadful)
