## Summary

***Glyptotermes contracticornis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Costa Rica: Navarro. Distribution: Costa Rica, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *contracticornis*: Latin contractus (contracted) + cornu (horn), contracted-horned
