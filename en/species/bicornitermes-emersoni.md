## Summary

***Bicornitermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Bicornitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1962; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kinshasa (Leopoldville). Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bicornitermes*: From Latin bi- (two) + cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
