## Summary

***Allodontermes rhodesiensis*** is a species of the genus *Allodontermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1914; the type locality is Zimbabwe: Harare (Salisbury). Distribution: Old World tropics (East Africa), southern Africa. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Allodontermes*: From Greek allos (other) + odous (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *rhodesiensis*: Named after the locality Rhodes
