## Summary

***Glyptotermes seeversi*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Mexico: Veracruz: Córdoba. Distribution: Honduras, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *seeversi*: Named after C. H. Seevers (American entomologist)
