## Summary

***Batillitermes clypeatus*** is a species of the genus *Batillitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1995; the type locality is Nigeria: 40 miles from Abjua on Bida road. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Batillitermes*: From Latin batillum (shovel) + termes.

Specific epithet *clypeatus*: Latin clypeatus (shielded)
