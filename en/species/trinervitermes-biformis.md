## Summary

***Trinervitermes biformis*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Bandarawela. Distribution: India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *biformis*: Latin bi- (two) + forma (form), two-formed
