## Summary

***Reticulitermes qingjiangensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao & Wang in 1982; the type locality is China: Jiangsu Province: Qingjiang. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *qingjiangensis*: Named after the locality Qingjiang
