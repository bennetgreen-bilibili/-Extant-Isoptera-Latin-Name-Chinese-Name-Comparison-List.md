## Summary

***Cryptotermes karachiensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Akhtar in 1974; the type locality is Pakistan: Sind: Karachi. Distribution: Pakistan, Karachi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *karachiensis*: Named after Karachi (Pakistan)
