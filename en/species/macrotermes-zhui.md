## Summary

***Macrotermes zhui*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2013; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Nanning outskirts. Distribution: China (Guangxi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *zhui*: Named after the Chinese termite taxonomist Shimao Zhu. Macrotermes zhui is a replacement name (Krishna 2013) for Macrotermes latinotus Zhu & Luo, 1985, a junior primary homonym of Macrotermes gilvus latinotus Kemner, 1934.
