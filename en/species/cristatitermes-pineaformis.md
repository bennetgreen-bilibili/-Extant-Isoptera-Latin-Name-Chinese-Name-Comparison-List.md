## Summary

***Cristatitermes pineaformis*** is a species of the genus *Cristatitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Miller in 1991; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: 48.5 km W Jabiru. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cristatitermes*: From Latin cristatus (crested) + termes.

Specific epithet *pineaformis*: Latin pinea (pine-cone) + -formis (shaped), pine-cone-shaped
