## Summary

***Neotermes wagneri*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Desneux in 1904; the type locality is Brasil: Rio de Janeiro: Serra dos Orgãos \[ 22.4867S 43.0613W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wagneri*: Named after Wagner
