## Summary

***Microcerotermes cameroni*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Madras: North Vellore District. Distribution: South Asia, India, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cameroni*: Named after P. Cameron
