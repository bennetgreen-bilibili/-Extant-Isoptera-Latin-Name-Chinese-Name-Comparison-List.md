## Summary

***Macrotermes malaccensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Malacca. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Cambodia, Thailand, Vietnam. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *malaccensis*: Named after the locality Malacc
