## Summary

***Anacanthotermes ubachi*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Navás in 1911; the type locality is Israel: near Dead Sea (Cercanias del Mar Muerto). Distribution: Middle East, Israel, Jordan, Palestine, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Turkey.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *ubachi*: Named after Ubach
