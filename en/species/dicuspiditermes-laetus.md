## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes laetus*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Myanmar: Lower Myanmar: east side of Dawna Hills, Sukli. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Thailand, Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, India, Malaysia, Myanmar, Vietnam. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *laetus*: Latin laetus (joyful, bright)
