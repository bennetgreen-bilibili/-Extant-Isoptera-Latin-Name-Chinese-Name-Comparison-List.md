## Summary

***Glyptotermes tsaii*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Huang & Zhu in 1986; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Gongshan County: Drung-Nu. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tsaii*: Named after the Chinese termite taxonomist Pang-hwa Tsai (1902–1983), a founder of Chinese termite studies.
