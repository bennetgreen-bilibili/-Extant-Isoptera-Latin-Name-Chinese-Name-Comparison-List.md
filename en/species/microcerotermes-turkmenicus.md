## Summary

***Microcerotermes turkmenicus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Luppova in 1976; the type locality is Turkmenistan: vicinity of Tedzhen. Distribution: Turkmenistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *turkmenicus*: Named after Turkmenistan
