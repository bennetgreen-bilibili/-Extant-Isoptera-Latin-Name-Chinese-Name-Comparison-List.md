## Summary

***Coatitermes clevelandi*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Coatitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Peru, Bolivia, Ecuador, Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coatitermes*: From Tupi Coati (coati) + termes.

Specific epithet *clevelandi*: Named after L. R. Cleveland
