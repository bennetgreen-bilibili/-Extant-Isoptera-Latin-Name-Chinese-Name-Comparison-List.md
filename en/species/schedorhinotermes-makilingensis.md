## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes makilingensis*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Acda in 2007; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna: Los Baños; Mt. Makiling. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *makilingensis*: Named after the locality Makiling
