## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes sisiri*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1997; the type locality is India: Kerala: Silent Valley. Distribution: India, Kerala.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *sisiri*: Named after a person "Sisir" (Indian name)
