## Summary

***Ancistrotermes hekouensis*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Wang in 1991; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Yao Autonomous County of Hekou. Distribution: Yunnan (Hekou 110m).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hekouensis*: Named after the locality Hekou
