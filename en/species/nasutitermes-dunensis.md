## Summary

***Nasutitermes dunensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thakur in 1969; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun: Dehra Dun Forest Division: Asarori Range: Mohamedpur Block. Distribution: India. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *dunensis*: From Latin duna (dune)
