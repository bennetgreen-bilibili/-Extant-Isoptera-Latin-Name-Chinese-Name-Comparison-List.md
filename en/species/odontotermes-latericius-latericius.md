## Summary

***Odontotermes latericius latericius*** is a subspecies of *Odontotermes latericius*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Odontotermes*. The subspecies was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Estcourt District. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Odontotermes latericius* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latericius*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
