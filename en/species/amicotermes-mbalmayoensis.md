***Amicotermes mbalmayoensis*** is a species of the genus *Amicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1999; the type locality is Cameroon: Mbalmayo Forest Reserve: Ekombitie. Distribution: Cameroon, Mbalmayo, Nigeria.
