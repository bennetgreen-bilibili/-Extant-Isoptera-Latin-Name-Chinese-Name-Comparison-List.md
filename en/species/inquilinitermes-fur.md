## Summary

***Inquilinitermes fur*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Inquilinitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[15.5961S 56.0967W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Inquilinitermes*: From Latin inquilinus (inquiline) + termes.

Specific epithet *fur*: Latin fur (thief)
