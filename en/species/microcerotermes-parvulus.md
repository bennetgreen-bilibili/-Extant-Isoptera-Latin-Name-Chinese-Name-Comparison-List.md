## Summary

***Microcerotermes parvulus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, Chad, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Senegal, Tanzania, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parvulus*: Latin parvulus (very small, diminutive)
