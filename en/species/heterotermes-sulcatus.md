## Summary

***Heterotermes sulcatus*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Venezuela, Suriname.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sulcatus*: Latin sulcatus (grooved)
