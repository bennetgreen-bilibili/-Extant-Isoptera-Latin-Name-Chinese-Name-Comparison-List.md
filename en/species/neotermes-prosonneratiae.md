## Summary

***Neotermes prosonneratiae*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Dariadigh: Cox’s Bazaar Division. Distribution: Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *prosonneratiae*: After the plant genus Sonneratia
