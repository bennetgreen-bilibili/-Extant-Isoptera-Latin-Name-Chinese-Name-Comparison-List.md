## Summary

***Glyptotermes tripurensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thakur in 1975; the type locality is India: Tripura: Tiliamura. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tripurensis*: Named after the locality Tripur
