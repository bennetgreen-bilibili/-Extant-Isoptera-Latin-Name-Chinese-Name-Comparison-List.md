## Summary

***Nasutitermes chhotanii*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Bose in 1997; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: Itanagar: Lake Ganga. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *chhotanii*: Named after O.B. Chhotani
