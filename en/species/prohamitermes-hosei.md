## Summary

***Prohamitermes hosei*** is a species of the genus *Prohamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1906; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Borneo, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prohamitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *hosei*: Named after Charles Hose
