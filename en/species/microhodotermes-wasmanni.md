## Summary

***Microhodotermes wasmanni*** is a species of the genus *Microhodotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Libya: Uadi (Wadi) M’bellum. Distribution: Libya, Egypt, Israel, Tunisia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microhodotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + hodos (path) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wasmanni*: Named after the entomologist E. Wasmann
