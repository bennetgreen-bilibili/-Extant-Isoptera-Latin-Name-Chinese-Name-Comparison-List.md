## Summary

***Fulleritermes contractus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Fulleritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Welgelegen. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Namibia, South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Fulleritermes*: Named after the entomologist C. Fuller; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *contractus*: Latin contractus (contracted)
