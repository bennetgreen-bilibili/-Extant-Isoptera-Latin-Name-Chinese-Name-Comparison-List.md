## Summary

***Reticulitermes largus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Ma in 1984; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Meihuashan, Liancheng. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *largus*: Latin largus (ample, abundant)
