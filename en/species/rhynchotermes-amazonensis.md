## Summary

***Rhynchotermes amazonensis*** is a species of the genus *Rhynchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantini & Cancello in 2016; the type locality is Brasil: Rondonia: Porto Velho, Mutum-Paraná \[9.4513S 64.8439W\]. Distribution: Colombia, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rhynchotermes*: From Greek rhynchos (beak, snout) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *amazonensis*: Named after the locality Amazon
