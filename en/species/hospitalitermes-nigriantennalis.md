## Summary

***Hospitalitermes nigriantennalis*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Syaukani & Thompson in 2016; the type locality is Indonesia: Kalimantan: Pararawen Nature Reserve. Distribution: Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *nigriantennalis*: From nigri + antennalis
