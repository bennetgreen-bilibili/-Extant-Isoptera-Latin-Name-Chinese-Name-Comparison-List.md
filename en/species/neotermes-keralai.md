## Summary

***Neotermes keralai*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Verma in 1972; the type locality is India: Kerala: Trivandrum: Chackai. Distribution: India, Kerala.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *keralai*: Named after Kerala (India)
