## Summary

***Anacanthotermes sawensis*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Al-Alawy, Abdul-Rassoul & Al-Azawi in 1990; the type locality is Iraq: Al-Mothana (Smawa), near Sawa Lake. Distribution: Iraq.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *sawensis*: Named after the locality Sawa (Lake Sawa, Iraq)
