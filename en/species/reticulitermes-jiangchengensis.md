## Summary

***Reticulitermes jiangchengensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1992; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Autonomous County of Jiangcheng: Kangping. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *jiangchengensis*: Named after the locality Jiangcheng
