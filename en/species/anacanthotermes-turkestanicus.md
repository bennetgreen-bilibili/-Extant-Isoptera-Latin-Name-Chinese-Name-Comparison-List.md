## Summary

***Anacanthotermes turkestanicus*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Jacobson in 1905; the type locality is Kazakhstan: Hungersteppe: station Golodnaja Stepj. Distribution: Middle East, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Iran, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *turkestanicus*: Named after Turkestan (Central Asia)
