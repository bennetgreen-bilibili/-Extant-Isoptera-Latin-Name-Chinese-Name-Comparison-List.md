## Summary

***Microtermes problematicus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé in 1937; the type locality is Senegal: Rufisque. Distribution: Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *problematicus*: Latin problematicus (problematic, doubtful)
