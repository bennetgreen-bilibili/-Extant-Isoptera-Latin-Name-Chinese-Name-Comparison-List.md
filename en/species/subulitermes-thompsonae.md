## Summary

***Subulitermes thompsonae*** is a species of the genus *Subulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]. Distribution: Peru, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Subulitermes*: From Latin subula (awl) + termes.

Specific epithet *thompsonae*: Named after Thompson
