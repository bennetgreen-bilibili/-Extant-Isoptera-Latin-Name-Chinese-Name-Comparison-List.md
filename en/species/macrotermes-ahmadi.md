## Summary

***Macrotermes ahmadi*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Tho in 1975; the type locality is Malaysia: Pahang: Fraser’s Hill. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malay Peninsula, Sumatra, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *ahmadi*: Named after M. Ahmad
