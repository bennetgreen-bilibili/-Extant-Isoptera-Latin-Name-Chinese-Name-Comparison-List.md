## Summary

***Cryptotermes cryptognathus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Jamaica: Booby South Point. Distribution: Jamaica.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *cryptognathus*: Greek kryptos (hidden) + gnathos (jaw), hidden-jawed
