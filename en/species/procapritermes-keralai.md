## Summary

***Procapritermes keralai*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani & Ferry in 1995; the type locality is India: Kerala: 5 km from road from Kothamangalam to Munnar. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *keralai*: Named after Kerala (India)
