## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes rothi*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Mt. Lambir. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *rothi*: Named after L. M. Roth (American entomologist)
