## Summary

***Coptotermes pamuae*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Solomon Islands: San Cristobal Island: Pamua. Distribution: Solomon Islands, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pamuae*: Named after Pamua, Solomon Islands
