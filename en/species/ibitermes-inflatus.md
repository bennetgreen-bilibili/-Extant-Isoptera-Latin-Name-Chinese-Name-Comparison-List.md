## Summary

***Ibitermes inflatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ibitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Vasconcellos in 2002; the type locality is Brasil: Paraíba: Reserva Biológica Guaribas \[6.8S 35.095W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ibitermes*: From Tupi Ibi (land, tree) + -termes.

Specific epithet *inflatus*: From infl + atus
