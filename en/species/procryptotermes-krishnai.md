## Summary

***Procryptotermes krishnai*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1961; the type locality is New Caledonia (French): Noumea. Distribution: New Caledonia, French New Caledonia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *krishnai*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna
