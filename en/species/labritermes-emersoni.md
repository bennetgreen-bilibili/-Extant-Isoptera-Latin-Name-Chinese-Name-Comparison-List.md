## Summary

***Labritermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Labritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Krishna & Adams in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Kapit District, Third Division: Batang Balleh: Sungei Mengiong: Nanga Tekalit Camp. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labritermes*: From Latin labrum (upper lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
