## Summary

***Neotermes malatensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1917; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Manila: Malate. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *malatensis*: Named after Malate, Manila, Philippines
