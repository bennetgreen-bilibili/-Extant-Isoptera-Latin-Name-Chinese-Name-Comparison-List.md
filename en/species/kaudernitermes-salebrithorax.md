## Summary

***Kaudernitermes salebrithorax*** is a species of the genus *Kaudernitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Madagascar: Tolanaro (Fort Dauphin); Seychelles: Silhouette. Distribution: Madagascar, Seychelles.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kaudernitermes*: Named after the explorer W. Kaudern; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *salebrithorax*: Latin salebra (roughness) + thorax (thorax), rough-thoraxed
