## Summary

***Procubitermes aburiensis*** is a species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Ghana: Accra. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea, Togo, Ghana, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procubitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *aburiensis*: Named after the locality Abur
