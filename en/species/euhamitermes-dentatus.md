## Summary

***Euhamitermes dentatus*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Thakur & Chatterjee in 1974; the type locality is India: Andhra Pradesh: Khammam Division: Annapradipalli Range, Lankapalli Block, compartment No. 266, Satpalli. Distribution: India, Andhra Pradesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *dentatus*: Latin dentatus (toothed)
