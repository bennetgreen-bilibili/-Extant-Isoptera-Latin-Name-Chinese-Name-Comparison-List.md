## Summary

***Syntermes crassilabrum*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1995; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Manaus, Reserva Ducke \[2.95S 59.95W\]. Distribution: Colombia, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *crassilabrum*: From the descriptive term 'crass' (thick)
