## Summary

***Glyptotermes truncatus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Colombia: Antioquia: Rio Porcecito \[ 6.55N 75.2167W \]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *truncatus*: Latin truncatus (truncated)
