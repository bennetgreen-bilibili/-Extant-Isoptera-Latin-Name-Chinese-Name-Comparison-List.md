## Summary

***Nasutitermes lividus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Burmeister in 1839; the type locality is Dominican Republic: Santo Domingo. Distribution: Haiti, Cuba, Dominican Republic.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *lividus*: From the descriptive term 'livid' (livid)
