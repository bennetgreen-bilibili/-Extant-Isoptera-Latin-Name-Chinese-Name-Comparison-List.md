## Summary

***Neocapritermes parvus*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[ 15.5961S 56.0967W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *parvus*: Latin parvus (small)
