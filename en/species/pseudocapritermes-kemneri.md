## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes kemneri*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Afzal in 1989; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Quoin Hill. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *kemneri*: Named after the entomologist N. A. Kemner
