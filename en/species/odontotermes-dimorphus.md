## Summary

***Odontotermes dimorphus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Li & Xiao in 1989; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Huashuichong Forest: Hexian. Distribution: Guangxi (Hexian Huashuichong Nature Reserve), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dimorphus*: Greek di- (two) + morphē (form), dimorphic
