## Summary

***Bicornitermes bicornis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Bicornitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1961; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bicornitermes*: From Latin bi- (two) + cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *bicornis*: From bi + cornis
