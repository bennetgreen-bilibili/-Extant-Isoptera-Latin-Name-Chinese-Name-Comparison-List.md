## Summary

***Parrhinotermes aequalis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia, Sarawak, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aequalis*: From aequ + alis
