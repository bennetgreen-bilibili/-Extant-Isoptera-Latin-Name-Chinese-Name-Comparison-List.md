## Summary

***Glyptotermes ueleensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1955; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Uele Forest: Tora, Yebo. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ueleensis*: Named after the locality Uele
