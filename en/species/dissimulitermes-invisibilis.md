## Summary

***Dissimulitermes invisibilis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Dissimulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Constantini & Cancello in 2020; the type locality is Brasil: Espírito Santo: Santa Teresa, Reserva Biológica de Augusto Ruschi \[ 19.9078S 40.5414W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dissimulitermes*: From Latin dissimilis (dissimilar) + termes.

Specific epithet *invisibilis*: Latin invisibilis (invisible)
