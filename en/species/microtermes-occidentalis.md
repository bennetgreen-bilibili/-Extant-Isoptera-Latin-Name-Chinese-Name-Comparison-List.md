## Summary

***Microtermes occidentalis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is South Africa: Graspan. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *occidentalis*: Latin occidentalis (western)
