## Summary

***Parrhinotermes shamimi*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bose in 1999; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: natural forest ca. 1 km from Lake Ganga, Itangar. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.
