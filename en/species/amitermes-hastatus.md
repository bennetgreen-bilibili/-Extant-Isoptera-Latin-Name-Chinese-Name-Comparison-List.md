## Summary

***Amitermes hastatus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Cape of Good Hope: Port Elizabeth. Distribution: southern Africa, Tanzania. This site records 11 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hastatus*: Latin hastatus (spear-shaped)
