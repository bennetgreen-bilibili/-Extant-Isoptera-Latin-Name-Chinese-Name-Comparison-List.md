## Summary

***Acorhinotermes subfusciceps*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Acorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: South America, French Guiana, Guyana, Colombia, Brazil, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acorhinotermes*: Contains Greek rhis (nose) + Latin termes; prefix derivation uncertain.

Specific epithet *subfusciceps*: From subfusci + ceps
