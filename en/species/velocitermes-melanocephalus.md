## Summary

***Velocitermes melanocephalus*** is a species of the genus *Velocitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: Pando: Rosario (near Lake Rogagua) \[13.9667S 66.8667W\]. Distribution: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Velocitermes*: From Latin velox (swift) + termes.

Specific epithet *melanocephalus*: Greek melas (black) + kephalē (head), black-headed
