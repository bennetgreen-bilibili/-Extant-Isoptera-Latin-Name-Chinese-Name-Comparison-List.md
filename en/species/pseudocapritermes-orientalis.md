## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes orientalis*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad & Akhtar in 1981; the type locality is Malaysia: Johore: Kluang Forest. Distribution: Pakistan, India, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *orientalis*: Latin orientalis (eastern)
