## Summary

***Microcerotermes shahroudiensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Ghayourfar in 2005; the type locality is Iran: Semnan Province: Shahroud, Ghaderabad. Distribution: Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *shahroudiensis*: Named after the locality Shahroud (Iran)
