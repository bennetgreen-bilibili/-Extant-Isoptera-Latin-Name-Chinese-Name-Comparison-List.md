## Summary

***Curvitermes minor*** is a species of the genus *Curvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[15.5961S 56.0967W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Curvitermes*: From Latin curvus (curved) + termes.

Specific epithet *minor*: Latin minor (smaller)
