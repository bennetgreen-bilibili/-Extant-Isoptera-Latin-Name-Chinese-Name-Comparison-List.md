## Summary

***Nasutitermes ehrhardti*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Brasil: Rio Grande do Sul; Santa Catarina: Blumenau \[26.91S 49.044W\]; Santa Catarina: Jaraguá do Sul \[26.4905S 49.0763W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *ehrhardti*: Named after Ehrhardt
