## Summary

***Stylotermes chengduensis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao & Zhu in 1980; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Chengdu. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chengduensis*: Named after the locality Chengdu
