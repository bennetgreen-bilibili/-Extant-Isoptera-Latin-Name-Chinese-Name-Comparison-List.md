## Summary

***Anoplotermes hondurensis*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Honduras: La Ceiba. Distribution: Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *hondurensis*: Named after the locality Hondur
