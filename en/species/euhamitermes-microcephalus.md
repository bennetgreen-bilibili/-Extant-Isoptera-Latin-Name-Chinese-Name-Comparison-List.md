## Summary

***Euhamitermes microcephalus*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Li in 1987; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Loudian. Distribution: Oriental region.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *microcephalus*: Greek mikros (small) + kephalē (head), small-headed
