## Summary

***Neotermes costaseca*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2018; the type locality is Peru: Lima: Lurín, Rio Lurín \[ 12.275S 76.879W \]. Distribution: Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *costaseca*: Portuguese costa (coast) + seca (dry)
