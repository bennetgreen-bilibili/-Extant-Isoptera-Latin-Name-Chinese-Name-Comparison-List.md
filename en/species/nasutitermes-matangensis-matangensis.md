## Summary

***Nasutitermes matangensis matangensis*** is a subspecies of *Nasutitermes matangensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), genus *Nasutitermes*. The subspecies was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Nasutitermes matangensis* below. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the subspecies.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *matangensis*: Named after Matang, Sarawak, Malaysia
