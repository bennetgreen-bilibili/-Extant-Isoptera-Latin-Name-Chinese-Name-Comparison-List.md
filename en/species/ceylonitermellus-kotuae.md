## Summary

***Ceylonitermellus kotuae*** is a species of the genus *Ceylonitermellus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Bugnion in 1914; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Kotua, 8 mi north of Galle. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ceylonitermellus*: From Ceylon (Sri Lanka) + diminutive -ellus (with termes).

Specific epithet *kotuae*: Named after Kotuwa (Galle, Sri Lanka)
