## Summary

***Sinocapritermes magnus*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Chonquing Shi. Distribution: China, Guizhou, Guangxi, Jiangxi, Chongqing.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *magnus*: Latin magnus (large)
