## Summary

***Prorhinotermes xishaensis*** is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Tsai in 1976; the type locality is China: Xisha Island. Distribution: China, Xisha Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prorhinotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *xishaensis*: Named after the locality Xisha
