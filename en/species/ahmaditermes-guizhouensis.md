## Summary

***Ahmaditermes guizhouensis*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li & Ping in 1982; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Cheting: Weinan Plantation. Distribution: China (Guizhou).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *guizhouensis*: After Guizhou (China)
