## Summary

***Silvestritermes euamignathus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Silvestritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]; Paraguay: Alto Paraná: Hernandarias (formerly Tacurú Pucú) \[25.3667S 54.6833W\]; Central: Asunción \[25.2667S 57.6667W\]; Guairá: Villa Rica \[25.75S 56.4333W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Venezuela, Bolivia, Paraguay. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Silvestritermes*: Named after the entomologist F. Silvestri; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *euamignathus*: Greek eu- (well) + gnathos (jaw)
