## Summary

***Odontotermes schmitzi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: West Africa, Benin, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *schmitzi*: Named after a person (Schmitz)
