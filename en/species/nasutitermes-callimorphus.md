## Summary

***Nasutitermes callimorphus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Costa Rica, Trinidad and Tobago, Bolivia, Ecuador, Guadeloupe (France), Martinique (France), Mexico, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *callimorphus*: Greek kalos (beautiful) + morphē (form), beautifully formed
