## Summary

***Speculitermes deccanensis*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Karnataka (Mysore): Bababudin Hills. Distribution: South Asia, Deccan, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *deccanensis*: Named after the locality Deccan
