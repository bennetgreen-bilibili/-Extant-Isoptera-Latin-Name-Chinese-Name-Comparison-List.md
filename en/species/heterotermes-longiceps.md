## Summary

***Heterotermes longiceps*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[ 15.627S 56.058W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longiceps*: Latin longus (long) + -ceps (head), long-headed
