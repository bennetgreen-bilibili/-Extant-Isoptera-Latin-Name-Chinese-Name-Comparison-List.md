## Summary

***Prorhinotermes ponapensis*** is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1917; the type locality is Micronesia: Pohnpei Island. Distribution: Micronesia, Pohnpei.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prorhinotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ponapensis*: Named after the locality Ponap
