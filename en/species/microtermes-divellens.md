## Summary

***Microtermes divellens*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Madagascar: Nosy Be. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *divellens*: Latin divellens (tearing apart)
