## Summary

***Polyspathotermes katangensis*** is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1927; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Lubumbashi. Distribution: Africa, Katanga, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *katangensis*: Named after the locality Katang
