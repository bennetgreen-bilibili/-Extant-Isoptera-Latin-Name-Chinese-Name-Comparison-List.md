## Summary

***Microcerotermes exiguus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Santarém \[ 2.43917S 54.705W \]. Distribution: Neotropics, Costa Rica, Brazil, Colombia, Venezuela, French Guiana, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *exiguus*: From the descriptive term 'exigu' (tiny)
