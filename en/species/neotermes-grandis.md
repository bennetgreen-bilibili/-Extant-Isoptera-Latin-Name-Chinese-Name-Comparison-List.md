## Summary

***Neotermes grandis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1930; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Mount Maquiling. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *grandis*: Latin grandis (great)
