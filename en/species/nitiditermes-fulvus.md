## Summary

***Nitiditermes fulvus*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1966; the type locality is Tanzania: Ugunga, Kakoma. Distribution: Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *fulvus*: From the descriptive term 'fulv' (tawny)
