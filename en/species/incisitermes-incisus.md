## Summary

***Incisitermes incisus*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Venezuela: Carabobo: Fila Saint Jean (ridge) \[ 10.3806N 67.9769W \]. Distribution: Guadeloupe, Barbados, Montserrat, Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Colombia, Cuba, Dominica, Netherlands Antilles, Puerto Rico, Saint Kitts and Nevis and Anguilla, Saint Lucia, French Saint Martin, Turks and Caicos Islands, Venezuela, British Virgin Islands, U.S. Virgin Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *incisus*: Latin incisus (incised, notched)
