## Summary

***Cryptotermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ranaweera in 1962; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Hewaheta. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
