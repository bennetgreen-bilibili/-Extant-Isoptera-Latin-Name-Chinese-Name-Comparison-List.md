## Summary

***Nasutitermes luzonicus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1914; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Los Banos. Distribution: Indonesia, Guam (USA), Malaysia, Philippines. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *luzonicus*: Named after the locality Luzon
