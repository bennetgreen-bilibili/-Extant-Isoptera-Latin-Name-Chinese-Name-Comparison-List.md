## Summary

***Proboscitermes tubuliferus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Proboscitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1907; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Proboscitermes*: From Greek proboskis (proboscis) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tubuliferus*: Latin tubulus (small tube) + -fer (bearing)
