## Summary

***Odontotermes yarangensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Huang in 1981; the type locality is China: Tibet: Motuo County. Distribution: Tibet (Medog Yarong 1000m), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *yarangensis*: Named after the type locality Yarang, Mêdog County, Tibet, China.
