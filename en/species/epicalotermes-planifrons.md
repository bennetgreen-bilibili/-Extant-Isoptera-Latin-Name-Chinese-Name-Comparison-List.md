## Summary

***Epicalotermes planifrons*** is a species of the genus *Epicalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1962; the type locality is Madagascar: 15 km SE Tuléar. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Epicalotermes*: From Greek epi- (upon) + kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *planifrons*: Latin planus (flat) + frons (front), flat-fronted
