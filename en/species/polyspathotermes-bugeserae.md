## Summary

***Polyspathotermes bugeserae*** is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Bouillon & Vincke in 1971; the type locality is Rwanda: Bugesera. Distribution: Rwanda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bugeserae*: Named after the locality Bugesera
