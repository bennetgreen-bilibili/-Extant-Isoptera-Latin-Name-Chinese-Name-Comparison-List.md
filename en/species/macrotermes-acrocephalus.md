## Summary

***Macrotermes acrocephalus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping in 1985; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Ruili. Distribution: Yunnan, China, Ruili.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *acrocephalus*: Greek akros (apex) + kephalē (head), pointed-headed
