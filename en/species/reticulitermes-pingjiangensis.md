## Summary

***Reticulitermes pingjiangensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Peng in 1983; the type locality is China: Hunan Province: Pingjiang. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *pingjiangensis*: Named after the locality Pingjiang
