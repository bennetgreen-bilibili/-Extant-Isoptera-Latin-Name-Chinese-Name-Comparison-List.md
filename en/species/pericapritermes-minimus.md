## Summary

***Pericapritermes minimus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1956; the type locality is Angola: Dundo. Distribution: Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *minimus*: Latin minimus (smallest)
