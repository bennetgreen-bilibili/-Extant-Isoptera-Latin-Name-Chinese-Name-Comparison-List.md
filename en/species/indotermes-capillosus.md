## Summary

***Indotermes capillosus*** is a species of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thakur in 1965; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun: Dehra Dun Forest Division, Asarori Range, Chandervani Block, Sal Forest. Distribution: Oriental region.

## Etymology

Genus name *Indotermes*: From India + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *capillosus*: From capill + osus
