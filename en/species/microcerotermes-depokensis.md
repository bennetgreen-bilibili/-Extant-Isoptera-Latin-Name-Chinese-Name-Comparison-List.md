## Summary

***Microcerotermes depokensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Depok, near Bogor. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *depokensis*: Named after the locality Depok
