## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes fissifex*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2001; the type locality is Malaysia: Selangor: Sungei Lalang Forest Reserve. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *fissifex*: Latin fissus (cleft) + -fex (maker)
