## Summary

***Reticulitermes chayuensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Huang in 1975; the type locality is China: Tibet: Chayu. Distribution: China (Tibet).

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *chayuensis*: Named after the locality Chayu
