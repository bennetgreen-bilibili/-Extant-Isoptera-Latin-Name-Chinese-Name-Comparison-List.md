## Summary

***Labritermes buttelreepeni*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Labritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labritermes*: From Latin labrum (upper lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *buttelreepeni*: Named after the entomologist H. von Buttel-Reepen
