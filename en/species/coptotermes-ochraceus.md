## Summary

***Coptotermes ochraceus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Xingyi County. Distribution: China (Guizhou). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ochraceus*: Latin ochraceus (ochre)
