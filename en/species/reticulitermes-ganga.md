## Summary

***Reticulitermes ganga*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bose in 1999; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: Lower Subansiri District: Itanagar: Lake Ganga. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *ganga*: Named after the Ganges (Ganga)
