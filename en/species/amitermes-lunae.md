## Summary

***Amitermes lunae*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn & Huchet in 2010; the type locality is Peru: La Libertad: Huaca de la Luna, near Trujillo \[ 8.135S 78.991W \]. Distribution: Peru, Ecuador.
