## Summary

***Odontotermes gurdaspurensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Punjab: Gurdaspur. Distribution: South Asia, India, Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gurdaspurensis*: Named after Gurdaspur (Punjab, India)
