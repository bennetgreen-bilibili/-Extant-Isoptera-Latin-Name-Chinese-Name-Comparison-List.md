## Summary

***Sinocapritermes sinicus*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Li & Xiao in 1989; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Huashuichong Forest: Hexian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *sinicus*: Named after the locality Sin
