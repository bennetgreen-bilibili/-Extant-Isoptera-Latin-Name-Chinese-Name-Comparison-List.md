## Summary

***Microtermes sindensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1906; the type locality is Pakistan: Sind: Karachi. Distribution: South Asia, Sindh, Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *sindensis*: Named after Sind (Pakistan)
