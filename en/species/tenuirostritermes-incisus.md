## Summary

***Tenuirostritermes incisus*** is a species of the genus *Tenuirostritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1922; the type locality is Honduras: San Pedro. Distribution: Mexico, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tenuirostritermes*: From Latin tenuis (slender) + rostrum (beak) + termes.

Specific epithet *incisus*: Latin incisus (incised, notched)
