## Summary

***Speculitermes triangularis*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *triangularis*: Latin triangularis (triangular)
