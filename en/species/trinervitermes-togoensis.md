## Summary

***Trinervitermes togoensis*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Ghana: Ho. Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Togo, Burkina Faso, India, Niger, Benin, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Ghana, Guinea, Mali, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Sudan, Uganda. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *togoensis*: Named after the locality Togo
