## Summary

***Coptotermes acinaciformis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Halls Creek. Distribution: Australia, introduced: New Zealand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *acinaciformis*: From acinaci + formis
