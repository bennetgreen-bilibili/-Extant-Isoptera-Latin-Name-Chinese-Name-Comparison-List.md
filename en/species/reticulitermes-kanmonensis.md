## Summary

***Reticulitermes kanmonensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Takematsu in 1999; the type locality is Japan: Yamaguchi Province: Atsugi. Distribution: Japan, Korean Peninsula, China, Taiwan, South Korea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *kanmonensis*: Named after the Kanmon Strait (Japan)
