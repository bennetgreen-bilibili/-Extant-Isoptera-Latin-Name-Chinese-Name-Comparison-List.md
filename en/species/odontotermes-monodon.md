## Summary

***Odontotermes monodon*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Gerstäcker in 1891; the type locality is Mozambique: Quilimane, Kikengo. Distribution: Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *monodon*: Greek monos (single) + odous (tooth), one-toothed
