## Summary

***Reticulitermes solidimandibulas*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Xiao in 1989; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Damingshan, Wuming. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *solidimandibulas*: Latin solidus (solid) + mandibula (mandible), solid-mandibled
