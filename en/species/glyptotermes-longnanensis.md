## Summary

***Glyptotermes longnanensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao & Zhu in 1980; the type locality is China: Gansu Province: Wen Xian. Distribution: China (Gansu).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longnanensis*: Named after the locality Longnan
