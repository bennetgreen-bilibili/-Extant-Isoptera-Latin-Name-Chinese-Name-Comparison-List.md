## Summary

***Labiotermes oreadicus*** is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 2006; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Serra da Mesa \[13.8288S 48.3211W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiotermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *oreadicus*: Greek Oreas (a mountain nymph, an Oread)
