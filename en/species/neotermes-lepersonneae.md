## Summary

***Neotermes lepersonneae*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1955; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mongbwalu. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lepersonneae*: Named after Lepersonne (likely Belgian geologist Jacques Lepersonne)
