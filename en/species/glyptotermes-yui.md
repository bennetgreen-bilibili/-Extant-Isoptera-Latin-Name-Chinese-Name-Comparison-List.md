## Summary

***Glyptotermes yui*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Wanling County. Distribution: China (Hainan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *yui*: Named after the Chinese termite taxonomist Qiwei You.
