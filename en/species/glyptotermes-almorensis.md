## Summary

***Glyptotermes almorensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gardner in 1945; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Almora District: Bajwar. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *almorensis*: Named after the locality Almora
