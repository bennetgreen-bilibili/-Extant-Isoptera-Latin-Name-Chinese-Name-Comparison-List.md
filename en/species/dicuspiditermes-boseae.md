## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes boseae*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1997; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: ca. 50 km NW of Nagercoil: Kulasekaram Forest Range: Mylar. Distribution: India, Tamil Nadu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *boseae*: Named after G. Bose
