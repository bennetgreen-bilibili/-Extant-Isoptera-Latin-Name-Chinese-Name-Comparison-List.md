## Summary

***Euchilotermes acutidens*** is a species of the genus *Euchilotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Ghana: Aburi. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euchilotermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + cheilos (lip, margin) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *acutidens*: From the descriptive term 'acut' (sharp)
