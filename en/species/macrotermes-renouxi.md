## Summary

***Macrotermes renouxi*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Rouland in 1993; the type locality is Congo: Mvouti district: Mayombe, near Koulila. Distribution: Cameroon, Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *renouxi*: Named after a person "Renoux" (identity unconfirmed)
