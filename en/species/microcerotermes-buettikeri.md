## Summary

***Microcerotermes buettikeri*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani & Bose in 1979; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Wadi Tumair. Distribution: Middle East, Saudi Arabia, Iran, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *buettikeri*: Named after Willi Büttiker
