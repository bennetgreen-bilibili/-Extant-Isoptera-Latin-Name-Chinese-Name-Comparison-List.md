## Summary

***Profastigitermes putnami*** is the only extant species of the genus *Profastigitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1960; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Profastigitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin fastigium (ridge) + termes.

Specific epithet *putnami*: Named in honor of the late Mr. Patrick Putnam who, with his wife Mrs. Ann Putnam, enabled the author to collect in the vicinity of his camp in the Belgian Congo.
