## Summary

***Nasutitermes diabolus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1907; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Nigeria, Uganda. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *diabolus*: Latin diabolus (devil)
