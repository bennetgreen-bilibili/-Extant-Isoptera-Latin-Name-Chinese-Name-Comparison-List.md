## Summary

***Nitiditermes testaceus*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1966; the type locality is Uganda: Kawanda. Distribution: Kenya, Rwanda, South Africa, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *testaceus*: Latin testaceus (brick-coloured)
