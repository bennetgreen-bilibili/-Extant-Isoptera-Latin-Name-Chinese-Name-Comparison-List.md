## Summary

***Reticulitermes aegeus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ghesini & Marini in 2015; the type locality is Cyprus: Kakopetria. Distribution: eastern Mediterranean, Croatia, Greece, Turkey, Cyprus.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *aegeus*: Latin Aegeus (Aegean, or the name Aegeus)
