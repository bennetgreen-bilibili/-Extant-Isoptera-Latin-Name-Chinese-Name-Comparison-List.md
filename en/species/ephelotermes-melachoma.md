## Summary

***Ephelotermes melachoma*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ephelotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Miller in 1991; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: 10 km NWN Mt. Bundey. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ephelotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *melachoma*: From the descriptive term 'mela' (black)
