## Summary

***Neotermes taishanensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xu & Han in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Taishan. Distribution: China, Guangdong.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *taishanensis*: Named after the locality Taishan
