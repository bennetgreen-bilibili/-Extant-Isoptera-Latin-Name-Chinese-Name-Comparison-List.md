## Summary

***Procapritermes holmgreni*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Dariadighi Forest. Distribution: Bangladesh, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *holmgreni*: Named after the termite taxonomist N. Holmgren
