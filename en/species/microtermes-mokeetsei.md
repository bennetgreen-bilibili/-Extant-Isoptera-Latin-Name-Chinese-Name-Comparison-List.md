## Summary

***Microtermes mokeetsei*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Mokeetse (Koedoes River. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *mokeetsei*: Named after Mokeetse
