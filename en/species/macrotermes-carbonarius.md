## Summary

***Macrotermes carbonarius*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Malaysia: Borneo; Penang. Distribution: Southeast Asia, East Asia, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Cambodia, Indonesia, Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *carbonarius*: Latin carbonarius (charcoal, carbon-coloured)
