## Summary

***Humutermes krishnai*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Humutermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2016; the type locality is French Guiana: Nouragues Inselberg station \[ 4.0884N 52.6814W \]. Distribution: Neotropics, Panama, Colombia, French Guiana, Brazil, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Humutermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *krishnai*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna
