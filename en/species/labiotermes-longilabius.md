## Summary

***Labiotermes longilabius*** is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[15.5961S 56.0967W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiotermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *longilabius*: From the descriptive term 'long' (long)
