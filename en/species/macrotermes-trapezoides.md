## Summary

***Macrotermes trapezoides*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Hainan Island: Ya County. Distribution: Hainan (Yaxian) and Yunnan (Baoshan), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *trapezoides*: Greek trapezion (trapezium) + -oides (like), trapezoid
