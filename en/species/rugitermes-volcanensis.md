## Summary

***Rugitermes volcanensis*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2020; the type locality is Bolivia: Santa Cruz: Amboró National Park \[ 18.1197S 63.6088W \]. Distribution: Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *volcanensis*: Latin volcanus (volcano) + -ensis: volcanic, of volcanic habitat
