## Summary

***Neotermes firmus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Kenya: River Luazomela near Guaso Nyiri N of Mt Kenya. Distribution: Kenya.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *firmus*: Latin firmus (firm, strong)
