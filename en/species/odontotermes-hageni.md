## Summary

***Odontotermes hageni*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Borneo. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hageni*: Named after H. A. Hagen
