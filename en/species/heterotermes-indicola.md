## Summary

***Heterotermes indicola*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Mumbai. Distribution: South Asia, Pakistan, India, Afghanistan, Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *indicola*: Latin India + -cola (dweller): dwelling in India
