## Summary

***Ancistrotermes dimorphus*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: ChinPing. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dimorphus*: Greek di- (two) + morphē (form), dimorphic
