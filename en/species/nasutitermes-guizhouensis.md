## Summary

***Nasutitermes guizhouensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1988; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Danzhai. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *guizhouensis*: After Guizhou (China)
