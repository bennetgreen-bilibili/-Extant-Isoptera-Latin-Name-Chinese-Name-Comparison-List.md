## Summary

***Cubitermes caesareus*** is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *caesareus*: Latin caesareus (of Caesar, imperial)
