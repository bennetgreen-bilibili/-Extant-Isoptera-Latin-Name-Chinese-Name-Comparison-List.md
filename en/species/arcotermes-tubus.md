## Summary

***Arcotermes tubus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Arcotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Fan in 1983; the type locality is China: Jiangxi Province: Xinfeng. Distribution: China (Jiangxi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Arcotermes*: From Latin arcus (bow, arch) + termes.

Specific epithet *tubus*: Latin tubus (tube)
