## Summary

***Odontotermes zambesiensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1914; the type locality is Mozambique: Villa Fontes. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zambesiensis*: Named after the locality Zambes
