## Summary

***Glyptotermes nicobarensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1981; the type locality is India: Nicobar Islands: Great Nicobar Island: Campbell Bay, Laksman Beach. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nicobarensis*: Named after the Nicobar Islands
