## Summary

***Nasutitermes bolivianus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Mojos \[14.5667S 68.8833W\]. Distribution: Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *bolivianus*: After Bolivia
