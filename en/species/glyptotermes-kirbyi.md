## Summary

***Glyptotermes kirbyi*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: swamp forest of Kateman. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kirbyi*: Named after the entomologist W. F. Kirby
