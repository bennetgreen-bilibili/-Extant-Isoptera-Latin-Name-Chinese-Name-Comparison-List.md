## Summary

***Procryptotermes leewardensis*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 2001; the type locality is Saint Martin (France): Marigot. Distribution: Caribbean.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *leewardensis*: Named after the Leeward Islands (West Indies)
