## Summary

***Grallatotermes weyeri*** is a species of the genus *Grallatotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1931; the type locality is Indonesia: Maluku: Ambon; Saparoea. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Grallatotermes*: From Latin grallator (stilt-walker) + termes.

Specific epithet *weyeri*: Named after Weyer
