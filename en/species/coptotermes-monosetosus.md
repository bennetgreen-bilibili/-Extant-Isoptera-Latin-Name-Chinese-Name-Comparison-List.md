## Summary

***Coptotermes monosetosus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Li in 1985; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Yaxian County. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *monosetosus*: Greek monos (single) + Latin setosus (bristly), one-bristled
