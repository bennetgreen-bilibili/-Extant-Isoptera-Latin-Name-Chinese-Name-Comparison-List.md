## Summary

***Neotermes aburiensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Ghana: Aburi. Distribution: West Africa, Nigeria, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aburiensis*: Named after the locality Abur
