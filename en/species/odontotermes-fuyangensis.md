## Summary

***Odontotermes fuyangensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Zhu in 1986; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Fuyang County. Distribution: China (Zhejiang).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fuyangensis*: Named after the locality Fuyang
