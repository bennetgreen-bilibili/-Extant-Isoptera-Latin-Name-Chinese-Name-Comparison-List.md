## Summary

***Neocapritermes guyana*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna & Araujo in 1968; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Makouria Creek \[ 6.4666N 58.5833W \]. Distribution: French Guiana, Brazil, Guyana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *guyana*: After Guyana
