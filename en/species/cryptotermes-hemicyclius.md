## Summary

***Cryptotermes hemicyclius*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bacchus in 1987; the type locality is Jamaica: Portland Ridge. Distribution: Jamaica.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *hemicyclius*: Greek hemi- (half) + kyklos (circle), semicircular
