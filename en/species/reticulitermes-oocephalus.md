## Summary

***Reticulitermes oocephalus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Li in 1981; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Dazhulan, Jianyang Xian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *oocephalus*: Greek ōon (egg) + kephalē (head), egg-headed
