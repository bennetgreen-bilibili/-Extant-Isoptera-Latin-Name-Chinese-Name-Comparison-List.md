## Summary

***Hospitalitermes papuanus*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1947; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Strickland River. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *papuanus*: After Papua (New Guinea)
