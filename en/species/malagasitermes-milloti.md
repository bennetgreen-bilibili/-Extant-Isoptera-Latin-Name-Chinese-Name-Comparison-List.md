## Summary

***Malagasitermes milloti*** is the only extant species of the genus *Malagasitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cachan in 1949; the type locality is Madagascar: Tampoketsa d’Ankazobé, Ambohitantely. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Malagasitermes*: From Malagasy (Madagascar) + termes.

Specific epithet *milloti*: Named after Millot (French zoologist Jacques Millot)
