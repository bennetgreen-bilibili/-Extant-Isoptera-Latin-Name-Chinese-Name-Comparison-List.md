## Summary

***Coptotermes dobonicus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1914; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Arue Island: Dobo. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dobonicus*: Named after the locality Dobon
