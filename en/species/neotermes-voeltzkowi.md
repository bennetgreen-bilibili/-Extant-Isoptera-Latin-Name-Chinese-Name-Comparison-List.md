## Summary

***Neotermes voeltzkowi*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1897; the type locality is Madagascar: Majunga. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *voeltzkowi*: Named after Alfred Voeltzkow (German naturalist)
