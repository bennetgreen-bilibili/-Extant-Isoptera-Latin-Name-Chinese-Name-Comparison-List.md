## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes planimentus*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1992; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jianshei County. Distribution: Yunnan (Jianshui1450 m), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *planimentus*: Latin planus (flat) + mentum (chin), flat-chinned
