## Summary

***Protermes minimus*** is a species of the genus *Protermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ruelle in 1971; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: 40 km ex Kasongo-Lunda to Panzi road. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea, Nigeria, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Protermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minimus*: Latin minimus (smallest)
