## Summary

***Anoplotermes howardi*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: Pando: Rosario (near Lake Rogagua) \[ 13.9667S 66.8667W \]. Distribution: Bolivia, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *howardi*: Named after Leland O. Howard
