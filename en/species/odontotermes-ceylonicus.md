## Summary

***Odontotermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Nalanda. Distribution: South Asia, Sri Lanka, India. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
