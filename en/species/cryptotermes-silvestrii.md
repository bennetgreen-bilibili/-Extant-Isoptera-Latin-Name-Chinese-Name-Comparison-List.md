## Summary

***Cryptotermes silvestrii*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bacchus in 1987; the type locality is Nigeria: Nkpoku. Distribution: Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *silvestrii*: Named after the entomologist F. Silvestri
