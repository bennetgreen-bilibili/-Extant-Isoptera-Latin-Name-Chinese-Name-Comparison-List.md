## Summary

***Gastrotermes spinatus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Gastrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2020; the type locality is Cameroon: Korup National Park. Distribution: West/Central Africa, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Gastrotermes*: From Greek gastēr (belly) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *spinatus*: From spin + atus
