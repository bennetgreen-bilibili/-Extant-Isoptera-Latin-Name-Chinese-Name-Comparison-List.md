## Summary

***Nitiditermes proximatus*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kindia; Mamou. Distribution: West Africa, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, Kenya, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *proximatus*: From proxim + atus
