## Summary

***Hospitalitermes bicolor*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Singapore: Pulo Brani. Distribution: Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *bicolor*: Latin bi- (two) + color (colour), two-coloured
