## Summary

***Heterotermes balwanti*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Mathur & Chhotani in 1969; the type locality is India: Orissa: Balukhand Forest Range, near Puri. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *balwanti*: Named after Balwant
