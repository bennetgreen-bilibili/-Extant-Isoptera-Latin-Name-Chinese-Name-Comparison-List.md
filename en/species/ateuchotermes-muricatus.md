## Summary

***Ateuchotermes muricatus*** is a species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Sibasa. Distribution: Malawi, South Africa, Eswatini.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ateuchotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + teuchos (implement, weapon) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *muricatus*: From muric + atus
