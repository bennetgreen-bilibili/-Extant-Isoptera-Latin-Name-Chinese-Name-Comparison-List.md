## Summary

***Rugitermes unicolor*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1952; the type locality is Guatemala: Salacóc, in the Alta Vera Páz. Distribution: Costa Rica, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *unicolor*: Latin unus (one) + color (colour), one-coloured
