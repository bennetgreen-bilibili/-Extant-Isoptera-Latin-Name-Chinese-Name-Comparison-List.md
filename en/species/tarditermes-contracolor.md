## Summary

***Tarditermes contracolor*** is the only extant species of the genus *Tarditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1960; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Pygmy Camp, 4 km N of Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Congo (Kinshasa), Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tarditermes*: From Latin tardus (slow) + termes.

Specific epithet *contracolor*: Latin contra (against) + color (colour), contrasting colour
