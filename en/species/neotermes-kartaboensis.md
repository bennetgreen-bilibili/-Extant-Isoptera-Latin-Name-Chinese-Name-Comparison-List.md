## Summary

***Neotermes kartaboensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: French Guiana, Guyana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kartaboensis*: Named after Kartabo (Guyana)
