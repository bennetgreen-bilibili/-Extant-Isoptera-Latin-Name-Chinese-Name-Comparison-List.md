## Summary

***Orthognathotermes wheeleri*** is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1923; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Amazon region, Colombia, Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wheeleri*: Named after the myrmecologist W. M. Wheeler
