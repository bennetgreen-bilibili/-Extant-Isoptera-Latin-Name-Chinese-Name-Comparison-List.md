## Summary

***Cahuallitermes intermedius*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Cahuallitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1922; the type locality is Honduras: La Ceiba. Distribution: Mexico, Guatemala, Honduras, Belize.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cahuallitermes*: From the place name Cahualli; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *intermedius*: Latin intermedius (intermediate)
