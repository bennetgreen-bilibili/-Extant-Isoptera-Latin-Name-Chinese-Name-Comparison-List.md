## Summary

***Reticulitermes yingdeensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Li in 1977; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Yingde. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *yingdeensis*: Named after the locality Yingde
