## Summary

***Rugitermes rugosus*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Brasil: Rio de Janeiro: Teresópolis \[ 22.409S 42.963W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay, Uruguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *rugosus*: Latin rugosus (wrinkled)
