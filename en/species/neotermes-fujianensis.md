## Summary

***Neotermes fujianensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1983; the type locality is China: Fujian province: Nanjimg County. Distribution: China, Fujian, Guangdong, Guangxi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fujianensis*: Named after the locality Fujian
