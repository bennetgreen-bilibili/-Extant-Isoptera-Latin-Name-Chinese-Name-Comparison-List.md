## Summary

***Nasutitermes sanctaeanae*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Argentina: Misiones: Santa Ana \[27.37S 55.58W\]. Distribution: Paraguay, Argentina.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *sanctaeanae*: Named after Santa Ana
