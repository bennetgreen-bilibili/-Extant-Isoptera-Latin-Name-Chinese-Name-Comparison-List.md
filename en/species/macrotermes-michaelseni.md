## Summary

***Macrotermes michaelseni*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1914; the type locality is Namibia: Okahandja. Distribution: Old World tropics, East Africa, southern Africa, Angola, Botswana, Cameroon, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, South Africa, Eswatini, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 4 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *michaelseni*: Named after W. Michaelsen
