## Summary

***Nasutitermes acajutlae*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Virgin Islands (USA): St. Thomas. Distribution: Colombia, Puerto Rico, United States, French Guiana, Trinidad and Tobago, Antigua and Barbuda, Barbados, Montserrat, Suriname, Venezuela, British Virgin Islands, United States Virgin Islands. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *acajutlae*: Named after Acajutla, El Salvador
