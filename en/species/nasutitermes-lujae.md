## Summary

***Nasutitermes lujae*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai). Distribution: Cameroon, Nigeria, Gabon, Uganda, Angola, Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire. This site records 5 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *lujae*: Named after Luja (Luxembourg naturalist Édouard Luja)
