## Summary

***Globitermes vadaensis*** is a species of the genus *Globitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Vada, near Tjandjoer. Distribution: Southeast Asian islands, Malay Archipelago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Globitermes*: From Latin globus (ball, sphere) + termes.

Specific epithet *vadaensis*: Named after the locality Vada (precise location uncertain).
