## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes butteli*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Malaysia: Taiping: Maxwell’s Hill. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *butteli*: Named after Buttel(-Reepen)
