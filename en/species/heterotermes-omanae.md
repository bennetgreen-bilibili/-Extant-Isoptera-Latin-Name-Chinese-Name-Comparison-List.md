## Summary

***Heterotermes omanae*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chhotani in 1988; the type locality is Oman: Bayt al Falaj Compound, near Muscat. Distribution: Oman.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *omanae*: Named after Oman
