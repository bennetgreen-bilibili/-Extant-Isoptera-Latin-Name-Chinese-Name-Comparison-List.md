## Summary

***Cyranotermes glaber*** is a species of the genus *Cyranotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1990; the type locality is Brasil: Amapá: Macapá, IEPA Fazendinha \[0.04N 51.1W\]. Distribution: French Guiana, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cyranotermes*: From Cyrano (de Bergerac, famed for his long nose) + termes, for the long nasus.

Specific epithet *glaber*: Latin glaber (smooth, hairless)
