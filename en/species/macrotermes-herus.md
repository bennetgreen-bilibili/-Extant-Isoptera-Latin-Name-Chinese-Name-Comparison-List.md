## Summary

***Macrotermes herus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1914; the type locality is Ethiopia: Dire-Dauna. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, East Africa, Ethiopia, Guinea, Kenya, Nigeria, Somalia, Sudan, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *herus*: Latin herus (master)
