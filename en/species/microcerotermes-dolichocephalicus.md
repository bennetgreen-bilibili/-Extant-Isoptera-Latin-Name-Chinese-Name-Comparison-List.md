## Summary

***Microcerotermes dolichocephalicus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Lam in 1992; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Rong County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dolichocephalicus*: From Greek dolichos (long) + kephale (head), long-headed
