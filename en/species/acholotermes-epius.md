## Summary

***Acholotermes epius*** is a species of the genus *Acholotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Zambia: Lusaka. Distribution: tropical Africa, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acholotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + cholē (bile) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *epius*: Greek ēpios (gentle)
