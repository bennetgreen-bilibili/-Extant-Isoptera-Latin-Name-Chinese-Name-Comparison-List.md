## Summary

***Reticulitermes levatoriceps*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by He & Qui in 1990; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Guiyang County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *levatoriceps*: From levatori + ceps
