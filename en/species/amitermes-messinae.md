## Summary

***Amitermes messinae*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Messina. Distribution: South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Kenya, Malawi, Saudi Arabia, Sudan, Tanzania, Yemen, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *messinae*: Named after Messina (Musina), South Africa
