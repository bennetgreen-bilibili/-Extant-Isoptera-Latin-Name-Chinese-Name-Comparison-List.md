## Summary

***Cornitermes acignathus*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Ecuador: Guayas: Guayaquil \[2.16667S 79.9W\]. Distribution: Costa Rica, Colombia, Panama, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *acignathus*: Latin acies (point, edge) + Greek gnathos (jaw), sharp-jawed
