## Summary

***Drepanotermes rubriceps*** is a species of the genus *Drepanotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory, near Alice Springs. Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Drepanotermes*: From Greek drepanon (sickle) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rubriceps*: From rubri + ceps
