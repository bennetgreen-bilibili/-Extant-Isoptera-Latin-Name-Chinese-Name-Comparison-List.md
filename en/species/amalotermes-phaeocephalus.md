## Summary

***Amalotermes phaeocephalus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Amalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Nigeria: Eastern Region: 40 mi from Port Harcourt on Owerri Road. Distribution: Central Africa, Congo Basin, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amalotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *phaeocephalus*: From the descriptive term 'phae' (brown)
