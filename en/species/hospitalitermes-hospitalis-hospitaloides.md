## Summary

***Hospitalitermes hospitalis hospitaloides*** is a subspecies of *Hospitalitermes hospitalis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), genus *Hospitalitermes*. The subspecies was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Miri Hills. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Hospitalitermes hospitalis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *hospitaloides*: Latin hospitalis (of a guest) + Greek -oides (like)
