## Summary

***Euhamitermes zhejianensis*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by He & Xia in 1983; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Quxian. Distribution: China, Zhejiang.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *zhejianensis*: Named after the locality Zhejian
