## Summary

***Procryptotermes hunsurensis*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thakur in 1975; the type locality is India: Mysore: Kalbetta, Hunsur. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hunsurensis*: Named after Hunsur (Karnataka, India)
