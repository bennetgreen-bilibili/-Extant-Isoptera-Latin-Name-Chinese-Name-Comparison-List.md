## Summary

***Reticulitermes mirus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao, Zhu & Zhao in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Zhaoquing. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *mirus*: Latin mirus (wonderful)
