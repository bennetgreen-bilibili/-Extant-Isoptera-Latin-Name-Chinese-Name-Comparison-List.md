## Summary

***Oriencapritermes kluangensis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Oriencapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad & Akhtar in 1981; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Kluang Forest. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Oriencapritermes*: From Latin Oriens (the East) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *kluangensis*: Named after the locality Kluang
