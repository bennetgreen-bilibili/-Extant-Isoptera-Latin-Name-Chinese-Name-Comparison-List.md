## Summary

***Microcerotermes brevior*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1905; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Astrolabe Bay: Stephansort. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brevior*: From the descriptive term 'brev' (short)
