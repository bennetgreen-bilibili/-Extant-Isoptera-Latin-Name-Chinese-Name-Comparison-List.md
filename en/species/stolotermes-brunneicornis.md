## Summary

***Stolotermes brunneicornis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Stolotermes*, in the family STOLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Australia: Tasmania. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stolotermes*: From Latin stolo (shoot, stolon) + termes.

Specific epithet *brunneicornis*: From brunnei + cornis
