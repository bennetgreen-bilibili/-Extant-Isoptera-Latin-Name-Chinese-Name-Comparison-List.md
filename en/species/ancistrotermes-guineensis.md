## Summary

***Ancistrotermes guineensis*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama: Rio Cassine. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Benin, Gambia, Senegal, Cameroon, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *guineensis*: After Guinea
