## Summary

***Reticulitermes leptomandibularis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hsia & Fan in 1965; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Yung-an. Distribution: East Asia, Taiwan, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *leptomandibularis*: From leptomandibul + aris
