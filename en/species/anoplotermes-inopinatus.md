## Summary

***Anoplotermes inopinatus*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 2006; the type locality is Bahamas: Eleuthera Island: North Bluff. Distribution: Bahamas.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *inopinatus*: Latin inopinatus (unexpected)
