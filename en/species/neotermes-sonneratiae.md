## Summary

***Neotermes sonneratiae*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Batavia. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sonneratiae*: After the plant genus Sonneratia
