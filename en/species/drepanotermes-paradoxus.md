## Summary

***Drepanotermes paradoxus*** is a species of the genus *Drepanotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Watson & Perry in 1981; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: 10 mi (16 km) SE of Roebourne. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Drepanotermes*: From Greek drepanon (sickle) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paradoxus*: Greek paradoxos (paradoxical, strange)
