## Summary

***Onkotermes corochus*** is a species of the genus *Onkotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Torales & Fontes in 2008; the type locality is Argentina: Chaco: Lapachito \[ 27.1667S 59.4W \]. Distribution: Argentina, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Onkotermes*: From Greek onkos (bend, hook, barb) + Latin termes. Greek onkos also means "bulk, tumor".

Specific epithet *corochus*: Etymology uncertain; likely from a South American indigenous language (corocho/corozo, a local name for palms/trees; the type locality Corrientes is Guaraní-speaking), not Greek or Latin. The Chinese name "" (tumored frons) is descriptive of the soldier's frontal projection.
