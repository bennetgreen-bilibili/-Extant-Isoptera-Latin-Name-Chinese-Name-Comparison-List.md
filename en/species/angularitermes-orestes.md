## Summary

***Angularitermes orestes*** is a species of the genus *Angularitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Araujo in 1970; the type locality is Brasil: Minas Gerais: Serra do Curral \[19.9667S 43.9167W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angularitermes*: From Latin angularis (angular) + termes.

Specific epithet *orestes*: Greek Orestes (mountain-dweller, or the hero)
