## Summary

***Heterotermes assu*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Constantino in 2000; the type locality is Brasil: Espírito Santo: Presidente Kennedy, Praia das Neves \[ 21.28S 40.97W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *assu*: Named after the locality Assu (Açu)
