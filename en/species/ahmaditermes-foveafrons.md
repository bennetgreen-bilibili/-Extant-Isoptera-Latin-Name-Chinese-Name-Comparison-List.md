## Summary

***Ahmaditermes foveafrons*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gao in 1988; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Mt. Tianmu. Distribution: Zhejiang (Tianmu Mountain).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *foveafrons*: From fovea + frons
