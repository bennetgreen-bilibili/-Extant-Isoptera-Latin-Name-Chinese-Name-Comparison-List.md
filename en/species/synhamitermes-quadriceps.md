## Summary

***Synhamitermes quadriceps*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Synhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Khandala, near Bombay. Distribution: southeastern South America, Argentina/Paraguay/Uruguay, South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Synhamitermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *quadriceps*: From quadri + ceps
