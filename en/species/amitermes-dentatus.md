## Summary

***Amitermes dentatus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Marudi. Distribution: insular Southeast Asia, China, Thailand. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.
