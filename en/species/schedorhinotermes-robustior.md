## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes robustior*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1909; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: New Britain: Ralum Louson. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *robustior*: Latin robustior (more robust)
