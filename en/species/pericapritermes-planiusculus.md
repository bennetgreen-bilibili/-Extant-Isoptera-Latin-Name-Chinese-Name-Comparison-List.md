## Summary

***Pericapritermes planiusculus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1988; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Congjiang Xiang. Distribution: Guizhou (Congjiang)..

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *planiusculus*: From planiusc + ulus
