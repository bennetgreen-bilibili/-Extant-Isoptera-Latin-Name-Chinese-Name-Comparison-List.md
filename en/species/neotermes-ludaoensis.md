## Summary

***Neotermes ludaoensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wu, Tsai & Li in 2024; the type locality is Taiwan: Green Island \[ 22.65N 121.48E \]. Distribution: Taiwan, Green Island.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ludaoensis*: Named after Lüdao (Green Island), Taiwan
