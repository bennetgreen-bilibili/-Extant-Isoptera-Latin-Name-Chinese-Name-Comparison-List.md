## Summary

***Psammotermes rajasthanicus*** is a species of the genus *Psammotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1960; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Jaisalmer District, about 20 miles east of Mohangarh, Balana. Distribution: northwestern India, Pakistan, Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Psammotermes*: From Greek psammos (sand) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rajasthanicus*: Named after Rajasthan, India
