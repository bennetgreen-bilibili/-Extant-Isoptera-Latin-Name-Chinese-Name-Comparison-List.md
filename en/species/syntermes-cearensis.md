## Summary

***Syntermes cearensis*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1995; the type locality is Brasil: Ceará: Chapada do Araripe \[7.33S 40W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cearensis*: Named after the locality Cear
