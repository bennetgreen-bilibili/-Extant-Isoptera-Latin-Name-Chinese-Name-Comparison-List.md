## Summary

***Adaiphrotermes choanensis*** is a species of the genus *Adaiphrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Pretoria: Arcadia. Distribution: Kenya, Malawi, South Africa, Eswatini, Tanzania, Zimbabwe.
