## Summary

***Embiratermes festivellus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[15.5961S 56.0967W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Embiratermes*: From Tupi Embirá (a tree) + -termes.

Specific epithet *festivellus*: Latin festivus (festive) + -ellus (small), somewhat festive
