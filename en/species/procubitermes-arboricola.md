## Summary

***Procubitermes arboricola*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1897; the type locality is Cameroon: Bonge. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procubitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *arboricola*: Latin arbor (tree) + -cola (dweller): tree-dwelling
