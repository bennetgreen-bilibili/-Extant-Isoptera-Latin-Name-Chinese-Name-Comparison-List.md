## Summary

***Basidentitermes trilobatus*** is a species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Noirot in 1955; the type locality is Angola: Luimbale: Serra do Moco. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Basidentitermes*: From Greek basis (base) + Latin dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *trilobatus*: Latin tri- (three) + lobatus (lobed), three-lobed
