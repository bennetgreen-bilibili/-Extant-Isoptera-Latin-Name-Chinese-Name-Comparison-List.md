## Summary

***Odontotermes mohandi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Verma & Purohit in 1993; the type locality is India: Saharanpur district: Mohand Forest. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mohandi*: Named after Mohand
