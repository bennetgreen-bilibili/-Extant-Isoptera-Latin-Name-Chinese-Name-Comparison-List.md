## Summary

***Microcerotermes nicobarensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1970; the type locality is India: Nicobar Islands: Car Nicobar Island: Arong village. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nicobarensis*: Named after the Nicobar Islands
