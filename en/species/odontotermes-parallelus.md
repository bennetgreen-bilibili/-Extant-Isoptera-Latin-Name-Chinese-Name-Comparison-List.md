## Summary

***Odontotermes parallelus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Li in 1986; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Huiyang County. Distribution: Guangdong (Dabu), Hainan (Wuzhi Mountain), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parallelus*: From the descriptive term 'parallel' (parallel)
