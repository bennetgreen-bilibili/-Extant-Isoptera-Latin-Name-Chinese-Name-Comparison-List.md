## Summary

***Microcerotermes crassus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is Myanmar: Meitkyina. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Philippines, Thailand, Myanmar, Singapore, Bangladesh, China, India, Malaysia, Vietnam. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *crassus*: From the descriptive term 'crass' (thick)
