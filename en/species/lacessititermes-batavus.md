## Summary

***Lacessititermes batavus*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: between Bogor and Batavia: Depok; Salak (Tjidaho); Tjigouha (Soekabomi). Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *batavus*: Latin Batavus (of Batavia, the Netherlands)
