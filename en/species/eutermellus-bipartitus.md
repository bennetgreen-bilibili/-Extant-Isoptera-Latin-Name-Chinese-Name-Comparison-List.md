## Summary

***Eutermellus bipartitus*** is a species of the genus *Eutermellus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eutermellus*: From Greek eu- (well) + termes + Latin diminutive -ellus.

Specific epithet *bipartitus*: Latin bipartitus (divided in two)
