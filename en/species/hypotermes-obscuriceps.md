## Summary

***Hypotermes obscuriceps*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Hypotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Trincomali. Distribution: India, Sri Lanka, Vietnam. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hypotermes*: From Greek hypo- (under, slightly) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *obscuriceps*: From obscuri + ceps
