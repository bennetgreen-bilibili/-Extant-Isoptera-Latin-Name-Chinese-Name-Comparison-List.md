## Summary

***Reticulitermes emei*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao, Zhu, Gong & Han in 1981; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Mt. Emei. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *emei*: Named after Mount Emei (Sichuan)
