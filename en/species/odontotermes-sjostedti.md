## Summary

***Odontotermes sjostedti*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Ganda Sundi. Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sjostedti*: Named after the entomologist Y. Sjöstedt
