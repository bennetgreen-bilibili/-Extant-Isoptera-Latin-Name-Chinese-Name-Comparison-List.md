## Summary

***Constrictotermes cyphergaster*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Constrictotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso do Sul: Ladário, Urucum \[19.155S 57.631W\]; Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Constrictotermes*: From Latin constrictus (constricted) + termes.

Specific epithet *cyphergaster*: Greek kryptos (hidden) + gastēr (belly), hidden-bellied
