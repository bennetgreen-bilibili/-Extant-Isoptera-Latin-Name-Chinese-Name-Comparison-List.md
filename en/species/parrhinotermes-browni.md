## Summary

***Parrhinotermes browni*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Harris in 1958; the type locality is Solomon Islands: Guadalcanal: Gold Ridge. Distribution: Indonesia, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *browni*: Named after Brown
