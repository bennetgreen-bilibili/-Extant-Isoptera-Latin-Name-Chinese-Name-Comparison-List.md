## Summary

***Macrotermes guangxiensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Han in 1986; the type locality is China: Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region: Nanning. Distribution: Guangxi, China, Nanning.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *guangxiensis*: Named after the locality Guangx
