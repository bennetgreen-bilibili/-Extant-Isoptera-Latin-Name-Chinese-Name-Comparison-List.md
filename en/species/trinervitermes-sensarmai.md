## Summary

***Trinervitermes sensarmai*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Bose in 1984; the type locality is India: Andhra Pradesh: North Vijayapuri, western slope of Hill colony. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *sensarmai*: Named after P.K. Sen-Sarma
