## Summary

***Odontotermes mediocris*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Ethiopia: Diré Daoua. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Ethiopia, Malawi, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mediocris*: Latin mediocris (moderate, middling)
