## Summary

***Cavitermes parvicavus*** is a species of the genus *Cavitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cavitermes*: From Latin cavus (hollow) + termes.

Specific epithet *parvicavus*: From the descriptive term 'parv' (small)
