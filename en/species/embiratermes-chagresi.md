## Summary

***Embiratermes chagresi*** is a species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Colombia, Panama, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Nicaragua.

## Etymology

Genus name *Embiratermes*: From Tupi Embirá (a tree) + -termes.

Specific epithet *chagresi*: Named after the locality Chagres
