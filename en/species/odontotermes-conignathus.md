## Summary

***Odontotermes conignathus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Xia & Fan in 1982; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *conignathus*: Greek kōnos (cone) + gnathos (jaw), cone-jawed
