## Summary

***Coptotermes sjostedti*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Cameroon: Bonge; Cape Debubdscha. Distribution: West Africa, Ivory Coast, Nigeria, Senegal, Angola, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Mozambique, São Tomé and Príncipe, Sierra Leone, Somalia, Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda; introduced: Guadeloupe (France). This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sjostedti*: Named after the entomologist Y. Sjöstedt
