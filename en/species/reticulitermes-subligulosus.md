## Summary

***Reticulitermes subligulosus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1992; the type locality is China: Hubei Province: Xingshan County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *subligulosus*: From subligul + osus
