## Summary

***Amitermes seminotus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1908; the type locality is Botswana: Kalahari: Kooa. Distribution: Botswana, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *seminotus*: Latin semi- (half) + notus (back), half-back
