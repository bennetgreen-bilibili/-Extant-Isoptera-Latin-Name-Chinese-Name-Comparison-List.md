## Summary

***Microtermes feae*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Equatorial Guinea: Bioko Island: Musola. Distribution: West Africa, Guinea-Bissau and Gulf of Guinea islands, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *feae*: Named after Leonardo Fea
