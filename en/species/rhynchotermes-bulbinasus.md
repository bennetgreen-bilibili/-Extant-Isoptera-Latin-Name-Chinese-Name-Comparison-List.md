## Summary

***Rhynchotermes bulbinasus*** is a species of the genus *Rhynchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2010; the type locality is Colombia: Sucre: Buenavista \[9.3145N 75.0285W\]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rhynchotermes*: From Greek rhynchos (beak, snout) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bulbinasus*: Latin bulbus (bulb) + nasus (nose), bulb-nosed
