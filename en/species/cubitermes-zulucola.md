## Summary

***Cubitermes zulucola*** is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Mkosi. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa (Zululand). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *zulucola*: Zulu (people, South Africa) + -cola (dweller): Zululand-dwelling
