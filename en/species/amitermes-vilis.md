## Summary

***Amitermes vilis*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Iran: Shiraz. Distribution: Middle East / Arabian Peninsula, Afghanistan, Turkmenistan. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *vilis*: Latin vilis (cheap, worthless)
