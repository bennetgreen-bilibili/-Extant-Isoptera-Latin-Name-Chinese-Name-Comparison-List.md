## Summary

***Heterotermes lauralinearum*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Carrijo in 2020; the type locality is Paraguay: Presidente Hayes: NW Rio Verde \[ 22.8358S 59.5608W \]. Distribution: Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lauralinearum*: Etymology uncertain; laura (perhaps personal name Laura, or Latin laurus 'laurel') + linearum (Latin linearis 'line-like', genitive plural), perhaps referring to a lineate feature. Etymology not given in the original (Carrijo 2020).
