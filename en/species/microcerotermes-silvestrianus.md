## Summary

***Microcerotermes silvestrianus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: beteeen Lukoela and Basoko. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *silvestrianus*: Named after F. Silvestri
