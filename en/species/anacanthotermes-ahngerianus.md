## Summary

***Anacanthotermes ahngerianus*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Jacobson in 1905; the type locality is Turkmenistan: Jagly-olum on Atrek River. Distribution: Middle East, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Iran, Turkmenistan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *ahngerianus*: Named after Ahnger
