## Summary

***Aciculitermes aciculatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Aciculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Aciculitermes*: From Latin acicula (little pin) + termes.

Specific epithet *aciculatus*: From acicul + atus
