## Summary

***Astalotermes quietus*** is a species of the genus *Astalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Ghana: Aburi. Distribution: West Africa, Central African Congo Basin, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Nigeria, Rwanda, Sierra Leone.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astalotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *quietus*: Latin quietus (quiet)
