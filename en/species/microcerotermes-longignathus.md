## Summary

***Microcerotermes longignathus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1955; the type locality is Pakistan: Baluchistan: Zhob District: Fort Sandeman. Distribution: Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longignathus*: Latin longus (long) + Greek gnathos (jaw), long-jawed
