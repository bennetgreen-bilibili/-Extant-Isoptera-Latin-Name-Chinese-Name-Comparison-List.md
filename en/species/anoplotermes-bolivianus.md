## Summary

***Anoplotermes bolivianus*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Tumupasa \[ 14.15S 67.9167W \]. Distribution: Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *bolivianus*: After Bolivia
