## Summary

***Reticulitermes paralucifugus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Zhang in 1989; the type locality is China: Shaanxi: Ningshan County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *paralucifugus*: From the descriptive term 'para' (beside)
