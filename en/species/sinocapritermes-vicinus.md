## Summary

***Sinocapritermes vicinus*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Xia, Gao, Pan & Tang in 1983; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Yibin. Distribution: China, Sichuan, Chongqing.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *vicinus*: Latin vicinus (neighbouring)
