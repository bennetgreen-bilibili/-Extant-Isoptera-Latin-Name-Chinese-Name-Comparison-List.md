## Summary

***Postelectrotermes tongyaii*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Ka-Chong. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *tongyaii*: Named after Tongyai
