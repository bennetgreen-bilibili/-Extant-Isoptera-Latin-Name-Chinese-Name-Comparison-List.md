## Summary

***Nasutitermes longinasus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Malacca; Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *longinasus*: From the descriptive term 'long' (long)
