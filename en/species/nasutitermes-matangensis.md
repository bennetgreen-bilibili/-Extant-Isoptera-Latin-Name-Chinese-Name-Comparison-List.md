## Summary

***Nasutitermes matangensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang. Distribution: Bangladesh, India, Myanmar, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, Vietnam. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *matangensis*: Named after Matang, Sarawak, Malaysia
