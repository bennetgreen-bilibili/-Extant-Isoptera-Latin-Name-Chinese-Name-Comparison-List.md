## Summary

***Noditermes profestus*** is a species of the genus *Noditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Tumbwe. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Noditermes*: From Latin nodus (knot, node) + termes.

Specific epithet *profestus*: Latin profestus (ordinary, non-festive)
