## Summary

***Caetetermes fontesi*** is a species of the genus *Caetetermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2022; the type locality is French Guiana: Petit Saut \[5.07N 53.032W\]. Distribution: South America, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Caetetermes*: From the Tupi place name Caeté + -termes.

Specific epithet *fontesi*: Named after L.R. Fontes
