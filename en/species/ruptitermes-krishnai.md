## Summary

***Ruptitermes krishnai*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Acioli & Constantino in 2015; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: San Juan-Laventille: Northern Range \[ 10.755N 61.431W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *krishnai*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna
