## Summary

***Orthognathotermes okeyma*** is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2009; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Parque Nacional das Emas \[ 18.1S 52.91W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *okeyma*: Named after the locality Okeyma
