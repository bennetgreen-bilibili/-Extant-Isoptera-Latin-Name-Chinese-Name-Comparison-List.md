## Summary

***Xylochomitermes aspinosus*** is a species of the genus *Xylochomitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Miller in 1991; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Kununurra. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Xylochomitermes*: From Greek xylon (wood) + chōma (mound) + termes.

Specific epithet *aspinosus*: From a- (without) + spinosus
