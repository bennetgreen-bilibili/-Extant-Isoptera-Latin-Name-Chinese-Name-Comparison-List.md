## Summary

***Neocapritermes unicornis*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1991; the type locality is Brasil: Amapá: Serra do Navio \[ 0.8955N 52.0022W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *unicornis*: From uni + cornis
