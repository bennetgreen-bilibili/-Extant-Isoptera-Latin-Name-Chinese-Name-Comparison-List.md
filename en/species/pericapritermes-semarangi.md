## Summary

***Pericapritermes semarangi*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Semarang. Distribution: India, Indonesia, Bangladesh, Brunei, China, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *semarangi*: Named after the locality Semarang (Central Java, Indonesia)
