## Summary

***Nasutitermes ephratae*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Suriname: Commewijne: Ephrata \[5.8166N 54.75W\]. Distribution: Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, French Guiana, Panama, Belize, Bolivia, Dominica, Ecuador, Guadeloupe (France), Guatemala, Honduras, Montserrat, Nicaragua, Paraguay, Peru, Puerto Rico, Suriname, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela, U.S. Virgin Islands. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *ephratae*: Named after the locality Ephrata (Suriname)
