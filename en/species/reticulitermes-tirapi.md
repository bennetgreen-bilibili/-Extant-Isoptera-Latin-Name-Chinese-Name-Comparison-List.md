## Summary

***Reticulitermes tirapi*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chhotani & Das in 1983; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: Tirap District: Nampong. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *tirapi*: Named after the locality Tirap (Arunachal Pradesh, India)
