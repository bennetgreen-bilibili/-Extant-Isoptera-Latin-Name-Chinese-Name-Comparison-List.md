## Summary

***Labritermes kistneri*** is a species of the genus *Labritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Krishna & Adams in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Selangor: Ulu Gombak. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labritermes*: From Latin labrum (upper lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *kistneri*: Named after the entomologist D. H. Kistner
