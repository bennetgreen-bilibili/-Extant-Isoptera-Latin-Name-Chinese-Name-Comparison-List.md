## Summary

***Hesperotermes infrequens*** is the only extant species of the genus *Hesperotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1927; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Wungong. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hesperotermes*: From Greek hesperos (western, evening) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *infrequens*: Latin infrequens (infrequent)
