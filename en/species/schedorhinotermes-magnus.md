## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes magnus*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: ChinPing. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *magnus*: Latin magnus (large)
