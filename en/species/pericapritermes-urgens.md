## Summary

***Pericapritermes urgens*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria; Ghana: ?; Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: Senegal, Gambia, Guinea, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, Nigeria, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *urgens*: Latin urgens (urgent)
