## Summary

***Stylotermes faveolus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chatterjee & Thakur in 1964; the type locality is India: Himachal Pradesh: Kulu: Sarvari Village, notified cremation ground area, near Kulu sawmills. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *faveolus*: Latin faveolus (honeycombed)
