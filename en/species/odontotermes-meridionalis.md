## Summary

***Odontotermes meridionalis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Yang in 1987; the type locality is China: Guangxi autonomous region: Shanglin. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *meridionalis*: Latin meridionalis (southern)
