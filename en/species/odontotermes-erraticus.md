## Summary

***Odontotermes erraticus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé in 1947; the type locality is Niger: South of Sahara, Irreoualem (near to Agadès). Distribution: West Africa, Senegal, Togo, Gambia, Niger.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *erraticus*: Latin erraticus (wandering, erratic)
