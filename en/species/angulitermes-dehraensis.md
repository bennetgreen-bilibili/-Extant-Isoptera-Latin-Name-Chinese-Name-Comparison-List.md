## Summary

***Angulitermes dehraensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Gardner in 1945; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun. Distribution: Pakistan, Afghanistan, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *dehraensis*: Named after the locality Dehra Dun
