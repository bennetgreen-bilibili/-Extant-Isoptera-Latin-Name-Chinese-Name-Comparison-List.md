## Summary

***Paraconvexitermes junceus*** is a species of the genus *Paraconvexitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Guyana, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Paraconvexitermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + Latin convexus (convex) + termes.

Specific epithet *junceus*: Latin junceus (rush-like)
