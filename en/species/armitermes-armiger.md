## Summary

***Armitermes armiger*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Armitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Motschulsky in 1855; the type locality is Panama: Obispo. Distribution: Panama, Costa Rica.

## Etymology

Genus name *Armitermes*: From Latin arma (arms, weapons) + termes.

Specific epithet *armiger*: From armi + ger
