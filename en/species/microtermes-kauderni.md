## Summary

***Microtermes kauderni*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1909; the type locality is Madagascar: St. Marie de Marovoay, Majunga. Distribution: Madagascar. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *kauderni*: Named after W. Kaudern
