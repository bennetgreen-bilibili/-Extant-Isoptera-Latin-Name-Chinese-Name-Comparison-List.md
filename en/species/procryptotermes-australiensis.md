***Procryptotermes australiensis*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gay in 1976; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: 15 km SW Bowen. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *australiensis*: Named after Australia
