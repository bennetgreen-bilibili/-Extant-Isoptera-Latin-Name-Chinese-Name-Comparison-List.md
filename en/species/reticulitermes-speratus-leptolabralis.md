## Summary

***Reticulitermes speratus leptolabralis*** is a subspecies of *Reticulitermes speratus*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, genus *Reticulitermes*. The subspecies was described by Morimoto in 1968; the type locality is Japan: Aichi, Kasugai, Tokushima, Yami. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Reticulitermes speratus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *leptolabralis*: Greek leptos (slender) + Latin labrum (lip): slender-lipped
