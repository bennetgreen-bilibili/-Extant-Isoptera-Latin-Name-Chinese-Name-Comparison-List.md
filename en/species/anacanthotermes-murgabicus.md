## Summary

***Anacanthotermes murgabicus*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Vasiljev in 1911; the type locality is Turkmenistan: Kreise Merv von Transkaspien: River Murgab near Schleuse Kazyhly-Beut. Distribution: Turkmenistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *murgabicus*: Named after Murgab (Murghab), Turkmenistan
