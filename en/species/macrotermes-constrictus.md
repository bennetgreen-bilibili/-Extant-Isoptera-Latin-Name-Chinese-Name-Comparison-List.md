## Summary

***Macrotermes constrictus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Li in 1985; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Dechang. Distribution: Sichuan, China, Dechang.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *constrictus*: From the descriptive term 'constrict' (constricted)
