## Summary

***Angulitermes paanensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 1965; the type locality is Myanmar: Karen State: Pa-an: Yegaw. Distribution: Myanmar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *paanensis*: Named after the locality Paan
