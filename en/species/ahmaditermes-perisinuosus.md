## Summary

***Ahmaditermes perisinuosus*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li & Xiao in 1989; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Huashuichong Forest: Hexian. Distribution: Guangxi (Hexian Huashuichong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *perisinuosus*: Latin per- (very) + sinuosus (wavy), very sinuous
