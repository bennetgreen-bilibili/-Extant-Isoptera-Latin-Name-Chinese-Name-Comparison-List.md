## Summary

***Microtermes dubius*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is Namibia: Dornveld near Windhoek. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *dubius*: Latin dubius (doubtful)
