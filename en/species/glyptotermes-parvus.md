## Summary

***Glyptotermes parvus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Fan & Xia in 1980; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Menghai. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parvus*: Latin parvus (small)
