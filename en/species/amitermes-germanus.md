## Summary

***Amitermes germanus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1915; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: Darwin: Fannie Bay. Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *germanus*: Latin germanus (genuine, brotherly/of the same stock)
