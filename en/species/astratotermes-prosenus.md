## Summary

***Astratotermes prosenus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Astratotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Nigeria: Northern Region: 30 miles from Lokoja on Okene Road. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astratotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *prosenus*: From Greek proxenos (public guest/representative); not stated in original description
