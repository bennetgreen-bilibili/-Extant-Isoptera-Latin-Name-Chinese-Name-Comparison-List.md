## Summary

***Amitermes loennbergianus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Kenya: somewhat north of Guaso Nyero. Distribution: Eritrea, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, Tanzania, Uganda, Yemen. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *loennbergianus*: Named after Lönnberg (Swedish zoologist Einar Lönnberg)
