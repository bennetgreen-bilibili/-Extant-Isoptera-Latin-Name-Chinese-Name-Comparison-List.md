## Summary

***Reticulitermes lii*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Huang in 1993; the type locality is China: Hubei Province: Chibi, Puqi City. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *lii*: Named after a person surnamed Li
