## Summary

***Odontotermes latiguloides*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Verma in 1973; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Bhilwara District, ca. 48 km southwest of Bhilwara, Mandalgarh. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latiguloides*: From latigul + oides
