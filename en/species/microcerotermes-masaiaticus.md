## Summary

***Microcerotermes masaiaticus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1954; the type locality is Kenya: in Rift Valley S of Longonot Mountain. Distribution: Kenya, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *masaiaticus*: Named after the Maasai (East Africa)
