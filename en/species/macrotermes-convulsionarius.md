## Summary

***Macrotermes convulsionarius*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by König in 1779; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Tanjore (Tranqueba). Distribution: South Asia, India, Sri Lanka. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *convulsionarius*: Latin convulsus (convulsed)
