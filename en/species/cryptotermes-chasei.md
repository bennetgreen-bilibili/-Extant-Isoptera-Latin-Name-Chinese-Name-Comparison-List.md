## Summary

***Cryptotermes chasei*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 1993; the type locality is Dominican Republic: La Altagracia Province: Juanillo. Distribution: Dominican Republic.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *chasei*: Named after Chase
