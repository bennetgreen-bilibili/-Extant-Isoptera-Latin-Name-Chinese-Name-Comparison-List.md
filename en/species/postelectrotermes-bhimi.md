## Summary

***Postelectrotermes bhimi*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Maiti in 1965; the type locality is India: Kerala: Kottayam District: Vandiperiyar village. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *bhimi*: Named after Bhim
