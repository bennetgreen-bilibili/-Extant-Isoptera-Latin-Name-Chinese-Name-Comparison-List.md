## Summary

***Pericapritermes fuscotibialis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1931; the type locality is China: Hong Kong. Distribution: Hong Kong..

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *fuscotibialis*: From fusco + tibialis
