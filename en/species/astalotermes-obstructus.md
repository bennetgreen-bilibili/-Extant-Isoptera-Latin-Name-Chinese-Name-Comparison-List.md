## Summary

***Astalotermes obstructus*** is a species of the genus *Astalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: tropical Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astalotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *obstructus*: Latin obstructus (blocked)
