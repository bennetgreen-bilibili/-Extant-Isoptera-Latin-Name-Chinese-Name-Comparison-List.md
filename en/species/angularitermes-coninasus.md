## Summary

***Angularitermes coninasus*** is a species of the genus *Angularitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Carrijo, Rocha, Cuezzo & Cancello in 2011; the type locality is Brasil: Rondônia: Porto Velho, Jaci Paraná, Ilha da Pedra \[9.15S 64.5833W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angularitermes*: From Latin angularis (angular) + termes.

Specific epithet *coninasus*: Greek kōnos (cone) + Latin nasus (nose), cone-nosed
