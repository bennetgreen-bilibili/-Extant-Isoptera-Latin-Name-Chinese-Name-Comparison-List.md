## Summary

***Odontotermes caffrariae*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1897; the type locality is South Africa: Natal (Caffraria). Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *caffrariae*: Named after Kaffraria (South Africa)
