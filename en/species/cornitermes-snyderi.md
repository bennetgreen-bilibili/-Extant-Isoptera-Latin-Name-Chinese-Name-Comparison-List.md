## Summary

***Cornitermes snyderi*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1952; the type locality is Bolivia: Pando: Rosario (near Lake Rogagua) \[13.9667S 66.8667W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Bolivia, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *snyderi*: Named after the termite taxonomist T. E. Snyder
