## Summary

***Atlantitermes kirbyi*** is a species of the genus *Atlantitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Nicaragua, Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Atlantitermes*: From the Atlantic + termes.

Specific epithet *kirbyi*: Named after the entomologist W. F. Kirby
