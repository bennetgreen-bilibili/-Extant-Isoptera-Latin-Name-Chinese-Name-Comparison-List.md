## Summary

***Coptotermes michaelseni*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1909; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Mundijong. Distribution: western Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *michaelseni*: Named after W. Michaelsen
