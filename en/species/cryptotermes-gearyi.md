## Summary

***Cryptotermes gearyi*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Dalby. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *gearyi*: Named after Geary
