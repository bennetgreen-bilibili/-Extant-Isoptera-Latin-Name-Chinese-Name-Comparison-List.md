## Summary

***Odontotermes terricola*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1902; the type locality is Cameroon: Johann-Albrechtshöhe. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, East Africa, Tanzania, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *terricola*: Latin terra (earth) + -cola (dweller): earth-dwelling
