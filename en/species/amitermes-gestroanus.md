## Summary

***Amitermes gestroanus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1912; the type locality is Ethiopia: Scioa: Gherba. Distribution: Ethiopia.
