## Summary

***Microcerotermes major*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Cachan in 1951; the type locality is Madagascar: Antsingy; Betroka; Joffreville; Nosy Mamoko; Sambirano. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *major*: Latin major (larger)
