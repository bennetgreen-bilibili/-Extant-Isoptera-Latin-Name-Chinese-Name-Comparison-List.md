## Summary

***Microcerotermes strunckii*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Sörensen in 1884; the type locality is Argentina: Chaco: Río de Oro \[ 27.0422S 58.5667W \]. Distribution: Neotropics, Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay, Peru, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *strunckii*: Named after a person "Strunck" (identity unconfirmed)
