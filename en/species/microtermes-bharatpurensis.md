## Summary

***Microtermes bharatpurensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Rathore in 1989; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Bharatpur district, 5 km northwest of Kumber Tehsil. Distribution: South Asia, Rajasthan, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *bharatpurensis*: Named after the locality Bharatpur
