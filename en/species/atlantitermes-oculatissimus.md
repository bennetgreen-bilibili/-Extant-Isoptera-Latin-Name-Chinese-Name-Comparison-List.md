## Summary

***Atlantitermes oculatissimus*** is a species of the genus *Atlantitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Peru, Ecuador, Guyana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Atlantitermes*: From the Atlantic + termes.

Specific epithet *oculatissimus*: From the descriptive term 'ocul' (eye)
