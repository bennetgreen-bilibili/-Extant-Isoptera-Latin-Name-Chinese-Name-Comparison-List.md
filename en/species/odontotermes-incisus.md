## Summary

***Odontotermes incisus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Deli. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *incisus*: Latin incisus (incised, notched)
