## Summary

***Glyptotermes longuisculus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: 28 miles south of Rutshuru. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longuisculus*: From longuisc + ulus
