## Summary

***Inquilinitermes johnchapmani*** is a species of the genus *Inquilinitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2014; the type locality is Bolivia: El Beni: Trinidad \[14.702S 64.89W\]. Distribution: Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Inquilinitermes*: From Latin inquilinus (inquiline) + termes.

Specific epithet *johnchapmani*: Named after John Chapman
