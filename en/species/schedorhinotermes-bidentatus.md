## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes bidentatus*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1920; the type locality is Philippines: Panay: Antique Province: Culasi. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bidentatus*: Latin bi- (two) + dentatus (toothed), two-toothed
