## Summary

***Microcerotermes bequaertianus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Limumbashi. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Côte d'Ivoire.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bequaertianus*: Named after J. Bequaert
