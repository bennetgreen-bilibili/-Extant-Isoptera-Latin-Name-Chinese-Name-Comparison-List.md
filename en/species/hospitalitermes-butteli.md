## Summary

***Hospitalitermes butteli*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bandar Baroe. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *butteli*: Named after Buttel(-Reepen)
