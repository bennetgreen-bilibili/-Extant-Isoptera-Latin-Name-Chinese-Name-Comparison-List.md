## Summary

***Caribitermes discolor*** is the only extant species of the genus *Caribitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Banks in 1919; the type locality is Puerto Rico: Adjuntas; Culebra Island; El Yunque; Manati. Distribution: Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Cuba, Haiti, Jamaica.

## Etymology

Genus name *Caribitermes*: From Carib (Caribbean) + termes.

Specific epithet *discolor*: Latin discolor (of different colour)
