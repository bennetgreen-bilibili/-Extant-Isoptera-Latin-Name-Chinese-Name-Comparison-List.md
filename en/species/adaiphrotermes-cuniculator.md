## Summary

***Adaiphrotermes cuniculator*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Adaiphrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Ghana: 40 mi from Tumu on Lawra Road. Distribution: West Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Adaiphrotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cuniculator*: Latin cuniculator (tunneller)
