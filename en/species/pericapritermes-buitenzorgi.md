## Summary

***Pericapritermes buitenzorgi*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Tjibodas; Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *buitenzorgi*: Named after the locality Buitenzorg (Bogor)
