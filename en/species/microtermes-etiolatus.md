## Summary

***Microtermes etiolatus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is Mozambique: Xinavane, Inkomati River. Distribution: Mozambique, South Africa, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *etiolatus*: Latin etiolatus (etiolated, pale)
