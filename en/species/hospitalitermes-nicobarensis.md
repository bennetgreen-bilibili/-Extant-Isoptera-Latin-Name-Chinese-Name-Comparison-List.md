## Summary

***Hospitalitermes nicobarensis*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1994; the type locality is India: Great Nicobar Island: near Campbell Bay, Lakshman Beach. Distribution: India (Nicobar Islands).

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *nicobarensis*: Named after the Nicobar Islands
