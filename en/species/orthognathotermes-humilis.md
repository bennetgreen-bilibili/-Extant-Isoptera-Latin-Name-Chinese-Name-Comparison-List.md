## Summary

***Orthognathotermes humilis*** is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1991; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[ 1.848S 65.523W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Guiana Shield.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *humilis*: Latin humilis (low, humble)
