## Summary

***Foraminitermes corniferus*** is a species of the genus *Foraminitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1905; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: West Africa, Togo, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Foraminitermes*: From Latin foramen (opening) + termes.

Specific epithet *corniferus*: From the descriptive term 'corn' (horn)
