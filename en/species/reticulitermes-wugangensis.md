## Summary

***Reticulitermes wugangensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Huang & Yin in 1983; the type locality is China: Hunan: Wugang. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *wugangensis*: Named after the locality Wugang
