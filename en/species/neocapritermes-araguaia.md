## Summary

***Neocapritermes araguaia*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna & Araujo in 1968; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Goiás, Fazenda Piedade \[ 15.645S 50.5813W \]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *araguaia*: After the Araguaia River (Brazil)
