## Summary

***Nasutitermes torresi*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Thursday Island. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *torresi*: Named after Torres
