## Summary

***Krishnacapritermes thakuri*** is a species of the genus *Krishnacapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1997; the type locality is India: Kerala: Silent Valley. Distribution: South Asia, India. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Krishnacapritermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *thakuri*: Named after M.L. Thakur
