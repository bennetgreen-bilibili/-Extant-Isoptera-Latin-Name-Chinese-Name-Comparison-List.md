## Summary

***Microtermes magnoculus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2013; the type locality is Somalia: Villaggio Duca degli Abruzzi. Distribution: Somalia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *magnoculus*: From magn + oculus
