## Summary

***Incisitermes didwanaensis*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Verma in 1973; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Nagaur District: Didwana. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *didwanaensis*: Named after the locality Didwana
