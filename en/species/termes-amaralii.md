## Summary

***Termes amaralii*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Seabra in 1919; the type locality is Sao Tome and Principe: Binda. Distribution: São Tomé and Príncipe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *amaralii*: Named after Amaral
