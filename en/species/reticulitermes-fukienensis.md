## Summary

***Reticulitermes fukienensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1924; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Baekliang; Fujian Province: Fuzhou (Foochow); Fujian Province: Muiwha, near Foochow; Fujian Province: in mountains back of Foochow; Fujian Province: in mountains back of Foochow, 1200 ft, near Kuliang. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *fukienensis*: Named after the locality Fukien
