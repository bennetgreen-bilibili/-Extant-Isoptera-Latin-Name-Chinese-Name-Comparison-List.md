## Summary

***Microcerotermes diversus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1920; the type locality is Iraq: Al Amarah (Amara). Distribution: Middle East, Iraq, Iran, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *diversus*: Latin diversus (diverse)
