## Summary

***Odontotermes distans*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Shevaroy Rocks. Distribution: India, Nepal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *distans*: Latin distans (distant)
