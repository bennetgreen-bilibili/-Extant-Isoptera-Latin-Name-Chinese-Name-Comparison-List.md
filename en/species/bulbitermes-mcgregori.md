## Summary

***Bulbitermes mcgregori*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1916; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Sarai near Paete. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *mcgregori*: Named after McGregor (likely American ornithologist R. C. McGregor)
