## Summary

***Coptotermes varicapitatus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Li in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Hainan Island. Distribution: China (Guangdong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *varicapitatus*: Latin varius (varied) + capitatus (headed), varied-headed
