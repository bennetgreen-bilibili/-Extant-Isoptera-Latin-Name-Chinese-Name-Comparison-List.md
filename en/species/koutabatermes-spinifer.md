## Summary

***Koutabatermes spinifer*** is the only extant species of the genus *Koutabatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Romero-Arias & Roisin in 2021; the type locality is Cameroon: Région Ouest, near Koutaba, Koumaja village (5.6244, 10.7542) \[ 5.6244N 10.7542E \]. Distribution: Neotropics (specific countries not verified), Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Koutabatermes*: From the place name Koutaba; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *spinifer*: Latin spina (spine) + -fer (bearing), spiny
