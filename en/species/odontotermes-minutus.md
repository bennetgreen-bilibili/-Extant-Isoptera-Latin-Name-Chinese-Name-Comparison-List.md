## Summary

***Odontotermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Amir in 1975; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Lampung: Way Kambas. Distribution: East Asia, China, Indonesia, Malaysia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
