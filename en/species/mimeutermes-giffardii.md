## Summary

***Mimeutermes giffardii*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Mimeutermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Ghana: Aburi; Guinea: Camayenne. Distribution: Cameroon, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mimeutermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *giffardii*: Named after Giffard
