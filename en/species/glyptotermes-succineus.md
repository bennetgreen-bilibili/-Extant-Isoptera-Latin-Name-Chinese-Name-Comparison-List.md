## Summary

***Glyptotermes succineus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Gong in 1986; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Libo County. Distribution: China (Guizhou).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *succineus*: Latin succineus (amber-coloured)
