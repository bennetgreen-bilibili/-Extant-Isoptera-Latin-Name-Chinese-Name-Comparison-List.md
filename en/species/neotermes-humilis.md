## Summary

***Neotermes humilis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Han in 1984; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Longgang, Ningming. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *humilis*: Latin humilis (low, humble)
