## Summary

***Procapritermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Yunnan (Jinping Mengla 580 m), Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
