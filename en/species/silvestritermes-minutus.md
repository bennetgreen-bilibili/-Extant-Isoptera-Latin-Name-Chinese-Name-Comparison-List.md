## Summary

***Silvestritermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Silvestritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname, Ecuador, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Silvestritermes*: Named after the entomologist F. Silvestri; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
