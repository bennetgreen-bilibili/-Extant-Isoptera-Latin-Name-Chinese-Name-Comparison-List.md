## Summary

***Coptotermes elisae*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Desneux in 1905; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Simbang, Huon Gulf. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Papua New Guinea. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *elisae*: Named after Elisa
