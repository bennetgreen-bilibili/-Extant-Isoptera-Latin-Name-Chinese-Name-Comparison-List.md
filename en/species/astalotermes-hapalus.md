## Summary

***Astalotermes hapalus*** is a species of the genus *Astalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Kenya: Ngong Hills (near Nairobi). Distribution: tropical Africa, Kenya.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astalotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hapalus*: Greek hapalos (soft, tender)
