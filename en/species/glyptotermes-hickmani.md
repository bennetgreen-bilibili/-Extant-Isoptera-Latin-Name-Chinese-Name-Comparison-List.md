## Summary

***Glyptotermes hickmani*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2021; the type locality is Paraguay: Cordillera: Nueva Colombia \[ 25.1747S 57.2876W \]. Distribution: Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hickmani*: Named after Hickman
