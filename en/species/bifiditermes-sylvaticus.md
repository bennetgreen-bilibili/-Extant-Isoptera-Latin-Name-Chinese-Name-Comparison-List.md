## Summary

***Bifiditermes sylvaticus*** is a species of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wilkinson in 1959; the type locality is Uganda: Ankole District, 8 mi S of Kichwamba. Distribution: Kenya, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bifiditermes*: From Latin bifidus (cleft in two) + termes.

Specific epithet *sylvaticus*: Latin silvaticus (of the forest, sylva = forest)
