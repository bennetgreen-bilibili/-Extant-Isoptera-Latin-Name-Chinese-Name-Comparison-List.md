## Summary

***Microtermes aluco*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Mali: Sikasso. Distribution: West Africa, Nigeria, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *aluco*: Latin aluco (an owl)
