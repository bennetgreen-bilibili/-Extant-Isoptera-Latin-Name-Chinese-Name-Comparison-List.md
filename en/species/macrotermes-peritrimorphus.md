## Summary

***Macrotermes peritrimorphus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Li & Xiao in 1989; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Huashuichong Forest: Hexian. Distribution: Guangxi (Hexian, Huashuichong) and Yunnan (Pingbian), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *peritrimorphus*: Greek peri- (around) + tri- (three) + morphē (form)
