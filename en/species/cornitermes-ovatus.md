## Summary

***Cornitermes ovatus*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1952; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Cururuzinho, rio Autaz \[3.58S 59.125W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *ovatus*: Latin ovatus (oval)
