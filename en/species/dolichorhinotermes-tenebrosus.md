## Summary

***Dolichorhinotermes tenebrosus*** is a species of the genus *Dolichorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: South America, Guyana, Bolivia, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dolichorhinotermes*: From Greek dolichos (long) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tenebrosus*: From tenebr + osus
