## Summary

***Anacanthotermes septentrionalis*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Jacobson in 1905; the type locality is Iran: Chosassan, between Kerat and Sengun; Turkmenistan: AkarTscheschmè (on River Tedshen); Geok-Tepe; Kopet-dagh; Mulla-Kara, station Perwal; Serachs. Distribution: Middle East, Iran, Afghanistan, Turkmenistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *septentrionalis*: Latin septentrionalis (northern)
