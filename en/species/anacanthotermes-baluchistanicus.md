## Summary

***Anacanthotermes baluchistanicus*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Akhtar in 1974; the type locality is Pakistan: Baluchistan: Chaman. Distribution: Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *baluchistanicus*: After Baluchistan (Pakistan)
