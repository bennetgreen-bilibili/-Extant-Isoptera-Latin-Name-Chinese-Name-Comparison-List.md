## Summary

***Coptotermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ahmad in 1953; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Colombo. Distribution: India, China, Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
