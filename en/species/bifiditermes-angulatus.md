## Summary

***Bifiditermes angulatus*** is a species of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wilkinson in 1959; the type locality is Tanzania: East Usambara: Amani, Kiswani. Distribution: East Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bifiditermes*: From Latin bifidus (cleft in two) + termes.

Specific epithet *angulatus*: From angul + atus
