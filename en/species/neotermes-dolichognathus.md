## Summary

***Neotermes dolichognathus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xu & Han in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Zijin. Distribution: Guangdong (Zijin).

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dolichognathus*: From dolicho + gnathus
