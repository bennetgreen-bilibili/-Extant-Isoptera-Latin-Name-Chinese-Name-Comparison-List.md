## Summary

***Nasutitermes fulleri*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Nigeria, Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *fulleri*: Named after C. Fuller
