## Summary

***Cryptotermes fatulus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1935; the type locality is Mexico: Madre Maria Island. Distribution: Ecuador, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *fatulus*: Latin fatuus (foolish) + diminutive
