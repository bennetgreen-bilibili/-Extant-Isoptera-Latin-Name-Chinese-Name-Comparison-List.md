## Summary

***Macrotermes jinghongensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Li in 1985; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: China (Yunnan), Jinghong.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *jinghongensis*: Named after the locality Jinghong
