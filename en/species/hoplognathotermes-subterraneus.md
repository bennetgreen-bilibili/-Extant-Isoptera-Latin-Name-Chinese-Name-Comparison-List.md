## Summary

***Hoplognathotermes subterraneus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Hoplognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Conakry; Kakoulima. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hoplognathotermes*: From Greek hoplon (weapon) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *subterraneus*: Latin subterraneus (subterranean)
