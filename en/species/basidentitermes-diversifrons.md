## Summary

***Basidentitermes diversifrons*** is a species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: Old World tropics, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Basidentitermes*: From Greek basis (base) + Latin dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *diversifrons*: Latin diversus (diverse) + frons (front), diverse-fronted
