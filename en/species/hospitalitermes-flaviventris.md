## Summary

***Hospitalitermes flaviventris*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Malaysia: Negeri Sembilan: Berhentian Tingi. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *flaviventris*: From the descriptive term 'flav' (yellow)
