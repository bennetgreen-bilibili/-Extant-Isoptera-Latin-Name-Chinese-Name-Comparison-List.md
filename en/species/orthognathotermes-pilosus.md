## Summary

***Orthognathotermes pilosus*** is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2009; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Nova Xavantina \[ 14.6733S 52.3531W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pilosus*: Latin pilosus (hairy)
