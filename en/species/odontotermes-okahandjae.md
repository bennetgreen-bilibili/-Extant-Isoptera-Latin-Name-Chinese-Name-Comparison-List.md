## Summary

***Odontotermes okahandjae*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is Namibia: Okahandja. Distribution: southern Africa, Namibia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *okahandjae*: Named after Okahandja, Namibia
