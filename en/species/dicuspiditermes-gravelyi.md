## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes gravelyi*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1922; the type locality is India: Koyna Valley: Helvak, ca. 2000–2100 ft; Maharashtra: Satara District: Kas; Vela, ca. 2000–2100 ft. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *gravelyi*: Named after F. H. Gravely
