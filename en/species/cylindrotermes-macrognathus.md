## Summary

***Cylindrotermes macrognathus*** is a species of the genus *Cylindrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cylindrotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1929; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Neotropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cylindrotermes*: From Greek kylindros (cylinder) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *macrognathus*: From macro + gnathus
