## Summary

***Thoracotermes brevinotus*** is a species of the genus *Thoracotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: West Africa, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire.

## Etymology

Genus name *Thoracotermes*: From Greek thōrax (thorax) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brevinotus*: From brevi + notus
