## Summary

***Stylotermes bengalensis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Mathur & Chhotani in 1959; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Darjeeling Division. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bengalensis*: Named after the locality Bengal
