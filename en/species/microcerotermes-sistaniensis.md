## Summary

***Microcerotermes sistaniensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Ghayourfar & Akhtar in 2005; the type locality is Iran: Sistan: Zabol region: Zehak city: Shadi village. Distribution: Iran, Sistan and Baluchestan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sistaniensis*: Named after the locality Sistan
