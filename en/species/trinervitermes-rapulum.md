## Summary

***Trinervitermes rapulum*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga. Distribution: South Africa, Ethiopia, Malawi, Tanzania, Zimbabwe. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *rapulum*: Latin rapum (turnip) + -ulum (small), small turnip
