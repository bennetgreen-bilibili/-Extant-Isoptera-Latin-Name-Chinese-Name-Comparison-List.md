## Summary

***Sinocapritermes sinensis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Tengchong, Xian. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *sinensis*: Latin Sinensis (of China)
