## Summary

***Cavitermes simplicinervis*** is a species of the genus *Cavitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Santarém \[2.43917S 54.705W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cavitermes*: From Latin cavus (hollow) + termes.

Specific epithet *simplicinervis*: Latin simplex (simple) + nervus (vein), simple-veined
