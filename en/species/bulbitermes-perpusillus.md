## Summary

***Bulbitermes perpusillus*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by John in 1925; the type locality is Singapore: Bukit Timah. Distribution: Malaysia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *perpusillus*: Latin per- (very) + pusillus (tiny), very tiny
