## Summary

***Nitiditermes aemulus*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Conakry. Distribution: Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *aemulus*: Latin aemulus (rival, emulating)
