## Summary

***Macrotermes longiceps*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Li & Ping in 1983; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Dialuo Mt. Distribution: Hainan, China, Diaoluo Mountain, Guangxi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *longiceps*: Latin longus (long) + -ceps (head), long-headed
