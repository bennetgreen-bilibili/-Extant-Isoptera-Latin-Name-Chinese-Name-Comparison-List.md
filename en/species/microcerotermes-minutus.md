## Summary

***Microcerotermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Wang Nok An. Distribution: China, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
