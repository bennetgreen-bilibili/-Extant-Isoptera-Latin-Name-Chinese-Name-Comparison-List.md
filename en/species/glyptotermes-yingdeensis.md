## Summary

***Glyptotermes yingdeensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li in 1987; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Yingde. Distribution: China (Guangdong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *yingdeensis*: Named after the locality Yingde
