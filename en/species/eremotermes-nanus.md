## Summary

***Eremotermes nanus*** is a species of the genus *Eremotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1960; the type locality is Sudan: Shambat. Distribution: Nigeria, Sudan, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eremotermes*: From Greek erēmos (desert, solitary) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nanus*: Greek nanos (dwarf)
