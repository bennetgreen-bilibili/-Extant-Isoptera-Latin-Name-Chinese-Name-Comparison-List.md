## Summary

***Microcerotermes maliki*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1974; the type locality is Pakistan: Peshawar. Distribution: Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *maliki*: Named after Malik
