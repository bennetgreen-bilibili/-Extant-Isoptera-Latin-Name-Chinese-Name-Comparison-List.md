## Summary

***Astalotermes irrixosus*** is a species of the genus *Astalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Sudan: Equatoria Province: Imatong Mountains. Distribution: tropical Africa, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astalotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *irrixosus*: Latin irrisor (mocker) or rixa (quarrel), quarrelsome
