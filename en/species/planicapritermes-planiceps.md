## Summary

***Planicapritermes planiceps*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Planicapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Bolivia, Ecuador, Suriname, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Planicapritermes*: From Latin planus (flat) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *planiceps*: Latin planus (flat) + -ceps (head), flat-headed
