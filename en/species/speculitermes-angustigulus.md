## Summary

***Speculitermes angustigulus*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by He in 1985; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: South Asia, India, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *angustigulus*: From angustig + ulus
