## Summary

***Amitermes acinacifer*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1959; the type locality is Kenya: Masabit district, 43 km from Masabit township on the Isiolo Road. Distribution: Ethiopia, Kenya.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *acinacifer*: From acinaci + fer
