## Summary

***Cubitermes conjenii*** is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Conjeni, 6 mi S White Mfolozi River. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *conjenii*: Named after Conjeni
