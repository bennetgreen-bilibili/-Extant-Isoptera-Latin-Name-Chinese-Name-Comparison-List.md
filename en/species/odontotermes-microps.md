## Summary

***Odontotermes microps*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga. Distribution: East Africa (Tanzania).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *microps*: Greek mikros (small) + ōps (eye), small-eyed
