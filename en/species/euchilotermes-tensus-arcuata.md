## Summary

***Euchilotermes tensus arcuata*** is a subspecies of *Euchilotermes tensus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), genus *Euchilotermes*. The subspecies was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Conakry; Kakoulima. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Euchilotermes tensus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euchilotermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + cheilos (lip, margin) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *arcuata*: Latin arcuatus (bowed, arcuate)
