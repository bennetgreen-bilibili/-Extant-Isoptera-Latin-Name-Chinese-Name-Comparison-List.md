## Summary

***Microtermes mangzhuangensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Huang & Zhu in 1991; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Mengla County: Mangzhuang. Distribution: Yunnan, China, Mengla.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *mangzhuangensis*: Named after a locality Mangzhuang, Yunnan, China
