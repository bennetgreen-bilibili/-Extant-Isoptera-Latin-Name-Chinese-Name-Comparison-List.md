## Summary

***Isognathotermes severus*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Kakoulima. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gambia, Guinea, Nigeria, Sudan, Tanzania. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *severus*: Latin severus (severe)
