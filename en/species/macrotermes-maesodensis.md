## Summary

***Macrotermes maesodensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: km 20 east of Mae Sod on road to Tak. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Vietnam, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *maesodensis*: Named after Mae Sot, Thailand
