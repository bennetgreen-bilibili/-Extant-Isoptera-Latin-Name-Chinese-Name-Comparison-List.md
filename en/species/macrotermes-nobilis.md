## Summary

***Macrotermes nobilis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Cameroon: Johann-Albrechtshöhe. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *nobilis*: Latin nobilis (noble)
