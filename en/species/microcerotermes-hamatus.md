## Summary

***Microcerotermes hamatus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Somkele. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hamatus*: Latin hamatus (hooked)
