## Summary

***Anoplotermes gracilis*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1922; the type locality is Panama: Ancon. Distribution: French Guiana, Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *gracilis*: Latin gracilis (slender)
