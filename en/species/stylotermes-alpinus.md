***Stylotermes alpinus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1983; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Mt. Gaoligong, Tengchong County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *alpinus*: Latin alpinus (alpine)
