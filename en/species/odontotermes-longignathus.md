## Summary

***Odontotermes longignathus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Malaysia: Taiping: Trong. Distribution: South Asia, Southeast Asia, Neotropics, Central and South America, India, Bangladesh, Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longignathus*: Latin longus (long) + Greek gnathos (jaw), long-jawed
