## Summary

***Adynatotermes moretelae*** is the only extant species of the genus *Adynatotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: the Moretele, Pienaars River. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Adynatotermes*: From Greek adynatos (powerless) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *moretelae*: Named after the locality Moretela
