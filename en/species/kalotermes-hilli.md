## Summary

***Kalotermes hilli*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Swan River. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hilli*: Named after the entomologist G. F. Hill
