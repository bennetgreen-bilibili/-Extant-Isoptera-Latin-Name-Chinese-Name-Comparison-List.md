## Summary

***Isognathotermes ugandensis kenyae*** is a subspecies of *Isognathotermes ugandensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), genus *Isognathotermes*. The subspecies was described by Josens & Deligne in 2025; the type locality is Kenya: Mukuyuni \[ 0.0269N 36.0051E \]. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Isognathotermes ugandensis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kenyae*: Named after Kenya
