## Summary

***Glyptotermes besarensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thakur in 1989; the type locality is Indonesia: Krakatau Islands: Rekata Besar. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *besarensis*: Named after the locality Besar
