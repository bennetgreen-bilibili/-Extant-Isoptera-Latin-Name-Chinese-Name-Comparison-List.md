## Summary

***Paracapritermes secundus*** is a species of the genus *Paracapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Miller in 1991; the type locality is Australia: Queensland, 24 km W by N of Cape Tribulation. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Paracapritermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *secundus*: Latin secundus (second)
