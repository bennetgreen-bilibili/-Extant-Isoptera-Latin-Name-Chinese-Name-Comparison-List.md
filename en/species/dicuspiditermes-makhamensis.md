## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes makhamensis*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Makham. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *makhamensis*: Named after the locality Makham
