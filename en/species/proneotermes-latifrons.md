## Summary

***Proneotermes latifrons*** is a species of the genus *Proneotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Venezuela: Carabobo: Las Trincheras \[ 10.3078N 68.0872W \]. Distribution: Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Proneotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latifrons*: From lati + frons
