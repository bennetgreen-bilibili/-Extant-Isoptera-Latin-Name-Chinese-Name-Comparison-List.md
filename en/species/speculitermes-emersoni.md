## Summary

***Speculitermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bose in 1984; the type locality is India: Kerala: Thekkadi. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
