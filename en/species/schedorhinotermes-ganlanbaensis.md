## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes ganlanbaensis*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xia & He in 1980; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ganlanbaensis*: Named after the locality Ganlanba
