## Summary

***Glyptotermes pellucidus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: Guyana, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pellucidus*: From the descriptive term 'pelluc' (transparent)
