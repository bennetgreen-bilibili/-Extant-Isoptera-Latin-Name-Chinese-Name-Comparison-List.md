## Summary

***Microtermes lounsburyi*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Pienaars River (Bushveld). Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Malawi. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *lounsburyi*: Named after C. P. Lounsbury
