## Summary

***Tenuirostritermes strenuus*** is a species of the genus *Tenuirostritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1860; the type locality is Mexico: Veracruz. Distribution: Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tenuirostritermes*: From Latin tenuis (slender) + rostrum (beak) + termes.

Specific epithet *strenuus*: Latin strenuus (vigorous)
