## Summary

***Velocitermes barrocoloradensis*** is a species of the genus *Velocitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Velocitermes*: From Latin velox (swift) + termes.

Specific epithet *barrocoloradensis*: Named after the locality Barrocolorad
