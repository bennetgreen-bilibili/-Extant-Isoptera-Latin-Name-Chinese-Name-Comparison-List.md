## Summary

***Microtermes jacobsoni*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Semarang. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *jacobsoni*: Named after E. Jacobson
