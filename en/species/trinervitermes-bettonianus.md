## Summary

***Trinervitermes bettonianus*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1905; the type locality is Kenya: Athi River. Distribution: Namibia, Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Kenya, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Malawi, Mozambique, Nigeria, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *bettonianus*: Named after Betton
