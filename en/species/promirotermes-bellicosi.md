## Summary

***Promirotermes bellicosi*** is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1912; the type locality is Tanzania: Moschi (Kilimanjaro). Distribution: Kenya, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *bellicosi*: From Latin bellicosus (warlike, belligerent)
