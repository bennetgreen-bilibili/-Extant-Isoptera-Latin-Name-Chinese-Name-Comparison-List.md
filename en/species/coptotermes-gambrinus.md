## Summary

***Coptotermes gambrinus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2011; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Morobe: Bulolo. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gambrinus*: After Gambrinus (mythical beer king)
