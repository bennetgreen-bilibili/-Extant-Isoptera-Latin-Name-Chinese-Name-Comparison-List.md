## Summary

***Cornitermes pugnax*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Peru, Guyana, Suriname, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *pugnax*: Latin pugnax (pugnacious)
