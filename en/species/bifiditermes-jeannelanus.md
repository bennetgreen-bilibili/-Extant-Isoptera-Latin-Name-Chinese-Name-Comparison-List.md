## Summary

***Bifiditermes jeannelanus*** is a species of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1915; the type locality is Tanzania: Kilimanjaro: Neu-Moschi. Distribution: Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bifiditermes*: From Latin bifidus (cleft in two) + termes.

Specific epithet *jeannelanus*: Named after René Jeannel
