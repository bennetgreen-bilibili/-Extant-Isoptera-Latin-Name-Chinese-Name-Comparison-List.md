## Summary

***Microtermes tragardhi*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Sudan: Kaka. Distribution: Old World tropics, East Africa, southern Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *tragardhi*: Named after Ivar Trägårdh (Swedish entomologist)
