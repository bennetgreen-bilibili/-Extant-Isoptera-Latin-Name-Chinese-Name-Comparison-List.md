## Summary

***Stylotermes halumicus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Liang, Wu & Li in 2017; the type locality is Taiwan: Taitung Co., Luanshan. Distribution: China (Taiwan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *halumicus*: From the Bunun word halum (pangolin)
