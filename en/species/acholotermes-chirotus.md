## Summary

***Acholotermes chirotus*** is a species of the genus *Acholotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: East Africa, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acholotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + cholē (bile) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chirotus*: Greek cheir (hand) + -otos (having)
