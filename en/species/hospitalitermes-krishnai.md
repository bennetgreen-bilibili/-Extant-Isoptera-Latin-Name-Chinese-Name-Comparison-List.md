## Summary

***Hospitalitermes krishnai*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Syaukani, Thompson & Yamane in 2011; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Lampung Province: Kotabumi, Sumber Jaya. Distribution: Indonesia, Lampung, southern Sumatra.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *krishnai*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna
