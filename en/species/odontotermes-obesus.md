## Summary

***Odontotermes obesus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Rambur in 1842; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Mumbai. Distribution: South Asia, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, Myanmar. This site records 6 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *obesus*: From the descriptive term 'obes' (obese)
