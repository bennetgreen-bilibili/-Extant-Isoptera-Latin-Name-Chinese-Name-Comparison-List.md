## Summary

***Neotermes gnathoferrum*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ware, Lal & Grimaldi in 2010; the type locality is Fiji: Colo-i-Suva, near Suva. Distribution: Fiji.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gnathoferrum*: From the descriptive term 'gnath' (jaw)
