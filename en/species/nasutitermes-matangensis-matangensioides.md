## Summary

***Nasutitermes matangensis matangensioides*** is a subspecies of *Nasutitermes matangensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), genus *Nasutitermes*. The subspecies was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Krakatau; Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Nasutitermes matangensis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *matangensioides*: Named after Matang + Greek -oides (like)
