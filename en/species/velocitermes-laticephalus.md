## Summary

***Velocitermes laticephalus*** is a species of the genus *Velocitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Covendo \[15.79S 66.975W\]. Distribution: Bolivia, Ecuador, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Velocitermes*: From Latin velox (swift) + termes.

Specific epithet *laticephalus*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
