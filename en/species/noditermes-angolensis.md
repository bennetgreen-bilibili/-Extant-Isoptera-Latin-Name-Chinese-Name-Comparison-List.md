## Summary

***Noditermes angolensis*** is a species of the genus *Noditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1974; the type locality is Angola: Alto Chicapa. Distribution: Angola.

## Etymology

Genus name *Noditermes*: From Latin nodus (knot, node) + termes.

Specific epithet *angolensis*: Named after the locality Angol
