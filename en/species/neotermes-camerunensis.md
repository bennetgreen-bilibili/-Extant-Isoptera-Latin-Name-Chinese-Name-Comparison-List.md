## Summary

***Neotermes camerunensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Cameroon: Mungo River. Distribution: Senegal, Cameroon, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *camerunensis*: Named after the locality Camerun
