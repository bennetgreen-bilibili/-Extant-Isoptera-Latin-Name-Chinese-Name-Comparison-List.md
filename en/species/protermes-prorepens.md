## Summary

***Protermes prorepens*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Protermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1907; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Nigeria, Sierra Leone.

## Etymology

Genus name *Protermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *prorepens*: Latin prorepens (creeping)
