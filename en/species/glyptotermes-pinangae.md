## Summary

***Glyptotermes pinangae*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pinangae*: Named after Penang (Pinang), Malaysia
