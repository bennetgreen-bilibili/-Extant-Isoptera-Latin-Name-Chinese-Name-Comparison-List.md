## Summary

***Procubitermes sinuosus*** is a species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kakoulima; Mamou. Distribution: West Africa, Nigeria, Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procubitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *sinuosus*: From sinu + osus
