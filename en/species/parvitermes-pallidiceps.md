## Summary

***Parvitermes pallidiceps*** is a species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Banks in 1919; the type locality is Haiti: Diquini; Manneville; Port au Prince; St. Marc. Distribution: Dominican Republic, Haiti.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parvitermes*: From Latin parvus (small) + termes.

Specific epithet *pallidiceps*: From pallidi + ceps
