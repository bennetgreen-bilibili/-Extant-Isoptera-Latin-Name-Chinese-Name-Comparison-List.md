## Summary

***Mirocapritermes hsuchiafui*** is a species of the genus *Mirocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yu & Ping in 1966; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Ying-chiang. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mirocapritermes*: From Latin mirus (wonderful) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *hsuchiafui*: Named after Hsu Chia-fu (Chinese)
