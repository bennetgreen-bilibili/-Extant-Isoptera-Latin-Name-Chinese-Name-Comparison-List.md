## Summary

***Labiotermes pelliceus*** is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson & Banks in 1965; the type locality is Guyana: East Berbice-Corentyne: Acarai Mountains, Itabu Creek \[1.7N 57.9167W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Guyana, Bolivia, Ecuador, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiotermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *pelliceus*: From pellic + eus
