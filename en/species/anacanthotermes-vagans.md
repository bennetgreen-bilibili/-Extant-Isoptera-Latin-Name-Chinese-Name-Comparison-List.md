## Summary

***Anacanthotermes vagans*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Iran: Shiraz, "Karak Island," Persian Gulf. Distribution: Middle East, Iran, Afghanistan, Iraq, Kuwait, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *vagans*: Latin vagans (wandering)
