## Summary

***Rugitermes occidentalis*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Argentina: Tucumán: Santa Ana \[ 27.4667S 65.6833W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *occidentalis*: Latin occidentalis (western)
