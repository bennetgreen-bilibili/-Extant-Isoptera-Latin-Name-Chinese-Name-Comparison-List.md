## Summary

***Neotermes lagunensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1920; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Paete. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lagunensis*: Named after Laguna (Philippines)
