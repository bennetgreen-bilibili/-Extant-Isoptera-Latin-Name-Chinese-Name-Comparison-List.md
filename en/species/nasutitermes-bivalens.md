## Summary

***Nasutitermes bivalens*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[15.5961S 56.0967W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Costa Rica, Colombia, Argentina.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *bivalens*: Latin bi- (two) + valens (strong)
