## Summary

***Grallatotermes niger*** is a species of the genus *Grallatotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thapa in 1964; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu (Madras): Tunacadur Range, Topslip, Mt. Stuart Block. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Grallatotermes*: From Latin grallator (stilt-walker) + termes.

Specific epithet *niger*: Latin niger (black)
