## Summary

***Glyptotermes tuberculatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Froggatt in 1897; the type locality is Australia: New South Wales: Uralla. Distribution: Australia, New Zealand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tuberculatus*: Latin tuberculatus (tuberculate)
