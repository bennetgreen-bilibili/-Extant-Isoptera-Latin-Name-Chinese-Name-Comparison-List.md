## Summary

***Coptotermes grandis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Huang in 1985; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Lianjiang County. Distribution: China (Fujian).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *grandis*: Latin grandis (great)
