## Summary

***Atlantitermes osborni*** is a species of the genus *Atlantitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Bartica District, Cow Island \[6.43N 58.623W\]. Distribution: Colombia, Brazil, French Guiana, Bolivia, Ecuador, Guyana, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Atlantitermes*: From the Atlantic + termes.

Specific epithet *osborni*: Named after the entomologist H. Osborn
