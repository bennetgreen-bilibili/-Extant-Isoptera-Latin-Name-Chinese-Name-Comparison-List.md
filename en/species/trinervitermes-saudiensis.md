## Summary

***Trinervitermes saudiensis*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1965; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Jiddah. Distribution: Saudi Arabia, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *saudiensis*: Named after the locality Saud
