## Summary

***Neotermes araguaensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1959; the type locality is Venezuela: Aragua: Rancho Grande \[ 10.3494N 67.6856W \]. Distribution: Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *araguaensis*: Named after the locality Aragua
