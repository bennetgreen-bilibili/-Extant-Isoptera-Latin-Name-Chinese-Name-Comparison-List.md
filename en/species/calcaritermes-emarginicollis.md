## Summary

***Calcaritermes emarginicollis*** is a species of the genus *Calcaritermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Panama: Rio Chinilla. Distribution: Central America, Costa Rica, Panama, Honduras, Belize.

## Etymology

Genus name *Calcaritermes*: From Latin calcar (spur) + termes.

Specific epithet *emarginicollis*: From emargini + collis
