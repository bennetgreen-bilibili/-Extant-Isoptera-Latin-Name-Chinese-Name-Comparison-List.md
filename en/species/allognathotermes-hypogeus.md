## Summary

***Allognathotermes hypogeus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Allognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kakoulima. Distribution: West Africa, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Allognathotermes*: From Greek allos (other) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hypogeus*: Greek hypo (under) + gē (earth), subterranean
