## Summary

***Glyptotermes tikaderi*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chhotani & Bose in 1985; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: Tirap District: Namdapha: Hornbill. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tikaderi*: Named after B. K. Tikader (Indian arachnologist)
