## Summary

***Odontotermes longzhouensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Lin in 1981; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Longzhou. Distribution: Guangxi (Shanglin), China. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longzhouensis*: Named after the locality Longzhou
