## Summary

***Anenteotermes improelatans*** is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Kenya: 9 mi from Londiani on Kisumu Road. Distribution: Kenya, Malawi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *improelatans*: From im- (without) + proelatans
