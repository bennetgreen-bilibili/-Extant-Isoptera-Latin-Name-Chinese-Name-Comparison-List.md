## Summary

***Stylotermes beesoni*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thakur in 1975; the type locality is India: Tripura: Tiliamura. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *beesoni*: Named after the forest entomologist C. F. C. Beeson
