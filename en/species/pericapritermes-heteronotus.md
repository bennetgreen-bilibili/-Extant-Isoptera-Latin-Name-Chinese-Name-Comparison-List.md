## Summary

***Pericapritermes heteronotus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kindia. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea, Nigeria, Sierra Leone.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *heteronotus*: From hetero + notus
