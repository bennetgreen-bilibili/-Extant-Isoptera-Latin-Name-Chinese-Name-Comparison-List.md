## Summary

***Nasutitermes elegantulus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe, Uganda. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *elegantulus*: Latin elegantulus (rather elegant)
