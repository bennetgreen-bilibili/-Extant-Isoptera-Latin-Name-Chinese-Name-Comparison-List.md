## Summary

***Ruptitermes reconditus*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Argentina: Chaco: Resistencia \[ 27.45S 58.9833W \]; Misiones: Posadas \[ 27.37S 55.9W \]; Misiones: Santa Ana \[ 27.37S 55.58W \]; Salta: Salta \[ 24.7833S 65.4167W \]; Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[ 15.627S 56.058W \]; Paraguay: Guairá: Villa Rica \[ 25.75S 56.4333W \]. Distribution: Neotropics, Panama, Brazil, Colombia, Venezuela, Bolivia, Ecuador, Argentina, French Guiana, Paraguay, Peru. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *reconditus*: Latin reconditus (hidden)
