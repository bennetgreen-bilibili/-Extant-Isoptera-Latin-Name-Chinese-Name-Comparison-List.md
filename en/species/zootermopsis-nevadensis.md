## Summary

***Zootermopsis nevadensis*** is a species of the genus *Zootermopsis*, in the family ARCHOTERMOPSIDAE. The species was described by Hagen in 1874; the type locality is United States: Nevada: Truckee. Distribution: western North America, western United States, western Canada, Mexico, introduced: Japan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Zootermopsis*: From Greek zōon (animal) + -termopsis (term- + -opsis).

Specific epithet *nevadensis*: Named after Nevada (USA)
