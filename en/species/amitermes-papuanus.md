## Summary

***Amitermes papuanus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Roisin in 1990; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Western Province: Morehead. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.
