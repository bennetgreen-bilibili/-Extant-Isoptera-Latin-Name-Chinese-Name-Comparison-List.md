## Summary

***Angulitermes nepalensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Mukherjee & Maiti in 2008; the type locality is Nepal: 10 miles NE Tanakpur, Nepal Forest. Distribution: Nepal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *nepalensis*: Named after the locality Nepal
