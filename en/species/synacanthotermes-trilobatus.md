## Summary

***Synacanthotermes trilobatus*** is a species of the genus *Synacanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kananga. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Synacanthotermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *trilobatus*: Latin tri- (three) + lobatus (lobed), three-lobed
