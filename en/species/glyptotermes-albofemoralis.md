## Summary

***Glyptotermes albofemoralis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wu, Tsai & Li in 2024; the type locality is Taiwan: Fushan Botanical Garden, Yilan Co. \[ 24.7569N 121.594E \]. Distribution: China (Taiwan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *albofemoralis*: From albo + femoralis
