## Summary

***Coptotermes remotus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1927; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: New Ireland: Kaewieng. Distribution: Papua New Guinea, Palau.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *remotus*: Latin remotus (remote)
