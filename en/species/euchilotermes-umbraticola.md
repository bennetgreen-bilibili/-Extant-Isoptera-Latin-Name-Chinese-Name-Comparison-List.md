## Summary

***Euchilotermes umbraticola*** is a species of the genus *Euchilotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: East Usambara; Daluni. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euchilotermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + cheilos (lip, margin) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *umbraticola*: From umbrati + cola
