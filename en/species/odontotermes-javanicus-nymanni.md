## Summary

***Odontotermes javanicus nymanni*** is a subspecies of *Odontotermes javanicus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Odontotermes*. The subspecies was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Java. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Odontotermes javanicus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nymanni*: Named after Nyman
