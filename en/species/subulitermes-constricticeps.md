## Summary

***Subulitermes constricticeps*** is a species of the genus *Subulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1991; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[1.848S 65.523W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Subulitermes*: From Latin subula (awl) + termes.

Specific epithet *constricticeps*: From constricti + ceps
