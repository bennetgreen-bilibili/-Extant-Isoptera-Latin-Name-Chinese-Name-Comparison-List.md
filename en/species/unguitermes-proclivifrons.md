## Summary

***Unguitermes proclivifrons*** is a species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Ruelle in 1973; the type locality is Zambia: 30 km ex Kitwe-Dola via Mwekera Forest Reserve. Distribution: Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Unguitermes*: From Latin unguis (claw) + termes.

Specific epithet *proclivifrons*: From proclivi + frons
