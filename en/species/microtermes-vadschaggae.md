## Summary

***Microtermes vadschaggae*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1907; the type locality is Tanzania: Kilimanjaro: Kibonoto; Kilimanjaro; Mischwald. Distribution: East Africa, Tanzania, Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *vadschaggae*: Named after the locality Vadschagga (Kilimanjaro, Tanzania)
