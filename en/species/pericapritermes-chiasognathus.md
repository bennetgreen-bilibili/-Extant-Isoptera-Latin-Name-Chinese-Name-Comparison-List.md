## Summary

***Pericapritermes chiasognathus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Cameroon: Lolodorf. Distribution: Republic of the Congo, Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Nigeria. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *chiasognathus*: Greek chiasma (cross) + gnathos (jaw), cross-jawed
