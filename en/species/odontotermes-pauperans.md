## Summary

***Odontotermes pauperans*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bissau. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Nigeria, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pauperans*: Latin pauperans (impoverishing)
