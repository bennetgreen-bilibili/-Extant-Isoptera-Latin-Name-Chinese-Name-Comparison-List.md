## Summary

***Microcerotermes insularis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1994; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: Middle Andaman Island: Bakultala. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *insularis*: Latin insularis (of islands)
