## Summary

***Neotermes rainbowi*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1926; the type locality is Tuvalu: Ellice Islands. Distribution: Cook Islands, Guam, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Tuvalu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rainbowi*: Named after W. J. Rainbow (Australian entomologist/arachnologist)
