## Summary

***Araujotermes parvellus*** is a species of the genus *Araujotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1923; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Araujotermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist R. L. Araujo; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parvellus*: From parv + ellus
