## Summary

***Eurytermes buddha*** is a species of the genus *Eurytermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bose & Maiti in 1966; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Coimbatore District: Coimbatore. Distribution: India, Tamil Nadu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eurytermes*: From Greek eurys (broad) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *buddha*: Sanskrit Buddha
