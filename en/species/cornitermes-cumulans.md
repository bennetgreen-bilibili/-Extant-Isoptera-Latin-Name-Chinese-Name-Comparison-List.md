## Summary

***Cornitermes cumulans*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Kollar in 1832; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Iperó, Ipanema \[23.4265S 47.5921W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay, Colombia, Bolivia, Ecuador. This site records 4 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *cumulans*: Latin cumulans (heaping)
