## Summary

***Pericapritermes nitobei*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Shiraki in 1909; the type locality is Taiwan: Maruyama. Distribution: South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, China, China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *nitobei*: Named after Nitobe (Japanese agronomist and educator Inazō Nitobe)
