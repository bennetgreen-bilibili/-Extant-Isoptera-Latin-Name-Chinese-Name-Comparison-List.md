## Summary

***Labiotermes laticephalus*** is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso do Sul: Ladário, Urucum \[19.155S 57.631W\]; Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Paraguay, Argentina.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiotermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *laticephalus*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
