## Summary

***Palmitermes impostor*** is the only extant species of the genus *Palmitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Hellemans & Roisin in 2017; the type locality is French Guiana: Petit Saut \[5.07N 53.032W\]. Distribution: Australia, Brazil, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Palmitermes*: From Latin palma (palm) + termes.

Specific epithet *impostor*: From im- (without) + postor
