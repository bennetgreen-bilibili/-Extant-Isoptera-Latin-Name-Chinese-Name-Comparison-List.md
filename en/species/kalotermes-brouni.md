## Summary

***Kalotermes brouni*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Froggatt in 1897; the type locality is New Zealand: Westfield, near Auckland. Distribution: New Zealand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brouni*: Named after Thomas Broun
