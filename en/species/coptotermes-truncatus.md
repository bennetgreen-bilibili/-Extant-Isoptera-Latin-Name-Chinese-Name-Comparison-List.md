## Summary

***Coptotermes truncatus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1897; the type locality is Madagascar: Nosy Be. Distribution: Madagascar, Seychelles (introduced).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *truncatus*: Latin truncatus (truncated)
