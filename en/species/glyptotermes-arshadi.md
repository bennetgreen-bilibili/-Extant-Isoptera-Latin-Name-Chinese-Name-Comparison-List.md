## Summary

***Glyptotermes arshadi*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Adampur. Distribution: Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *arshadi*: Named after Arshad
