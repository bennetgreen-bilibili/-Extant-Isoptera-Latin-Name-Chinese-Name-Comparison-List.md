## Summary

***Tumulitermes nichollsi*** is a species of the genus *Tumulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Yalgoo. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tumulitermes*: From Latin tumulus (mound) + termes.

Specific epithet *nichollsi*: Named after Nicholls
