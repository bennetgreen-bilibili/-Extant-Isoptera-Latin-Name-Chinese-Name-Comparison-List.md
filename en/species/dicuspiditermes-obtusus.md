## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes obtusus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1923; the type locality is India: Orissa: Chilka Lake: Barkuda Island. Distribution: India. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *obtusus*: Latin obtusus (blunt)
