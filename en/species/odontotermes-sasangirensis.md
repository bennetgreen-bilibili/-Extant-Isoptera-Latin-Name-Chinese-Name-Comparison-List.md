## Summary

***Odontotermes sasangirensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thakur in 1989; the type locality is India: Gujarat: Junagarh District: Sasan Gir Forest. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sasangirensis*: Named after Sasangire (Tanzania)
