## Summary

***Mirocapritermes jiangchengensis*** is a species of the genus *Mirocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1995; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mirocapritermes*: From Latin mirus (wonderful) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *jiangchengensis*: Named after the locality Jiangcheng
