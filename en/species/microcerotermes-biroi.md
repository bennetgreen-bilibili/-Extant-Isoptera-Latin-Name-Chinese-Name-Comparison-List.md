## Summary

***Microcerotermes biroi*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1905; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Kranket (Graget) Island. Distribution: South Asia, Oceania, Australia, Papua New Guinea, India, Indonesia, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Tonga.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *biroi*: Named after Lajos Biró
