## Summary

***Microcerotermes kudremukhae*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1997; the type locality is India: Karnataka: 11 km from Kudremukh: Koppa Division, Kalasa Forest Range. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kudremukhae*: Named after Kudremukh (Karnataka, India)
