## Summary

***Microcerotermes debilicornis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Mtewa: Somkhele, Lake Sibayi. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *debilicornis*: Latin debilis (weak) + cornu (horn), weak-horned
