## Summary

***Coatitermes mazaruniensis*** is a species of the genus *Coatitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Guyana, Ecuador, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coatitermes*: From Tupi Coati (coati) + termes.

Specific epithet *mazaruniensis*: Named after the locality Mazarun
