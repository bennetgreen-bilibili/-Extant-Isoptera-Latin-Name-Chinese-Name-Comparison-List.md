## Summary

***Apicotermes arquieri*** is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé & Noirot in 1955; the type locality is Central African Republic: Bossembélé. Distribution: Central African Republic, Congo Basin, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Apicotermes*: From Latin apex (tip, summit) + termes.

Specific epithet *arquieri*: Named after Arquier
