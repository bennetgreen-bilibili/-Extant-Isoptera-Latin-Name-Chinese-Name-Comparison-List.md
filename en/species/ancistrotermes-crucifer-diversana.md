## Summary

***Ancistrotermes crucifer diversana*** is a subspecies of *Ancistrotermes crucifer*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Ancistrotermes*. The subspecies was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Kakoulima; Kindia. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Ancistrotermes crucifer* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *diversana*: Latin diversus (diverse, different)
