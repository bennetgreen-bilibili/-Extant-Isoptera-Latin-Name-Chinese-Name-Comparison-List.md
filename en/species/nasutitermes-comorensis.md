## Summary

***Nasutitermes comorensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1910; the type locality is Comoros: Mohéli. Distribution: Comoros.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *comorensis*: Named after the locality Comor
