## Summary

***Incisitermes rhyzophorae*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hernández in 1994; the type locality is Cuba: Las Tunas: Cayo Sevilla. Distribution: Cuba, Bahamas.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *rhyzophorae*: After the plant genus Rhizophora
