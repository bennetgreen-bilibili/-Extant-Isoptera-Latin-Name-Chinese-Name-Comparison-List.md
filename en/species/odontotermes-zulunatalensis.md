## Summary

***Odontotermes zulunatalensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand. Distribution: southern Africa (South Africa).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zulunatalensis*: Named after the locality Zulunatal
