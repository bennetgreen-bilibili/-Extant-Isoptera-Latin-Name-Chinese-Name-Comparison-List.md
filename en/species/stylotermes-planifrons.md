## Summary

***Stylotermes planifrons*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chen in 1984; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Chongquing. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *planifrons*: Latin planus (flat) + frons (front), flat-fronted
