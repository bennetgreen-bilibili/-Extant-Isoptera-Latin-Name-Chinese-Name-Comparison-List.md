## Summary

***Coptotermes longignathus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xia & He in 1986; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Hekou County. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longignathus*: Latin longus (long) + Greek gnathos (jaw), long-jawed
