## Summary

***Angularitermes nasutissimus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Angularitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Guyana, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angularitermes*: From Latin angularis (angular) + termes.

Specific epithet *nasutissimus*: Latin nasutus (nasute) + -issimus (most), most nasute
