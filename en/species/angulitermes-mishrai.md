## Summary

***Angulitermes mishrai*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Sen-Sarma & Thakur in 1975; the type locality is India: Tripura: Ambasa, Zoelchara. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *mishrai*: Named after Mishra
