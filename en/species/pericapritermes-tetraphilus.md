## Summary

***Pericapritermes tetraphilus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1922; the type locality is Bangladesh: Chittagong Hill Tracts: Rangamati. Distribution: Guangdong (Shantou, Shaoguan), Fujian (Pinghe), Yunnan (Yunjinghong 500 m, Hekou 90 m, Pingbian, Jinping Mengla 370, 420, 450, 680 m, Mojiang 1400 m, Santaishan, Simao, Manyun); India; Bangladesh; Myanmar. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *tetraphilus*: From the descriptive term 'tetra' (four)
