## Summary

***Microcerotermes novaecaledoniae*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1915; the type locality is New Caledonia (French): Bogota-Rücken; Coné; La Madeleine; Umgebung von Yaté; near Canala. Distribution: Oceania, New Caledonia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *novaecaledoniae*: Named after New Caledonia
