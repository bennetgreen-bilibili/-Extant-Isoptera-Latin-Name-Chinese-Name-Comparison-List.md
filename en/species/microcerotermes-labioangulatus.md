## Summary

***Microcerotermes labioangulatus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Sen-Sarma & Thakur in 1975; the type locality is India: Tripura: Ambasa, Zoelchara. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *labioangulatus*: From labioangul + atus
