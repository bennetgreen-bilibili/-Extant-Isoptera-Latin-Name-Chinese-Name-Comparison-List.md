## Summary

***Nasutitermes arborum*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Smeathman in 1781; the type locality is Sierra Leone: Njala. Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Gambia, Nigeria, Senegal, Togo, Benin, Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Sierra Leone, Uganda. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *arborum*: Latin arborum (of trees)
