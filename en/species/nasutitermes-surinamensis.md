## Summary

***Nasutitermes surinamensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Brasil: Amapá: near Calçoene \[2.496N 50.9448W\]; Suriname: Commewijne: Ephrata \[5.8166N 54.75W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Bolivia, Guyana, Peru, Ecuador, Suriname, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *surinamensis*: Named after the locality Surinam
