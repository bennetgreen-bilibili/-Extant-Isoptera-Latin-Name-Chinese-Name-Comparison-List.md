## Summary

***Odontotermes globicola*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Anurhadhapura. Distribution: South Asia, India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *globicola*: From globi + cola
