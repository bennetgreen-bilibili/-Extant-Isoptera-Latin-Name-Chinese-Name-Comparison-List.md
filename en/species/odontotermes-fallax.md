## Summary

***Odontotermes fallax*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Tanzania: Meru Mt. Distribution: East Africa, Tanzania, Kenya, Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fallax*: From the descriptive term 'fallax' (deceptive)
