## Summary

***Machadotermes rigidus*** is a species of the genus *Machadotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Collins in 1977; the type locality is Cameroon: Sanaga-Maritime: Edea-Marienberg Forest Reserve: Lac Tissongo. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Machadotermes*: Named after a person (Machado); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rigidus*: Latin rigidus (rigid)
