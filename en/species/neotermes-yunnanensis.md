## Summary

***Neotermes yunnanensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xu & Han in 1985; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *yunnanensis*: After Yunnan (China)
