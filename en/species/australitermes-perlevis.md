## Summary

***Australitermes perlevis*** is a species of the genus *Australitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: 55 km southward from Darwin: 34 mile Siding, NT Railway. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Australitermes*: From Latin australis (southern) + termes.

Specific epithet *perlevis*: Latin per- (very) + levis (smooth), very smooth
