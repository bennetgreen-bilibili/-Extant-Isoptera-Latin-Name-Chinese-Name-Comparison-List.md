## Summary

***Ampoulitermes wynaadensis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ampoulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathur & Thapa in 1962; the type locality is India: Kerala: Wynaad, Periyar Forest Range. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ampoulitermes*: From Latin ampulla (flask) + termes.

Specific epithet *wynaadensis*: Named after the locality Wynaad
