## Summary

***Reticulitermes lucifugus siculus*** is a subspecies of *Reticulitermes lucifugus*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, genus *Reticulitermes*. The subspecies was described by Ghesini, Luchetti & Marini in 2024; the type locality is Italy: Sicily. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Reticulitermes lucifugus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *siculus*: Latin siculus (of Sicily)
