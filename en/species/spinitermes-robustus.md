## Summary

***Spinitermes robustus*** is a species of the genus *Spinitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Ixiamas \[13.769S 68.127W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Spinitermes*: From Latin spina (spine) + termes.

Specific epithet *robustus*: Latin robustus (robust)
