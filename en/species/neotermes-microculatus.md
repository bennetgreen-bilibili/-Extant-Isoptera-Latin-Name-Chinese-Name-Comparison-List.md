## Summary

***Neotermes microculatus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *microculatus*: Latin micro- (small) + oculatus (eyed): small-eyed
