## Summary

***Euhamitermes indicus*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Shevaroy Hills. Distribution: India, Tamil Nadu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *indicus*: After India
