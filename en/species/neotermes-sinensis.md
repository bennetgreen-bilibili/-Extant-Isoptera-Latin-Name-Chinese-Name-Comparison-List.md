## Summary

***Neotermes sinensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1924; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: West River: Ting Wu Shan Monastery. Distribution: China, Guangdong.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sinensis*: Latin Sinensis (of China)
