## Summary

***Odontotermes sellathorax*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Xia & Fan in 1982; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Bishan. Distribution: Sichuan (Bishan), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sellathorax*: Latin sella (saddle) + thorax (thorax), saddle-thoraxed
