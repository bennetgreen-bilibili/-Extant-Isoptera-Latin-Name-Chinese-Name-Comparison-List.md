## Summary

***Indotermes yunnanensis*** is a species of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by He & Xia in 1981; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Indotermes*: From India + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *yunnanensis*: After Yunnan (China)
