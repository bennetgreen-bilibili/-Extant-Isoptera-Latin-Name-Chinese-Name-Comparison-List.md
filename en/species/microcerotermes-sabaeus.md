## Summary

***Microcerotermes sabaeus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1957; the type locality is Yemen: Beihan. Distribution: Saudi Arabia, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sabaeus*: Latin Sabaeus (of Sheba, Arabian)
