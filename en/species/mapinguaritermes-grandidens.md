## Summary

***Mapinguaritermes grandidens*** is a species of the genus *Mapinguaritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mapinguaritermes*: From Mapinguari (a mythical Amazonian beast) + termes.

Specific epithet *grandidens*: From the descriptive term 'grand' (great)
