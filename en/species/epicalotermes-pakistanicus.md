## Summary

***Epicalotermes pakistanicus*** is a species of the genus *Epicalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Akhtar in 1974; the type locality is Pakistan: Baluchistan: Gwadar. Distribution: Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Epicalotermes*: From Greek epi- (upon) + kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pakistanicus*: After Pakistan
