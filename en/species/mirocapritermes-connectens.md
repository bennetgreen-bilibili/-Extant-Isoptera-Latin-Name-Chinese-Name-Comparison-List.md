## Summary

***Mirocapritermes connectens*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Mirocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mirocapritermes*: From Latin mirus (wonderful) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *connectens*: Latin connectens (connecting)
