## Summary

***Ahmaditermes xiangyunensis*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Gong in 1989; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Xiangyun County. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *xiangyunensis*: Named after the locality Xiangyun
