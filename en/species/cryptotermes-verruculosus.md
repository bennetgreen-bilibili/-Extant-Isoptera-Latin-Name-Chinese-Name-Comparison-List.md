## Summary

***Cryptotermes verruculosus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: South America, Bolivia, Guyana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *verruculosus*: From verrucul + osus
