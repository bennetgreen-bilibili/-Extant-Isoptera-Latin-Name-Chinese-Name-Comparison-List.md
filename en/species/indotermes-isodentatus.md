## Summary

***Indotermes isodentatus*** is a species of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Menghan. Distribution: Oriental region.

## Etymology

Genus name *Indotermes*: From India + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *isodentatus*: Greek isos (equal) + Latin dentatus (toothed), equal-toothed
