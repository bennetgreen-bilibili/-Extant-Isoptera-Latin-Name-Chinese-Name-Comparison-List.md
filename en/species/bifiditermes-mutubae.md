## Summary

***Bifiditermes mutubae*** is a species of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Harris in 1948; the type locality is Uganda: Kawanda Agricultural Research Station. Distribution: Kenya, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bifiditermes*: From Latin bifidus (cleft in two) + termes.

Specific epithet *mutubae*: Named after the locality Mutuba
