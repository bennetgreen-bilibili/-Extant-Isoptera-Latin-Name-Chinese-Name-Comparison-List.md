## Summary

***Odontotermes amaniensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Tanzania: Amani. Distribution: East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *amaniensis*: Named after the locality Aman
