## Summary

***Amitermes guineensis*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1992; the type locality is Nigeria: 43 miles from Maiduguri on Potiskum road. Distribution: West Africa.
