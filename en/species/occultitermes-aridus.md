## Summary

***Occultitermes aridus*** is a species of the genus *Occultitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gay in 1977; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Carnarvon Range. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Occultitermes*: From Latin occultus (hidden) + termes.

Specific epithet *aridus*: Latin aridus (arid, dry)
