## Summary

***Silvestritermes duende*** is a species of the genus *Silvestritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2012; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Serra do Cachimbo \[9.2719S 54.9394W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Silvestritermes*: Named after the entomologist F. Silvestri; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *duende*: Spanish/Portuguese duende (goblin)
