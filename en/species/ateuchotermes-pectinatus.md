## Summary

***Ateuchotermes pectinatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Kenya: Kaptagat. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ateuchotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + teuchos (implement, weapon) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pectinatus*: From pectin + atus
