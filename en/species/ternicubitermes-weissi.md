## Summary

***Ternicubitermes weissi*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Congo: Brazzaville. Distribution: Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Liberia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *weissi*: Named after Weiss
