## Summary

***Macrotermes hopini*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1956; the type locality is Myanmar: Myitkyina District: Hopin. Distribution: Myanmar, northeastern India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *hopini*: Named after the locality Hopin (Myanmar)
