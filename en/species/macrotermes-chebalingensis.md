## Summary

***Macrotermes chebalingensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1993; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Shixing County: Chebaling. Distribution: Guangdong, China, Shixing Chebaling.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *chebalingensis*: Named after the locality Chebaling
