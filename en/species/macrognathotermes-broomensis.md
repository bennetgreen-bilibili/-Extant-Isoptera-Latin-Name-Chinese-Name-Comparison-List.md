## Summary

***Macrognathotermes broomensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Mjöberg in 1920; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Broome. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrognathotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *broomensis*: Named after the locality Broom
