## Summary

***Tumulitermes marcidus*** is a species of the genus *Tumulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Torres Strait: Moa (Banks) Island. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tumulitermes*: From Latin tumulus (mound) + termes.

Specific epithet *marcidus*: Latin marcidus (withered)
