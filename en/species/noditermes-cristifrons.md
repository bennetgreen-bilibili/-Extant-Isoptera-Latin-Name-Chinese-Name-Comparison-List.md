## Summary

***Noditermes cristifrons*** is a species of the genus *Noditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai). Distribution: West Africa, Gambia, Senegal, Togo, Burkina Faso, Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Noditermes*: From Latin nodus (knot, node) + termes.

Specific epithet *cristifrons*: From cristi + frons
