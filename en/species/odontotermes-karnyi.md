## Summary

***Odontotermes karnyi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Bogor. Distribution: Southeast Asia (Indonesia).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *karnyi*: Named after Heinrich Hugo Karny
