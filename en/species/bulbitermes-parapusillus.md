## Summary

***Bulbitermes parapusillus*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Prew. Distribution: Bangladesh, India, Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *parapusillus*: From the descriptive term 'para' (beside)
