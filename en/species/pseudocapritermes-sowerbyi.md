## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes sowerbyi*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1924; the type locality is China: Kuliang, "in mountains Sowerby back of Foochow, 2500 ft.". Distribution: China. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *sowerbyi*: Named after A. de C. Sowerby (British naturalist, collector in China)
