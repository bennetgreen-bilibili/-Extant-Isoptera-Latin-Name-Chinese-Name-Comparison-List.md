## Summary

***Trichotermes ducis*** is a species of the genus *Trichotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1914; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kimuenza. Distribution: Old World tropics, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trichotermes*: From Greek thrix (hair) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ducis*: Latin ducis (of a leader)
