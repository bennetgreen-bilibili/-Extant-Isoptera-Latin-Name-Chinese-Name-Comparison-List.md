## Summary

***Longicaputermes sinaicus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Longicaputermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Egypt: Sinai Peninsula: Gabr. Amir. Distribution: Sinai Peninsula, Egypt, Israel.

## Etymology

Genus name *Longicaputermes*: From Latin longus (long) + caput (head) + termes.

Specific epithet *sinaicus*: Named after Sinai (peninsula, Egypt)
