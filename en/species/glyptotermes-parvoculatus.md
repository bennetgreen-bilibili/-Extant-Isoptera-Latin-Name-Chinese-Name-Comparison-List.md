## Summary

***Glyptotermes parvoculatus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: San Juan-Laventille: Northern Range \[ 10.755N 61.431W \]. Distribution: Caribbean, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parvoculatus*: From parvocul + atus
