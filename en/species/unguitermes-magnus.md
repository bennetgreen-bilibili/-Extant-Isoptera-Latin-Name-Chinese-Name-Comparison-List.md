## Summary

***Unguitermes magnus*** is a species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Ruelle in 1973; the type locality is Nigeria: Eastern Region, 19 km from Enugu on Onitsha Road. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Unguitermes*: From Latin unguis (claw) + termes.

Specific epithet *magnus*: Latin magnus (large)
