## Summary

***Cornitermes falcatus*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1952; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Autazes, Santa Amélia \[3.359S 59.283W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *falcatus*: Latin falcatus (sickle-shaped)
