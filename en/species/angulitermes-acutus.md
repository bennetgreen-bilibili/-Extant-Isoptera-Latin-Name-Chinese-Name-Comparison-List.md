## Summary

***Angulitermes acutus*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Mathur & Sen-Sarma in 1961; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Tinnevelly District: Pallayamcottah. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *acutus*: Latin acutus (sharp)
