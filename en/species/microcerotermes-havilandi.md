## Summary

***Microcerotermes havilandi*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *havilandi*: Named after the entomologist G. D. Haviland
