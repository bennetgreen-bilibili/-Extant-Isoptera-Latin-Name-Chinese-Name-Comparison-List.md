## Summary

***Longipeditermes kistneri*** is a species of the genus *Longipeditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Ahmad in 1985; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Lengkong Forest Reserve. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Longipeditermes*: From Latin longus (long) + pes (foot) + termes.

Specific epithet *kistneri*: Named after the entomologist D. H. Kistner
