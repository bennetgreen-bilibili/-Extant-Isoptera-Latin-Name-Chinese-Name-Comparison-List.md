## Summary

***Macrotermes gratus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Togo: Misahöhe. Distribution: West Africa, Togo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *gratus*: Latin gratus (pleasing)
