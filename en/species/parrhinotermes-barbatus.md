## Summary

***Parrhinotermes barbatus*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2011; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Southern Highlands: Lake Kutubu. Distribution: Indonesia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *barbatus*: From barb + atus
