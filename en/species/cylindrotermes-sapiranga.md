## Summary

***Cylindrotermes sapiranga*** is a species of the genus *Cylindrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cylindrotermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2007; the type locality is Brasil: Bahia: Mata de São João, Reserva da Sapiranga \[ 12.565S 38.032W \]. Distribution: Neotropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cylindrotermes*: From Greek kylindros (cylinder) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sapiranga*: From the Tupi name sapiranga, from sapi 'eye' + pyranga 'red', i.e. 'red-eyed'.
