## Summary

***Reticulitermes aurantius*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1987; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Danzhai. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *aurantius*: From the descriptive term 'aur' (gold)
