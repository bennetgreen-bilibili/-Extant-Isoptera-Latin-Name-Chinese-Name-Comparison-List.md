## Summary

***Nasutitermes profuscipennis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Adampur. Distribution: Bangladesh, India, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *profuscipennis*: From profusci + pennis
