## Summary

***Indotermes rongrensis*** is a species of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Assam: Garo Hills District: Rongrengiri. Distribution: Oriental region. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Indotermes*: From India + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rongrensis*: Named after the locality Rongrengiri (Garo Hills, Meghalaya, India)
