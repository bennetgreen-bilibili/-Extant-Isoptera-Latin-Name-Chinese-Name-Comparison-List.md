## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes punjabensis*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is Pakistan: Lyallpur. Distribution: Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *punjabensis*: Named after the locality Punjab
