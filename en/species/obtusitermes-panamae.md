## Summary

***Obtusitermes panamae*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Obtusitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Panama: Quipo. Distribution: Panama, Costa Rica, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Obtusitermes*: From Latin obtusus (blunt) + termes.

Specific epithet *panamae*: Named after Panama
