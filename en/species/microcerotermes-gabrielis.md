## Summary

***Microcerotermes gabrielis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1955; the type locality is Iraq: Tauq am Tschai. Distribution: South Asia, Middle East, Afghanistan, Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gabrielis*: Named after Gabriel
