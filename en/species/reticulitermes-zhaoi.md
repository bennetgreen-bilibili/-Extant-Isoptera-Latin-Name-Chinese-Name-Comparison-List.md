## Summary

***Reticulitermes zhaoi*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Li in 1983; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Jianyang County: Dazhulan. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *zhaoi*: Named after the Chinese entomologist Xiufu Zhao (1919–2004).
