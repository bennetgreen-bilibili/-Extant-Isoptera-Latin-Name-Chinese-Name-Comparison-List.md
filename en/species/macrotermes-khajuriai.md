## Summary

***Macrotermes khajuriai*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Assam, Khasi-Jaintia Hills District, Umsa Nongkharai. Distribution: northeastern India, Bhutan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *khajuriai*: Named after Khajuria
