## Summary

***Velocitermes beebei*** is a species of the genus *Velocitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Velocitermes*: From Latin velox (swift) + termes.

Specific epithet *beebei*: Named after William Beebe
