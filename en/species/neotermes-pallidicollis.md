## Summary

***Neotermes pallidicollis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1902; the type locality is Cameroon: Johann-Albrechtshöhe. Distribution: Cameroon, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pallidicollis*: From pallidi + collis
