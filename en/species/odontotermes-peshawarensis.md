## Summary

***Odontotermes peshawarensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1974; the type locality is Pakistan: 8 miles from Peshawar. Distribution: Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *peshawarensis*: Named after the locality Peshawar
