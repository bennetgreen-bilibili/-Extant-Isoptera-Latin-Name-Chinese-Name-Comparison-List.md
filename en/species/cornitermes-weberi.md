## Summary

***Cornitermes weberi*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1952; the type locality is Guyana: East Berbice-Corentyne: Oronoque River \[2.7N 57.42W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *weberi*: Named after a person; probably honors the American myrmecologist Neal A. Weber, but the etymology statement was not seen; uncertain.
