## Summary

***Nasutitermes longinasoides*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Lamag District: Balat. Distribution: Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *longinasoides*: From longinas + oides
