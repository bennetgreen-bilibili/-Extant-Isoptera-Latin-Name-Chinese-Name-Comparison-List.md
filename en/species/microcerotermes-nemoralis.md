## Summary

***Microcerotermes nemoralis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: Kasulu. Distribution: Tanzania, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nemoralis*: Latin nemoralis (of woods)
