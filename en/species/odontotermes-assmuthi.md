## Summary

***Odontotermes assmuthi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Borivali Jungle near Bombay. Distribution: South Asia (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *assmuthi*: Named after Assmuth
