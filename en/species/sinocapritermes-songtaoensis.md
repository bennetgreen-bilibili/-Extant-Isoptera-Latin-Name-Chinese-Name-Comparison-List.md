## Summary

***Sinocapritermes songtaoensis*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by He in 1993; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Songtao County. Distribution: China, Guizhou.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *songtaoensis*: Named after the locality Songtao
