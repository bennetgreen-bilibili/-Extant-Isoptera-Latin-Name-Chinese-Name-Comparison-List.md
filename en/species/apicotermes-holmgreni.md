## Summary

***Apicotermes holmgreni*** is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1956; the type locality is Malawi: near Nsakaru Hill on Namwera Road. Distribution: Malawi, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Apicotermes*: From Latin apex (tip, summit) + termes.

Specific epithet *holmgreni*: Named after the termite taxonomist N. Holmgren
