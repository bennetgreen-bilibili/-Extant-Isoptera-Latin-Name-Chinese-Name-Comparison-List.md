## Summary

***Glyptotermes insulanus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Sao Tome and Principe: Roça Infante D. Henrique. Distribution: São Tomé and Príncipe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *insulanus*: From Latin insulanus, of islands
