## Summary

***Microcerotermes palestinensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Spaeth in 1964; the type locality is Israel: Wadi Abyad (Nahal Lavan). Distribution: Middle East, Israel, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *palestinensis*: Named after the locality Palestin
