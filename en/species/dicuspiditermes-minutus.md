## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Riaz in 1992; the type locality is Malaysia: Johore: Kluang Forest. Distribution: Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
