## Summary

***Nasutitermes schoutedeni*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Basongo. Distribution: Angola, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Uganda, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa). This site records 5 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *schoutedeni*: Named after H. Schouteden
