***Occasitermes occasus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Occasitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1909; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Collie. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Occasitermes*: Etymology uncertain (perhaps Latin occasus 'setting, west'); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *occasus*: Latin occasus (setting, west)
