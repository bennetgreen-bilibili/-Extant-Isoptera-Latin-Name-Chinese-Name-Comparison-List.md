## Summary

***Thoracotermes macrothorax*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Thoracotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Cameroon: Lolodorf. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, Angola, Cameroon, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Nigeria, Sierra Leone.

## Etymology

Genus name *Thoracotermes*: From Greek thōrax (thorax) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *macrothorax*: From macro + thorax
