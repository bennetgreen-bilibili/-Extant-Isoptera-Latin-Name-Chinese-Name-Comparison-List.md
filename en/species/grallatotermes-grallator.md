## Summary

***Grallatotermes grallator*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Grallatotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1905; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Graget Island (= Kranket Island?). Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Grallatotermes*: From Latin grallator (stilt-walker) + termes.

Specific epithet *grallator*: Latin grallator (stilt-walker)
