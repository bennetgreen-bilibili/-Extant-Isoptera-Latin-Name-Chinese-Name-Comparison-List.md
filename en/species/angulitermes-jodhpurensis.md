## Summary

***Angulitermes jodhpurensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Verma in 1977; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Jodhpur. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *jodhpurensis*: Named after the locality Jodhpur
