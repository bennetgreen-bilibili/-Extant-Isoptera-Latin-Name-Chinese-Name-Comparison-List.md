## Summary

***Cryptotermes roonwali*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chhotani in 1970; the type locality is India: Kerala: North Malabar: Beliapatam. Distribution: southern India, Kerala, Karnataka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *roonwali*: Named after the termite taxonomist M. L. Roonwal
