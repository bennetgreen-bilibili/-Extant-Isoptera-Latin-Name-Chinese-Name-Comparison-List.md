## Summary

***Tumulitermes pastinator*** is a species of the genus *Tumulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1915; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: 34 miles Siding, Northern Territory Railway, approximately 6 km NE Southport. Distribution: Australia. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tumulitermes*: From Latin tumulus (mound) + termes.

Specific epithet *pastinator*: Latin pastinator (digger)
