## Summary

***Odontotermes maledictus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ghidini in 1941; the type locality is Ethiopia: Caschei; Elolo; Gongabaino. Distribution: Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *maledictus*: Latin maledictus (cursed)
