## Summary

***Neotermes collarti*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1955; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Ituri: Forêt de Kawa. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *collarti*: Named after Albert Collart
