## Summary

***Microcerotermes distinctus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1909; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Coolgardie. Distribution: Oceania, Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *distinctus*: From the descriptive term 'distinct' (distinct)
