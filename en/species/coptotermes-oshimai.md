## Summary

***Coptotermes oshimai*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light & Davis in 1929; the type locality is Indonesia: Sulawesi: Maros. Distribution: Indonesia (Sulawesi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *oshimai*: Named after Oshima (Japanese termite specialist Masamitsu Oshima)
