## Summary

***Coptotermes peregrinator*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Saleier Islands: Pamatata. Distribution: Indonesia (Sulawesi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *peregrinator*: Latin peregrinator (traveller)
