## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes angustignathus*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Malaysia: Penang. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *angustignathus*: From angusti + gnathus
