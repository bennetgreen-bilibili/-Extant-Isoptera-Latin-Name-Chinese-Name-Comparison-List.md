## Summary

***Cryptotermes sukauensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Lamag District: Sukau. Distribution: Malaysia (Sabah).

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *sukauensis*: Named after the locality Sukau
