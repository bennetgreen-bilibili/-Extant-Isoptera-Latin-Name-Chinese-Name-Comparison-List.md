## Summary

***Lacessititermes jacobsoni*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1930; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Fort de Kock. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *jacobsoni*: Named after E. Jacobson
