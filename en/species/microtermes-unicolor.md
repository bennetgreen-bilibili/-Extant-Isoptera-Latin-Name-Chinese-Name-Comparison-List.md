## Summary

***Microtermes unicolor*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1933; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun. Distribution: South Asia, Pakistan, India. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *unicolor*: Latin unus (one) + color (colour), one-coloured
