## Summary

***Bulbitermes bulbiceps*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Maiti & Saha in 2000; the type locality is India: Assam: Jorhat District, ca. 20 km SW of Jorhat towards Colaghat. Distribution: South Asia, India, Assam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *bulbiceps*: Latin bulbus (bulb) + -ceps (head), bulb-headed
