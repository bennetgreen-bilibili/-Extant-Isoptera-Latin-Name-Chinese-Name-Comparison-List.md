## Summary

***Microtermes najdensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1964; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Riyadh. Distribution: Arabian Peninsula, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *najdensis*: Named after the locality Najd
