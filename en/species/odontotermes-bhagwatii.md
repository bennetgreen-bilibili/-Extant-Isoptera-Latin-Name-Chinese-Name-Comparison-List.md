## Summary

***Odontotermes bhagwatii*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thakur in 1967; the type locality is India: Punjab: Pathankot, near the railway station. Distribution: South Asia (India).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bhagwatii*: Named after Bhagwat
