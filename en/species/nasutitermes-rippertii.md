## Summary

***Nasutitermes rippertii*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Rambur in 1842; the type locality is Cuba: Havana. Distribution: Cuba, Bahamas, Jamaica. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *rippertii*: Named after a person "Rippert" (likely a collector)
