## Summary

***Termes splendens*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Ghana: Kedje Keta. Distribution: Ghana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *splendens*: From the descriptive term 'splend' (splendid)
