## Summary

***Cucurbitermes sinensis*** is a species of the genus *Cucurbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li & Ping in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Longsheng County: Huaping forest. Distribution: China, Guangdong.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cucurbitermes*: From Latin cucurbita (gourd) + termes.

Specific epithet *sinensis*: Latin Sinensis (of China)
