## Summary

***Reticulitermes dabieshanensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wang & Li in 1984; the type locality is China: Henan Province: Shangcheng County: Borenao Mountain. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *dabieshanensis*: Named after the locality Dabieshan
