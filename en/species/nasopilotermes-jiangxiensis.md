## Summary

***Nasopilotermes jiangxiensis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Nasopilotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by He in 1988; the type locality is China: Jiangxi Province: Jiulian Mountains. Distribution: China (Jiangxi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasopilotermes*: From Latin nasus (nose) + pilus (hair) + termes.

Specific epithet *jiangxiensis*: Named after the locality Jiangx
