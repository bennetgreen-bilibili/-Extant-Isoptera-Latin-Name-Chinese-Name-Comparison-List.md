## Summary

***Stylotermes choui*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1981; the type locality is China: Hunan Province: Huitong. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *choui*: Named after the Chinese entomologist Chou Io
