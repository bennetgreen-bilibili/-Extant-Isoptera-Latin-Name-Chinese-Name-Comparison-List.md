## Summary

***Ancistrotermes wasmanni*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder & Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai). Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wasmanni*: Named after the entomologist E. Wasmann
