## Summary

***Amitermes gallagheri*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1988; the type locality is Oman: Jabal Hamar, west of Rayzut. Distribution: Oman, Saudi Arabia, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gallagheri*: Named after Gallagher
