## Summary

***Parvitermes antillarum*** is a species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Dominican Republic: Santo Domingo. Distribution: Haiti, Dominican Republic.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parvitermes*: From Latin parvus (small) + termes.

Specific epithet *antillarum*: Named after the Antilles
