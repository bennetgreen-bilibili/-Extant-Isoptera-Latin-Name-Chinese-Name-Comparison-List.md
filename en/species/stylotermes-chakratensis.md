## Summary

***Stylotermes chakratensis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Mathur & Thapa in 1963; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Chakrata. Distribution: China, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chakratensis*: Named after the locality Chakrat
