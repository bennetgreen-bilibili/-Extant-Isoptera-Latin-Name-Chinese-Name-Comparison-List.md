## Summary

***Kalotermes serrulatus*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gay in 1977; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: 65 km WNW Townsville. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *serrulatus*: From serrul + atus
