## Summary

***Nasutitermes horni*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Bandarawela. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *horni*: Named after Walther Horn
