## Summary

***Ophiotermes ugandaensis*** is a species of the genus *Ophiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1925; the type locality is Uganda: Kamozi. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ophiotermes*: From Greek ophis (snake) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ugandaensis*: Named after the locality Uganda
