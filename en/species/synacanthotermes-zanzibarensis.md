## Summary

***Synacanthotermes zanzibarensis*** is a species of the genus *Synacanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1915; the type locality is Tanzania: Zanzibar Island: Haïtajawa Hill. Distribution: East Africa, Tanzania, Ethiopia, Kenya.

## Etymology

Genus name *Synacanthotermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zanzibarensis*: Named after the locality Zanzibar
