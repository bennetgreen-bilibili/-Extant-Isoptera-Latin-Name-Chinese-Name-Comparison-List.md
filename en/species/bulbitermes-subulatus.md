## Summary

***Bulbitermes subulatus*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bandar Baroe. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *subulatus*: From subul + atus
