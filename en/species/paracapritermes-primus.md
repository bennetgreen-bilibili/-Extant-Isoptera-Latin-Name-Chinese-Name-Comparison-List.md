## Summary

***Paracapritermes primus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Paracapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Mt. Molloy. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Paracapritermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *primus*: Latin primus (first)
