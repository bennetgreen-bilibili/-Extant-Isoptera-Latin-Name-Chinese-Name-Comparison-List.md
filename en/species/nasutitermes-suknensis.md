## Summary

***Nasutitermes suknensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Prashad & Sen-Sarma in 1959; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Sukna. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *suknensis*: Named after the locality Sukna (Darjeeling district, West Bengal, India)
