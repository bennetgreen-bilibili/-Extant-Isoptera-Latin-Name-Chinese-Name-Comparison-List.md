## Summary

***Constrictotermes rupestris*** is a species of the genus *Constrictotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1997; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Serra da Mesa \[13.8288S 48.3211W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Constrictotermes*: From Latin constrictus (constricted) + termes.

Specific epithet *rupestris*: From the descriptive term 'rupes' (rock)
