## Summary

***Homallotermes pilosus*** is a species of the genus *Homallotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Mathur & Thapa in 1962; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Madras: Topslip. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Homallotermes*: From Greek homalos (even, level) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pilosus*: Latin pilosus (hairy)
