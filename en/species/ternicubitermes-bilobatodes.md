## Summary

***Ternicubitermes bilobatodes*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama. Distribution: Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *bilobatodes*: Latin bi- (two) + lobatus (lobed) + Greek -odes (like), two-lobed
