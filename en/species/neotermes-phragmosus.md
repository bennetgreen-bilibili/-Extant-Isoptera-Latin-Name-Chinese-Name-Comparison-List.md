## Summary

***Neotermes phragmosus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krecek & Scheffrahn in 2003; the type locality is Cuba: Guantánamo Province: Tortuguilla. Distribution: Mexico, Cuba.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *phragmosus*: Greek phragma (partition) + -osus, partitioned
