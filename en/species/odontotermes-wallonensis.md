## Summary

***Odontotermes wallonensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Ahmadnagar District: Sangamner: Wallon. Distribution: South Asia (India). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wallonensis*: Named after the locality Wallon
