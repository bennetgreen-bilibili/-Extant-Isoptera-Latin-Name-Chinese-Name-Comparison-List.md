## Summary

***Anenteotermes polyscolus*** is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Ghana: 10 mi N Wenchi on Bamboi Road. Distribution: West Africa, Central Africa, Congo Basin.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *polyscolus*: Greek polys (many) + skolios (curved), many-curved
