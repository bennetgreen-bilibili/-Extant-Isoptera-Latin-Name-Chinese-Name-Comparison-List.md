## Summary

***Nasutitermes proximus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Uruguay: Maldonado: La Sierra \[34.7667S 55.4167W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Uruguay. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *proximus*: Latin proximus (nearest)
