## Summary

***Odontotermes culturarum*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Tanzania: Myombo. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, East Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *culturarum*: From Latin cultura (cultivation)
