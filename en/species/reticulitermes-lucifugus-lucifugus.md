## Summary

***Reticulitermes lucifugus lucifugus*** is a subspecies of *Reticulitermes lucifugus*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, genus *Reticulitermes*. The subspecies was described by Rossi in 1792; the type locality is (country unknown): "Shores of Mediterranean". No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Reticulitermes lucifugus* below. This site records 5 junior synonyms for the subspecies.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *lucifugus*: Latin lux (light) + fugus (fleeing), light-avoiding
