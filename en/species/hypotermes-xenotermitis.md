## Summary

***Hypotermes xenotermitis*** is a species of the genus *Hypotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1896; the type locality is Myanmar: Palon (Pegù). Distribution: India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hypotermes*: From Greek hypo- (under, slightly) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *xenotermitis*: Greek xenos (strange) + termes (termite), alien termite
