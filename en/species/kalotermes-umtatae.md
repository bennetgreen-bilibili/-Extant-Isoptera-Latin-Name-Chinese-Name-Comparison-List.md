## Summary

***Kalotermes umtatae*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1949; the type locality is South Africa: Transkei: Mpane Forest, Umtata coastal belt. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *umtatae*: Named after Umtata (South Africa)
