## Summary

***Polyspathotermes sulcifrons*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Cameroon: Lolodorf. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sulcifrons*: Latin sulcus (groove) + frons (front), grooved-fronted
