## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes tenuis*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1923; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Simeulue, Sinabang. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tenuis*: Latin tenuis (slender)
