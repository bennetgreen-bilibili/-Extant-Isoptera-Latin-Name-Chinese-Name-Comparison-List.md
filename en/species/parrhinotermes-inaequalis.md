## Summary

***Parrhinotermes inaequalis*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *inaequalis*: From in- (without) + aequalis
