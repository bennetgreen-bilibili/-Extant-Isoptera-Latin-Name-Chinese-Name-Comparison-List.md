## Summary

***Microtermes subhyalinus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Kindia. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Gambia, Burkina Faso, Saudi Arabia, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *subhyalinus*: From subhyal + inus
