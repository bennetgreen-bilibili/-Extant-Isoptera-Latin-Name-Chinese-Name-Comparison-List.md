## Summary

***Hypotermes wayaoensis*** is a species of the genus *Hypotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Wang in 1990; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Baoshan City: Wayao. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Hypotermes*: From Greek hypo- (under, slightly) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wayaoensis*: Named after the type locality Wayao, Yunnan, China.
