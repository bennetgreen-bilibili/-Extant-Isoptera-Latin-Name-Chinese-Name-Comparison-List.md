## Summary

***Kalotermes isaloensis*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Cachan in 1949; the type locality is Madagascar: Isalo. Distribution: Madagascar, Comoros.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *isaloensis*: Named after the locality Isalo
