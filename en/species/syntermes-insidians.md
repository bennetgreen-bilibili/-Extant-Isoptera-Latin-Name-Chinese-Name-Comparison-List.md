## Summary

***Syntermes insidians*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1945; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Pitangueiras \[21.02S 48.22W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *insidians*: Latin insidians (lying in wait)
