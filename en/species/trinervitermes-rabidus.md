## Summary

***Trinervitermes rabidus*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1859; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Colombo. Distribution: Sri Lanka, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *rabidus*: Latin rabidus (rabid)
