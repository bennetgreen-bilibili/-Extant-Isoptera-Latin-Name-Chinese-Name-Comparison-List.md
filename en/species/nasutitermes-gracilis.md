## Summary

***Nasutitermes gracilis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1916; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon; Laguna; Sarai, near Paete. Distribution: Indonesia, Philippines. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *gracilis*: Latin gracilis (slender)
