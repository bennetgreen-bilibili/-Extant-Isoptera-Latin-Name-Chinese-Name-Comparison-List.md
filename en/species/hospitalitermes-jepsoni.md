## Summary

***Hospitalitermes jepsoni*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is Myanmar: River Pyinmana: Kaing. Distribution: Cambodia, Myanmar, Bangladesh, India, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *jepsoni*: Named after Jepson
