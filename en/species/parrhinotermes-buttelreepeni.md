## Summary

***Parrhinotermes buttelreepeni*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bandar Baroe; Malaysia: Taiping: Maxwell’s Hill. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Sumatra, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *buttelreepeni*: Named after the entomologist H. von Buttel-Reepen
