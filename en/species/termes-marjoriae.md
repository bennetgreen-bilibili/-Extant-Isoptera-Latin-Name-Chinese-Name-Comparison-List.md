## Summary

***Termes marjoriae*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is Myanmar: Selen. Distribution: Yunnan (Jinghong500 m); Myanmar, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *marjoriae*: Named after Marjorie
