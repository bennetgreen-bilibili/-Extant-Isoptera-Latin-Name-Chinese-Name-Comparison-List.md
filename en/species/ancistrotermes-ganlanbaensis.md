## Summary

***Ancistrotermes ganlanbaensis*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Huang in 1991; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong County: Ganlanba. Distribution: Yunnan (Jinghong 500m).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ganlanbaensis*: Named after the locality Ganlanba
