## Summary

***Foraminitermes harrisi*** is a species of the genus *Foraminitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 1963; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Keyberg, 10 km S Lubumbashi. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Foraminitermes*: From Latin foramen (opening) + termes.

Specific epithet *harrisi*: Named after Harris
