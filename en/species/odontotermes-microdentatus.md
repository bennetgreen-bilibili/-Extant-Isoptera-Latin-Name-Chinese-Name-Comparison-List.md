## Summary

***Odontotermes microdentatus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehradun: Dobhalwala. Distribution: South Asia (India).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *microdentatus*: From microdent + atus
