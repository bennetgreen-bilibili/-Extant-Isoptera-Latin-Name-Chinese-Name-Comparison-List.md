## Summary

***Pericapritermes paetensis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1920; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Paete. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *paetensis*: Named after Paete, Laguna, Philippines
