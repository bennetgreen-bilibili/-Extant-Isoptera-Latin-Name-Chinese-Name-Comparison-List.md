## Summary

***Reticulitermes urbis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bagnères, Uva & Clément in 2003; the type locality is France: Isère: Domène. Distribution: Europe, Croatia, Greece, Italy, France.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *urbis*: Latin urbis (of the city)
