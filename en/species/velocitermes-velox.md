## Summary

***Velocitermes velox*** is a species of the genus *Velocitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Mojos \[14.5667S 68.8833W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Velocitermes*: From Latin velox (swift) + termes.

Specific epithet *velox*: Latin velox (swift)
