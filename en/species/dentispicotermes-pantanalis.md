## Summary

***Dentispicotermes pantanalis*** is a species of the genus *Dentispicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Nova Xavantina \[ 14.6733S 52.3531W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Cerrado.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dentispicotermes*: From Latin dens (tooth) + spica (spike) + termes.

Specific epithet *pantanalis*: After the Pantanal wetland (Brazil)
