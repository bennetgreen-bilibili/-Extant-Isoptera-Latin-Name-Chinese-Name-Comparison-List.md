## Summary

***Heterotermes aureus*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1920; the type locality is United States: Arizona: Santa Catalina Mountains: Sabino Canyon. Distribution: southwestern North America, southwestern United States, Mexico. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aureus*: Latin aureus (golden)
