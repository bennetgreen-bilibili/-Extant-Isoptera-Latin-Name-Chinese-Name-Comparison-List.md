## Summary

***Ceylonitermellus hantanae*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ceylonitermellus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Hantana. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ceylonitermellus*: From Ceylon (Sri Lanka) + diminutive -ellus (with termes).

Specific epithet *hantanae*: Named after Hantana (Sri Lanka)
