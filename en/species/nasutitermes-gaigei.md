## Summary

***Nasutitermes gaigei*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Bolivia, Trinidad and Tobago, Guyana, Martinique (France), Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *gaigei*: Named after Frederick M. Gaige
