## Summary

***Glyptotermes ignotus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wilkinson in 1959; the type locality is Uganda: 19 miles from Kampala on Jinia road. Distribution: Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ignotus*: Latin ignotus (unknown)
