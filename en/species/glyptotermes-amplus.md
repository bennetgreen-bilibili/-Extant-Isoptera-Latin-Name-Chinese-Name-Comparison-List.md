## Summary

***Glyptotermes amplus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn, Su & Krecek in 2001; the type locality is Saint Lucia: Edmond Forest Reserve. Distribution: Saint Lucia, Martinique.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *amplus*: Latin amplus (ample, large)
