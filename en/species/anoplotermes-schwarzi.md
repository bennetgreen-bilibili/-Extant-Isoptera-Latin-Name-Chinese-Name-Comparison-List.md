## Summary

***Anoplotermes schwarzi*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Banks in 1919; the type locality is Cuba: Cayamas. Distribution: Cuba.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *schwarzi*: Named after E. A. Schwarz (American entomologist)
