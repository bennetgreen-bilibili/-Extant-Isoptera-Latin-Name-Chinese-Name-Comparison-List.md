## Summary

***Neotermes saleierensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Indonesia: Sulawesi: Saleier Islands: Pamatata. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *saleierensis*: Named after the locality Saleier (Selayar) Islands, South Sulawesi, Indonesia
