## Summary

***Parrhinotermes ruiliensis*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Huang in 1982; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Ruili. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ruiliensis*: Named after the locality Ruil
