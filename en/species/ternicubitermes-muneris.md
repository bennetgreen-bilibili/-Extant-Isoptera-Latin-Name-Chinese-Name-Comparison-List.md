## Summary

***Ternicubitermes muneris*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Welgelegen. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *muneris*: Latin muneris (of a duty/gift)
