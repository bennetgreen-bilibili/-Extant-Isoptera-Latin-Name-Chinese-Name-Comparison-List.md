## Summary

***Eurytermes assmuthi assmuthi*** is a subspecies of *Eurytermes assmuthi*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), genus *Eurytermes*. The subspecies was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Khandala, near Bombay. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Eurytermes assmuthi* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eurytermes*: From Greek eurys (broad) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *assmuthi*: Named after Assmuth
