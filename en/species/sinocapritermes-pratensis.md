## Summary

***Sinocapritermes pratensis*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1993; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Shixing County: Xianrendong of Chebaling. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *pratensis*: Latin pratensis (of meadows, pratum = meadow)
