## Summary

***Syntermes magnoculus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Chapada dos Guimarães \[15.4606S 55.7497W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *magnoculus*: From magn + oculus
