## Summary

***Procapritermes dakshinae*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani & Ferry in 1995; the type locality is India: Kerala: on road from Kolattupuzha to Nedumangad, vicinity of village Arippal. Distribution: India, Kerala.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *dakshinae*: From Sanskrit dakṣiṇa (southern)
