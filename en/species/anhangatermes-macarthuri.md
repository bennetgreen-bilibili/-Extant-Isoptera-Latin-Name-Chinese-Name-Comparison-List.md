## Summary

***Anhangatermes macarthuri*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Anhangatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1990; the type locality is Brasil: Amapá: Serra do Navio \[0.8955N 52.0022W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anhangatermes*: From Tupi Anhangá (a mythical forest spirit) or the place name + -termes.

Specific epithet *macarthuri*: Named after MacArthur
