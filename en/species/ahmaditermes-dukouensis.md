## Summary

***Ahmaditermes dukouensis*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Deng in 1987; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Dukou city. Distribution: China (Sichuan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dukouensis*: Named after the locality Dukou
