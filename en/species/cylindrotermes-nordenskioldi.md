## Summary

***Cylindrotermes nordenskioldi*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Cylindrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cylindrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Río Tuichi \[ 14.28S 68.54W \]. Distribution: Neotropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cylindrotermes*: From Greek kylindros (cylinder) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nordenskioldi*: Named after Nordenskiöld (Swedish-Finnish explorer A. E. Nordenskiöld)
