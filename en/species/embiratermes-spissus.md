## Summary

***Embiratermes spissus*** is a species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson & Banks in 1957; the type locality is Guyana: East Berbice-Corentyne: Acarai Mountains, Itabu Creek \[1.7N 57.9167W\]. Distribution: Colombia, Peru, Brazil, Ecuador, Guyana, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Embiratermes*: From Tupi Embirá (a tree) + -termes.

Specific epithet *spissus*: Latin spissus (dense)
