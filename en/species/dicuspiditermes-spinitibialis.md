## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes spinitibialis*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2001; the type locality is Thailand: Tung Sa-Lang Luang. Distribution: East Asia, China, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *spinitibialis*: From spini + tibialis
