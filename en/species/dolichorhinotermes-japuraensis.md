## Summary

***Dolichorhinotermes japuraensis*** is a species of the genus *Dolichorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Constantino in 1991; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[ 1.848S 65.523W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Guyana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dolichorhinotermes*: From Greek dolichos (long) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *japuraensis*: Named after the locality Japura
