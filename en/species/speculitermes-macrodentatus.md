## Summary

***Speculitermes macrodentatus*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Pitsanulok Province: Tung Sa-Lang Luang National Park. Distribution: southern China, Thailand, Cambodia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *macrodentatus*: From macrodent + atus
