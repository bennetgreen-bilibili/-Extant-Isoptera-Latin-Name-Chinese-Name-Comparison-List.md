## Summary

***Reticulitermes oreophilus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Li in 1983; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Huaping of Longsheng Gezu Zizhixian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *oreophilus*: Greek oros (mountain) + philos (loving), mountain-loving
