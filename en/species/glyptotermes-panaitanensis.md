## Summary

***Glyptotermes panaitanensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thakur in 1989; the type locality is Indonesia: Panaitan Island. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *panaitanensis*: Named after Panaitan Island, Indonesia
