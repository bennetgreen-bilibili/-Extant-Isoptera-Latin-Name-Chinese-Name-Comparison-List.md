## Summary

***Neotermes sanctaecrucis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Solomon Islands: Santa Cruz Archipelago: Graciosa Bay. Distribution: Solomon Islands, Vanuatu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sanctaecrucis*: Latin sancta (holy) + crux (cross), holy cross
