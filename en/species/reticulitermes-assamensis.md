## Summary

***Reticulitermes assamensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gardner in 1945; the type locality is India: Assam: Sadiya. Distribution: India, Bhutan, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *assamensis*: Named after the locality Assam
