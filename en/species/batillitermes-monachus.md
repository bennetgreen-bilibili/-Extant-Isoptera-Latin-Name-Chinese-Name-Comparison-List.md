## Summary

***Batillitermes monachus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Batillitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Uys in 1994; the type locality is Zimbabwe: Insiza, 48 km ex Bulawayo-Gwelo. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Batillitermes*: From Latin batillum (shovel) + termes.

Specific epithet *monachus*: Latin monachus (monk)
