## Summary

***Nasutitermes pluriarticulatus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *pluriarticulatus*: Latin pluri- (many) + articulatus (jointed), many-jointed
