## Summary

***Hyleotermes brevipilus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Hyleotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Guyana, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hyleotermes*: From Greek hylē (forest, wood) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brevipilus*: From the descriptive term 'brev' (short)
