## Summary

***Hospitalitermes sharpi*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Negeri Sembilan: Berhentian Tingi; Negeri Sembilan: Sungei Ujong (Seremban); Singapore: ?. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *sharpi*: Named after D. Sharp
