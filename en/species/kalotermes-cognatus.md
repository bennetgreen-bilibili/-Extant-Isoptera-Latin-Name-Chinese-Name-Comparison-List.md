## Summary

***Kalotermes cognatus*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gay in 1976; the type locality is New Zealand: Kermadec Islands: Raoul Island. Distribution: New Zealand, Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cognatus*: From cogn + atus
