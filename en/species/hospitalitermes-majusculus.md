## Summary

***Hospitalitermes majusculus*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by He & Gao in 1984; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Damenglong: Baka. Distribution: Yunnan (Damenglong, Baka), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *majusculus*: Latin majusculus (somewhat larger)
