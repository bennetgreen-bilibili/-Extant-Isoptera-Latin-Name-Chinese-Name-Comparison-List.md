## Summary

***Macrotermes serrulatus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is Myanmar: Mogok. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Myanmar, Bangladesh, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *serrulatus*: From serrul + atus
