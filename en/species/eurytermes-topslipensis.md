## Summary

***Eurytermes topslipensis*** is a species of the genus *Eurytermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thapa in 1963; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Topslip: Mount Stuart. Distribution: South Asia, Western Ghats, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eurytermes*: From Greek eurys (broad) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *topslipensis*: Named after the locality Topslip
