## Summary

***Unguitermes bouilloni*** is a species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1965; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Takundi: River Kwando. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Unguitermes*: From Latin unguis (claw) + termes.

Specific epithet *bouilloni*: Named after André Bouillon
