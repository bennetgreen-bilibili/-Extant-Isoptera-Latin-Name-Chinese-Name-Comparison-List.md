## Summary

***Polyspathotermes intercalatus*** is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Congo: Brazzaville. Distribution: Angola, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *intercalatus*: Latin intercalatus (intercalated)
