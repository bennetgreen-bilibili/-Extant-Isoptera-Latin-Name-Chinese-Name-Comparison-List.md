## Summary

***Ruptitermes arboreus*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: Brazil and neighboring South American countries, Colombia, Guyana, French Guiana, Suriname.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *arboreus*: Latin arboreus (arboreal)
