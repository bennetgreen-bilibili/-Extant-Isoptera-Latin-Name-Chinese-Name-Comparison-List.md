## Summary

***Glyptotermes daiyunensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Huang in 1986; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Daiyunshan. Distribution: China (Fujian).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *daiyunensis*: Named after the locality Daiyun
