## Summary

***Odontotermes brunneus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is India: Bengal. Distribution: South Asia, India, unspecified. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brunneus*: Latin brunneus (brown)
