## Summary

***Coptotermes sepangensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1956; the type locality is Malaysia: Selangor: Sepang. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sepangensis*: Named after the locality Sepang (Malaysia)
