## Summary

***Odontotermes yunnanensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Pu-erh. Distribution: Yunnan, China, Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *yunnanensis*: After Yunnan (China)
