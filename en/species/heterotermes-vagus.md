## Summary

***Heterotermes vagus*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1927; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: Darwin. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *vagus*: Latin vagus (wandering)
