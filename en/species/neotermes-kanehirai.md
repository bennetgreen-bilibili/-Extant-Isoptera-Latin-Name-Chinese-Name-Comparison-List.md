## Summary

***Neotermes kanehirai*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1917; the type locality is Palau: Palao Island. Distribution: Taiwan, Palau.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kanehirai*: Named after Ryōzō Kanehira
