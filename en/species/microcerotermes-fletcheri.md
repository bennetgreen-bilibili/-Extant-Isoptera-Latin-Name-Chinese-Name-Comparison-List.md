## Summary

***Microcerotermes fletcheri*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Karnataka: Mysore: Bababudin Hills. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fletcheri*: Named after Fletcher
