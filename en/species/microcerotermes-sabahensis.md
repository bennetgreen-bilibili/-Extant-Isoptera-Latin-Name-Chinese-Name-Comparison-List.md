## Summary

***Microcerotermes sabahensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Sandakan North District: Sibuga Forest Reserve, Kebun China. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sabahensis*: Named after the locality Sabah
