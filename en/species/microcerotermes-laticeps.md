## Summary

***Microcerotermes laticeps*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Estcourt. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *laticeps*: Latin latus (broad) + -ceps (head), broad-headed
