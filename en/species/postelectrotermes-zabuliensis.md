## Summary

***Postelectrotermes zabuliensis*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ravan & Akhtar in 1999; the type locality is Iran: Zabul \[Jalahi\]. Distribution: West Asia, Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *zabuliensis*: Named after the locality Zabul (Afghanistan)
