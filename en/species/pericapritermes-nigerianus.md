## Summary

***Pericapritermes nigerianus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Nigeria: Ibadan; Lagos; Olokemeji. Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Gambia, Ghana, Nigeria. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *nigerianus*: Named after Nigeria
