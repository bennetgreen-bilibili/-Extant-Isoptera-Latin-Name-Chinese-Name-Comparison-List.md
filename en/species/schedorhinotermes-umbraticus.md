## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes umbraticus*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1927; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: New Britain: Bai, near Rabaul. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *umbraticus*: Latin umbraticus (shaded, of the shade; umbra = shadow)
