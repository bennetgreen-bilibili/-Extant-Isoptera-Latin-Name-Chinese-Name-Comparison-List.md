## Summary

***Ahmaditermes crassinasus*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Hainan Island. Distribution: Hainan (Xinglong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *crassinasus*: From the descriptive term 'crass' (thick)
