## Summary

***Glyptotermes ficus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Rongjiang. Distribution: China (Guizhou).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ficus*: Latin ficus (fig)
