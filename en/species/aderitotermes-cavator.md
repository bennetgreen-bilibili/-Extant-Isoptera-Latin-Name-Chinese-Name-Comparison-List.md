## Summary

***Aderitotermes cavator*** is a species of the genus *Aderitotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Nigeria: Northern Region: Samaru, near Zaria. Distribution: West Africa, Cameroon, Gambia, Ivory Coast, Nigeria.
