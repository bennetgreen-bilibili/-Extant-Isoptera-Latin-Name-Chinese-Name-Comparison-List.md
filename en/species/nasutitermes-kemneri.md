## Summary

***Nasutitermes kemneri*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder & Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *kemneri*: Named after the entomologist N. A. Kemner
