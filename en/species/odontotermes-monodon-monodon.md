## Summary

***Odontotermes monodon monodon*** is a subspecies of *Odontotermes monodon*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Odontotermes*. The subspecies was described by Gerstäcker in 1891; the type locality is Mozambique: Quilimane, Kikengo. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Odontotermes monodon* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *monodon*: Greek monos (single) + odous (tooth), one-toothed
