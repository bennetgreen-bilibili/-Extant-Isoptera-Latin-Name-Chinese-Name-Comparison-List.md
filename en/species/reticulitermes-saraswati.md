## Summary

***Reticulitermes saraswati*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Khasi-Jaintia Hills District: Shillong. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *saraswati*: Named after "Saraswati" (Indian name or the goddess)
