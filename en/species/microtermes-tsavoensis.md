## Summary

***Microtermes tsavoensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Bacchus in 1997; the type locality is Kenya: Maktau, Tsavo Park West. Distribution: East Africa, Tsavo, Kenya.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *tsavoensis*: Named after the locality Tsavo
