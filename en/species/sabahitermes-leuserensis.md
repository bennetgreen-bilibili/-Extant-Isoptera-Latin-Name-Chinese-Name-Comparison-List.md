## Summary

***Sabahitermes leuserensis*** is a species of the genus *Sabahitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gathorne-Hardy in 2001; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Aceh Tenggara: Ketambe field station. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sabahitermes*: From Sabah (Malaysia) + termes.

Specific epithet *leuserensis*: Named after the locality Leuser
