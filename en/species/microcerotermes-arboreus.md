## Summary

***Microcerotermes arboreus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: Neotropics, Panama, Colombia, Brazil, French Guiana, Bolivia, Cayman Islands, Guyana, Haiti, Honduras, Nicaragua, Peru, Puerto Rico, Suriname, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *arboreus*: Latin arboreus (arboreal)
