## Summary

***Microcerotermes rambanensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thakur in 1964; the type locality is India: Jammu and Kashmir: Ramban: Maitra Forest. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rambanensis*: Named after the locality Ramban (Jammu and Kashmir, India)
