## Summary

***Odontotermes luoyangensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wang & Li in 1984; the type locality is China: Henan Province: Luoyang City: Longmen Xishan. Distribution: Henan, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *luoyangensis*: Named after the locality Luoyang
