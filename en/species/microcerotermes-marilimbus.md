## Summary

***Microcerotermes marilimbus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1984; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Wanning. Distribution: Hainan (Wanning, Ledong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *marilimbus*: Latin mare (sea) + limbus (border)
