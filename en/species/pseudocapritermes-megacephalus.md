## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes megacephalus*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Afzal in 1989; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang. Distribution: Pakistan, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *megacephalus*: From the descriptive term 'meg' (large)
