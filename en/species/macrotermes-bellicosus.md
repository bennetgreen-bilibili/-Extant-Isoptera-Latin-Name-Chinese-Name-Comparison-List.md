## Summary

***Macrotermes bellicosus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Smeathman in 1781; the type locality is Sierra Leone: Njala. Distribution: Old World tropics, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Togo, Benin, Ghana, Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Kenya, Liberia, Malawi, Mauritania, Mozambique, Niger, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Somalia, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda, Yemen. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *bellicosus*: Latin bellicosus (warlike)
