## Summary

***Microtermes grassei*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ghidini in 1955; the type locality is Ivory Coast: Akakro. Distribution: West Africa, Togo, Gambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *grassei*: Named after the zoologist P.-P. Grassé
