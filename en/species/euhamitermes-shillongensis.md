## Summary

***Euhamitermes shillongensis*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1960; the type locality is India: Assam: Shillong. Distribution: South Asia, Meghalaya, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *shillongensis*: Named after the locality Shillong
