## Summary

***Ahmaditermes dinghuensis*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Mt. Dinghushan: Zhaoqing. Distribution: China, Vietnam. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dinghuensis*: Named after the locality Dinghu
