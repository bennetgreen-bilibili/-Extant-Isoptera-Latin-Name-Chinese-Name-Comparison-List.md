## Summary

***Furculitermes hendrickxi*** is a species of the genus *Furculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1960; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Furculitermes*: From Latin furcula (little fork) + termes.

Specific epithet *hendrickxi*: Named after Hendrickx
