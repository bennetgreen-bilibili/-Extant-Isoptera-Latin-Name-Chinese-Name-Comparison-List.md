## Summary

***Stylotermes triplanus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Liu in 1981; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Huili Xian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *triplanus*: Latin triplanus (three-planed, tri- + planus)
