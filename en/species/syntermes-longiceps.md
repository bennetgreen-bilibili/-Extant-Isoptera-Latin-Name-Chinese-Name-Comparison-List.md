## Summary

***Syntermes longiceps*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1995; the type locality is Brasil: Roraima: São Luiz \[1.015N 60.04W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longiceps*: Latin longus (long) + -ceps (head), long-headed
