## Summary

***Mirocapritermes snyderi*** is a species of the genus *Mirocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Ukhia. Distribution: Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mirocapritermes*: From Latin mirus (wonderful) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *snyderi*: Named after the termite taxonomist T. E. Snyder
