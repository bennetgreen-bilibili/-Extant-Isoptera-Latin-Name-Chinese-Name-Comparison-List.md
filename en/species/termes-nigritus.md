## Summary

***Termes nigritus*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *nigritus*: From the descriptive term 'nigr' (black)
