## Summary

***Reticulitermes chinensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1923; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Suifu. Distribution: China, India, Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *chinensis*: Latin Chinensis (of China)
