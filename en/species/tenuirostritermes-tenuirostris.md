## Summary

***Tenuirostritermes tenuirostris*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Tenuirostritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1904; the type locality is Mexico: Jalisco; Veracruz: Orizaba. Distribution: United States, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tenuirostritermes*: From Latin tenuis (slender) + rostrum (beak) + termes.

Specific epithet *tenuirostris*: From tenui + rostris
