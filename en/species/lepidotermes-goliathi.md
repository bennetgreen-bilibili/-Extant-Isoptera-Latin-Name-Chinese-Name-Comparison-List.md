## Summary

***Lepidotermes goliathi*** is a species of the genus *Lepidotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga District: Ngomeni. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lepidotermes*: From Greek lepis (scale) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *goliathi*: Named after Goliath (alluding to large size)
