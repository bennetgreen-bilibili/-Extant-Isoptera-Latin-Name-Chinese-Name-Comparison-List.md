## Summary

***Glyptotermes eucalypti*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Froggatt in 1897; the type locality is Australia: New South Wales: Sydney. Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *eucalypti*: After the plant genus Eucalyptus
