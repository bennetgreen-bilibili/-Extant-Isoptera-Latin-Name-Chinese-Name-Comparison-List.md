## Summary

***Glyptotermes dilatatus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bugnion & Popoff in 1910; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Ambalangoda. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dilatatus*: From dilat + atus
