## Summary

***Postelectrotermes longus*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Seychelles: Aldabra: Takamaka. Distribution: Comoros, Madagascar, Seychelles. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *longus*: Latin longus (long)
