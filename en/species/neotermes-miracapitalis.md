## Summary

***Neotermes miracapitalis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xu & Han in 1985; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinping. Distribution: Yunnan (Jinping).

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *miracapitalis*: Latin mirus (wonderful) + caput (head), wondrous-headed
