## Summary

***Nasutitermes seghersi seghersi*** is a subspecies of *Nasutitermes seghersi*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), genus *Nasutitermes*. The subspecies was described by Roisin & Pasteels in 1996; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Madang Province: Yoro. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Nasutitermes seghersi* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *seghersi*: Named after a person "Seghers" (identity unconfirmed)
