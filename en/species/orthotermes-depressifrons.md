## Summary

***Orthotermes depressifrons*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Orthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Ghana: Aburi. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *depressifrons*: From depressi + frons
