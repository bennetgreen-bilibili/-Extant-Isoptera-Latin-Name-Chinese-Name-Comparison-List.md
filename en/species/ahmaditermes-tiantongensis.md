## Summary

***Ahmaditermes tiantongensis*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xin in 1993; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Mt. Tiantong. Distribution: China (Zhejiang).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tiantongensis*: Named after the locality Tiantong
