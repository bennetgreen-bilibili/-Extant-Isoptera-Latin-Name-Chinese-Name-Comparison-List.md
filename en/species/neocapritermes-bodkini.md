## Summary

***Neocapritermes bodkini*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1923; the type locality is Guyana: Potaro-Siparuni: Tumatumari \[ 5.27N 59.14W \]. Distribution: Colombia, Guyana, Brazil, Peru. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *bodkini*: Named after Bodkin
