## Summary

***Cryptotermes merwei*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Fuller in 1921; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Pinetown, Illovo. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *merwei*: Named after van der Merwe
