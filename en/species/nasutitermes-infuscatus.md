## Summary

***Nasutitermes infuscatus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1902; the type locality is Malawi: Zomba. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania, Zimbabwe, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *infuscatus*: From infusc + atus
