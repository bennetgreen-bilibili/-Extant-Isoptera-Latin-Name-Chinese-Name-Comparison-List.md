## Summary

***Calcaritermes colei*** is a species of the genus *Calcaritermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1962; the type locality is Mexico: San Luis Potosi, above El Naranjo. Distribution: Mexico, Belize, Guatemala, Honduras.

Genus name *Calcaritermes*: From Latin calcar (spur) + termes.

Specific epithet *colei*: Named after Cole
