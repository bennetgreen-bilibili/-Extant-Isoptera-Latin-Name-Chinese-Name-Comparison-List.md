## Summary

***Araujotermes nanus*** is a species of the genus *Araujotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1991; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[1.848S 65.523W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Araujotermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist R. L. Araujo; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nanus*: Greek nanos (dwarf)
