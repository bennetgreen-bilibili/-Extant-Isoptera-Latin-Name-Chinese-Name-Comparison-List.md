## Summary

***Bifiditermes pintoi*** is a species of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Talawila near Puttalam. Distribution: Sri Lanka, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bifiditermes*: From Latin bifidus (cleft in two) + termes.

Specific epithet *pintoi*: Named after Pinto
