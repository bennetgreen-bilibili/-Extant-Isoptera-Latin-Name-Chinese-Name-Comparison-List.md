## Summary

***Incisitermes laterangularis*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Han in 1982; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Ninghai. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *laterangularis*: From laterangul + aris
