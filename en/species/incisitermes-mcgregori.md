## Summary

***Incisitermes mcgregori*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1921; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Rizal Province: Culi Culi, near Manila. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *mcgregori*: Named after McGregor (likely American ornithologist R. C. McGregor)
