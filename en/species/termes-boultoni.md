## Summary

***Termes boultoni*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Coaton & Sheasby in 1978; the type locality is Namibia: 48 km ex Rundu Grootfontein. Distribution: Zambia, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *boultoni*: Named after Boulton
