## Summary

***Coptotermes bentongensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1956; the type locality is Malaysia: Pahang: Bentong. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bentongensis*: Named after the locality Bentong
