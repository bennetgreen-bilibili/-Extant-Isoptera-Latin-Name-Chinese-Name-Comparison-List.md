## Summary

***Cryptotermes cylindroceps*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Curaçao: Santa Barbara. Distribution: South America, Venezuela, Colombia, Aruba, Bonaire, Curaçao.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *cylindroceps*: From cylindro + ceps
