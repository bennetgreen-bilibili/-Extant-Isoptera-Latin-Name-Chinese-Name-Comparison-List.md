## Summary

***Ahmaditermes lipingensis*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1988; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Liping. Distribution: Guizhou (Liping).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lipingensis*: Named after the locality Liping
