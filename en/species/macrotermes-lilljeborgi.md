## Summary

***Macrotermes lilljeborgi*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1896; the type locality is Cameroon: Kitta. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *lilljeborgi*: Named after Lilljeborg (Swedish zoologist Wilhelm Lilljeborg)
