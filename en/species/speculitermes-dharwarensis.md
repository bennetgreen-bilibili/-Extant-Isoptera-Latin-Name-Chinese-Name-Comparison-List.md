## Summary

***Speculitermes dharwarensis*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1964; the type locality is India: Karnataka (Mysore): Dharwar. Distribution: South Asia, Karnataka, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *dharwarensis*: Named after the locality Dharwar
