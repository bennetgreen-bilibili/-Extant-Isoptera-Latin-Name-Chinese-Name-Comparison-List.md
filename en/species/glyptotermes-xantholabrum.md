## Summary

***Glyptotermes xantholabrum*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1926; the type locality is Samoa: Upolu Island: Apia. Distribution: Samoa, Papua New Guinea, French Polynesia, Vanuatu. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *xantholabrum*: Greek xanthos (yellow) + Latin labrum (lip), yellow-lipped
