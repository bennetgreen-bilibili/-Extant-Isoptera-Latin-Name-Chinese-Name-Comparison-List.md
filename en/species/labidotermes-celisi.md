## Summary

***Labidotermes celisi*** is the only extant species of the genus *Labidotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Deligne & Pasteels in 1969; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Butembo. Distribution: Central African Republic, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labidotermes*: From Greek labis (forceps) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *celisi*: Named after Celis
