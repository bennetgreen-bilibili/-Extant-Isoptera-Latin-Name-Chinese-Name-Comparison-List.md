## Summary

***Nasutitermes simaluris*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1923; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Pulu Babi; Sumatra: Simeulue; Sumatra: Simeulue, Sinabang. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *simaluris*: After Simeulue Island (Indonesia)
