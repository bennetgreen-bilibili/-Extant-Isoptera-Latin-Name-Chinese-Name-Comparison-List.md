## Summary

***Zootermopsis laticeps*** is a species of the genus *Zootermopsis*, in the family ARCHOTERMOPSIDAE. ## Etymology

Genus name *Zootermopsis*: From Greek zōon (animal) + -termopsis (term- + -opsis).

Specific epithet *laticeps*: Latin latus (broad) + -ceps (head), broad-headed
