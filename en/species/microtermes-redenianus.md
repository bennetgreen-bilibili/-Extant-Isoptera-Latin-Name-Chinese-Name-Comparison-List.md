## Summary

***Microtermes redenianus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga. Distribution: East Africa, Tanzania, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *redenianus*: Named after a person "Reden" (identity unconfirmed)
