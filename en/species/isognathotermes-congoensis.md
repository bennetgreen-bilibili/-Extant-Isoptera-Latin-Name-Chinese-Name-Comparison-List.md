## Summary

***Isognathotermes congoensis*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Banana. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *congoensis*: After Congo
