## Summary

***Unguitermes unidentatus*** is a species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Ruelle in 1973; the type locality is Namibia: 40 km ex Rundu-Grootfontein. Distribution: Namibia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Unguitermes*: From Latin unguis (claw) + termes.

Specific epithet *unidentatus*: From unident + atus
