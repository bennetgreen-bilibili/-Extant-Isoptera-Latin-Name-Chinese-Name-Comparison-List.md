## Summary

***Acanthotermes acanthothorax*** is the only extant species of the genus *Acanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1898; the type locality is Cameroon: Ekundu; Kitta. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, East Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acanthotermes*: From Greek akantha (thorn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *acanthothorax*: From acantho + thorax
