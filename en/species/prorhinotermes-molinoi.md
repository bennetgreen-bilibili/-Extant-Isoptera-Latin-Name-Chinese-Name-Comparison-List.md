## Summary

***Prorhinotermes molinoi*** is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Panama: Largo Remo Island. Distribution: Panama, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prorhinotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *molinoi*: Named after Molino
