## Summary

***Malaysiotermes holmgreni*** is a species of the genus *Malaysiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1968; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak, 12 miles S of Kuching. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Malaysiotermes*: From Malaysia + termes.

Specific epithet *holmgreni*: Named after the termite taxonomist N. Holmgren
