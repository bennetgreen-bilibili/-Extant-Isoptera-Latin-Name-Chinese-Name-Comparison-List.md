## Summary

***Sinocapritermes xiushanensis*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by He in 1993; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Xiushan County. Distribution: China, Chongqing.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *xiushanensis*: Named after the locality Xiushan
