## Summary

***Nasutitermes olidus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1926; the type locality is Fiji: Taveuni. Distribution: Fiji.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *olidus*: Latin olidus (smelly)
