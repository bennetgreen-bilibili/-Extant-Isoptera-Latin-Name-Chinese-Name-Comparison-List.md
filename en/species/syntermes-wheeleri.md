## Summary

***Syntermes wheeleri*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1945; the type locality is Brasil: "Rio" (probably Rio de Janeiro) \[22.9S 43.23W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wheeleri*: Named after the myrmecologist W. M. Wheeler
