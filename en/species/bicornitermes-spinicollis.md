## Summary

***Bicornitermes spinicollis*** is a species of the genus *Bicornitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wilkinson in 1958; the type locality is Nigeria: Eastern Region: Nkpoku, 19 km from Port Harcourt on Owerri road. Distribution: Cameroon, Ghana, Nigeria, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bicornitermes*: From Latin bi- (two) + cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *spinicollis*: From spini + collis
