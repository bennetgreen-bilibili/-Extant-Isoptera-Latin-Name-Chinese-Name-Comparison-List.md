## Summary

***Coptotermes silvaticus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Harris in 1968; the type locality is Gabon: Belinga, 70 km ENE Makokou. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *silvaticus*: Latin silvaticus (of the forest, silva = forest)
