## Summary

***Trinervitermes disparatus*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Bathellier in 1927; the type locality is Vietnam: Cauda, near Nhatrang. Distribution: Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *disparatus*: From dispar + atus
