## Summary

***Coptotermes longistriatus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Huang in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Nanhai County. Distribution: China (Guangdong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longistriatus*: Latin longi- (long) + striatus (striated): long-striated
