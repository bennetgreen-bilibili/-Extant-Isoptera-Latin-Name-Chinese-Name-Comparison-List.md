## Summary

***Pericapritermes dolichocephalus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by John in 1925; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Kota Bahru; Malaysia: Selangor: Kajang. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *dolichocephalus*: Greek dolichos (long) + kephalē (head), long-headed
