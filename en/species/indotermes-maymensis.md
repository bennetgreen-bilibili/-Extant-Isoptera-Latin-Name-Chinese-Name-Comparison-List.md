## Summary

***Indotermes maymensis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1958; the type locality is Myanmar: Maymyo: Botanical Garden. Distribution: Myanmar, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Indotermes*: From India + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *maymensis*: Named after the locality Maym
