## Summary

***Silvestritermes gnomus*** is a species of the genus *Silvestritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1991; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[1.848S 65.523W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Silvestritermes*: Named after the entomologist F. Silvestri; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gnomus*: Greek gnomos (gnome)
