## Summary

***Hirtitermes brabazoni*** is a species of the genus *Hirtitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gathorne-Hardy in 2001; the type locality is Indonesia: Sulawesi: Dumoga-Bone National Park. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hirtitermes*: From Latin hirtus (hairy) + termes.

Specific epithet *brabazoni*: Named after Brabazon
