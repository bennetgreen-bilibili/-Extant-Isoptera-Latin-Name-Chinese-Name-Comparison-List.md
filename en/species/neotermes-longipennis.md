## Summary

***Neotermes longipennis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1930; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Fort de Kock. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longipennis*: Latin longus (long) + penna (wing), long-winged
