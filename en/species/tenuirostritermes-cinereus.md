## Summary

***Tenuirostritermes cinereus*** is a species of the genus *Tenuirostritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Buckley in 1862; the type locality is United States: Texas: Llano County; Texas: San Saba County. Distribution: United States, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tenuirostritermes*: From Latin tenuis (slender) + rostrum (beak) + termes.

Specific epithet *cinereus*: Latin cinereus (ash-grey)
