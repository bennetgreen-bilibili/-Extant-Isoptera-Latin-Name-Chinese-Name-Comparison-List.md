## Summary

***Reticulitermes wuyishanensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Huang in 1986; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Wuyishan. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *wuyishanensis*: Named after the locality Wuyishan
