## Summary

***Sinonasutitermes erectinasus*** is a species of the genus *Sinonasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Hainan Island: Lohwei. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinonasutitermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + nasutus (nasute) + termes.

Specific epithet *erectinasus*: From e- (without) + rectinasus
