## Summary

***Proneotermes tacicuarensis*** is a species of the genus *Proneotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Jurado-Vargas, Méndez-Montiel, García-Díaz & Cruz-de León in 2025; the type locality is Mexico: Michoacán: Tacicuaro. Distribution: Mexico (Michoacán).

## Etymology

Genus name *Proneotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tacicuarensis*: Named after the locality Tacicuaro (Michoacán, Mexico)
