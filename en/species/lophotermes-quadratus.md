## Summary

***Lophotermes quadratus*** is a species of the genus *Lophotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1927; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: Stapleton. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lophotermes*: From Greek lophos (crest, ridge) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *quadratus*: Latin quadratus (square)
