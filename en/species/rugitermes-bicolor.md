## Summary

***Rugitermes bicolor*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: Venezuela, Guyana, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *bicolor*: Latin bi- (two) + color (colour), two-coloured
