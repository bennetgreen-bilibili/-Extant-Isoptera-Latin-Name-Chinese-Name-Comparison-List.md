## Summary

***Bulbitermes constrictus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *constrictus*: From the descriptive term 'constrict' (constricted)
