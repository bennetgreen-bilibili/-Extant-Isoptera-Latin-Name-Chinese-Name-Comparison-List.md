## Summary

***Pericapritermes durga*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Cherrapunji. Distribution: India, Bhutan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *durga*: Durga, Hindu goddess
