## Summary

***Lacessititermes holmgreni*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Light & Wilson in 1936; the type locality is Philippines: Mindanao: Surigao Province: Dinagat Island: Dinagat. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *holmgreni*: Named after the termite taxonomist N. Holmgren
