## Summary

***Termes laticornis*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *laticornis*: From lati + cornis
