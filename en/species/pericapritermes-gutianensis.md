## Summary

***Pericapritermes gutianensis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Li & Ma in 1983; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Shanghang: Gutian. Distribution: China (Fujian).

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *gutianensis*: Named after the locality Gutian
