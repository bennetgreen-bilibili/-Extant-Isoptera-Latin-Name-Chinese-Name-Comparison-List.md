## Summary

***Mirocapritermes concaveus*** is a species of the genus *Mirocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Khao Yai. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mirocapritermes*: From Latin mirus (wonderful) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *concaveus*: From concav + eus
