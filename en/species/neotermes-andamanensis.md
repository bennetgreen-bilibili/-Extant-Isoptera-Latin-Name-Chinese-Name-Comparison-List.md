## Summary

***Neotermes andamanensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1933; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: North Andaman Island. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *andamanensis*: Named after the locality Andaman
