## Summary

***Reticulitermes qingdaoensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Ma in 1987; the type locality is China: Shandong Province: Quingdao. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *qingdaoensis*: Named after the locality Qingdao
