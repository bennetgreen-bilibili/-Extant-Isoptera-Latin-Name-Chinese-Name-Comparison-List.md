## Summary

***Syntermes molestus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Burmeister in 1839; the type locality is Brasil: Bahia. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Peru, French Guiana, Venezuela, Bolivia, Ecuador, Guyana, Suriname. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *molestus*: Latin molestus (troublesome)
