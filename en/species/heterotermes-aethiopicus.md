## Summary

***Heterotermes aethiopicus*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Ethiopia: Kottouki Dagaga. Distribution: Ethiopia, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Sudan, United Arab Emirates, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aethiopicus*: After Ethiopia (Africa)
