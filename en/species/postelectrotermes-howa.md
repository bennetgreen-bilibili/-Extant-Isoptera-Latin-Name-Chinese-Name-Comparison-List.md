## Summary

***Postelectrotermes howa*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1897; the type locality is Madagascar: Fenerive. Distribution: Indian Ocean islands, Madagascar, Réunion, Comoros, Mauritius, Réunion (France). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *howa*: After the Hova people (Madagascar)
