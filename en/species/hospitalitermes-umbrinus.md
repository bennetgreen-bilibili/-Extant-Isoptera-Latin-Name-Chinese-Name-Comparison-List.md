## Summary

***Hospitalitermes umbrinus*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *umbrinus*: Latin umbrinus (umber, brown)
