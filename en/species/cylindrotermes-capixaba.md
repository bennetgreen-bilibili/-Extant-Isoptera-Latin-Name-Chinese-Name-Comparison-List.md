## Summary

***Cylindrotermes capixaba*** is a species of the genus *Cylindrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cylindrotermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2007; the type locality is Brasil: Espírito Santo: Cariacica \[ 20.2639S 40.42W \]. Distribution: Neotropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cylindrotermes*: From Greek kylindros (cylinder) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *capixaba*: After capixaba (native of Espírito Santo, Brazil)
