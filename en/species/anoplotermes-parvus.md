## Summary

***Anoplotermes parvus*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1923; the type locality is Panama: Frijoles. Distribution: Brazil, Guiana Shield, Amazon region, Central America and Caribbean. This site records 4 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *parvus*: Latin parvus (small)
