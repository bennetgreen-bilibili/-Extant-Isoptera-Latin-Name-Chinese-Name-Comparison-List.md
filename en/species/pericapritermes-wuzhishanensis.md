## Summary

***Pericapritermes wuzhishanensis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Li in 1982; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Wuzhishan. Distribution: China (Hainan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *wuzhishanensis*: Named after the locality Wuzhishan
