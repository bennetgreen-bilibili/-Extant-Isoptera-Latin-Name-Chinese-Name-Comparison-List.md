## Summary

***Coptotermes lacteus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: New South Wales: Shoalhaven. Distribution: eastern Australia. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lacteus*: Latin lacteus (milky)
