## Summary

***Angulitermes bhagsunagensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Thakur in 2008; the type locality is India: Himachal Pradesh: Bhagsunag, Dharamshala. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *bhagsunagensis*: Named after the locality Bhagsunag
