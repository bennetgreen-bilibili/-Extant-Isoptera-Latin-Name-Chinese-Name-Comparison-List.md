## Summary

***Termes medioculatus*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *medioculatus*: From mediocul + atus
