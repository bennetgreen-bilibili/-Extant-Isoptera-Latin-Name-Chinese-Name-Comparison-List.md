## Summary

***Macrotermes gilvus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Bogor. Distribution: Old World tropics, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Cambodia, Timor-Leste, Myanmar, Vietnam. This site records 12 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *gilvus*: Latin gilvus (yellowish-grey)
