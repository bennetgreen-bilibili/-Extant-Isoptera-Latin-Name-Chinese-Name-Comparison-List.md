## Summary

***Rhinotermes marginalis*** is a species of the genus *Rhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Linnaeus in 1758; the type locality is Suriname: Marowijne: Confluence of Perica Creek and Cottica River \[ 5.669N 54.447W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Caribbean, Bolivia, Ecuador, Peru, Suriname, Venezuela. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rhinotermes*: From Greek rhis (nose) + Latin termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *marginalis*: Latin marginalis (marginal)
