## Summary

***Dentispicotermes trapezia*** is a species of the genus *Dentispicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2024; the type locality is Paraguay: Concepción: Arroyito: "Private Reserve forest" \[ 23.05S 56.728W \]. Distribution: Bolivia, Paraguay, Pantanal–Chaco.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dentispicotermes*: From Latin dens (tooth) + spica (spike) + termes.

Specific epithet *trapezia*: Greek trapezion (trapezium)
