## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes holmgreni*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bahsoemboe; Sumatra: Pajacombo; Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *holmgreni*: Named after the termite taxonomist N. Holmgren
