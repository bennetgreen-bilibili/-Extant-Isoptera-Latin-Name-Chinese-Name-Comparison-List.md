## Summary

***Neocapritermes braziliensis*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: União, rio Mataurá \[ 5.582S 60.815W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Peru, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *braziliensis*: Named after the locality Brazil
