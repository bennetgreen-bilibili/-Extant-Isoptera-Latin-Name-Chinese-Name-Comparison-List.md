## Summary

***Rugitermes tinto*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2020; the type locality is Colombia: Risaralda: Pereira \[ 4.81N 75.695W \]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *tinto*: Spanish/Portuguese tinto (dark red, dyed)
