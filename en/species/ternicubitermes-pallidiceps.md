## Summary

***Ternicubitermes pallidiceps*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Lubumbashi. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *pallidiceps*: From pallidi + ceps
