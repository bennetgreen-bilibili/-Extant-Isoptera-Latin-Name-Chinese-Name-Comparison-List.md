## Summary

***Termes incognitus*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Fontaine et al. in 2025; the type locality is French Guiana. Distribution: French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *incognitus*: From in- (without) + cognitus
