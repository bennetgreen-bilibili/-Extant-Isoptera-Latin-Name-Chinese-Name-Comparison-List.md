## Summary

***Amitermes coachellae*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1930; the type locality is United States: California: Palm Springs Station, Mission Canyon; Nevada: Upper Coachella Valley, Boulder. Distribution: United States.
