## Summary

***Microcerotermes zuluensis rubriceps*** is a subspecies of *Microcerotermes zuluensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae), genus *Microcerotermes*. The subspecies was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Mkosi. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Microcerotermes zuluensis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rubriceps*: From rubri + ceps
