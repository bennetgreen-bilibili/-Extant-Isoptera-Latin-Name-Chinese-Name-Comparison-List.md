## Summary

***Cryptotermes pingyangensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by He & Xia in 1983; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Pingyang. Distribution: Zhejiang (Pingyang).

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *pingyangensis*: Named after the locality Pingyang
