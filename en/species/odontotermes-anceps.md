## Summary

***Odontotermes anceps*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Ethiopia: ("Abyssinia"). Distribution: East Africa (Kenya, Ethiopia, Tanzania).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *anceps*: From the descriptive term 'anceps' (two-headed)
