## Summary

***Gnathamitermes perplexus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Gnathamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Banks in 1920; the type locality is United States: Texas: Victoria. Distribution: United States, Mexico. This site records 6 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Gnathamitermes*: Contains Greek gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes; further derivation uncertain.

Specific epithet *perplexus*: From the descriptive term 'perplex' (perplexing)
