## Summary

***Allognathotermes ivorensis*** is a species of the genus *Allognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé & Noirot in 1955; the type locality is Ivory Coast: Ferkessédougou. Distribution: West Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Allognathotermes*: From Greek allos (other) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ivorensis*: Named after Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire)
