## Summary

***Incisitermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1933; the type locality is Mexico: Colima State: Jala, near Colima. Distribution: Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
