## Summary

***Lacessititermes atrior*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1912; the type locality is Indonesia: Java; Malaysia: ?Borneo: Bukit Timah \[in Singapore, not Sarawak\]. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *atrior*: From a- (without) + trior
