## Summary

***Nasutitermes latifrons*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1896; the type locality is Cameroon: Ekundu. Distribution: Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Benin, Ghana, Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe, Sierra Leone, Sudan, Togo, Uganda. This site records 5 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *latifrons*: From lati + frons
