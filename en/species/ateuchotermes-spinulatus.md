## Summary

***Ateuchotermes spinulatus*** is a species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ateuchotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + teuchos (implement, weapon) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *spinulatus*: From spinul + atus
