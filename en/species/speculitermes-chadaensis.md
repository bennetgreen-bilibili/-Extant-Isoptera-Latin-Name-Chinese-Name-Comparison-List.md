## Summary

***Speculitermes chadaensis*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thapa in 1964; the type locality is India: Madhya Pradesh: South Mandala Forest Division: Karanjia Forest Range, 884 m: Chada. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *chadaensis*: Named after the locality Chada
