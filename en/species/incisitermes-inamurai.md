## Summary

***Incisitermes inamurai*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1912; the type locality is Taiwan: Pingtung Co.: Wanlitong. Distribution: China (Taiwan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *inamurai*: Named after Inamura (Japanese)
