## Summary

***Neotermes tectonae*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Dammerman in 1916; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: near Samarang, Kedoeng-Djati. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tectonae*: From Latin Of Tectona (teak tree)
