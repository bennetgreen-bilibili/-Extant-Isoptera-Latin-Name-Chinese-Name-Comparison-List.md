## Summary

***Polyspathotermes inclitus*** is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Zambia: Banguelo. Distribution: Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *inclitus*: Latin inclitus (renowned)
