## Summary

***Glyptotermes sicki*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Brasil: Rio de Janeiro: Ilha Grande \[ 23.133S 44.171W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sicki*: Named after Helmut Sick (German-Brazilian ornithologist)
