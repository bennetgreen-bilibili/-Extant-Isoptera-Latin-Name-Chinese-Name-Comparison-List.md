## Summary

***Odontotermes planiceps*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisantu. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *planiceps*: Latin planus (flat) + -ceps (head), flat-headed
