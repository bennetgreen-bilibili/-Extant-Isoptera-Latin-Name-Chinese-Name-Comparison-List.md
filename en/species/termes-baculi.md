## Summary

***Termes baculi*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Cameroon: Ekundu. Distribution: Old World tropics, Cameroon, Nigeria, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *baculi*: From Latin baculum (staff, stick)
