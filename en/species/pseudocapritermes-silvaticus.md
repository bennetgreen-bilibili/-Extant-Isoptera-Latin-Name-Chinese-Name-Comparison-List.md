## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes silvaticus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Depok. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *silvaticus*: Latin silvaticus (of the forest, silva = forest)
