## Summary

***Globitermes brachycerastes*** is a species of the genus *Globitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Han in 1987; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: Yunnan (Jinghong 500 m), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Globitermes*: From Latin globus (ball, sphere) + termes.

Specific epithet *brachycerastes*: Greek brachys (short) + keras (horn), short-horned
