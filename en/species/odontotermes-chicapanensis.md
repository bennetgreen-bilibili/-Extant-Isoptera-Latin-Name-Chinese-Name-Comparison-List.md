## Summary

***Odontotermes chicapanensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1956; the type locality is Angola: Étang Shá-Mutuka: Alto Chicapa, 400 km SSE of Dundo. Distribution: southern Africa, Angola.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chicapanensis*: Named after the locality Chicapan
