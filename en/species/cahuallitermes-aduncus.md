## Summary

***Cahuallitermes aduncus*** is a species of the genus *Cahuallitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1994; the type locality is Mexico: Chiapas, 5 miles NE Chiapa. Distribution: Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cahuallitermes*: From the place name Cahualli; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aduncus*: Latin aduncus (hooked)
