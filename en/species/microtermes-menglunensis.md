## Summary

***Microtermes menglunensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Huang in 1991; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Mengla County: Menglun. Distribution: Yunnan, China, Menglun.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *menglunensis*: Named after the locality Menglun
