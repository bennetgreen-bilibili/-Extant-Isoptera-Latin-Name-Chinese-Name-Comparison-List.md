## Summary

***Glyptotermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
