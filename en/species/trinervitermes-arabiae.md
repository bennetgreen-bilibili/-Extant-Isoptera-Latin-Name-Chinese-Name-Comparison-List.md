## Summary

***Trinervitermes arabiae*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1957; the type locality is Yemen: Dhala. Distribution: Saudi Arabia, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *arabiae*: Named after Arabia
