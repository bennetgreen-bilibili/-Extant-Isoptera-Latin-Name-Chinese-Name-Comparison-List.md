## Summary

***Angulitermes truncatus*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Ghana: Accra. Distribution: Senegal, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *truncatus*: Latin truncatus (truncated)
