## Summary

***Incisitermes furvus*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 1994; the type locality is Puerto Rico: Guajataca. Distribution: Puerto Rico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *furvus*: Latin furvus (dark, black)
