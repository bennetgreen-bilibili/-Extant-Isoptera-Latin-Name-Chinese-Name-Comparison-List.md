## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes seclusus*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1933; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Babinda. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *seclusus*: Latin seclusus (secluded)
