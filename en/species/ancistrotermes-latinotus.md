## Summary

***Ancistrotermes latinotus*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1912; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Luapula. Distribution: southern Africa, Malawi, South Africa, Zambia, Angola, Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Mozambique, Eswatini, Tanzania, Uganda, Zimbabwe. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latinotus*: From lati + notus
