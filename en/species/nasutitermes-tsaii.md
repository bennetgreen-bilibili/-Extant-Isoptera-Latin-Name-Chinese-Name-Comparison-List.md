## Summary

***Nasutitermes tsaii*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Huang & Han in 1987; the type locality is China: Tibet: Medog. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *tsaii*: Named after the Chinese termite taxonomist Pang-hwa Tsai (1902–1983), a founder of Chinese termite studies.
