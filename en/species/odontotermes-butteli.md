## Summary

***Odontotermes butteli*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bah Boelian; Sumatra: Lau Boentoe; Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat; Malaysia: Selangor: Gap. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *butteli*: Named after Buttel(-Reepen)
