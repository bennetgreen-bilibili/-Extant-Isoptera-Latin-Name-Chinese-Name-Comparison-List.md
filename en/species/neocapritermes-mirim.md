## Summary

***Neocapritermes mirim*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Bandeira & Cancello in 1992; the type locality is Brasil: Roraima: Ilha de Maracá \[ 3.4N 61.65W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *mirim*: Tupi mirim (small)
