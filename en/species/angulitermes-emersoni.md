## Summary

***Angulitermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Dariadighi. Distribution: Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
