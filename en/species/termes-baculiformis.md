## Summary

***Termes baculiformis*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1909; the type locality is Madagascar: St. Marie de Marovoay, Majunga. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *baculiformis*: Latin baculum (rod) + -formis (shaped), rod-shaped
