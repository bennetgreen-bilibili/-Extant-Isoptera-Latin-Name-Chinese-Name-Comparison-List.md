## Summary

***Protermes hirticeps*** is a species of the genus *Protermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: East Africa, Uganda, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Protermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hirticeps*: Latin hirtus (hairy) + -ceps (head), hairy-headed
