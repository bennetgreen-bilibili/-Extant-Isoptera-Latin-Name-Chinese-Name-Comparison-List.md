## Summary

***Euhamitermes quadratceps*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Li in 1987; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Dabu County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *quadratceps*: From quadrat + ceps
