## Summary

***Mimeutermes sorex*** is a species of the genus *Mimeutermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mimeutermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sorex*: Latin sorex (shrew)
