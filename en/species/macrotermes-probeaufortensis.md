## Summary

***Macrotermes probeaufortensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Kalabakan District: Umas Umas. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *probeaufortensis*: Named after the locality Beaufort (Sabah, Malaysia), with prefix pro- ("near")
