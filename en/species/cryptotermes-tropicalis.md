## Summary

***Cryptotermes tropicalis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gay & Watson in 1982; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Gadgarra State Forest, 17 km E by S Yungaburra. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *tropicalis*: Greek tropikos (tropical) + Latin -alis, tropical
