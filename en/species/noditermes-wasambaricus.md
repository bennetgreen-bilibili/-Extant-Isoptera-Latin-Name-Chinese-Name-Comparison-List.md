## Summary

***Noditermes wasambaricus*** is a species of the genus *Noditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: Amani: Panusi. Distribution: Kenya, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Noditermes*: From Latin nodus (knot, node) + termes.

Specific epithet *wasambaricus*: Named after the locality Wasambara (Usambara, Tanzania)
