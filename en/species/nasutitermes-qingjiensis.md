## Summary

***Nasutitermes qingjiensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li in 1979; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Longquan County: Ruixiang Commune: Qingjie Brigade, Huangnikeng. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *qingjiensis*: Named after the locality Qingj
