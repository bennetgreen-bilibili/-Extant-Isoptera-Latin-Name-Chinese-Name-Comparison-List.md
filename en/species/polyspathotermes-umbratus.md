## Summary

***Polyspathotermes umbratus*** is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: East Usambara: Mt. Daluni. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea, Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *umbratus*: Latin umbratus (shaded)
