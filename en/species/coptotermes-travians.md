## Summary

***Coptotermes travians*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Marudi. Distribution: Sumatra, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *travians*: Latin travians (uncertain)
