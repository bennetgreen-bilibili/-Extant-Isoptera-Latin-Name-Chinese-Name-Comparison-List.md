## Summary

***Ophiotermes grandilabius*** is a species of the genus *Ophiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Cameroon: Bipindi. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ophiotermes*: From Greek ophis (snake) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *grandilabius*: From the descriptive term 'grand' (great)
