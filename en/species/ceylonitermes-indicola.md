## Summary

***Ceylonitermes indicola*** is a species of the genus *Ceylonitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Thakur in 1976; the type locality is India: Kerala: Nilambur, near Circuit House. Distribution: India, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ceylonitermes*: From Ceylon (Sri Lanka) + termes.

Specific epithet *indicola*: Latin India + -cola (dweller): dwelling in India
