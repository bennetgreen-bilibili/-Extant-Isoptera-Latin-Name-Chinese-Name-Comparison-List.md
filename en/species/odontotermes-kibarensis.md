## Summary

***Odontotermes kibarensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1923; the type locality is Uganda: Kampala: Kibara. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Angola, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kibarensis*: Named after the locality Kibar
