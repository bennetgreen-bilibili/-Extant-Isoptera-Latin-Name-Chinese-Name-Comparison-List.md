## Summary

***Angulitermes nilensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1962; the type locality is Sudan: Khartoum: Burri Woods. Distribution: Senegal, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Kenya, Sudan, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *nilensis*: Named after the locality Nil
