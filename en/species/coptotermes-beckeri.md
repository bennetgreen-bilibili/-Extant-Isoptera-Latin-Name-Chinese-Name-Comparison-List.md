## Summary

***Coptotermes beckeri*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Mathur & Chhotani in 1969; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Chennai \[Madras\]. Distribution: southern India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *beckeri*: Named after Becker
