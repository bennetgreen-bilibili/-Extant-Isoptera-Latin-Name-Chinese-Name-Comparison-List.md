## Summary

***Noditermes lamanianus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Noditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1905; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Central Africa, Republic of the Congo, Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Noditermes*: From Latin nodus (knot, node) + termes.

Specific epithet *lamanianus*: Named after Laman
