## Summary

***Acholotermes imbellis*** is a species of the genus *Acholotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acholotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + cholē (bile) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *imbellis*: Latin in- (without) + bellum (war), unwarlike
