## Summary

***Cryptotermes colombianus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Casalla, Scheffrahn & Korb in 2016; the type locality is Colombia: Magdalena: Tayrona National Natural Park, Gairaca Bay \[ 11.3152N 74.1032W \]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *colombianus*: Named after Colombia
