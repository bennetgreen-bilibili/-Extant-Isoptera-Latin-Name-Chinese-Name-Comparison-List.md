## Summary

***Nitiditermes truncatus*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand. Distribution: South Africa. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *truncatus*: Latin truncatus (truncated)
