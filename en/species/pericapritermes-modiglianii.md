## Summary

***Pericapritermes modiglianii*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1922; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Doloc-Tolong. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *modiglianii*: Named after Modigliani (Italian explorer and naturalist Elio Modigliani)
