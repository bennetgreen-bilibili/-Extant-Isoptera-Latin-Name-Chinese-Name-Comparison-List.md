## Summary

***Cryptotermes pyrodomus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bacchus in 1987; the type locality is Barbados: Highland boiling house. Distribution: Barbados, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *pyrodomus*: From the descriptive term 'pyr' (fire)
