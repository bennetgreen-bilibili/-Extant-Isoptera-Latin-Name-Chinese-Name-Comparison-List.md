## Summary

***Cryptotermes secundus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1925; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: Darwin. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *secundus*: Latin secundus (second)
