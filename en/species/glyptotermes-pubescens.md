## Summary

***Glyptotermes pubescens*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Puerto Rico: Aibonito. Distribution: Caribbean.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pubescens*: Latin pubescens (pubescent)
