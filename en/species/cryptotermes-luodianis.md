## Summary

***Cryptotermes luodianis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xia, Gao & Deng in 1983; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Loudian. Distribution: China (Guizhou).

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *luodianis*: After Luodian (Guizhou, China)
