## Summary

***Nasutitermes corporaali*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1922; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Preanger: Tjigembong. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *corporaali*: Named after J. B. Corporaal
