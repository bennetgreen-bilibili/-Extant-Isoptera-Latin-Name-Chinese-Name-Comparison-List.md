## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: ChinPing. Distribution: Yunnan (Jinping Mengla580 m), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
