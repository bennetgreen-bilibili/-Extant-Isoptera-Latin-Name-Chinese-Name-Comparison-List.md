## Summary

***Glyptotermes schmidti*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Vanuatu: Espiritu Santo Island: Hog Harbor. Distribution: Vanuatu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *schmidti*: Named in honor of the late Dr. K. P. Schmidt, Chief Curator of Zoology, Chicago Natural History Museum.
