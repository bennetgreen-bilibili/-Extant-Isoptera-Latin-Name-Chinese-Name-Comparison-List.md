## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes makassarensis*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Sulawesi: Makassar. Distribution: Indonesia, India, Nicobar Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *makassarensis*: Named after the locality Makassar
