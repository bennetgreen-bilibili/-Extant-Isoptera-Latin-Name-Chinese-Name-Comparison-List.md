## Summary

***Ovambotermes sylvaticus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Ovambotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Coaton in 1971; the type locality is Namibia: 16 km ex Rundu-Gootfontein. Distribution: Namibia, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ovambotermes*: From the name Ovambo; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sylvaticus*: Latin silvaticus (of the forest, sylva = forest)
