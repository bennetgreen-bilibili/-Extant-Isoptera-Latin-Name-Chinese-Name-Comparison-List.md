## Summary

***Isognathotermes minitabundus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Welgelegen. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minitabundus*: Latin minitabundus (threatening)
