## Summary

***Nasutitermes acangussu*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Bandeira & Fontes in 1979; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Fazenda Aruanã \[3.07S 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *acangussu*: From Tupi acanga (head) + ussu (big), meaning 'big head'
