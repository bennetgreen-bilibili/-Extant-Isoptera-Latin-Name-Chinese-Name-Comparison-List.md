## Summary

***Microcerotermes nervosus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1927; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: Darwin. Distribution: Oceania, Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nervosus*: From nerv + osus
