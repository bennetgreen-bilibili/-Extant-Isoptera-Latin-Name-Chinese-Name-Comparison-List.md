## Summary

***Amitermes foreli*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Colombia: Atlántico: Barranquilla \[ 10.9639N 74.7964W \]. Distribution: Amazon region, Central America and Caribbean, Colombia, Venezuela. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
