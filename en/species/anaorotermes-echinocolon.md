## Summary

***Anaorotermes echinocolon*** is the only extant species of the genus *Anaorotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Nigeria: 30 miles from Lokoja on Okene Road. Distribution: Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anaorotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *echinocolon*: From the descriptive term 'echin' (spiny)
