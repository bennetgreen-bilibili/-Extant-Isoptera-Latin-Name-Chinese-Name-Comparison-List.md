## Summary

***Reticulitermes yaeyamanus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Morimoto in 1968; the type locality is Japan: Hateruma-mori; Iriomote; Ishigaki; Omotodake; Shirihama; Yoshihara. Distribution: Japan, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *yaeyamanus*: Named after Yaeyama (Japan)
