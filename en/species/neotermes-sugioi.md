## Summary

***Neotermes sugioi*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Yashiro in 2019; the type locality is Japan: Okinawa, Chibana. Distribution: East Asia, Japan, Taiwan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sugioi*: Named after Professor Koji Sugio (Japanese entomologist)
