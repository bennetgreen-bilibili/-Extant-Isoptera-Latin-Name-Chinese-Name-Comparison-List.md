## Summary

***Rinacapritermes silvius*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Rinacapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Amina & Rajmohana in 2022; the type locality is India: Kerala: Ernakulam, Urulanthanni \[ 10.1281N 76.755E \]. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rinacapritermes*: From Greek rhis (nose) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *silvius*: Latin silva (forest), of the woods
