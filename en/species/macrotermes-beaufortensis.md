## Summary

***Macrotermes beaufortensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Beaufort District: Beaufort. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Singapore, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *beaufortensis*: Named after the locality Beaufort
