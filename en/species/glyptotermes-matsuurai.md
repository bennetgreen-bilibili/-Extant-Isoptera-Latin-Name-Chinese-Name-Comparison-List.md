## Summary

***Glyptotermes matsuurai*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Yashiro & Lo in 2024; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Cairns, Manunda \[ 16.9131S 145.752E \]. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *matsuurai*: Named after Matsuura (Japanese entomologist)
