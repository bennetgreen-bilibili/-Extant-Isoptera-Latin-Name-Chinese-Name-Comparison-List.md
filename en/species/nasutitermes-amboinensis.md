## Summary

***Nasutitermes amboinensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1931; the type locality is Indonesia: Maluku: Ambon. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *amboinensis*: Named after the locality Amboin
