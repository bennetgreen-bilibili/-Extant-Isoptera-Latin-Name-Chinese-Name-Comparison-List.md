## Summary

***Nasutitermes mexicanus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1933; the type locality is Mexico: Colima State: Colima; Colima State: Jala; Colima State: Madrid. Distribution: Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *mexicanus*: Named after the locality Mexic
