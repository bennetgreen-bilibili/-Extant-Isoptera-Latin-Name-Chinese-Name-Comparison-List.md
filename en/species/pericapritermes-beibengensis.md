## Summary

***Pericapritermes beibengensis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Huang & Han in 1987; the type locality is China: Tibet: Medog. Distribution: China (Tibet).

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *beibengensis*: Named after the locality Beibeng
