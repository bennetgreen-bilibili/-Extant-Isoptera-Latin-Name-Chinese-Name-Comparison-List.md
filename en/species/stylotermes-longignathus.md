## Summary

***Stylotermes longignathus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao, Zhu & Han in 1981; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Chengdu. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longignathus*: Latin longus (long) + Greek gnathos (jaw), long-jawed
