## Summary

***Pseudhamitermes longignathus*** is a species of the genus *Pseudhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Huay Yang. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudhamitermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *longignathus*: Latin longus (long) + Greek gnathos (jaw), long-jawed
