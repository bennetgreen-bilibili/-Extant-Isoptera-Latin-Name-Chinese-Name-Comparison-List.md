## Summary

***Procryptotermes speiseri*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1915; the type locality is Vanuatu: Ambrym. Distribution: Vanuatu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *speiseri*: Named after a person "Speiser" (possibly Paul Speiser, German entomologist)
