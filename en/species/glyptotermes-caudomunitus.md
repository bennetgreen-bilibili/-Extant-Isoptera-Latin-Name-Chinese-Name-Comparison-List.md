## Summary

***Glyptotermes caudomunitus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Depok. Distribution: Indonesia, India, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *caudomunitus*: From the descriptive term 'caud' (tail)
