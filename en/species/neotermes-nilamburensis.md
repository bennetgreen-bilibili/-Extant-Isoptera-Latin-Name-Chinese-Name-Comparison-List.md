## Summary

***Neotermes nilamburensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thakur in 1978; the type locality is India: Kerala: Nilambur. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nilamburensis*: Named after the locality Nilambur
