## Summary

***Incisitermes snyderi*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1933; the type locality is United States: Texas: Cameron County: Brownsville: Fort Brown. Distribution: United States, Mexico, Bahamas, Bermuda, Costa Rica, Cuba, Panama, Puerto Rico, Turks and Caicos Islands, British Virgin Islands, U.S. Virgin Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *snyderi*: Named after the termite taxonomist T. E. Snyder
