## Summary

***Reticulitermes leiboensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao & Xia in 1983; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Leibo. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *leiboensis*: Named after the locality Leibo
