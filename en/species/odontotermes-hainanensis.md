## Summary

***Odontotermes hainanensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1924; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Kachek. Distribution: South Asia, Southeast Asia, East Asia, China, Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, Cambodia, India, Myanmar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hainanensis*: After Hainan (China)
