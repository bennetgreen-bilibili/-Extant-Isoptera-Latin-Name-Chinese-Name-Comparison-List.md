## Summary

***Pseudomicrotermes alboniger*** is the only extant species of the genus *Pseudomicrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Nigeria, Zimbabwe, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa).

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudomicrotermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + mikros (small) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *alboniger*: From alboni + ger
