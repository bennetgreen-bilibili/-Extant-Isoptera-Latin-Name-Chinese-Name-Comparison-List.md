## Summary

***Amitermes importunus*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1959; the type locality is Malawi: Central Province: 53 km from Kasungu on the Lilongwe Road. Distribution: Malawi, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *importunus*: Latin importunus (troublesome)
