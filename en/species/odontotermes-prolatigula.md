## Summary

***Odontotermes prolatigula*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Bose in 1997; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: East Siang District, 7 km from Zero to Boleng, Nambung. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *prolatigula*: Latin pro- (front) + latus (broad) + gula (throat/tongue)
