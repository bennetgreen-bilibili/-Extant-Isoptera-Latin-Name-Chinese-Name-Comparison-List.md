## Summary

***Ancistrotermes crucifer*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1897; the type locality is Cameroon: Abo; Sierra Leone: ?. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *crucifer*: Latin crux (cross) + -fer (bearing), cross-bearing
