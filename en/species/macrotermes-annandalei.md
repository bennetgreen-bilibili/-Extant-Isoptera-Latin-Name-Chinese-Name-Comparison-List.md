## Summary

***Macrotermes annandalei*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Myanmar: Lower Myanmar: Moulmein. Distribution: Southeast Asia, East Asia, Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, China, India, Myanmar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *annandalei*: Named after the entomologist Nelson Annandale
