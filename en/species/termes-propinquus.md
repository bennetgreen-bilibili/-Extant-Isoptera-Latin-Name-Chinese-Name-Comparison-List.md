## Summary

***Termes propinquus*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *propinquus*: Latin propinquus (near, neighbouring)
