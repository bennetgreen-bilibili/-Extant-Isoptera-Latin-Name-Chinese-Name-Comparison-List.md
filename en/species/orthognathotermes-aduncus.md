## Summary

***Orthognathotermes aduncus*** is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Guiana Shield, Bolivia, Paraguay, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aduncus*: Latin aduncus (hooked)
