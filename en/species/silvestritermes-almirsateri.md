## Summary

***Silvestritermes almirsateri*** is a species of the genus *Silvestritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2012; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Poconé \[16.26S 56.629W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Silvestritermes*: Named after the entomologist F. Silvestri; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *almirsateri*: Named after Almir Sater
