## Summary

***Heterotermes wittmeri*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chhotani & Bose in 1982; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Hakimah. Distribution: Saudi Arabia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wittmeri*: Named after W. Wittmer (Swiss entomologist)
