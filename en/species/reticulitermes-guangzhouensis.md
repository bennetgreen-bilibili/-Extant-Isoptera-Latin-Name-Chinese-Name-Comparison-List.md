## Summary

***Reticulitermes guangzhouensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Guangzhou. Distribution: China. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *guangzhouensis*: Named after the locality Guangzhou
