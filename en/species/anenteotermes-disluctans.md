## Summary

***Anenteotermes disluctans*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Uganda: Kawanda. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *disluctans*: Latin disluctans (struggling)
