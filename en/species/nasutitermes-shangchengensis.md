## Summary

***Nasutitermes shangchengensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wang & Li in 1984; the type locality is China: Henan Province: Shangcheng County: Dongfengjian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *shangchengensis*: Named after the locality Shangcheng
