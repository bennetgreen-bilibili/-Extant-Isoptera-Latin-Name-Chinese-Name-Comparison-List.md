## Summary

***Glyptotermes lighti*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Marshall Islands: Arno Atoll: Ine Island. Distribution: Marshall Islands, Solomon Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lighti*: Named after S. F. Light
