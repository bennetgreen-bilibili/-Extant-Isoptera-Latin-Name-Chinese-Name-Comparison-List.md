## Summary

***Macrotermes planicapitatus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Gao & Lau in 1996; the type locality is China: Hong Kong: New Territories: Sheung Shui, Tai Lung Village. Distribution: China (Hong Kong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *planicapitatus*: From planicapit + atus
