## Summary

***Microtermes luteus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga District: Mwakijembe. Distribution: Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *luteus*: Latin luteus (yellow)
