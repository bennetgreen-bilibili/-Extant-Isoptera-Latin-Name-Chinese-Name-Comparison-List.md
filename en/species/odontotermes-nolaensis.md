## Summary

***Odontotermes nolaensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Central African Republic: Nola. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Senegal, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nolaensis*: Named after Nola, Central African Republic
