## Summary

***Rhynchotermes armatus*** is a species of the genus *Rhynchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2019; the type locality is Colombia: Cundinamarca: Pandi \[4.19N 74.486W\]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rhynchotermes*: From Greek rhynchos (beak, snout) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *armatus*: From arm + atus
