## Summary

***Microtermes upembae*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1958; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: "Parc National de l’Upemba, River Lupiala, affl. dr. Lufira.". Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *upembae*: Named after Upemba (DR Congo)
