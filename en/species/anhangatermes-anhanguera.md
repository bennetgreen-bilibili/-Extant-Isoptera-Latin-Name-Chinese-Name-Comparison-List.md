## Summary

***Anhangatermes anhanguera*** is a species of the genus *Anhangatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oliveira & Cunha in 2014; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Anápolis, Campus da Universidade Estadual de Goiás \[16.383S 48.9453W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anhangatermes*: From Tupi Anhangá (a mythical forest spirit) or the place name + -termes.

Specific epithet *anhanguera*: Tupi Anhangá (evil spirit)
