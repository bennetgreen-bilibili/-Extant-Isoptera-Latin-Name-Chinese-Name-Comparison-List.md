## Summary

***Hospitalitermes flavoantennaris*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1923; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Simeulue, Sinabang. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *flavoantennaris*: From flavoantenn + aris
