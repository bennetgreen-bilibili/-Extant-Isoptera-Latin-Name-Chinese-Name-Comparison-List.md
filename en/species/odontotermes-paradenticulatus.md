## Summary

***Odontotermes paradenticulatus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1947; the type locality is Philippines: Leyte, west of Tarragona, east shore of Lake Bito. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paradenticulatus*: From paradenticul + atus
