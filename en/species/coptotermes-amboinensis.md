## Summary

***Coptotermes amboinensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1931; the type locality is Indonesia: Maluku: Ambon. Distribution: Maluku Islands, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *amboinensis*: Named after the locality Amboin
