## Summary

***Eurytermes boveni*** is a species of the genus *Eurytermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1966; the type locality is India: Madya Pradesh: Mandala District: Kanha National Park. Distribution: Oriental region, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eurytermes*: From Greek eurys (broad) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *boveni*: Named after van Boven
