## Summary

***Anoplotermes susanae*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn, Carrijo & Castro in 2023; the type locality is Brasil: Rondonia: Porto Velho, Mutum-Paraná \[ 9.4513S 64.8439W \]. Distribution: Ecuador, Brazil, French Guiana, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *susanae*: Named after a person "Susana" (female name)
