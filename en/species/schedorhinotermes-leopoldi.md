## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes leopoldi*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1933; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bierun. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *leopoldi*: Named after Leopold
