## Summary

***Coxotermes boukokoensis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Coxotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé & Noirot in 1955; the type locality is Central African Republic: Boukoko, near Mbaiki. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Congo, Nigeria, Central African Republic.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coxotermes*: From Latin coxa (hip, coxa) + termes.

Specific epithet *boukokoensis*: Named after the locality Boukoko
