## Summary

***Coptotermes frenchi*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1932; the type locality is Australia: Victoria: Melbourne, Hawthorne. Distribution: Australia; introduced: New Zealand. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *frenchi*: Named after French
