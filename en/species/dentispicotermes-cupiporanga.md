## Summary

***Dentispicotermes cupiporanga*** is a species of the genus *Dentispicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Bandeira & Cancello in 1992; the type locality is Brasil: Roraima: Ilha de Maracá \[ 3.4N 61.65W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dentispicotermes*: From Latin dens (tooth) + spica (spike) + termes.

Specific epithet *cupiporanga*: From Guaraní cupi 'termite' (from cupi'i 'wood-dwelling ant') + Tupi poranga 'beautiful', i.e. 'beautiful termite'.
