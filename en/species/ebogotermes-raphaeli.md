## Summary

***Ebogotermes raphaeli*** is the only extant species of the genus *Ebogotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn & Roisin in 2021; the type locality is Cameroon: Mbalmayo Forest Reserve: Ebogo \[ 3.382N 11.463E \]. Distribution: Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ebogotermes*: From the place name Ebogo; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *raphaeli*: Named for Raphael Awoumou Onana, the chief of Ebogo village, a resilient guide, and expert on Cameroonian termites.
