## Summary

***Ancistrotermes dubius*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mauda: Upper Uele. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dubius*: Latin dubius (doubtful)
