## Summary

***Atlantitermes snyderi*** is a species of the genus *Atlantitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Trinidad, Guyana, Peru, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Atlantitermes*: From the Atlantic + termes.

Specific epithet *snyderi*: Named after the termite taxonomist T. E. Snyder
