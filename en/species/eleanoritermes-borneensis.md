## Summary

***Eleanoritermes borneensis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Eleanoritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1968; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang. Distribution: Borneo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eleanoritermes*: Named after a person (Eleanor); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *borneensis*: After Borneo
