## Summary

***Glyptotermes parki*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Rwanda: Kakitumba (Kakatumba). Distribution: Rwanda, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parki*: Named after Park
