## Summary

***Microcerotermes subtilis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1897; the type locality is Madagascar: Majunga; Seychelles: Aldabra Island. Distribution: Madagascar, Réunion, Comoros, Europa Island (France), Glorioso Islands (France), Seychelles, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *subtilis*: From subt + ilis
