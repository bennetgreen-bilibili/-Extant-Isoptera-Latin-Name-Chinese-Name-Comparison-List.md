## Summary

***Termes fatalis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Linnaeus in 1758; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Neotropics, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, etc., northern South America, Bolivia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *fatalis*: Latin fatalis (fatal)
