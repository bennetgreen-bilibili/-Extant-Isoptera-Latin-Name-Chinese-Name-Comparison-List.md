## Summary

***Microcerotermes annandalei*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1923; the type locality is India: Orissa: Chilka Lake: Barkuda Island. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Thailand, India, Malaysia, Myanmar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *annandalei*: Named after the entomologist Nelson Annandale
