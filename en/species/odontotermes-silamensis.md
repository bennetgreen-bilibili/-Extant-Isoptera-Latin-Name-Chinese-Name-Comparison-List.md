## Summary

***Odontotermes silamensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Lahad Datu District: Silam. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *silamensis*: Named after the locality Mount Silam (Sabah, Malaysia)
