## Summary

***Cryptotermes perforans*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Colombo. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *perforans*: From the descriptive term 'perfor' (perforate)
