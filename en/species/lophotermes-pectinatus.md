## Summary

***Lophotermes pectinatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Lophotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Miller in 1991; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Weipa. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lophotermes*: From Greek lophos (crest, ridge) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pectinatus*: From pectin + atus
