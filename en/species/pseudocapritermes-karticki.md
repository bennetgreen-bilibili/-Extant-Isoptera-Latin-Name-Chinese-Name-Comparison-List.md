## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes karticki*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Bose in 1997; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Mawphlang. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *karticki*: Named after Kartic(k)
