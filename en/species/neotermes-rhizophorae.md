## Summary

***Neotermes rhizophorae*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1994; the type locality is India: Nicobar Islands: Nancowry. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rhizophorae*: After the plant genus Rhizophora
