## Summary

***Nasutitermes octopilis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Banks in 1918; the type locality is Guyana: Potaro-Siparuni: Tukeit \[5.2N 59.45W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Guyana, India, Peru, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *octopilis*: Latin octo (eight) + pilus (hair), eight-haired
