## Summary

***Nasutitermes koiari*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roisin & Pasteels in 1996; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Central Province: Sirinumu Dam. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *koiari*: Named after the Koiari people (Papua New Guinea)
