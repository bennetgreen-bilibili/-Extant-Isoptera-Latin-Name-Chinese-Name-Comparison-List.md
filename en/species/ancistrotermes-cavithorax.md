## Summary

***Ancistrotermes cavithorax*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Benin, Gambia, Cameroon, Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Uganda. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cavithorax*: Latin cavus (hollow) + thorax (thorax), hollow-thoraxed
