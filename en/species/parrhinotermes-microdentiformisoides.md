## Summary

***Parrhinotermes microdentiformisoides*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Beaufort District: Beaufort. Distribution: Thailand, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *microdentiformisoides*: From microdentiformis + oides
