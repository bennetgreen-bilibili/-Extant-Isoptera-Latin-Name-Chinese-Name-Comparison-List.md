## Summary

***Dolichorhinotermes neli*** is a species of the genus *Dolichorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ensaf & Betsch in 2002; the type locality is French Guiana: Route de Cacao, 1.2 km avant Bellevue \[ 4.548N 52.4915W \]. Distribution: French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dolichorhinotermes*: From Greek dolichos (long) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *neli*: Named after Nel
