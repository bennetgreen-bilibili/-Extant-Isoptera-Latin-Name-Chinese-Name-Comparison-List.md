## Summary

***Prorhinotermes spectabilis*** is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1989; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Hekou Yaozu Zizhixian. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prorhinotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *spectabilis*: Latin spectabilis (spectacular)
