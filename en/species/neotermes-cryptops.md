## Summary

***Neotermes cryptops*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Tanzania: Ugalla River: Ituri Ferry. Distribution: Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cryptops*: Greek kryptos (hidden) + ōps (eye), hidden-eyed
