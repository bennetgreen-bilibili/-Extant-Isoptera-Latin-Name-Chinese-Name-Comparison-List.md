## Summary

***Macrotermes aleemi*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Ukhia. Distribution: South Asia, India, Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *aleemi*: Named after Aleem
