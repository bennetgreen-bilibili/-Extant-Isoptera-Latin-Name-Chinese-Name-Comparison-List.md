## Summary

***Procryptotermes edwardsi*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 1999; the type locality is Jamaica: Cousin Cave. Distribution: Caribbean.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *edwardsi*: Named after Edwards
