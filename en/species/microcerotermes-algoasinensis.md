## Summary

***Microcerotermes algoasinensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Cape Province: Port Elizabeth (Algoa Bay). Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *algoasinensis*: Named after Algoa Bay (South Africa)
