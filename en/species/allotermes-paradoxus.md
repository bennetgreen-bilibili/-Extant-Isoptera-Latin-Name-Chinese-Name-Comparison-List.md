## Summary

***Allotermes paradoxus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Allotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1910; the type locality is Madagascar: Tulear. Distribution: Indian Ocean islands (Réunion), Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Allotermes*: From Greek allos (other, different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paradoxus*: Greek paradoxos (paradoxical, strange)
