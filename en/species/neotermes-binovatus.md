## Summary

***Neotermes binovatus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Han in 1984; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Hainan Island. Distribution: Hainan (Lehui).

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *binovatus*: From binov + atus
