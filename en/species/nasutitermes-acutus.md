## Summary

***Nasutitermes acutus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Wonosobo. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *acutus*: Latin acutus (sharp)
