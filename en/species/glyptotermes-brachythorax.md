## Summary

***Glyptotermes brachythorax*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Ya. Distribution: China (Hainan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brachythorax*: From brachy + thorax
