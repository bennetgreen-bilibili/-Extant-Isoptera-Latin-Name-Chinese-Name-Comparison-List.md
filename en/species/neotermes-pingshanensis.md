## Summary

***Neotermes pingshanensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tan & Peng in 2009; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Pingshan. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pingshanensis*: Named after the locality Pingshan
