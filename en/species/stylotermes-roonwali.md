## Summary

***Stylotermes roonwali*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Sandakan South District: Lungmanis. Distribution: India, Brunei, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *roonwali*: Named after the termite taxonomist M. L. Roonwal
