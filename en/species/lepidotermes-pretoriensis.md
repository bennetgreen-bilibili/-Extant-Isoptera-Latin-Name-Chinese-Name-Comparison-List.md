## Summary

***Lepidotermes pretoriensis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Lepidotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1914; the type locality is South Africa: Cape Province: Kimberley. Distribution: southern Africa, Pretoria, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lepidotermes*: From Greek lepis (scale) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pretoriensis*: Named after Pretoria (South Africa)
