## Summary

***Nasutitermes krishna*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1970; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: Little Andaman: Kwate-tu-Kwage. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *krishna*: Named after K. Krishna or the Hindu deity Krishna
