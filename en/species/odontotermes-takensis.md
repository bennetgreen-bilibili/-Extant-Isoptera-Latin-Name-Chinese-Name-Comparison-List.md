## Summary

***Odontotermes takensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Tak. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *takensis*: Named after the locality Tak (Thailand)
