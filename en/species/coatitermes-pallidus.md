## Summary

***Coatitermes pallidus*** is a species of the genus *Coatitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: El Beni: Ivón \[11.13S 66.13W\]. Distribution: Colombia, Bolivia, Ecuador, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coatitermes*: From Tupi Coati (coati) + termes.

Specific epithet *pallidus*: Latin pallidus (pale)
