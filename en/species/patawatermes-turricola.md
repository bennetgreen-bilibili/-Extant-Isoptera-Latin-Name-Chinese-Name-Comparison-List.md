## Summary

***Patawatermes turricola*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Patawatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[ 15.5961S 56.0967W \]. Distribution: South America, Colombia, Brazil, Peru, Ecuador, Venezuela, Paraguay, Argentina, Bolivia, Panama, Trinidad and Tobago. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Patawatermes*: From the place name Patawa; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *turricola*: Latin turris (tower) + -cola (dweller): tower-dwelling
