## Summary

***Odontotermes angustignathus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Cheli. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Vietnam, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *angustignathus*: From angusti + gnathus
