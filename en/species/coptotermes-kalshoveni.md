## Summary

***Coptotermes kalshoveni*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Semarang. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kalshoveni*: Named after L. G. E. Kalshoven
