## Summary

***Microcerotermes parvus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal. Distribution: Old World tropics, Congo, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Togo, South Africa, Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Gabon, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parvus*: Latin parvus (small)
