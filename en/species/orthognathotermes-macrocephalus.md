## Summary

***Orthognathotermes macrocephalus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Mojos \[ 14.5667S 68.8833W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *macrocephalus*: Greek makros (large) + kephalē (head), large-headed
