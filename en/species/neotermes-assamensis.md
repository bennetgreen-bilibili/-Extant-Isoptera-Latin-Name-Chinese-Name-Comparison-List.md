## Summary

***Neotermes assamensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti & Saha in 2000; the type locality is India: Assam: Dibrugarh. Distribution: India, Assam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *assamensis*: Named after the locality Assam
