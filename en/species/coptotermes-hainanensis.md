## Summary

***Coptotermes hainanensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Tsai in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Hainan Island: Danxiaan County. Distribution: China (Hainan). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hainanensis*: After Hainan (China)
