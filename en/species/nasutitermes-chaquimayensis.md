## Summary

***Nasutitermes chaquimayensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: San Fermín \[13.9056S 68.9722W\]; Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]; Puno: Llinquipata \[13.8167S 70.6333W\]; Puno: Llinquipata \[13.8167S 70.6333W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Peru, Bolivia, Paraguay. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *chaquimayensis*: Named after the locality Chaquimayo
