## Summary

***Procapritermes zhangfengensis*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1995; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Longchuan County: Zhangfeng. Distribution: Yunnan (Longchuan890 m), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *zhangfengensis*: Named after the locality Zhangfeng
