## Summary

***Cornitermes silvestrii*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *silvestrii*: Named after the entomologist F. Silvestri
