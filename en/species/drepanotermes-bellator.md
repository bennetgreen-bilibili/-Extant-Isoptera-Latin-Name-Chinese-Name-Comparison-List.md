## Summary

***Drepanotermes bellator*** is a species of the genus *Drepanotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Watson & Perry in 1981; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: 7 mi (11 km) E of Alice Springs. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Drepanotermes*: From Greek drepanon (sickle) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bellator*: Latin bellator (warrior)
