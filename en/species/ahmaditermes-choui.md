## Summary

***Ahmaditermes choui*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Tong in 1989; the type locality is China: Hong Kong. Distribution: Hong Kong.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *choui*: Named after the Chinese entomologist Chou Io
