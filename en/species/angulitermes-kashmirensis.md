## Summary

***Angulitermes kashmirensis*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1971; the type locality is India: Jammu and Kashmir, ca. 10 km east of Chineni near confluence of River Tawi and Binisangh Nullah. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *kashmirensis*: Named after the locality Kashmir
