## Summary

***Cryptotermes papulosus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gay & Watson in 1982; the type locality is Australia: South Australia: Lameroo. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *papulosus*: Latin papulosus (pimply)
