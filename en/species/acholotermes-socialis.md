## Summary

***Acholotermes socialis*** is a species of the genus *Acholotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Cameroon: Mungo River. Distribution: Central African Republic, Congo Basin, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Acholotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + cholē (bile) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *socialis*: Latin socialis (social)
