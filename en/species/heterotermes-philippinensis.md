## Summary

***Heterotermes philippinensis*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1921; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Manila: Philippine Normal School. Distribution: Philippines, Madagascar, Mauritius.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *philippinensis*: Named after the locality Philippin
