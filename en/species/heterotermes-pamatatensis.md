## Summary

***Heterotermes pamatatensis*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Saleier Islands: Pamatata. Distribution: Indonesia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pamatatensis*: Named after the locality Pamatata
