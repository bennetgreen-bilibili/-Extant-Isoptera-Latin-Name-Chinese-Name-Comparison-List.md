## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes cacuminatus*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2001; the type locality is Malaysia: Selangor: Sungei Buloh Forest Reserve, 150 ft. altitude. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *cacuminatus*: Latin cacuminatus (pointed, peaked)
