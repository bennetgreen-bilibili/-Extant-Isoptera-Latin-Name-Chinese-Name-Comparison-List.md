## Summary

***Microcerotermes longiceps*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Cachan in 1949; the type locality is Madagascar: Bas Sambirano; Behara; Joffreville; Nosy Mamoko. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longiceps*: Latin longus (long) + -ceps (head), long-headed
