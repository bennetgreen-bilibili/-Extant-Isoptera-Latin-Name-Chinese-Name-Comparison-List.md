## Summary

***Neotermes acceptus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *acceptus*: Latin acceptus (acceptable, welcome)
