## Summary

***Glyptotermes finniganensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Eldridge in 1996; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: 6 km WNW Mt. Finnigan. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *finniganensis*: Named after Finnigan (Australia)
