## Summary

***Odontotermes nilensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Sudan: Kaka. Distribution: West Africa, Senegal, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nilensis*: Named after the locality Nil
