## Summary

***Nasutitermes aduncus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: El Beni: Rurrenabaque \[14.44S 67.525W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Peru, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *aduncus*: Latin aduncus (hooked)
