## Summary

***Lophotermes leptognathus*** is a species of the genus *Lophotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Miller in 1991; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: Liverpool River, Arnhem Land. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lophotermes*: From Greek lophos (crest, ridge) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *leptognathus*: From lepto + gnathus
