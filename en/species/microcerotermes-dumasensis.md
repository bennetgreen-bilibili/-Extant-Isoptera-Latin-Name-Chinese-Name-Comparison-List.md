## Summary

***Microcerotermes dumasensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Thakur in 1989; the type locality is India: Gujarat: Surat District: Dumas seacoast. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dumasensis*: Named after the locality Dumas
