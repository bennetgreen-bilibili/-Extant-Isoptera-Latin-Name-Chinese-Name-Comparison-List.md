## Summary

***Cavitermes tuberosus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Cavitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Barakara \[6.3833N 58.6667W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Trinidad and Tobago, Bolivia, Guyana, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cavitermes*: From Latin cavus (hollow) + termes.

Specific epithet *tuberosus*: From tuber + osus
