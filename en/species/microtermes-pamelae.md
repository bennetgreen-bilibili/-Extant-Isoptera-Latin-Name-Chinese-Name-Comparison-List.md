## Summary

***Microtermes pamelae*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Bacchus in 1997; the type locality is Zambia: Northern Province: Mbala. Distribution: Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *pamelae*: Named after Pamela
