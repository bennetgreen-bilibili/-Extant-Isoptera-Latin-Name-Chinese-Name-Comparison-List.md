## Summary

***Nasutitermes princeps*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1905; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Madang (Friedrich-Wilhelmshafen). Distribution: Australia, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *princeps*: Latin princeps (first, chief)
