## Summary

***Nasutitermes brevioculatus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[15.5961S 56.0967W\]; Paraguay: Concepción: Puerto Max, Estancia Postillon \[22.648S 57.763W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *brevioculatus*: From breviocul + atus
