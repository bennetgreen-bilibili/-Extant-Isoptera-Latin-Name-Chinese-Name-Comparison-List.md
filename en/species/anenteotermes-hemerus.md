## Summary

***Anenteotermes hemerus*** is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Sudan: Mount Bangenze. Distribution: Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hemerus*: Greek hēmeros (tame)
