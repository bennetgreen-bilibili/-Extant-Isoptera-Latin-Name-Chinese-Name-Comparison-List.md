## Summary

***Reticulitermes maopingensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Zhu, Huang & Wang in 1993; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Maoping. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *maopingensis*: Named after the locality Maoping
