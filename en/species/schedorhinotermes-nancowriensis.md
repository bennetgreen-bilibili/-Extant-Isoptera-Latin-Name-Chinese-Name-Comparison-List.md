## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes nancowriensis*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1994; the type locality is India: Nicobar Islands: Nancowry. Distribution: India, Nicobar Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nancowriensis*: Named after the locality Nancowr
