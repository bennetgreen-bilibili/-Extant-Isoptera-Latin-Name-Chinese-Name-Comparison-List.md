## Summary

***Spinitermes trispinosus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Spinitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Santarém \[2.43917S 54.705W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Paraguay, Peru, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Spinitermes*: From Latin spina (spine) + termes.

Specific epithet *trispinosus*: Latin tri- (three) + spinosus (spiny), three-spined
