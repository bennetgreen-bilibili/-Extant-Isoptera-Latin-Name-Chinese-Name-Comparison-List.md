## Summary

***Nasutitermes pilosus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: Pando: Cachuela Esperanza \[10.5333S 65.6333W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *pilosus*: Latin pilosus (hairy)
