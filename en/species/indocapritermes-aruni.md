## Summary

***Indocapritermes aruni*** is the only extant species of the genus *Indocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1997; the type locality is India: Kerala: Silent Valley. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Indocapritermes*: From India + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *aruni*: Named after Arun
