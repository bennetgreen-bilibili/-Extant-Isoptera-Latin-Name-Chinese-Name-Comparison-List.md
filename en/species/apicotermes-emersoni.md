## Summary

***Apicotermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bouillon in 1966; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: 5 km from the Catholic Mission of Mbata-Mbenge, Mayumbe. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Apicotermes*: From Latin apex (tip, summit) + termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
