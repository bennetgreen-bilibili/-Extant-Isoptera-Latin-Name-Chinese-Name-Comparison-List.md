## Summary

***Kalotermes jepsoni*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Maskeliya. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *jepsoni*: Named after Jepson
