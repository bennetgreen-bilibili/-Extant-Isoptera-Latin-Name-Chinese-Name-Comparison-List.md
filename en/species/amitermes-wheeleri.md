## Summary

***Amitermes wheeleri*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1906; the type locality is United States: Texas: Belton. Distribution: United States, Mexico. This site records 4 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wheeleri*: Named after the myrmecologist W. M. Wheeler
