## Summary

***Coptotermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Heneratgoda; Peradeniya; Seenigoda Estate. Distribution: southern India, Sri Lanka, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
