## Summary

***Amitermes excellens*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1923; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Guiana Shield, Venezuela.
