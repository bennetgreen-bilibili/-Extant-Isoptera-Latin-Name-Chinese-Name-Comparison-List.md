## Summary

***Astalotermes impedians*** is a species of the genus *Astalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Zambia: Ndola. Distribution: tropical Africa, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astalotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *impedians*: From im- (without) + pedians
