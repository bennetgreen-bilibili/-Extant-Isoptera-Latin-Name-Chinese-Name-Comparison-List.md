## Summary

***Odontotermes patruus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Mufungwa; Katanga: Sankisia. Distribution: Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *patruus*: Latin patruus (of an uncle)
