## Summary

***Odontotermes lautus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Pretoria. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lautus*: Latin lautus (elegant)
