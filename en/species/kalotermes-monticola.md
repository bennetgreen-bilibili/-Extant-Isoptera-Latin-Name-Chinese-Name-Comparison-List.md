## Summary

***Kalotermes monticola*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1925; the type locality is Algeria: Massif de l’Edough. Distribution: Algeria, North Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *monticola*: From monti + cola
