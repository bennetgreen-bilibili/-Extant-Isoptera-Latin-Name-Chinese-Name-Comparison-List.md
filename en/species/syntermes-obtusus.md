## Summary

***Syntermes obtusus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Paraguay: Guairá: Villa Rica \[25.75S 56.4333W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Bolivia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *obtusus*: Latin obtusus (blunt)
