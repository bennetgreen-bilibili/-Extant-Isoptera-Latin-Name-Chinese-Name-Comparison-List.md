## Summary

***Cryptotermes abruptus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1998; the type locality is Mexico: Quintana Roo State: on Isla de Cozumel near Caracola. Distribution: Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *abruptus*: Latin abruptus (abrupt)
