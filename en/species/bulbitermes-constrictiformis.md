## Summary

***Bulbitermes constrictiformis*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bahsoemboe; Malaysia: Taiping: Gunong Hijau; Taiping: Maxwell’s Hill. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia, Sumatra, Malaysia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *constrictiformis*: From constricti + formis
