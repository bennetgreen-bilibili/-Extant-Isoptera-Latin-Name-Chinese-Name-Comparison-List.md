## Summary

***Glyptotermes chinpingensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: ChinPing. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chinpingensis*: Named after Chinping (Jinping, Yunnan)
