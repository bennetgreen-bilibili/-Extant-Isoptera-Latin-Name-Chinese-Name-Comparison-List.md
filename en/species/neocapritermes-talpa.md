## Summary

***Neocapritermes talpa*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[ 13.45S 70.4W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Colombia, French Guiana, Peru. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *talpa*: Latin talpa (mole)
