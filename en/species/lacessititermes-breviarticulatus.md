## Summary

***Lacessititermes breviarticulatus*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *breviarticulatus*: From breviarticul + atus
