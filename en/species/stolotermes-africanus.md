## Summary

***Stolotermes africanus*** is a species of the genus *Stolotermes*, in the family STOLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1942; the type locality is South Africa: Cape Province, 8 mi W of Coldstream. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stolotermes*: From Latin stolo (shoot, stolon) + termes.

Specific epithet *africanus*: After Africa
