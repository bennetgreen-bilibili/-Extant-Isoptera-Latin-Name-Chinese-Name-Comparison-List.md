## Summary

***Nasutitermes bolivari*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1959; the type locality is Venezuela: Bolivar: Guayaraca (near Mt. Auyantepui) \[5.68333N 62.5333W\]. Distribution: Bolivia, Brazil, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *bolivari*: Named after Ignacio Bolívar
