## Summary

***Macuxitermes triceratops*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Macuxitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Cancello & Bandeira in 1992; the type locality is Brasil: Roraima: Ilha de Maracá \[3.4N 61.65W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macuxitermes*: From Macuxi (an Amazonian indigenous people) + termes.

Specific epithet *triceratops*: Greek tri- (three) + keras (horn) + ōps (face), three-horned-faced
