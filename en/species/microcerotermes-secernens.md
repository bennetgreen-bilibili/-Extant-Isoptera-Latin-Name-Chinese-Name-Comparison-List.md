## Summary

***Microcerotermes secernens*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Schmitz in 1917; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *secernens*: Latin secernens (separating)
