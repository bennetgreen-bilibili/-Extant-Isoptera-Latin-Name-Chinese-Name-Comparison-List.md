## Summary

***Glyptotermes maculifrons*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Li in 1983; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Huangkeng: Jianyang County. Distribution: China (Fujian).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *maculifrons*: From maculi + frons
