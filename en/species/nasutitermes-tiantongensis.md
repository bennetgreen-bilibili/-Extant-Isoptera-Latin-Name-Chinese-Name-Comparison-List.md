## Summary

***Nasutitermes tiantongensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Zhou & Xu in 1993; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Mt. Tiantong. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *tiantongensis*: Named after the locality Tiantong
