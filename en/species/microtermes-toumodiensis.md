## Summary

***Microtermes toumodiensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé in 1937; the type locality is Ivory Coast: Toumodi: Akakro. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Togo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *toumodiensis*: Named after the locality Toumodi (Côte d'Ivoire)
