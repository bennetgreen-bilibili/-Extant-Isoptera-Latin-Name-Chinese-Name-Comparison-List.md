## Summary

***Reticulitermes rusti*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chen in 2025; the type locality is United States: California: University of California Riverside \[ 33.9667N 117.327W \]. ## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *rusti*: Named after Michael K. Rust (UCR entomologist)
