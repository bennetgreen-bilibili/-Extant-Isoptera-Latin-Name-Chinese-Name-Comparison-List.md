## Summary

***Anenteotermes cicur*** is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Yase, 12 km from Yangambi. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.
