## Summary

***Neocapritermes villosus*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[ 13.45S 70.4W \]. Distribution: Peru, Brazil, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *villosus*: Latin villosus (shaggy)
