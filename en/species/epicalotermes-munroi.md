## Summary

***Epicalotermes munroi*** is a species of the genus *Epicalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1949; the type locality is South Africa: Saltpan, near Hammanskraal. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Epicalotermes*: From Greek epi- (upon) + kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *munroi*: Named after Munro
