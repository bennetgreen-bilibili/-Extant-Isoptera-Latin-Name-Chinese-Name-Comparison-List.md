## Summary

***Cornicapritermes mucronatus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Cornicapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1950; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Penal Settlement \[6.4N 58.658W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornicapritermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *mucronatus*: From mucron + atus
