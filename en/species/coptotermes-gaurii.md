## Summary

***Coptotermes gaurii*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Krishna in 1955; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Marambekana. Distribution: Sri Lanka, Nicobar Islands (India).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gaurii*: Named after Gauri
