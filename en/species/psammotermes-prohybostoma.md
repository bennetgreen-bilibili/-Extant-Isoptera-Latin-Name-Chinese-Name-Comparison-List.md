## Summary

***Psammotermes prohybostoma*** is a species of the genus *Psammotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ravan & Akhtar in 1999; the type locality is Iran: Chabahar (Tees). Distribution: Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Psammotermes*: From Greek psammos (sand) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *prohybostoma*: Greek pro- (front) + hybos (hump) + stoma (mouth)
