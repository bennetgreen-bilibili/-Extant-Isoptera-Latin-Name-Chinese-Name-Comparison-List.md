## Summary

***Incisitermes lisae*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2024; the type locality is Mexico: Yucatan:Temozón \[ 20.82N 88.19W \]. Distribution: Belize, Guatemala, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *lisae*: Named after Lisa
