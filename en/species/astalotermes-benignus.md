## Summary

***Astalotermes benignus*** is a species of the genus *Astalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Guinea: Mt. Nimba: Pierré Richaud. Distribution: tropical Africa, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Astalotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *benignus*: Latin benignus (benign, kind)
