## Summary

***Hypotermes makhamensis*** is a species of the genus *Hypotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Makham. Distribution: Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hypotermes*: From Greek hypo- (under, slightly) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *makhamensis*: Named after the locality Makham
