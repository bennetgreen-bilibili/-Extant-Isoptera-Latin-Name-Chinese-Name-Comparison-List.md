## Summary

***Labiotermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Araujo in 1954; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: São Paulo \[23.53S 46.62W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Argentina, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiotermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
