## Summary

***Euhamitermes yuntaishanensis*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Huang in 1987; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Yongping County, Yuntai Mountain. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *yuntaishanensis*: Named after Yuntai Shan (China)
