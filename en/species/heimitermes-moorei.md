## Summary

***Heimitermes moorei*** is a species of the genus *Heimitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1959; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Pygmy Camp, 4 km N of Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heimitermes*: Named after a person (Heim); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *moorei*: Named after Moore
