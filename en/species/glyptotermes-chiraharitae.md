## Summary

***Glyptotermes chiraharitae*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Amina & Rajmohana in 2016; the type locality is India: Kerala: Kozhikode, Kakkayam \[ 11.499N 75.895E \]. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chiraharitae*: Named after the locality Chiraharit
