## Summary

***Labiocapritermes microcephalus*** is a species of the genus *Labiocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Amina & Rajmohana in 2025; the type locality is India: Kerala. Distribution: India (Kerala).

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiocapritermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *microcephalus*: Greek mikros (small) + kephalē (head), small-headed
