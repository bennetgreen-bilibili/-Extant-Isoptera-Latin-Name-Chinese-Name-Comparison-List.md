## Summary

***Coptotermes shanghaiensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xia & He in 1986; the type locality is China: Shanghai. Distribution: Shanghai, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *shanghaiensis*: Named after the locality Shangha
