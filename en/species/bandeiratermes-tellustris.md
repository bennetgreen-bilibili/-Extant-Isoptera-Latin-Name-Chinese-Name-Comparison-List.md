## Summary

***Bandeiratermes tellustris*** is a species of the genus *Bandeiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1990; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[1.848S 65.523W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bandeiratermes*: Named after a person (Bandeira); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tellustris*: Latin tellus (earth), of the earth
