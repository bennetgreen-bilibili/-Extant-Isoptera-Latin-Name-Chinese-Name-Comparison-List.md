## Summary

***Microcerotermes raja*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1964; the type locality is India: Rajasthan, ca. 10 km NE Jodhpur, Balsamand. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *raja*: Sanskrit raja (king)
