## Summary

***Pericapritermes mohri*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Bogor; Java: Depok; Orte Kali Bata. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *mohri*: Named after Mohr
