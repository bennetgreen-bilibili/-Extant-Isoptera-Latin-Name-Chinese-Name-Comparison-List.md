## Summary

***Ahmaditermes laticephalus*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Tung Sa-Lang Luang. Distribution: China, Vietnam, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *laticephalus*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
