## Summary

***Isognathotermes rectimalatus*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Josens & Deligne in 2025; the type locality is Angola: Mussende \[ 10.5528S 15.6582E \]. Distribution: Angola.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rectimalatus*: From rectimal + atus
