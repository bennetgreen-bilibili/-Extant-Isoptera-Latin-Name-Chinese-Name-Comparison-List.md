## Summary

***Neotermes brevinotus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1932; the type locality is Costa Rica: Sandalo. Distribution: Costa Rica, Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brevinotus*: From brevi + notus
