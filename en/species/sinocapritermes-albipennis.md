## Summary

***Sinocapritermes albipennis*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: ChinPing. Distribution: China, Yunnan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *albipennis*: From albi + pennis
