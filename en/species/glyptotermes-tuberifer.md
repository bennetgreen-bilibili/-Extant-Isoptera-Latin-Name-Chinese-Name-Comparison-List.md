## Summary

***Glyptotermes tuberifer*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Saint Vincent & Grenadines: Sandy Bay. Distribution: Caribbean, Guatemala.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tuberifer*: From tuberi + fer
