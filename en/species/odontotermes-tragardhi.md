## Summary

***Odontotermes tragardhi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Estcourt; Natal: Stanford Hill; Natal: Sweetwater. Distribution: southern Africa (South Africa).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tragardhi*: Named after Ivar Trägårdh (Swedish entomologist)
