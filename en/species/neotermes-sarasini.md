## Summary

***Neotermes sarasini*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1915; the type locality is New Caledonia (French): Prony, 100 m, Loyalty Islands: Lifou. Distribution: New Caledonia, Samoa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sarasini*: Named after Fritz and Paul Sarasin (Swiss naturalists)
