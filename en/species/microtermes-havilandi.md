## Summary

***Microtermes havilandi*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Amanzimtoti; Natal: Stanford Hill; Zululand: Mfolozi Junction; Zululand: Somkele; Zululand: Umhlazinga. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *havilandi*: Named after the entomologist G. D. Haviland
