## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes jiangchengensis*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1992; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: autonomous county of Jiangcheng Hani and Yi. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *jiangchengensis*: Named after the locality Jiangcheng
