## Summary

***Odontotermes guizhouensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1988; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Panxian Tequ. Distribution: China (Guizhou).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *guizhouensis*: After Guizhou (China)
