## Summary

***Macrotermes ukuzii*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Ukuzi River. Distribution: southern Africa, Zimbabwe, South Africa, Eswatini, Tanzania. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *ukuzii*: Named after Ukuz
