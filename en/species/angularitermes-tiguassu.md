## Summary

***Angularitermes tiguassu*** is a species of the genus *Angularitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cancello & Brandão in 1996; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Goiânia \[16.67S 49.27W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angularitermes*: From Latin angularis (angular) + termes.

Specific epithet *tiguassu*: Tupi -assu (large)
