## Summary

***Parvitermes collinsae*** is a species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn & Roisin in 1995; the type locality is Dominican Republic: Barahona: Fondo Negro. Distribution: Dominican Republic.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parvitermes*: From Latin parvus (small) + termes.

Specific epithet *collinsae*: Named after Collins
