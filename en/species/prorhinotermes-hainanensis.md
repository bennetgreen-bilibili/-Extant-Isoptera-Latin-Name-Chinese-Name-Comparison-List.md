## Summary

***Prorhinotermes hainanensis*** is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1989; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Ya. Distribution: China, Hainan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prorhinotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hainanensis*: After Hainan (China)
