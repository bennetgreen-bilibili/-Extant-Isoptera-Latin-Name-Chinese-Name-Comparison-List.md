## Summary

***Glyptotermes daweishanensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Huang, Zhu, Ping, He, Li & Gao in 2000; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: ChinPing. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *daweishanensis*: Named after the locality Daweishan
