## Summary

***Pseudacanthotermes harrisensis*** is a species of the genus *Pseudacanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1962; the type locality is Sudan: Tozi Research Farm. Distribution: Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudacanthotermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *harrisensis*: Named after the locality Harris
