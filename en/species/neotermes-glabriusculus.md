## Summary

***Neotermes glabriusculus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oliveira in 1979; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Reserva Biológica Alto da Serra de Paranapiacaba \[ 23.7795S 46.3162W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *glabriusculus*: From glabriusc + ulus
