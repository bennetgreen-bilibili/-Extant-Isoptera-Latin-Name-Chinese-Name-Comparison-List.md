## Summary

***Nasutitermes arenarius*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Santarém \[2.43917S 54.705W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Suriname, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *arenarius*: From the descriptive term 'aren' (sand)
