## Summary

***Promirotermes rotundifrons*** is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1908; the type locality is Botswana: Kalahari: Kooa. Distribution: Botswana, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *rotundifrons*: From rotundi + frons
