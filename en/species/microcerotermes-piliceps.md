## Summary

***Microcerotermes piliceps*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Solomon Islands: Ugi Island. Distribution: Oceania, Solomon Islands, Papua New Guinea. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *piliceps*: From pili + ceps
