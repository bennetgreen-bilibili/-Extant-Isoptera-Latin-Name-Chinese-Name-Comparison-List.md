## Summary

***Prohamitermes mirabilis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Prohamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Mt. Lambir; Singapore: ?. Distribution: Malaysia, Borneo, Singapore, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prohamitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *mirabilis*: Latin mirabilis (wonderful)
