## Summary

***Odontotermes bequaerti*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Thysville. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bequaerti*: Named after J. Bequaert
