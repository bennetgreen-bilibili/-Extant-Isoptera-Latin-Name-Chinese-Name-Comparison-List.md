## Summary

***Glyptotermes taruni*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bose in 1999; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: Lower Subansari District, 23 km from Yazali, near Lichi. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *taruni*: Named after Tarun
