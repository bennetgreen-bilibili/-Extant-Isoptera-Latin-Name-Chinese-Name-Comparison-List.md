## Summary

***Tenuirostritermes briciae*** is a species of the genus *Tenuirostritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1922; the type locality is Honduras: Lombardia. Distribution: Mexico, Nicaragua, Belize, Guatemala, Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tenuirostritermes*: From Latin tenuis (slender) + rostrum (beak) + termes.

Specific epithet *briciae*: Named after Bricia
