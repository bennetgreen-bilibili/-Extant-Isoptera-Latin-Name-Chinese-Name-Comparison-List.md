## Summary

***Glyptotermes angustus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Panama: Rio Chinilla. Distribution: Costa Rica, Belize, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *angustus*: Latin angustus (narrow)
