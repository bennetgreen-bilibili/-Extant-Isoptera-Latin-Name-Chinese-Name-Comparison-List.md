## Summary

***Syntermes praecellens*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1945; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: São Paulo \[23.53S 46.62W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *praecellens*: From the descriptive term 'praecell' (excellent)
