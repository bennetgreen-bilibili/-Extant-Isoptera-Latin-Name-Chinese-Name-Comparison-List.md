## Summary

***Cyrilliotermes strictinasus*** is a species of the genus *Cyrilliotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: South America, Brazil. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cyrilliotermes*: Named after a person (Cyrillio); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *strictinasus*: Latin strictus (tight) + nasus (nose), tight-nosed
