## Summary

***Tumulitermes pulleinei*** is a species of the genus *Tumulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mjöberg in 1920; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Ravenshoe. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tumulitermes*: From Latin tumulus (mound) + termes.

Specific epithet *pulleinei*: Named after R. H. Pulleine (Australian naturalist/arachnologist)
