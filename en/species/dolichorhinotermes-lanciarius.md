## Summary

***Dolichorhinotermes lanciarius*** is a species of the genus *Dolichorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Engel & Krishna in 2007; the type locality is Ecuador: Morona-Santiago: Gualaquiza \[ 3.4S 78.55W \]. Distribution: Bolivia, Ecuador, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dolichorhinotermes*: From Greek dolichos (long) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lanciarius*: Latin lanciarius (spearman)
