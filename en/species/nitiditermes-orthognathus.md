## Summary

***Nitiditermes orthognathus*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Faradje. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *orthognathus*: Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw), straight-jawed
