## Summary

***Nasutitermes proatripennis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Ka-Chong. Distribution: Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *proatripennis*: From proatri + pennis
