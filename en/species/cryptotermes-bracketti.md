## Summary

***Cryptotermes bracketti*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 2006; the type locality is Bahamas: San Salvador Island, Dixon Settlement. Distribution: Bahamas, San Salvador Island.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *bracketti*: Named after Brackett
