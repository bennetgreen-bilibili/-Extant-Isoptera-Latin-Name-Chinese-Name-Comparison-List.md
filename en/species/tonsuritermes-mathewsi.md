## Summary

***Tonsuritermes mathewsi*** is a species of the genus *Tonsuritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Constantini & Cancello in 2018; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Chapada dos Guimarães \[ 15.4606S 55.7497W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tonsuritermes*: From Latin tonsus (shorn) + termes.

Specific epithet *mathewsi*: Named after Mathews
