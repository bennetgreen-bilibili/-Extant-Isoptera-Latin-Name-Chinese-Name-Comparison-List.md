## Summary

***Neotermes hirtellus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[ 15.5961S 56.0967W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Argentina.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hirtellus*: Latin hirtus (hairy) + -ellus (small), somewhat hairy
