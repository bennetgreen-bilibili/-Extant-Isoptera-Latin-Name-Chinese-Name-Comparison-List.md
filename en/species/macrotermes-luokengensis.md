## Summary

***Macrotermes luokengensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Lin & Shi in 1982; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Dianbai County. Distribution: Guangdong, China, Dianbai.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *luokengensis*: Named after Luokeng, Guangdong, China
