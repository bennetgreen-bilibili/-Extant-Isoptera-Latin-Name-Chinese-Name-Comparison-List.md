## Summary

***Odontotermes flammifrons*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Abimva (Abimwa, Upper Uele). Distribution: West Africa, Burkina Faso, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Sudan, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *flammifrons*: Latin flamma (flame) + frons (front), flame-fronted
