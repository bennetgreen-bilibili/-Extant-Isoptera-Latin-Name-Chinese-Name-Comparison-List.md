## Summary

***Paracapritermes kraepelinii*** is a species of the genus *Paracapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1909; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Mundaring. Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Paracapritermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *kraepelinii*: Named after K. Kraepelin
