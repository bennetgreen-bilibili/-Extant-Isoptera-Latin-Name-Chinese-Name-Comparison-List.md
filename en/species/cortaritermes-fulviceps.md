## Summary

***Cortaritermes fulviceps*** is a species of the genus *Cortaritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Argentina: Córdoba: Cernadas (old train station) \[34.2264S 62.878W\]; Entre Ríos: Chajarí \[30.7667S 57.9833W\]; Entre Ríos: Concordia \[31.4S 58.0333W\]; Misiones: Posadas \[27.37S 55.9W\]; San Luis: San Luis \[33.3S 66.35W\]; Santa Fé: Sunchales \[30.9333S 61.5667W\]; Paraguay: Guairá: Villa Rica \[25.75S 56.4333W\]; Itapúa: Encarnación \[27.252S 55.914W\]; Uruguay: Canelones: Francisco Soca (formerly Mosquitos) \[34.6833S 55.6833W\]; Maldonado: La Sierra \[34.7667S 55.4167W\]. Distribution: Argentina, Uruguay, Brazil, Paraguay. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.
