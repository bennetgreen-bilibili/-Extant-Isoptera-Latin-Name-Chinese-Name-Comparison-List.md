## Summary

***Australitermes dilucidus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Australitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Doongul State Forest, near Aramara. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Australitermes*: From Latin australis (southern) + termes.

Specific epithet *dilucidus*: Latin dilucidus (clear, lucid)
