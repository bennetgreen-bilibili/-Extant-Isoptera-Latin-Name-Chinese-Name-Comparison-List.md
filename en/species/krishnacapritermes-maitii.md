## Summary

***Krishnacapritermes maitii*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Krishnacapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1997; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Pumbarai: Palni Hills. Distribution: India, Western Ghats.

## Etymology

Genus name *Krishnacapritermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *maitii*: Named after P.K. Maiti
