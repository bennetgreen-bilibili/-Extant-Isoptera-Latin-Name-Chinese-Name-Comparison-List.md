## Summary

***Nitiditermes berghei*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1960; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Keyberg, near Lubumbashi. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *berghei*: Named after van den Berghe
