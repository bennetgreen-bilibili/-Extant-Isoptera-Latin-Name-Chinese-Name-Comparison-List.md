## Summary

***Atlantitermes guarinim*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Atlantitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Fontes in 1979; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Itanhaém \[24.1831S 46.7889W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Atlantitermes*: From the Atlantic + termes.

Specific epithet *guarinim*: Tupi guarinim (a tree/fruit)
