## Summary

***Glyptotermes latignathus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao & Zhu in 1982; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Chengdu. Distribution: China (Sichuan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latignathus*: Latin latus (broad) + Greek gnathos (jaw), broad-jawed
