## Summary

***Globitermes globosus globosus*** is a subspecies of *Globitermes globosus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae), genus *Globitermes*. The subspecies was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Mt. Lambir. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Globitermes globosus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Globitermes*: From Latin globus (ball, sphere) + termes.

Specific epithet *globosus*: Latin globosus (globular)
