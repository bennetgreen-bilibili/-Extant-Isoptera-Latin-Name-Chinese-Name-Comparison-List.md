## Summary

***Amitermes desertorum*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1902; the type locality is Algeria: "dans le Souf, entre Maouiet-Fezan et Tougourt.". Distribution: Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Tunisia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *desertorum*: From Latin desertum (desert), of deserts
