## Summary

***Odontotermes giriensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Assam: Garo Hills District: Rongrengiri. Distribution: South Asia, East Asia, China, India, Bangladesh, Bhutan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *giriensis*: Named after the locality Giri (or Sanskrit giri, mountain)
