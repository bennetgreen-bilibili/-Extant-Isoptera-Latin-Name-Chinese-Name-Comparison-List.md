## Summary

***Stylotermes fletcheri*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Shevaroy Hills; Tamil Nadu: Coimbatore. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fletcheri*: Named after Fletcher
