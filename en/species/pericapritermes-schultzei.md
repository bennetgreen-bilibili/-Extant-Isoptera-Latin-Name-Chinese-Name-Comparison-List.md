## Summary

***Pericapritermes schultzei*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Sepik. Distribution: Indonesia, Australia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *schultzei*: Named after L. Schultze
