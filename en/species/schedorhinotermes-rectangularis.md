## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes rectangularis*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Chanthaburi. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rectangularis*: From rectangul + aris
