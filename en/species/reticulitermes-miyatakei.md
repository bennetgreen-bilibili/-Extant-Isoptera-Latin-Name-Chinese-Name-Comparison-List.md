## Summary

***Reticulitermes miyatakei*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Morimoto in 1968; the type locality is Japan: Amami-Oshima; Ohara; Santaro-toge; Tokunoshima. Distribution: Japan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *miyatakei*: Named after Miyatake
