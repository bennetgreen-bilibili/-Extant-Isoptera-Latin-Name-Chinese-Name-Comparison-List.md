## Summary

***Stylotermes mecocephalus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Li in 1978; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province:: Jingxi Xian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mecocephalus*: Greek mēkos (length) + kephalē (head), long-headed
