## Summary

***Odontotermes palmquisti*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1907; the type locality is Tanzania: Kilimanjaro: Kibonoto. Distribution: East Africa, Tanzania, Kenya, Malawi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *palmquisti*: Named after Palmquist
