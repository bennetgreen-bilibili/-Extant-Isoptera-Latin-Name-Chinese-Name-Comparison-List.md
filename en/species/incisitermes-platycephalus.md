## Summary

***Incisitermes platycephalus*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1933; the type locality is Mexico: Colima State: Madrid, near Colima. Distribution: Mexico, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *platycephalus*: From the descriptive term 'plat' (broad)
