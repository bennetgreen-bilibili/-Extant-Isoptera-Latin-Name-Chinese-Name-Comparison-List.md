## Summary

***Trinervitermes gratiosus*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Luluabourg. Distribution: Angola, Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *gratiosus*: Latin gratiosus (gracious)
