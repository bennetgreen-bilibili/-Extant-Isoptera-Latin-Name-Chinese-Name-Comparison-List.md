## Summary

***Calcaritermes snyderi*** is a species of the genus *Calcaritermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1962; the type locality is El Salvador: Volcan de Santa Ana. Distribution: El Salvador, Panama, Belize, Guatemala, Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Calcaritermes*: From Latin calcar (spur) + termes.

Specific epithet *snyderi*: Named after the termite taxonomist T. E. Snyder
