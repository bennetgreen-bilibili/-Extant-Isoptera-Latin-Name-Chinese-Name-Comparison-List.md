## Summary

***Bulbitermes johorensis*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Pervez in 1986; the type locality is Malaysia: Johore: 20 km N of Mersing: Guong Arong Forest Reserve. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *johorensis*: Named after the locality Johor
