## Summary

***Macrotermes vikaspurensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thakur, Kumar & Tyagi in 2011; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehradun, Vikaspur \[ 30.478N 77.769E \]. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *vikaspurensis*: Named after the locality Vikaspur (India)
