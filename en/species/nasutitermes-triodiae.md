## Summary

***Nasutitermes triodiae*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Hall’s Creek, Kimberly Goldfield. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea. This site records 4 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *triodiae*: After the plant genus Triodia
