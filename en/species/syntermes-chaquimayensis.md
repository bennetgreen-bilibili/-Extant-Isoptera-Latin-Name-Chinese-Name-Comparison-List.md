## Summary

***Syntermes chaquimayensis*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Llinquipata \[13.8167S 70.6333W\]. Distribution: Colombia, Bolivia, Brazil, Peru, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chaquimayensis*: Named after the locality Chaquimayo
