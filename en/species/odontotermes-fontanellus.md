## Summary

***Odontotermes fontanellus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1925; the type locality is China: Jiangsu Province: Tschingkiang. Distribution: Jiangsu (Zhenjiang), Anhui, Yunnan, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fontanellus*: Latin fontana (spring) + -ellus (small), little spring
