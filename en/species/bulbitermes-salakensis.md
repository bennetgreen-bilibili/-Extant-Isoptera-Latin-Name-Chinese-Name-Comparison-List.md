## Summary

***Bulbitermes salakensis*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Depok, near Bogor; Kali Bata, near Bogor; Tjandjoer, Vada; near Tjidaho. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *salakensis*: Named after the locality Mount Salak (West Java, Indonesia)
