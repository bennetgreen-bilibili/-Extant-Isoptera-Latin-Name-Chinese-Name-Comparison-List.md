## Summary

***Cryptotermes cymatofrons*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Bahamas: Cat Island, Hermitage. Distribution: Cuba, Bahamas.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *cymatofrons*: Greek kyma (wave) + Latin frons (front), wavy-fronted
