## Summary

***Macrotermes denticulatus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Li & Ping in 1983; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong. Distribution: Yunnan, China, Xiaomenglun, Hainan, Xinglong, Guangxi, Pingxiang.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *denticulatus*: Latin denticulatus (finely toothed)
