## Summary

***Apicotermes gurgulifex*** is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1956; the type locality is Angola: Lunda, near Vila Henrique de Carvalho. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Apicotermes*: From Latin apex (tip, summit) + termes.

Specific epithet *gurgulifex*: Latin gurgulio (weevil) + -fex (maker)
