## Summary

***Eucryptotermes breviceps*** is a species of the genus *Eucryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Constantino in 1997; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Balbina (hydroelectric dam) \[ 1.513S 59.8419W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eucryptotermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *breviceps*: From brevi + ceps
