## Summary

***Cylindrotermes brevipilosus*** is a species of the genus *Cylindrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cylindrotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: El Beni: Ivón \[ 11.13S 66.13W \]. Distribution: Neotropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cylindrotermes*: From Greek kylindros (cylinder) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brevipilosus*: From brevipil + osus
