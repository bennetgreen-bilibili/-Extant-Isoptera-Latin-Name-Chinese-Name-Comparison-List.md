## Summary

***Trinervitermes sudanicus*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Mali: Toukoto. Distribution: Mali.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *sudanicus*: Named after the locality Sudan
