## Summary

***Ruptitermes piliceps*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Acioli & Constantino in 2015; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Niquelândia, Codemin \[ 14.1332S 48.34W \]. Distribution: South America, Colombia, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *piliceps*: From pili + ceps
