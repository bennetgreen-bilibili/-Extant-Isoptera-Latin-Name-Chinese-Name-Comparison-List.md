## Summary

***Macrotermes chaiglomi*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Bangkok, Bang Khen. Distribution: Southeast Asia, East Asia, China, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *chaiglomi*: Named after the locality Chai Glom
