## Summary

***Reticulitermes speratus speratus*** is a subspecies of *Reticulitermes speratus*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, genus *Reticulitermes*. The subspecies was described by Kolbe in 1885; the type locality is Japan: Hakodate, Kaga-Yashiki, Mohezi, Yedo. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Reticulitermes speratus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *speratus*: Latin speratus (hoped for)
