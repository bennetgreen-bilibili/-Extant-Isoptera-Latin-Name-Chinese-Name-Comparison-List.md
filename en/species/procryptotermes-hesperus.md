## Summary

***Procryptotermes hesperus*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 2001; the type locality is Bahamas: North Andros Island, 3 km SE Coakley Town. Distribution: Bahamas, Cuba, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hesperus*: Greek hesperos (western, evening)
