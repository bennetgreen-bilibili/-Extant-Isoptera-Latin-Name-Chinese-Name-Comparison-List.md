## Summary

***Calcaritermes guatemalae*** is a species of the genus *Calcaritermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Guatemala: Mixco. Distribution: Central America, Belize, Guatemala, Mexico, Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Calcaritermes*: From Latin calcar (spur) + termes.

Specific epithet *guatemalae*: Named after Guatemala
