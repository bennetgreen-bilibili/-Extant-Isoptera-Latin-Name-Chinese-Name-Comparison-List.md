## Summary

***Reticulitermes citrinus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Li in 1982; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Longquan County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *citrinus*: Latin citrinus (citrine, lemon-yellow)
