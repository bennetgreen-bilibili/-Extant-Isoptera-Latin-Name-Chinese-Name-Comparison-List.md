## Summary

***Ancistrotermes equatorius*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1966; the type locality is Uganda: Karamoja district, 40 miles southwest of Moroto. Distribution: Nigeria, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *equatorius*: Latin aequator (equator), equatorial
