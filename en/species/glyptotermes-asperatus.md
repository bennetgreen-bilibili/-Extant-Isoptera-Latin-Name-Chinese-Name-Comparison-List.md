## Summary

***Glyptotermes asperatus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Costa Rica: Santa Clara: Hamburg Farm. Distribution: Costa Rica.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *asperatus*: From asper + atus
