## Summary

***Velocitermes paucipilis*** is a species of the genus *Velocitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Velocitermes*: From Latin velox (swift) + termes.

Specific epithet *paucipilis*: From paucip + ilis
