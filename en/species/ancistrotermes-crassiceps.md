## Summary

***Ancistrotermes crassiceps*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Wang in 1991; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Jinghong County: Manfeilong. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *crassiceps*: From crassi + ceps
