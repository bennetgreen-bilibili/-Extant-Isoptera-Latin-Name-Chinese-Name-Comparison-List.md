## Summary

***Procapritermes longignathus*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Tung Sa-Lang Luang. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *longignathus*: Latin longus (long) + Greek gnathos (jaw), long-jawed
