## Summary

***Neotermes nigeriensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Nigeria: Oban District. Distribution: Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nigeriensis*: Named after the locality Niger
