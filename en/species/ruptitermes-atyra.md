## Summary

***Ruptitermes atyra*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Acioli & Constantino in 2015; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Benjamin Constant, Nova Aliança \[ 4.35S 69.6W \]. Distribution: South America, Colombia, Brazil, Panama, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *atyra*: Named after the locality Atyra
