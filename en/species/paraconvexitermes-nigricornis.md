## Summary

***Paraconvexitermes nigricornis*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Paraconvexitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]. Distribution: Colombia, French Guiana, Brazil, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Paraconvexitermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + Latin convexus (convex) + termes.

Specific epithet *nigricornis*: From nigri + cornis
