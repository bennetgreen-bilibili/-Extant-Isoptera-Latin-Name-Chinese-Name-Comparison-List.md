## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes tikadari*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Assam: Cherrapunji. Distribution: India, Bhutan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *tikadari*: Named after B. K. Tikader (Indian arachnologist)
