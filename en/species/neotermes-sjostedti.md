## Summary

***Neotermes sjostedti*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Desneux in 1908; the type locality is Vanuatu: Espiritu Santo. Distribution: Vanuatu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sjostedti*: Named after the entomologist Y. Sjöstedt
