## Summary

***Microcerotermes brachygnathus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kindia. Distribution: Old World tropics, Burkina Faso, Indonesia, Guinea, South Africa, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brachygnathus*: From brachy + gnathus
