## Summary

***Heterotermes cardini*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Bahamas: Andros Island. Distribution: Caribbean, Puerto Rico, Guadeloupe, Bahamas, Panama, United States.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cardini*: Named after Cardin
