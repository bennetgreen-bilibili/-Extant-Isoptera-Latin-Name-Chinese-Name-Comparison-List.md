## Summary

***Termes melindae*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1960; the type locality is Belize: Melinda Forest Station, Stann Creek Valley. Distribution: Central America, Mexico, Belize, Cayman Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *melindae*: Named after Melinde (Kenya)
