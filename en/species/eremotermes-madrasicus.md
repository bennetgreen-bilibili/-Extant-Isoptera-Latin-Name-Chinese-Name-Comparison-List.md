## Summary

***Eremotermes madrasicus*** is a species of the genus *Eremotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu (Madras), 10 miles north of Madras City: Villivakkam. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eremotermes*: From Greek erēmos (desert, solitary) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *madrasicus*: Named after the locality Madras
