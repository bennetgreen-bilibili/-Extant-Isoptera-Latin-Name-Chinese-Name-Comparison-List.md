## Summary

***Drepanotermes magnificus*** is a species of the genus *Drepanotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Mjöberg in 1920; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Kimberley District: Lower Lewarynga. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Drepanotermes*: From Greek drepanon (sickle) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *magnificus*: Latin magnificus (magnificent, grand)
