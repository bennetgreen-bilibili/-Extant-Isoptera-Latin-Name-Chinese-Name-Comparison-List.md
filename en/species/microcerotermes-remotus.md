## Summary

***Microcerotermes remotus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1984; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Ya. Distribution: Hainan (Yaxian, Dongfang, Ledong, Lingshui).

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *remotus*: Latin remotus (remote)
