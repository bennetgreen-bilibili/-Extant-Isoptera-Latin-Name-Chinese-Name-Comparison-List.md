## Summary

***Cryptotermes darwini*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1935; the type locality is Ecuador: Galápagos: Isla Floreana (Charles) \[ 1.2951S 90.4384W \]. Distribution: Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *darwini*: Named after C. Darwin
