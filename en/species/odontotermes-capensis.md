## Summary

***Odontotermes capensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by De Geer in 1778; the type locality is South Africa: Cape of Good Hope (Kapland): Near Humansdorp on Seekoei River. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *capensis*: From Latin capensis, of the Cape (of Good Hope)
