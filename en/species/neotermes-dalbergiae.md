## Summary

***Neotermes dalbergiae*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kalshoven in 1930; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Banjoemas: Kedoe. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dalbergiae*: After the plant genus Dalbergia
