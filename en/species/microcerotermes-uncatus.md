## Summary

***Microcerotermes uncatus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 1965; the type locality is Myanmar: Maymyo, 24 miles east of Mandalay. Distribution: Myanmar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *uncatus*: From unc + atus
