## Summary

***Basidentitermes potens*** is a species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Conakry; Mamou. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Gambia, Togo, Nigeria, Senegal, Cameroon, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Basidentitermes*: From Greek basis (base) + Latin dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *potens*: Latin potens (powerful)
