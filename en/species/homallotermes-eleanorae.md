## Summary

***Homallotermes eleanorae*** is a species of the genus *Homallotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1972; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Homallotermes*: From Greek homalos (even, level) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *eleanorae*: Named after Eleanor
