## Summary

***Euchilotermes tensus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Euchilotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: West Africa, Gambia, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euchilotermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + cheilos (lip, margin) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tensus*: Latin tensus (stretched)
