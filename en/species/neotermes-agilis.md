## Summary

***Neotermes agilis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1902; the type locality is Cameroon: Johann-Albrechtshöhe. Distribution: Cameroon, Ghana, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *agilis*: From the descriptive term 'agil' (agile)
