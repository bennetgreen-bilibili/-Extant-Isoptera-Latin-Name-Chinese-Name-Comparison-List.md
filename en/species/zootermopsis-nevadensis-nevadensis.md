## Summary

***Zootermopsis nevadensis nevadensis*** is a subspecies of *Zootermopsis nevadensis*, in the family ARCHOTERMOPSIDAE, genus *Zootermopsis*. The subspecies was described by Hagen in 1874; the type locality is United States: Nevada: Truckee. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Zootermopsis nevadensis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Zootermopsis*: From Greek zōon (animal) + -termopsis (term- + -opsis).

Specific epithet *nevadensis*: Named after Nevada (USA)
