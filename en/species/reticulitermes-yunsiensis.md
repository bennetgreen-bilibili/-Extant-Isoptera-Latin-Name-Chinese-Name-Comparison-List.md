## Summary

***Reticulitermes yunsiensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Huang in 1986; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Daiyunshan. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *yunsiensis*: Named after the type locality Yunsi, Fujian, China.
