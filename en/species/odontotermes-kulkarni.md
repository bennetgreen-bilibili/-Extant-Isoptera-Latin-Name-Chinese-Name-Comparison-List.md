## Summary

***Odontotermes kulkarni*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1959; the type locality is India: Karnataka: Bijapur. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kulkarni*: Named after Kulkarni
