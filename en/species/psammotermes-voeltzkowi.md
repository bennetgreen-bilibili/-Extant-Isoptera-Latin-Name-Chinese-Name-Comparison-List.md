## Summary

***Psammotermes voeltzkowi*** is a species of the genus *Psammotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1910; the type locality is Madagascar: Tulear. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Psammotermes*: From Greek psammos (sand) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *voeltzkowi*: Named after Alfred Voeltzkow (German naturalist)
