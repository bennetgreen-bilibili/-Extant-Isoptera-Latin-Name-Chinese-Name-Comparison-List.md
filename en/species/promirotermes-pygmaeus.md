## Summary

***Promirotermes pygmaeus*** is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1963; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Garamba. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *pygmaeus*: From pygma + eus
