## Summary

***Pericapritermes topslipensis*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Thakur in 1976; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: South Coimbatore Forest Division: Topslip. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *topslipensis*: Named after the locality Topslip
