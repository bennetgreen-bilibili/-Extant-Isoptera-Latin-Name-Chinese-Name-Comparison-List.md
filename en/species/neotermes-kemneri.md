## Summary

***Neotermes kemneri*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya: Gannoruwa. Distribution: South Asia, India, Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kemneri*: Named after the entomologist N. A. Kemner
