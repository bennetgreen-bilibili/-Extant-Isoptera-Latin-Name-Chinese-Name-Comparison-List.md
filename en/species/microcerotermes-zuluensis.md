## Summary

***Microcerotermes zuluensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Entendweni, Mkosi, Mtewa, Somkele. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, southern Africa. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zuluensis*: Named after the locality Zulu
