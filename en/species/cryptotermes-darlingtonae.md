## Summary

***Cryptotermes darlingtonae*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Dominica: Springfield Station. Distribution: Caribbean, Guadeloupe, Saint Lucia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *darlingtonae*: Named after P. J. Darlington
