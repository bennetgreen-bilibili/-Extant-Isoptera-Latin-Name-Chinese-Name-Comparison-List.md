## Summary

***Neotermes krishnai*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bose in 1984; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Salem. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *krishnai*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna
