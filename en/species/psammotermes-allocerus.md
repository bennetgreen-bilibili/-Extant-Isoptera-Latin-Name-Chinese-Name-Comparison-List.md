## Summary

***Psammotermes allocerus*** is a species of the genus *Psammotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1908; the type locality is Namibia: Lüderitz (Namaland: Lüderitzbucht). Distribution: southern Africa, Namibia, South Africa, Angola, Botswana, Egypt.

## Etymology

Genus name *Psammotermes*: From Greek psammos (sand) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *allocerus*: From allo + cerus
