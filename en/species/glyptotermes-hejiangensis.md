## Summary

***Glyptotermes hejiangensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao in 1984; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Hejiang. Distribution: China (Sichuan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *hejiangensis*: Named after the locality Hejiang
