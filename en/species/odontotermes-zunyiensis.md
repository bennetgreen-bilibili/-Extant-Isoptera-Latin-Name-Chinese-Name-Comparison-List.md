## Summary

***Odontotermes zunyiensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Li & Ping in 1982; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Zhuengyi. Distribution: China (Guizhou).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zunyiensis*: Named after the locality Zuny
