## Summary

***Odontotermes parvidens*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is Bangladesh: Noakhali District: Chaumahani; India: Assam: Gauhati: Kamakhya Hill, Gauhati Town; Karnataka: on road from Mysore to Mercara; Punjab: Gurdaspur District: Dhar. Distribution: South Asia, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Myanmar. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *parvidens*: From the descriptive term 'parv' (small)
