## Summary

***Machadotermes inflatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Machadotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1974; the type locality is Angola: Dundo: Parc Carrisso. Distribution: Old World tropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Machadotermes*: Named after a person (Machado); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *inflatus*: From infl + atus
