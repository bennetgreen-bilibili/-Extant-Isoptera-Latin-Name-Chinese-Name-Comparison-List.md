## Summary

***Basidentitermes demoulini*** is a species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1963; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Garamba. Distribution: Old World tropics, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Basidentitermes*: From Greek basis (base) + Latin dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *demoulini*: Named after Georges Demoulin
