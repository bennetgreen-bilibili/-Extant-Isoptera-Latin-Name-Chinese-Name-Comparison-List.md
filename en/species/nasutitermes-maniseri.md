## Summary

***Nasutitermes maniseri*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by John in 1920; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso do Sul: Nalique (Kadiweu Indigenous Land) \[20.5207S 57.0348W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *maniseri*: Named after Maniser
