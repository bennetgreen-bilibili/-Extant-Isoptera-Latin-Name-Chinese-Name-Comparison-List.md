## Summary

***Angulitermes obtusus*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Karnataka: Bellary District: Hospet. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *obtusus*: Latin obtusus (blunt)
