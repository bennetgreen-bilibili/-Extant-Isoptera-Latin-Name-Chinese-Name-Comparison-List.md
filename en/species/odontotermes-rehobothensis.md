## Summary

***Odontotermes rehobothensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1914; the type locality is Namibia: Rehoboth. Distribution: Namibia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rehobothensis*: Named after Rehoboth (Namibia)
