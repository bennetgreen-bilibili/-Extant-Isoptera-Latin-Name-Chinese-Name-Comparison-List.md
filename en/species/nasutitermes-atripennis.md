## Summary

***Nasutitermes atripennis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *atripennis*: From atri + pennis
