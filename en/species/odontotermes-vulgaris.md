## Summary

***Odontotermes vulgaris*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, East Africa, southern Africa, Angola, Namibia, South Africa, Eswatini, Zimbabwe. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *vulgaris*: Latin vulgaris (common)
