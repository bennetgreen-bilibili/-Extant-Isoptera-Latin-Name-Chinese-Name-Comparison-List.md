## Summary

***Procapritermes setiger*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Marudi. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *setiger*: From seti + ger
