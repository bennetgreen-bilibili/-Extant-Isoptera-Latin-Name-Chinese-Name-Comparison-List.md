## Summary

***Ceylonitermes paulosus*** is a species of the genus *Ceylonitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ipe & Mathew in 2019; the type locality is India: Kerala: Kottayam, CMS College Campus. Distribution: Sri Lanka, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ceylonitermes*: From Ceylon (Sri Lanka) + termes.

Specific epithet *paulosus*: Greek paulos (little)
