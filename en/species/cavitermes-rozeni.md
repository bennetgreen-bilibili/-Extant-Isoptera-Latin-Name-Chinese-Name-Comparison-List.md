## Summary

***Cavitermes rozeni*** is a species of the genus *Cavitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2003; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Manaus, Campos Sales \[3.0012S 60.0431W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cavitermes*: From Latin cavus (hollow) + termes.

Specific epithet *rozeni*: Named after a person "Rozen" (possibly J. G. Rozen, American entomologist)
