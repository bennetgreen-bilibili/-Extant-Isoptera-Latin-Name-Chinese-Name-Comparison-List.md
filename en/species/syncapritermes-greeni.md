## Summary

***Syncapritermes greeni*** is the only extant species of the genus *Syncapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by John in 1925; the type locality is Malaysia: Kuala Lumpur; Selangor; Selangor: Kajang. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syncapritermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *greeni*: Named after E. Ernest Green
