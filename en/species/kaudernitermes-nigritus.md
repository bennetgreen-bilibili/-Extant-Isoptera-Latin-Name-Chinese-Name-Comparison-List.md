## Summary

***Kaudernitermes nigritus*** is a species of the genus *Kaudernitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1897; the type locality is Madagascar: Fenerive. Distribution: Madagascar, Seychelles. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kaudernitermes*: Named after the explorer W. Kaudern; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nigritus*: From the descriptive term 'nigr' (black)
