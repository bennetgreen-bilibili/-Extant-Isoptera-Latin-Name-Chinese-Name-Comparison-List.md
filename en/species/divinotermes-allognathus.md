## Summary

***Divinotermes allognathus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Divinotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Divinotermes*: Etymology uncertain (perhaps Latin divinus 'divine' or a personal name); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *allognathus*: From allo + gnathus
