## Summary

***Basidentitermes amicus*** is a species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1936; the type locality is Tanzania: Songea. Distribution: Old World tropics, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Basidentitermes*: From Greek basis (base) + Latin dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *amicus*: Latin amicus (friendly)
