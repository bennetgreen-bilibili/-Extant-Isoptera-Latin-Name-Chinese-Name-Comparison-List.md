## Summary

***Pericapritermes pallidipes*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1898; the type locality is Cameroon: Kribi. Distribution: Sierra Leone, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *pallidipes*: From pallidi + pes
