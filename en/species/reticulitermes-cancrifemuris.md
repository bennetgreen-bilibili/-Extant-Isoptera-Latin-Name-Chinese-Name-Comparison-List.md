## Summary

***Reticulitermes cancrifemuris*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Zhu in 1984; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Lianshan forestry farm. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *cancrifemuris*: Latin cancer (crab) + femur (thigh), crab-thighed
