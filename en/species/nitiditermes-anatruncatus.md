## Summary

***Nitiditermes anatruncatus*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Conjeni. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *anatruncatus*: From anatrunc + atus
