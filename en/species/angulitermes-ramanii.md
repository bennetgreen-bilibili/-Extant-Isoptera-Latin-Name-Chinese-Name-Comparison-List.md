## Summary

***Angulitermes ramanii*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Bose & Das in 1982; the type locality is India: Orissa: Chilka Lake: Barkuda Island. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *ramanii*: Named after a person "Raman/Ramani" (identity unconfirmed)
