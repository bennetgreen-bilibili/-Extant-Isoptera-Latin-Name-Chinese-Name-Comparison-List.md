## Summary

***Procryptotermes fryeri*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Seychelles: Aldabra: Takamaka. Distribution: Seychelles.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fryeri*: Named after Fryer
