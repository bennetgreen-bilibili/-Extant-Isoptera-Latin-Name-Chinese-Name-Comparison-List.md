## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes fontanellus*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Thakur & Chatterjee in 1971; the type locality is India: Goa: South Goa Forest Division: Sangeum Forest Range, Satida mixed forests. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *fontanellus*: Latin fontana (spring) + -ellus (small), little spring
