## Summary

***Syntermes peruanus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Mojos \[14.5667S 68.8833W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *peruanus*: Named after the locality Peru
