## Summary

***Dolichorhinotermes latilabrum*** is a species of the genus *Dolichorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Bolivia: El Beni: Rio Ivón \[ 11.13S 66.13W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, French Guiana, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dolichorhinotermes*: From Greek dolichos (long) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latilabrum*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
