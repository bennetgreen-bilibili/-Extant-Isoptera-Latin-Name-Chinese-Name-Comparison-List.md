## Summary

***Cryptotermes chacoensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roisin in 2003; the type locality is Argentina: Formosa: Parque Nacional Pilcomayo \[ 25.046S 58.0594W \]. Distribution: Paraguay, Argentina, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *chacoensis*: Named after the locality Chaco
