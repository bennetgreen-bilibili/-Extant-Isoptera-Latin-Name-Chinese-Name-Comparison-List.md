## Summary

***Procapritermes prosetiger*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Ka-Chong. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *prosetiger*: From proseti + ger
