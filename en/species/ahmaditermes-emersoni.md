## Summary

***Ahmaditermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Maiti in 1977; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Darjeeling District, ca. 5 km north-east of Teesta Bridge. Distribution: Bhutan, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
