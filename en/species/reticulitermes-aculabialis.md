## Summary

***Reticulitermes aculabialis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Huang in 1977; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Chengdu. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *aculabialis*: Latin aculeus (sting) + labialis (lip), spiny-lipped
