## Summary

***Parvitermes wolcotti*** is a species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Puerto Rico: Boquerón. Distribution: Puerto Rico, United States, British Virgin Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parvitermes*: From Latin parvus (small) + termes.

Specific epithet *wolcotti*: Named after A. B. Wolcott (American termitologist)
