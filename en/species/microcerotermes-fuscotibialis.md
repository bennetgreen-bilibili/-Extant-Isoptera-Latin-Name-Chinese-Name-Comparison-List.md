## Summary

***Microcerotermes fuscotibialis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1896; the type locality is Cameroon: Bonge. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fuscotibialis*: From fusco + tibialis
