## Summary

***Kalotermes monticola monticola*** is a subspecies of *Kalotermes monticola*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, genus *Kalotermes*. The subspecies was described by Sjöstedt in 1925; the type locality is Algeria: Massif de l’Edough. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Kalotermes monticola* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *monticola*: From monti + cola
