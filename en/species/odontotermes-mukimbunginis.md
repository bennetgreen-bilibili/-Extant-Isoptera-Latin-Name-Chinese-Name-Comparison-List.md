## Summary

***Odontotermes mukimbunginis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mukimbunginis*: After Mukimbungu (Congo)
