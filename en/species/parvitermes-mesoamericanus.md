## Summary

***Parvitermes mesoamericanus*** is a species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2016; the type locality is Honduras: S. Pinalillo. Distribution: Guatemala, Nicaragua, Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parvitermes*: From Latin parvus (small) + termes.

Specific epithet *mesoamericanus*: Named after the locality Mesoameric
