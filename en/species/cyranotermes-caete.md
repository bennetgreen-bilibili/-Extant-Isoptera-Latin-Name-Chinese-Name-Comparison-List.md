## Summary

***Cyranotermes caete*** is a species of the genus *Cyranotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cancello in 1987; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Serra dos Carajás \[6.07S 50.066W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cyranotermes*: From Cyrano (de Bergerac, famed for his long nose) + termes, for the long nasus.

Specific epithet *caete*: After the Caeté people (Brazil)
