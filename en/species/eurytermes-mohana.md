## Summary

***Eurytermes mohana*** is a species of the genus *Eurytermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Rathore in 1995; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Mandal, 71 km E Bhilwara. Distribution: Oriental region, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eurytermes*: From Greek eurys (broad) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mohana*: Named after Mohana (person or place)
