## Summary

***Ahmaditermes sikkimensis*** is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mukherjee & Maiti in 2008; the type locality is India: Sikkim: Ranipool, near Gangtok. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ahmaditermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist Muzaffer Ahmad; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sikkimensis*: Named after the locality Sikkim
