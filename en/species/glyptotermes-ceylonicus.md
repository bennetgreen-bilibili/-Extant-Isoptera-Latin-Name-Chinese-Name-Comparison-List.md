## Summary

***Glyptotermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
