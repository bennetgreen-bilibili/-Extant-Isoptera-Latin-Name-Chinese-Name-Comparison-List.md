## Summary

***Ternicubitermes subcrenulatus*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kakoulima. Distribution: Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *subcrenulatus*: From subcrenul + atus
