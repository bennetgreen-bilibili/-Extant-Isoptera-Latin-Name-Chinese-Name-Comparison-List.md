## Summary

***Stylotermes wuyinicus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Ping in 1981; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Shaowu, Xian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *wuyinicus*: Named after the locality Wuyi (Fujian, China)
