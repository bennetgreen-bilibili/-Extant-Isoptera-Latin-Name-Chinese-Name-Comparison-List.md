## Summary

***Ternicubitermes glebae*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Tanzania: Kilimanjaro. Distribution: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *glebae*: From Latin gleba (clod of earth)
