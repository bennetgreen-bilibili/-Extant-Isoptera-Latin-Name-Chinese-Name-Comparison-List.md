## Summary

***Microtermes calvus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: St. Gabriel near Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *calvus*: Latin calvus (bald)
