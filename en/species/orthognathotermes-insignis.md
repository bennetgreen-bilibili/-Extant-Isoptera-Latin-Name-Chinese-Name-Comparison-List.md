## Summary

***Orthognathotermes insignis*** is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Araujo in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: São Paulo, Morumbi (district) \[ 23.61S 46.715W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *insignis*: Latin insignis (remarkable)
