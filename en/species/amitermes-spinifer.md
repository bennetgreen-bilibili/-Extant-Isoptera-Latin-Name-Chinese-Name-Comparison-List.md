## Summary

***Amitermes spinifer*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Senegal: Dakar; Hann; Thiès. Distribution: West Africa, East Africa, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *spinifer*: Latin spina (spine) + -fer (bearing), spiny
