## Summary

***Macrotermes natalensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Estcourt. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, East Africa, southern Africa, Angola, Botswana, Central African Republic, Chad, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya, Liberia, Madagascar, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, Nigeria, Senegal, South Africa, Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *natalensis*: Named after the locality Natal
