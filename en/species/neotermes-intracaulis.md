## Summary

***Neotermes intracaulis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 2003; the type locality is Virgin Islands (USA): St. Croix: Sion Hill. Distribution: Caribbean, Saint Barthélemy, Guadeloupe, Aruba, Barbados, Bonaire, French Saint Martin, U.S. Virgin Islands, Colombia, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *intracaulis*: Latin intra (within) + caulis (stem), within-stem
