## Summary

***Cryptotermes naudei*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1950; the type locality is South Africa: North Zululand: Maputa. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *naudei*: Named after Naudé
