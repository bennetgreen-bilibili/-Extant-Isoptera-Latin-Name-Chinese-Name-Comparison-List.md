## Summary

***Bulbitermes rosae*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Tjidaho; Java: Tjitjourouk; Java: near Bogor on Berge Salak. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *rosae*: Named after a person "Rosa", or from Latin rosa (rose); uncertain
