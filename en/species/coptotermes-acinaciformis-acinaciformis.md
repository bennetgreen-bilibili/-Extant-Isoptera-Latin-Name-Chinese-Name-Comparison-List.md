## Summary

***Coptotermes acinaciformis acinaciformis*** is a subspecies of *Coptotermes acinaciformis*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, genus *Coptotermes*. The subspecies was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Halls Creek. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Coptotermes acinaciformis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *acinaciformis*: From acinaci + formis
