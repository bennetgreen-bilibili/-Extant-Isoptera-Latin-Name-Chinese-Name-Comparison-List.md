## Summary

***Bifiditermes sibayiensis*** is a species of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Coaton in 1949; the type locality is South Africa: Diki Bush, 16 km. from Lake Sibayi. Distribution: Namibia, South Africa, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bifiditermes*: From Latin bifidus (cleft in two) + termes.

Specific epithet *sibayiensis*: Named after the locality Lake Sibaya (KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa)
