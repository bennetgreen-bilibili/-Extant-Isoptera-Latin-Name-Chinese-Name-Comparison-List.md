## Summary

***Pericapritermes latignathus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Tjibodas. Distribution: Indonesia, Cambodia, Vietnam, Bangladesh, China, Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *latignathus*: Latin latus (broad) + Greek gnathos (jaw), broad-jawed
