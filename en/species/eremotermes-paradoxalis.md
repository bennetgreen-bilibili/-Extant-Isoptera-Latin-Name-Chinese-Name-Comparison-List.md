## Summary

***Eremotermes paradoxalis*** is a species of the genus *Eremotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1912; the type locality is India: Karnataka: Bangalore. Distribution: India, Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eremotermes*: From Greek erēmos (desert, solitary) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paradoxalis*: From paradox + alis
