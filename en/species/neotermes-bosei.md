## Summary

***Neotermes bosei*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1933; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun: Mathranwala. Distribution: India, Bhutan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bosei*: Named after G. Bose
