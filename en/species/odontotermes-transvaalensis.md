## Summary

***Odontotermes transvaalensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1902; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Mphome. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Botswana, Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *transvaalensis*: Named after Transvaal, South Africa
