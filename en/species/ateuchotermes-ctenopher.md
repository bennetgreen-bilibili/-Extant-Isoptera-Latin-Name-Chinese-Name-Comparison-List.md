## Summary

***Ateuchotermes ctenopher*** is a species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Gabon: 18 km from Makokou. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ateuchotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + teuchos (implement, weapon) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ctenopher*: Greek kteis (comb) + -phoros (bearing), comb-bearing
