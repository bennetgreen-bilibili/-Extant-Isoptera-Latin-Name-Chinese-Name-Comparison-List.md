## Summary

***Macrotermes ituriensis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Ituri. Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *ituriensis*: Named after the locality Itur
