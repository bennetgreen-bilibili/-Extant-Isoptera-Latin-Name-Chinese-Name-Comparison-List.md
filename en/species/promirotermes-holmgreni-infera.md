## Summary

***Promirotermes holmgreni infera*** is a subspecies of *Promirotermes holmgreni*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae), genus *Promirotermes*. The subspecies was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Senegal: Thiès. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Promirotermes holmgreni* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *infera*: Latin inferus (lower, beneath)
