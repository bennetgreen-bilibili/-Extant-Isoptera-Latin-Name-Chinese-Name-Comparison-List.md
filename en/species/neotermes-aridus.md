## Summary

***Neotermes aridus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wilkinson in 1959; the type locality is Kenya: Voi. Distribution: Ethiopia, Kenya.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aridus*: Latin aridus (arid, dry)
