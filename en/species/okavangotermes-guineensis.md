## Summary

***Okavangotermes guineensis*** is a species of the genus *Okavangotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1995; the type locality is Ghana: 3 miles from Larabanga on Sawala road. Distribution: Ghana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Okavangotermes*: From the place name Okavango; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *guineensis*: After Guinea
