## Summary

***Mycterotermes meringocephalus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Mycterotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1965; the type locality is Yemen: Burum, W of Mukalla. Distribution: Oman, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mycterotermes*: From Greek myktēr (nose, snout) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *meringocephalus*: Greek mēringos + kephalē (head)
