## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes tiwarii*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Thakur in 1963; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: South Andaman Island: Chouldhari. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tiwarii*: Named after Tiwar
