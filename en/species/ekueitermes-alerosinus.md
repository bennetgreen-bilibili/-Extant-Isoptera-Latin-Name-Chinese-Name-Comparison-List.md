## Summary

***Ekueitermes alerosinus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Ekueitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Duran-Bautista & Celis-Daza in 2026; the type locality is Colombia: Caquetá: Florencia: La Viciosa, Centro de Investigaciones Amazónicas CIMAZ, Macagual \[ 1.4969N 75.6661W \]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ekueitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *alerosinus*: Named after Alero, combining the names of Alejandro Duran and his parents Leofrey Duran and Rosario Bautista
