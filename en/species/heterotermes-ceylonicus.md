## Summary

***Heterotermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
