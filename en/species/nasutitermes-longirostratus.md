## Summary

***Nasutitermes longirostratus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: San Fermín \[13.9056S 68.9722W\]; Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]; Puno: Llinquipata \[13.8167S 70.6333W\]. Distribution: Bolivia, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *longirostratus*: From longirostr + atus
