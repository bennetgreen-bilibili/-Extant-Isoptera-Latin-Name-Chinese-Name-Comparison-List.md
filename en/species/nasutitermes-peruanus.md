## Summary

***Nasutitermes peruanus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]. Distribution: Peru, Ecuador, Brazil, Bolivia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *peruanus*: Named after the locality Peru
