## Summary

***Schievitermes globicornis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Schievitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Roisin in 2022; the type locality is French Guiana: Petit Saut Dam Road, Carbet Maman Lézard \[ 5.0672N 52.9992W \]. Distribution: French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schievitermes*: Named after a person (Schiev); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *globicornis*: From globi + cornis
