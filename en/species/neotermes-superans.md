## Summary

***Neotermes superans*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1928; the type locality is Ethiopia: Djem-Djem forest. Distribution: Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *superans*: From the descriptive term 'super' (above)
