## Summary

***Unguitermes acutifrons*** is a species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Unguitermes*: From Latin unguis (claw) + termes.

Specific epithet *acutifrons*: From acuti + frons
