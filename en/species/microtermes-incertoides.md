## Summary

***Microtermes incertoides*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Ahmednagar District: Wallon. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *incertoides*: From incert + oides
