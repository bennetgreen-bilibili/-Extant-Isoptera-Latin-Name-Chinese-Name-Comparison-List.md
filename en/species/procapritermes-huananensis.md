## Summary

***Procapritermes huananensis*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yu & Ping in 1966; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Tan-hsien. Distribution: Guangxi, Yunnan (Yingjiang 870 m), Hainan (Qiongzhong Limuling 350 m, Wuzhi Mountain 700 m), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *huananensis*: Named after the locality Huanan
