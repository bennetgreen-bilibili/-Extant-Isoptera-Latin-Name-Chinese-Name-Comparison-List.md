## Summary

***Speculitermes latalabrum*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). ## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *latalabrum*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
