## Summary

***Xylochomitermes melvillensis*** is a species of the genus *Xylochomitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1915; the type locality is Australia: Northern Territory: Melville Island: Garden Point (as Fort Dundas). Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Xylochomitermes*: From Greek xylon (wood) + chōma (mound) + termes.

Specific epithet *melvillensis*: Named after the locality Melvill
