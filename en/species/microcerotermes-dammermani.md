## Summary

***Microcerotermes dammermani*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Maiti in 1966; the type locality is Indonesia: Riouw (Rhio) Archipelago: Durian. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dammermani*: Named after K. W. Dammerman
