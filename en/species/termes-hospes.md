## Summary

***Termes hospes*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Cameroon: Bonge. Distribution: Old World tropics, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Senegal, Cameroon, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya, Nigeria, Uganda. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *hospes*: Latin hospes (host, guest)
