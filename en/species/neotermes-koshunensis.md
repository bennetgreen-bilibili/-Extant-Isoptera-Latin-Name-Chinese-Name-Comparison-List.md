## Summary

***Neotermes koshunensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Shiraki in 1909; the type locality is Taiwan: Nanrenshan, Pingtung Co. \[ 22.0817N 120.835E \]. Distribution: East Asia, Taiwan, Japan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *koshunensis*: Named after Koshun (Hengchun, Taiwan)
