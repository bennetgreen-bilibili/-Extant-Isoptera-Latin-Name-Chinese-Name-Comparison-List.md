## Summary

***Leucopitermes paraleucops*** is a species of the genus *Leucopitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Morimoto in 1976; the type locality is Malaysia: Negeri Sembilan: Pasoh Forest Reserve. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Leucopitermes*: From Greek leukos (white) + Latin termes; further derivation uncertain.

Specific epithet *paraleucops*: From the descriptive term 'para' (beside)
