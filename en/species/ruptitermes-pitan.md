## Summary

***Ruptitermes pitan*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Acioli & Constantino in 2015; the type locality is Brasil: Distrito Federal: Fazenda Água Limpa \[ 15.9667S 47.9061W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *pitan*: Named after the locality Pitan
