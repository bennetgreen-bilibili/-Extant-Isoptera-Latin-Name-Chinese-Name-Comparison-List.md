## Summary

***Lacessititermes yamanei*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Syaukani in 2010; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Solok, Gunung Talang, Lubuk Selasih, Air Sirah. Distribution: Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *yamanei*: Named after Yamane
