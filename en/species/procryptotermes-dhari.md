## Summary

***Procryptotermes dhari*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1963; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Coimbatore, grounds of the Agricultural College \[approx. 11°00?N, 77°00?E\]. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dhari*: Named after the locality Dhar
