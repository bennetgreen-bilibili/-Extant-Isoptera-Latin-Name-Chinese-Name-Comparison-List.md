## Summary

***Reticulitermes hesperus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Banks in 1920; the type locality is United States: California: San Bernardino Mountains: Little Bear Lake. Distribution: western North America, western United States, southwestern Canada.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *hesperus*: Greek hesperos (western, evening)
