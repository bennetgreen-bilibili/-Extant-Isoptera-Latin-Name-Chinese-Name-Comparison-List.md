## Summary

***Basidentitermes aurivillii*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1897; the type locality is Cameroon: Mungo River. Distribution: West Africa, Togo, Nigeria, Ghana, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Basidentitermes*: From Greek basis (base) + Latin dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *aurivillii*: Named after C. Aurivillius
