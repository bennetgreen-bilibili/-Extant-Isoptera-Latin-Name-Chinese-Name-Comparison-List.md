## Summary

***Cryptotermes bengalensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Sunderbans. Distribution: South Asia, India, Sri Lanka, China, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *bengalensis*: Named after the locality Bengal
