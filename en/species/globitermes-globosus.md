## Summary

***Globitermes globosus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Globitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Mt. Lambir. Distribution: Southeast Asian islands, Malay Archipelago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Globitermes*: From Latin globus (ball, sphere) + termes.

Specific epithet *globosus*: Latin globosus (globular)
