## Summary

***Cryptotermes parvifrons*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: Diego Martin: William's Bay \[ 10.6833N 61.6167W \]. Distribution: Grenada, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *parvifrons*: From parvi + frons
