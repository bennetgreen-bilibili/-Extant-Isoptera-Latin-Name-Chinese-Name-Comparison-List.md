## Summary

***Glyptotermes nissanensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Green Islands: Nissan Island. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nissanensis*: Named after the locality Nissan
