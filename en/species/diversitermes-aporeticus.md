## Summary

***Diversitermes aporeticus*** is a species of the genus *Diversitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Diversitermes*: From Latin diversus (diverse) + termes.

Specific epithet *aporeticus*: Greek aporetikos (doubtful, perplexed)
