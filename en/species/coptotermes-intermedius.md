## Summary

***Coptotermes intermedius*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama: Rio Cassine. Distribution: West Africa, Ivory Coast, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Nigeria, Oman. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *intermedius*: Latin intermedius (intermediate)
