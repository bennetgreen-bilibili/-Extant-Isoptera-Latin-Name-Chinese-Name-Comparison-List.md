## Summary

***Eutermellus aquilinus*** is a species of the genus *Eutermellus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1965; the type locality is Ghana: 20 mi from Bolgatanga on Tamale Road. Distribution: tropical Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eutermellus*: From Greek eu- (well) + termes + Latin diminutive -ellus.

Specific epithet *aquilinus*: Latin aquilinus (eagle-like)
