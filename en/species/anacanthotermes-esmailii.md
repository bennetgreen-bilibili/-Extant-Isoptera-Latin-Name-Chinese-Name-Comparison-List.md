## Summary

***Anacanthotermes esmailii*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ghayourfar in 1998; the type locality is Iran: North of Khorassan province: Sarakhs, Khangiran. Distribution: Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *esmailii*: Named after Esmaili
