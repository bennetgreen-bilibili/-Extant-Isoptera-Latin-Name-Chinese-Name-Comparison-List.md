## Summary

***Lacessititermes kolapisensis*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Sandakan North District: Kolapis Forest, mile 4. Distribution: Malaysia (Sabah).

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *kolapisensis*: Named after Kolapis (Sabah, Malaysia)
