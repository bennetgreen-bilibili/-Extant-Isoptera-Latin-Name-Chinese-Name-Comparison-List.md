## Summary

***Glyptotermes orthognathus*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Chen in 1985; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Da Xian County. Distribution: China (Sichuan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *orthognathus*: Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw), straight-jawed
