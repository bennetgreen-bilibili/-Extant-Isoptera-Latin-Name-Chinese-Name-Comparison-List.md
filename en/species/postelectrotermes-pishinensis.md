## Summary

***Postelectrotermes pishinensis*** is a species of the genus *Postelectrotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ahmad in 1955; the type locality is Pakistan: Baluchistan: District Quetta: Pishin. Distribution: South Asia, Balochistan, Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Postelectrotermes*: From Latin post (after) + Greek ēlektron (amber) + termes (possibly alluding to amber fossils).

Specific epithet *pishinensis*: Named after Pishin, Balochistan, Pakistan
