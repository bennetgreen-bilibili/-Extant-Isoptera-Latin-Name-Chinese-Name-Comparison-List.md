## Summary

***Odontotermes profeae*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Singra. Distribution: South Asia (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *profeae*: Named after a person "Profe" (identity unconfirmed)
