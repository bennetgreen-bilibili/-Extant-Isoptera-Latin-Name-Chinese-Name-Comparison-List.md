## Summary

***Syntermes brevimalatus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1945; the type locality is Guyana: East Berbice-Corentyne: Oronoque River \[2.7N 57.42W\]. Distribution: Colombia, Guyana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brevimalatus*: From brevimal + atus
