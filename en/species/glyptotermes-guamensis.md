## Summary

***Glyptotermes guamensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Guam (USA): Yigo. Distribution: Guam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *guamensis*: Named after the locality Guam
