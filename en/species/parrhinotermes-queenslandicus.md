## Summary

***Parrhinotermes queenslandicus*** is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Mjöberg in 1920; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Malanda. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parrhinotermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *queenslandicus*: Named after the locality Queensland
