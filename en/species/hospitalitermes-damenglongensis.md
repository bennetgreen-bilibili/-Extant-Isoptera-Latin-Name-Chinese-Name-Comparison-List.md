## Summary

***Hospitalitermes damenglongensis*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by He & Gao in 1984; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Damenglong. Distribution: China (Yunnan), Jinghong (Damenglong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *damenglongensis*: Named after the locality Damenglong
