## Summary

***Tumulitermes apiocephalus*** is a species of the genus *Tumulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1909; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Northhampton or Jurrandale. Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tumulitermes*: From Latin tumulus (mound) + termes.

Specific epithet *apiocephalus*: Latin apium (pear) + Greek kephalē (head), pear-headed
