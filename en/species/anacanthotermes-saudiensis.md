## Summary

***Anacanthotermes saudiensis*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chhotani & Bose in 1982; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Jebel Banban. Distribution: Saudi Arabia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *saudiensis*: Named after the locality Saud
