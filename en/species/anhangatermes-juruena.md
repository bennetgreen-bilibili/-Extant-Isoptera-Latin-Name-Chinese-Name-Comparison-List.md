## Summary

***Anhangatermes juruena*** is a species of the genus *Anhangatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oliveira & Constantino in 2014; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Juruena \[10.3594S 58.4903W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anhangatermes*: From Tupi Anhangá (a mythical forest spirit) or the place name + -termes.

Specific epithet *juruena*: After the Juruena River (Brazil)
