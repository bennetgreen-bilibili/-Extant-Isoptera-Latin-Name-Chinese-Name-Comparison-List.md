## Summary

***Odontotermes prewensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Manzoor & Akhtar in 2005; the type locality is Thailand: Prew. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *prewensis*: Named after the locality Prewe
