## Summary

***Paraconvexitermes acangapua*** is a species of the genus *Paraconvexitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cancello & Noirot in 2003; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Benevides, Fazenda Morelândia \[1.22S 48.28W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Paraconvexitermes*: From Greek para- (beside) + Latin convexus (convex) + termes.

Specific epithet *acangapua*: From Tupi acanga (head) + pua (round), referring to the head shape
