## Summary

***Microcerotermes labioangulatus labioangulatus*** is a subspecies of *Microcerotermes labioangulatus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae), genus *Microcerotermes*. The subspecies was described by Sen-Sarma & Thakur in 1975; the type locality is India: Tripura: Ambasa, Zoelchara. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Microcerotermes labioangulatus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *labioangulatus*: From labioangul + atus
