## Summary

***Odontotermes longigula*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roy-Noël in 1969; the type locality is Senegal: Niokolo-Kobo region, near clearing of Siminti. Distribution: West Africa, Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longigula*: From the descriptive term 'long' (long)
