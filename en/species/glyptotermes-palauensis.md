## Summary

***Glyptotermes palauensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Palau: Koror Island. Distribution: Palau, Micronesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *palauensis*: Named after the locality Palau
