## Summary

***Nasutitermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya: experimental station. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
