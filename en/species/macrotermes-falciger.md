## Summary

***Macrotermes falciger*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Gerstäcker in 1891; the type locality is Tanzania: Mbusini. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, East Africa, southern Africa, Benin, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Guinea, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Eswatini, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 5 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *falciger*: Latin falx (sickle) + -ger (bearing), sickle-bearing
