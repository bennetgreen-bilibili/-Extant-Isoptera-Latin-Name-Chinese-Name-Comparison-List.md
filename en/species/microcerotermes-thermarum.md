## Summary

***Microcerotermes thermarum*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Bela-Bela (Warmbaths). Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *thermarum*: From Latin thermae (hot springs)
