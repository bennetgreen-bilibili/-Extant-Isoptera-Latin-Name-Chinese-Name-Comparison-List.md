## Summary

***Anacanthotermes macrocephalus*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Desneux in 1906; the type locality is Pakistan: Sind: Karachi. Distribution: India, Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *macrocephalus*: Greek makros (large) + kephalē (head), large-headed
