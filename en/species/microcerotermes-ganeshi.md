## Summary

***Microcerotermes ganeshi*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Bose in 1984; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Salem: Karambapatty Reserve Forest. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ganeshi*: Named after Ganesh (Hindu deity Ganesha)
