## Summary

***Apicotermes occultus*** is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kakoulima. Distribution: Guinea, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Apicotermes*: From Latin apex (tip, summit) + termes.

Specific epithet *occultus*: Latin occultus (hidden)
