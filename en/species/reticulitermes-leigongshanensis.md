## Summary

***Reticulitermes leigongshanensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Zhu, Huang, Wang & Han in 1993; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Leigong Mountain. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *leigongshanensis*: Named after the locality Leigongshan
