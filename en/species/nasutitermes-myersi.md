## Summary

***Nasutitermes myersi*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1933; the type locality is Brasil: Roraima: Uraricoera \[3.469N 60.9962W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *myersi*: Named after Myers (British entomologist J. G. Myers)
