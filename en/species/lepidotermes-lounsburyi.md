## Summary

***Lepidotermes lounsburyi*** is a species of the genus *Lepidotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Pretoria. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lepidotermes*: From Greek lepis (scale) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lounsburyi*: Named after C. P. Lounsbury
