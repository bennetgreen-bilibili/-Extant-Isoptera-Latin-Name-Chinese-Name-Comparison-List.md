## Summary

***Odontotermes karawajevi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by John in 1925; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Depok. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *karawajevi*: Named after Vladimir Karawajew
