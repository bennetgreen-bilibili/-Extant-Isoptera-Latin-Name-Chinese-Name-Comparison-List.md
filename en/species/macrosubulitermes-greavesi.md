## Summary

***Macrosubulitermes greavesi*** is the only extant species of the genus *Macrosubulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Gadgarra. Distribution: Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrosubulitermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin subula (awl) + termes.

Specific epithet *greavesi*: Named after Greaves
