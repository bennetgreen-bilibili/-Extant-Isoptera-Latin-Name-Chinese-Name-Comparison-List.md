## Summary

***Cyrilliotermes crassinasus*** is a species of the genus *Cyrilliotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino & Carvalho in 2012; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Guatapará, Horto Florestal de Guarani \[21.42S 48.1W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cyrilliotermes*: Named after a person (Cyrillio); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *crassinasus*: From the descriptive term 'crass' (thick)
