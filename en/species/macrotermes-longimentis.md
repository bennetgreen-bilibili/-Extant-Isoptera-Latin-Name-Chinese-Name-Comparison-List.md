## Summary

***Macrotermes longimentis*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Luo in 1987; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Beihai outskirts. Distribution: Guangxi, China, Beihai.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *longimentis*: From the descriptive term 'long' (long)
