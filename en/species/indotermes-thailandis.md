## Summary

***Indotermes thailandis*** is a species of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1963; the type locality is Thailand: Pitsanulok Province: Tung Sa-Lang Luang National Park. Distribution: Thailand.

Specific epithet *thailandis*: After Thailand
