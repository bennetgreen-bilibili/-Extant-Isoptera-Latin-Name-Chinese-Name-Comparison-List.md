## Summary

***Kalotermes phoeniciae*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ghesini & Marini in 2015; the type locality is Lebanon: Benouaiti. Distribution: eastern Mediterranean coast, Levant.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *phoeniciae*: Named after Phoenicia
