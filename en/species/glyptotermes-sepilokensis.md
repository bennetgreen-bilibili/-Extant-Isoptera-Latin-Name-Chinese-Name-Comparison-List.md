## Summary

***Glyptotermes sepilokensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Sandakan South District: Sepilok Forest Reserve. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sepilokensis*: Named after the locality Sepilok
