## Summary

***Noirotitermes noiroti*** is the only extant species of the genus *Noirotitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Cancello & Myles in 2000; the type locality is Brasil: Piauí: Corrente, Fazenda Maracujá \[10.348S 45.166W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Noirotitermes*: Named after the termite biologist C. Noirot; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *noiroti*: Named after C. Noirot
