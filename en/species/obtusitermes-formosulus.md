## Summary

***Obtusitermes formosulus*** is a species of the genus *Obtusitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cuezzo & Cancello in 2009; the type locality is Venezuela: Sucre: Parque Nacional de Mochima \[10.29N 64.38W\]. Distribution: Venezuela, Panama, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Obtusitermes*: From Latin obtusus (blunt) + termes.

Specific epithet *formosulus*: Latin formosus (beautiful) + -ulus (small), dainty
