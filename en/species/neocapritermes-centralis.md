## Summary

***Neocapritermes centralis*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1932; the type locality is Costa Rica: Santa Clara: Hamburg Farm. Distribution: Panama, Costa Rica.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *centralis*: Latin centralis (central)
