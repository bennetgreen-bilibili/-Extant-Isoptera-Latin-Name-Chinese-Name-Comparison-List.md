## Summary

***Ephelotermes cheeli*** is a species of the genus *Ephelotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Mjöberg in 1920; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Cape York: Hann Creek. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
