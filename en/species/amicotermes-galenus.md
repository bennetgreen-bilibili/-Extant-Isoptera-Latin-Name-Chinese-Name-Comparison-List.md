## Summary

***Amicotermes galenus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Amicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amicotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *galenus*: Named after Galen (the Greek physician)
