## Summary

***Glyptotermes jinyunensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chen & Ping in 1985; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Jinyun Mountain of Chonquing City. Distribution: China (Sichuan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *jinyunensis*: Named after the locality Jinyun
