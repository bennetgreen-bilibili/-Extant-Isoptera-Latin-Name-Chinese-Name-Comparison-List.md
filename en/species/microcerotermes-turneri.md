## Summary

***Microcerotermes turneri*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Mackay. Distribution: Oceania, Australia. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *turneri*: Named after Turner
