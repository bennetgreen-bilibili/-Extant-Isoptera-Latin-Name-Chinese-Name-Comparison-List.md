## Summary

***Incisitermes repandus*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1926; the type locality is Samoa: Upolu Island: Apia. Distribution: Vanuatu, Australia, Fiji, Marshall Islands, Samoa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *repandus*: Latin repandus (with wavy margin)
