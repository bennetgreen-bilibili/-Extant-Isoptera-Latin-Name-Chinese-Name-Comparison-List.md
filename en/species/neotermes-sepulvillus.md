## Summary

***Neotermes sepulvillus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sepulvillus*: From Latin se- (without) + pulvillus (small pad); referring to the lack of a pulvillus
