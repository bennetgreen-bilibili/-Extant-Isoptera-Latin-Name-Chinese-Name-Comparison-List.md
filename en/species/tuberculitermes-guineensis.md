## Summary

***Tuberculitermes guineensis*** is a species of the genus *Tuberculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Kindia; Mamou. Distribution: Cameroon, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tuberculitermes*: From Latin tuberculum (small swelling) + termes.

Specific epithet *guineensis*: After Guinea
