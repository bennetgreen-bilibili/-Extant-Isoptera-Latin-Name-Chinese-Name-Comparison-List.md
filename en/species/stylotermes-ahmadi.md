## Summary

***Stylotermes ahmadi*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Ukhia. Distribution: China, Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ahmadi*: Named after M. Ahmad
