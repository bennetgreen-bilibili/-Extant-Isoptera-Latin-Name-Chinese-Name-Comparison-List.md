## Summary

***Hydrecotermes kawaii*** is a species of the genus *Hydrecotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2016; the type locality is French Guiana: Nouragues Inselberg station \[ 4.0884N 52.6814W \]. Distribution: Colombia, French Guiana, Brazil, Argentina, Bolivia, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hydrecotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kawaii*: Named after Kawai (Japanese)
