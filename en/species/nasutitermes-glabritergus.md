## Summary

***Nasutitermes glabritergus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder & Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Costa Rica: Colombiana. Distribution: Costa Rica, Panama, Guatemala, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *glabritergus*: From the descriptive term 'glabr' (smooth)
