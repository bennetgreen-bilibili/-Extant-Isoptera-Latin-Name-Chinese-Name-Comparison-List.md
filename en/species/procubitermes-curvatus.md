## Summary

***Procubitermes curvatus*** is a species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne. Distribution: Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procubitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *curvatus*: From curv + atus
