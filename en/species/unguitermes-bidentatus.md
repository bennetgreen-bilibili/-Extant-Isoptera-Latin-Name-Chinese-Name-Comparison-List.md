## Summary

***Unguitermes bidentatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Unguitermes*: From Latin unguis (claw) + termes.

Specific epithet *bidentatus*: Latin bi- (two) + dentatus (toothed), two-toothed
