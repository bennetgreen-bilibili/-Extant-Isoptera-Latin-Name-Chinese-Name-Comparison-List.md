## Summary

***Glyptotermes rotundifrons*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rotundifrons*: From rotundi + frons
