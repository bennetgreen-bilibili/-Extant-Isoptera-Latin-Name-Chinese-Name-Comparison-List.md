## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes sanctaecrucis*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Solomon Islands: Santa Cruz Archipelago: Graciosa Bay. Distribution: Solomon Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sanctaecrucis*: Latin sancta (holy) + crux (cross), holy cross
