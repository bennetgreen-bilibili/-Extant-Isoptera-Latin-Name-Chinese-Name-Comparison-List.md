## Summary

***Coptotermes premrasmii*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Ka-Chong. Distribution: Thailand, Nicobar Islands (India).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *premrasmii*: Named after Premrasmi
