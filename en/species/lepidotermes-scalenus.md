## Summary

***Lepidotermes scalenus*** is a species of the genus *Lepidotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Uys in 1994; the type locality is South Africa: Cape Province: top of Hells Kloof Pass, 25 km ex AnnisfonteinBlackies Prospect. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lepidotermes*: From Greek lepis (scale) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *scalenus*: Greek skalēnos (uneven, scalene)
