## Summary

***Procubitermes wasmanni*** is a species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Cameroon: Bipindi. Distribution: Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procubitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *wasmanni*: Named after the entomologist E. Wasmann
