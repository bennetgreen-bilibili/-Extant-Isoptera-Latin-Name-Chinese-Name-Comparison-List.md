## Summary

***Neocapritermes talpoides*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna & Araujo in 1968; the type locality is Ecuador: Morona-Santiago: Rio Zamora \[ 3.51333S 78.5219W \]. Distribution: Colombia, Brazil, Bolivia, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *talpoides*: Latin talpa (mole) + Greek -oides (like), mole-like
