## Summary

***Nasutitermes garoensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Assam: Garo Hills District: Rongrengiri. Distribution: Bhutan, China, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *garoensis*: Named after the locality Garo
