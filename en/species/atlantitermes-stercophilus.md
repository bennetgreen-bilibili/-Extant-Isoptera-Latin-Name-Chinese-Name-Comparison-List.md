## Summary

***Atlantitermes stercophilus*** is a species of the genus *Atlantitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino & DeSouza in 1997; the type locality is Brasil: Minas Gerais: Rio Paranaíba \[19.2S 46.25W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Atlantitermes*: From the Atlantic + termes.

Specific epithet *stercophilus*: Greek sterko (dung) + philos (loving), dung-loving
