## Summary

***Alyscotermes trestus*** is a species of the genus *Alyscotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Kenya: Narok District: Mara, 9 miles from Olokurto. Distribution: Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Alyscotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *trestus*: From Greek τρητός (trētos) 'perforated, having a hole in it' (LSJ, from τετραίνω tetrainō 'to bore'), Latinized as trestus, perhaps referring to the spiny enteric valve armature of the worker caste.
