## Summary

***Pericapritermes urgens confinis*** is a subspecies of *Pericapritermes urgens*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae), genus *Pericapritermes*. The subspecies was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Kakoulima; Kindia. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Pericapritermes urgens* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *confinis*: Latin confinis (adjoining, neighbouring)
