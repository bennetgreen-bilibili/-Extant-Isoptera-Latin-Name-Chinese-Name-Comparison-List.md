## Summary

***Macrotermes barneyi*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1924; the type locality is China: Hong Kong: Hong Kong University, China: Amoy Harbour, China: near Kuliang, in the mountains near Foochow. Distribution: East Asia, China, Hong Kong, China, Macau, China, Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *barneyi*: Named after Barney
