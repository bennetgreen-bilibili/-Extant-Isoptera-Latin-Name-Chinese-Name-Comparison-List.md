## Summary

***Lacessititermes thailandicus*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sen-Sarma in 1966; the type locality is Thailand: Chanthaburi. Distribution: Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *thailandicus*: Named after the locality Thailand
