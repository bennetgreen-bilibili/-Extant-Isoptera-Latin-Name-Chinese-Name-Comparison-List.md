## Summary

***Ateuchotermes sentosus*** is a species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Nigeria: Port Harcourt. Distribution: Cameroon, Congo, Guinea, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ateuchotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + teuchos (implement, weapon) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sentosus*: Latin sentosus (thorny)
