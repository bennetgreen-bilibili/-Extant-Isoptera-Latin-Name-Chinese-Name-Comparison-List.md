## Summary

***Incisitermes pacificus*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Banks in 1901; the type locality is Ecuador: Galápagos: Isla Fernandina (Narborough) \[ 0.39S 91.517W \]; Galápagos: Isla Isabela (Albemarle) \[ 0.83S 91.11W \]. Distribution: Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *pacificus*: From Latin pacificus, of the Pacific
