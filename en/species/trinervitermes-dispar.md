## Summary

***Trinervitermes dispar*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1902; the type locality is Malawi: Zomba. Distribution: Eritrea, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Namibia, South Africa, Eswatini, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 7 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *dispar*: From the descriptive term 'dispar' (unequal)
