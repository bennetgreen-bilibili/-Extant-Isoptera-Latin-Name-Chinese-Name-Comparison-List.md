## Summary

***Pseudacanthotermes piceus*** is a species of the genus *Pseudacanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Zimbabwe: Harare (Salisbury). Distribution: Uganda, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudacanthotermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *piceus*: Latin piceus (pitch-black)
