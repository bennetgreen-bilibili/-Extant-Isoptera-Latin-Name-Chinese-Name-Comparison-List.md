## Summary

***Cyranotermes karipuna*** is a species of the genus *Cyranotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Rocha, Carrijo & Cancello in 2012; the type locality is Brasil: Rondônia: Porto Velho, Jaci Paraná \[8.76194S 63.9039W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cyranotermes*: From Cyrano (de Bergerac, famed for his long nose) + termes, for the long nasus.

Specific epithet *karipuna*: After the Karipuna people (Brazil)
