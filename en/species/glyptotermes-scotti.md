## Summary

***Glyptotermes scotti*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Seychelles: Silhouette. Distribution: Seychelles.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *scotti*: Named after Hugh Scott (British entomologist)
