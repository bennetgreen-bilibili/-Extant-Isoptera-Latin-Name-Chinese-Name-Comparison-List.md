## Summary

***Incisitermes fruticavus*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Rust in 1979; the type locality is United States: California, 1.1 mi N Aguanga. Distribution: United States.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *fruticavus*: Latin frutex (shrub) + cavus (hollow), shrub-hollow
