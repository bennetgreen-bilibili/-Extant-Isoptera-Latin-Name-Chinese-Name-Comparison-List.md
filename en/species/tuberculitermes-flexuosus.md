## Summary

***Tuberculitermes flexuosus*** is a species of the genus *Tuberculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Roy-Noël in 1969; the type locality is Senegal: Niokolo-Koba region, near pool of Siminti. Distribution: West Africa, Burkina Faso.

## Etymology

Genus name *Tuberculitermes*: From Latin tuberculum (small swelling) + termes.

Specific epithet *flexuosus*: From flexu + osus
