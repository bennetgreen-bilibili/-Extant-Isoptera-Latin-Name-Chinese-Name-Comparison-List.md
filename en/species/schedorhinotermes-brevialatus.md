## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes brevialatus*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Sarawak, Malaysia, Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brevialatus*: From brevial + atus
