## Summary

***Cubitermes tenuiceps*** is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Lubumbashi. Distribution: Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *tenuiceps*: From tenui + ceps
