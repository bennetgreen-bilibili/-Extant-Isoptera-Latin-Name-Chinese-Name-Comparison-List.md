## Summary

***Microtermes somaliensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1912; the type locality is Somalia: Bardera. Distribution: East Africa, Somalia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *somaliensis*: Named after Somalia
