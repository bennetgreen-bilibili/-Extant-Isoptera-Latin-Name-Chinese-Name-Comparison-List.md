## Summary

***Glyptotermes latithorax*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Fan & Xia in 1980; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Nada. Distribution: China (Guangdong).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latithorax*: From lati + thorax
