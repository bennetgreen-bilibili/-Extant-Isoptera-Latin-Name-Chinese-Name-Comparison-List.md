## Summary

***Odontotermes aurora*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Tanzania: Zanzibar. Distribution: East Africa, Tanzania, Zanzibar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *aurora*: From the descriptive term 'aur' (gold)
