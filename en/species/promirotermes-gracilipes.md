## Summary

***Promirotermes gracilipes*** is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Schmitz in 1917; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *gracilipes*: From gracili + pes
