## Summary

***Promirotermes orthoceps*** is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Banana. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *orthoceps*: From ortho + ceps
