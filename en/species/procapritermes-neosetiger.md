## Summary

***Procapritermes neosetiger*** is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Sandakan North: Kolapis Forest. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procapritermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *neosetiger*: From neoseti + ger
