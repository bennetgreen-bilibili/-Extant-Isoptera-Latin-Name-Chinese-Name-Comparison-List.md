## Summary

***Nasutitermes maheensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Seychelles: Longue Island; Mahé. Distribution: Seychelles.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *maheensis*: Named after the locality Mahe
