## Summary

***Rugitermes ursulae*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Casalla, Scheffrahn & Korb in 2021; the type locality is Colombia: Sucre: Colosó \[ 9.5435N 75.3488W \]. Distribution: Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *ursulae*: Named after Ursula
