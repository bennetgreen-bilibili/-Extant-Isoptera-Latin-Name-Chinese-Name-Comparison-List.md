## Summary

***Isognathotermes modicus*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Josens & Deligne in 2025; the type locality is Gabon: Forêt des Abeilles \[ 0.3167S 12.1167E \]. Distribution: Gabon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *modicus*: Latin modicus (moderate, of modest size)
