## Summary

***Microtermes lepidus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Senegal: Dakar. Distribution: West Africa, Senegal, Burkina Faso, Gambia, Mali.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *lepidus*: Latin lepidus (charming)
