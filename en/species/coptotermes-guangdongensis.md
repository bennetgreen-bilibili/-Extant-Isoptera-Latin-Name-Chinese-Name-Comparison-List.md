## Summary

***Coptotermes guangdongensis*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Guangzhou. Distribution: China (Guangdong). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *guangdongensis*: Named after the locality Guangdong
