## Summary

***Cylindrotermes flangiatus*** is a species of the genus *Cylindrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cylindrotermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]. Distribution: Neotropics.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cylindrotermes*: From Greek kylindros (cylinder) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *flangiatus*: flange + Latin -atus, flanged (low confidence)
