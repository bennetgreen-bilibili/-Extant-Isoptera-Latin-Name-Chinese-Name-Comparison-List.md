## Summary

***Microtermes obesi*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1912; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Khandala. Distribution: South Asia, Southeast Asia, India, Pakistan, Thailand, Cambodia, Iran. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *obesi*: From Latin obesus (fat, plump)
