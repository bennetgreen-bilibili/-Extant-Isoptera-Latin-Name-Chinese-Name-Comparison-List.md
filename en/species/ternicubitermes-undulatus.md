## Summary

***Ternicubitermes undulatus*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Pretoria. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *undulatus*: From undul + atus
