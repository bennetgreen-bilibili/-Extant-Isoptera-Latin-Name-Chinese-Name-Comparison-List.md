## Summary

***Cryptotermes primus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hill in 1921; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Townsville. Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *primus*: Latin primus (first)
