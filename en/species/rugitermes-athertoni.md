## Summary

***Rugitermes athertoni*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1932; the type locality is Polynesia (French): Marquesas: Hivaoa: Atuona Valley; Marquesas: Hivaoa: Pouau; Marquesas: Hivaoa: Tenaeka Valley; Marquesas: Nukahiva, Toovi, Teuanui; Marquesas: Nukuhiva, Tapuaooa; Marquesas: Uapou, Hakahetau Valley, Koputukea. Distribution: French Polynesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *athertoni*: Named after Atherton
