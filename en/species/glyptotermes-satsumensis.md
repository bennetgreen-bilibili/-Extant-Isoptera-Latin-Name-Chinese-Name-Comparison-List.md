## Summary

***Glyptotermes satsumensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Matsumura in 1907; the type locality is Japan: Kagoshima \[ 31.9N 130.45E \]. Distribution: Japan, China (Taiwan). This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *satsumensis*: Named after Satsuma (Japan)
