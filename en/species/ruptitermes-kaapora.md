## Summary

***Ruptitermes kaapora*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Acioli & Constantino in 2015; the type locality is Paraguay: Boquerón: La Patria, Parque Nacional Teniente Enciso \[ 21.2069S 61.6569W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *kaapora*: Caapora, a Tupi forest spirit
