## Summary

***Nasutitermes banksi*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Potaro-Siparuni: Tukeit \[5.2N 59.45W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Guyana, Bolivia, Peru. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *banksi*: Named after N. Banks
