## Summary

***Heterotermes paradoxus*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Mackay. Distribution: Australia, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paradoxus*: Greek paradoxos (paradoxical, strange)
