## Summary

***Cryptotermes declivis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Guangdong: Chaochow. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *declivis*: From the descriptive term 'decliv' (declivous)
