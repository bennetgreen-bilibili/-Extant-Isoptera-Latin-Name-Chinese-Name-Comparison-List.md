## Summary

***Termes panamaensis*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1923; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Colombia, Panama, Ecuador, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Mexico, Nicaragua, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela, United States Virgin Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *panamaensis*: Named after the locality Panama
