## Summary

***Ceylonitermes escherichi*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Ceylonitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ceylonitermes*: From Ceylon (Sri Lanka) + termes.

Specific epithet *escherichi*: Named after K. Escherich
