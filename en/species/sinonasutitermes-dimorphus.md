## Summary

***Sinonasutitermes dimorphus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Sinonasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li in 1986; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Nanxiong County: Maozifeng Mt. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinonasutitermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + nasutus (nasute) + termes.

Specific epithet *dimorphus*: Greek di- (two) + morphē (form), dimorphic
