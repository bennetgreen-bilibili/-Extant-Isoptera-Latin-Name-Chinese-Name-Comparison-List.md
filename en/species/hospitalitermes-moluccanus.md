## Summary

***Hospitalitermes moluccanus*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1947; the type locality is Indonesia: Maluku (Moluccas): Waigu Island. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *moluccanus*: Named after the locality Molucc
