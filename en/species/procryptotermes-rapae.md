## Summary

***Procryptotermes rapae*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light & Zimmerman in 1936; the type locality is Polynesia (French): Rapa Iti. Distribution: French Polynesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *rapae*: Named after the locality Rapa (French Polynesia)
