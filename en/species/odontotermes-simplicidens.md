## Summary

***Odontotermes simplicidens*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Cameroon: Ekundu. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Gabon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *simplicidens*: Latin simplex (simple) + dens (tooth), simple-toothed
