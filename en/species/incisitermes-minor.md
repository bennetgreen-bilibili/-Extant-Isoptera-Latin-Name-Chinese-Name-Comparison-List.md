## Summary

***Incisitermes minor*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is United States: California: San Diego. Distribution: western United States, northern Mexico; introduced: Canada, China, Hawaii (United States), Italy, Japan. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *minor*: Latin minor (smaller)
