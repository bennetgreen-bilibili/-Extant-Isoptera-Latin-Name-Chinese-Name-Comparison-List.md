## Summary

***Odontotermes interveniens*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Namibia: Okahandja. Distribution: southern Africa, Namibia, Angola.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *interveniens*: Latin interveniens (intervening)
