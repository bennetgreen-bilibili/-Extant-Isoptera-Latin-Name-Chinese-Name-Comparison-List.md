## Summary

***Microcerotermes losbanosensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1914; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Los Banos. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *losbanosensis*: Named after Los Baños, Philippines
