## Summary

***Microtermes pallidiventris*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1953; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Lubumbashi. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *pallidiventris*: From the descriptive term 'pallid' (pale)
