## Summary

***Odontotermes fulleri*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Niangara. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fulleri*: Named after C. Fuller
