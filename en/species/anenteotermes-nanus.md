## Summary

***Anenteotermes nanus*** is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Cameroon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nanus*: Greek nanos (dwarf)
