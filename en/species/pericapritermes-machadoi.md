## Summary

***Pericapritermes machadoi*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Noirot in 1955; the type locality is Angola: Hoque. Distribution: Angola.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *machadoi*: Named after Machado (Portuguese entomologist A. de Barros Machado)
