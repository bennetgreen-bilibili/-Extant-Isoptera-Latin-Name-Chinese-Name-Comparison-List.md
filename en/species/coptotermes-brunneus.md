## Summary

***Coptotermes brunneus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gay in 1955; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia, 51 mi NNW Galena. Distribution: western Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *brunneus*: Latin brunneus (brown)
