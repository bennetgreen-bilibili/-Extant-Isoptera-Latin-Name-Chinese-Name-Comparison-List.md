## Summary

***Apicotermes lamani*** is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Congo: Madzia-District, 55 km west of Brazzaville. Distribution: Angola, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Apicotermes*: From Latin apex (tip, summit) + termes.

Specific epithet *lamani*: Named after Laman
