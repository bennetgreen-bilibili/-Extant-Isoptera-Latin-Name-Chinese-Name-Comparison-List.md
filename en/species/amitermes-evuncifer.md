## Summary

***Amitermes evuncifer*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama: Rio Cassine. Distribution: West Africa, Central Africa, East Africa. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *evuncifer*: Latin e- (out) + uncus (hook) + -fer (bearing), hooked
