## Summary

***Eremotermes senegalensis*** is a species of the genus *Eremotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Mampouya, Fleck & Nel in 1997; the type locality is Senegal: Richard-Toll County: Richard-Toll, 376 km North of Dakar. Distribution: Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eremotermes*: From Greek erēmos (desert, solitary) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *senegalensis*: Named after the locality Senegal
