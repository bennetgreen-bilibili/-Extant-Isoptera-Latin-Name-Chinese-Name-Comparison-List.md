## Summary

***Neotermes amplilabralis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xu & Han in 1985; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Heko. Distribution: Yunnan (Hekou).

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *amplilabralis*: From ampli + labralis
