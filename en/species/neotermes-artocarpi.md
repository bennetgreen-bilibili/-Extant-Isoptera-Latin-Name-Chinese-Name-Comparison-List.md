## Summary

***Neotermes artocarpi*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia, Sarawak, Indonesia, Myanmar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *artocarpi*: After the plant genus Artocarpus
