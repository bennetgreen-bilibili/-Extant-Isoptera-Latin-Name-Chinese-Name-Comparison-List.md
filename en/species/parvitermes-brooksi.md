## Summary

***Parvitermes brooksi*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Cuba: Cienfuegos: Soledad. Distribution: Cuba, Bahamas.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parvitermes*: From Latin parvus (small) + termes.

Specific epithet *brooksi*: Named after Brooks
