## Summary

***Emersonitermes thekadensis*** is the only extant species of the genus *Emersonitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathur & Sen-Sarma in 1959; the type locality is India: Kerala (Travancore): Thekaday. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Emersonitermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *thekadensis*: Named after Thekadi, India
