## Summary

***Odontotermes agilis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Liberia: Monrovia, Liberia: Monrovia. Distribution: West Africa, Liberia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *agilis*: From the descriptive term 'agil' (agile)
