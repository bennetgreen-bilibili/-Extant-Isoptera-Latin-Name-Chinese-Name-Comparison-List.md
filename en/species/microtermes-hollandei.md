## Summary

***Microtermes hollandei*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé in 1937; the type locality is Senegal: Dakar. Distribution: West Africa, Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *hollandei*: Named after André Hollande
