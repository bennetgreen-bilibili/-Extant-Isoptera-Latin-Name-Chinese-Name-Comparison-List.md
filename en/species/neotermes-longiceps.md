## Summary

***Neotermes longiceps*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Xu & Han in 1985; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Dongfang. Distribution: China, Hainan, Hong Kong.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longiceps*: Latin longus (long) + -ceps (head), long-headed
