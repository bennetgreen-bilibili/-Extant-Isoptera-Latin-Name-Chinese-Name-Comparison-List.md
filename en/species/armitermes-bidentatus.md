## Summary

***Armitermes bidentatus*** is a species of the genus *Armitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2012; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Cristianópolis \[17.1992S 48.7058W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Armitermes*: From Latin arma (arms, weapons) + termes.

Specific epithet *bidentatus*: Latin bi- (two) + dentatus (toothed), two-toothed
