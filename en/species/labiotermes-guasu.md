## Summary

***Labiotermes guasu*** is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino & Acioli in 2006; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Benjamin Constant, Nova Aliança \[4.35S 69.6W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiotermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *guasu*: Tupi -guasu (large)
