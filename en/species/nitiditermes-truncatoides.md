## Summary

***Nitiditermes truncatoides*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Mbazwane Swamp \[approx. 27°30?S, 32°20?E\]. Distribution: South Africa. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *truncatoides*: From truncat + oides
