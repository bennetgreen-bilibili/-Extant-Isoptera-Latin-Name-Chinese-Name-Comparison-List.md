## Summary

***Glyptotermes kunakensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Kunak-Mostyn District: Kunak. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kunakensis*: Named after Kunak (Sabah, Malaysia)
