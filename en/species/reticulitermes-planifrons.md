## Summary

***Reticulitermes planifrons*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li & Ping in 1981; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Huanggang Shan, Chong’ an Xian. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *planifrons*: Latin planus (flat) + frons (front), flat-fronted
