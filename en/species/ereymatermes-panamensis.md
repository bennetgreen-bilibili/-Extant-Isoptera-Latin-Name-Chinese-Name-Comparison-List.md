## Summary

***Ereymatermes panamensis*** is a species of the genus *Ereymatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roisin in 1995; the type locality is Panama: Barro Colorado Island. Distribution: Central America, Panama.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ereymatermes*: From the place name Ereyma; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *panamensis*: Named after the locality Panam
