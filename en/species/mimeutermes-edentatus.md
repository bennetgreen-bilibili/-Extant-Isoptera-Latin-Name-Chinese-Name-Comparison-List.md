## Summary

***Mimeutermes edentatus*** is a species of the genus *Mimeutermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1956; the type locality is Ghana: Accra. Distribution: Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mimeutermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *edentatus*: Latin e- (without) + dentatus (toothed), toothless
