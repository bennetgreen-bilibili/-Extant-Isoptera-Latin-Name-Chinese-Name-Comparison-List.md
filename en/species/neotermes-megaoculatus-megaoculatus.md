## Summary

***Neotermes megaoculatus megaoculatus*** is a subspecies of *Neotermes megaoculatus*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, genus *Neotermes*. The subspecies was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Neotermes megaoculatus* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *megaoculatus*: From megaocul + atus
