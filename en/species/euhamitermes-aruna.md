## Summary

***Euhamitermes aruna*** is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani in 1975; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: Kameng Division: Charduar Forest, towards Bhalukpong. Distribution: Oriental region.

## Etymology

Genus name *Euhamitermes*: From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.

Specific epithet *aruna*: Named after Aruna
