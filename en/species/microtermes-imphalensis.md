## Summary

***Microtermes imphalensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Manipur: Imphal Valley. Distribution: South Asia, Manipur, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *imphalensis*: Named after the locality Imphal
