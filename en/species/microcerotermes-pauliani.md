## Summary

***Microcerotermes pauliani*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2013; the type locality is Madagascar: Ambatofinandrahana (Ambositra); Behara. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pauliani*: Named after Paulian (French entomologist Renaud Paulian)
