## Summary

***Odontotermes horni*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Nalanda. Distribution: Old World tropics, India, Sri Lanka, Nigeria, Bhutan, Cambodia, Vietnam. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *horni*: Named after Walther Horn
