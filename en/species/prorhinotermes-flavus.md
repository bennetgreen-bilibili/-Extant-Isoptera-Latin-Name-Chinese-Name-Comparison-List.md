## Summary

***Prorhinotermes flavus*** is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bugnion & Popoff in 1910; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Ambalangoda. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, South Asia, India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Taiwan. This site records 9 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Prorhinotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *flavus*: Latin flavus (yellow)
