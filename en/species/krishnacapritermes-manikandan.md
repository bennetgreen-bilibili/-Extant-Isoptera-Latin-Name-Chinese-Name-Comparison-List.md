## Summary

***Krishnacapritermes manikandan*** is a species of the genus *Krishnacapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Amina & Rajmohana in 2019; the type locality is India: Kerala: Idukki (Amar shola-Munnar). Distribution: India, Kerala.

## Etymology

Genus name *Krishnacapritermes*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *manikandan*: Named after a person (Manikandan)
