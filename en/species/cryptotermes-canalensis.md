## Summary

***Cryptotermes canalensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1915; the type locality is New Caledonia (French): Canala. Distribution: New Caledonia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *canalensis*: Named after the Canal Zone (Panama)
