## Summary

***Amitermes aporema*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1993; the type locality is Brasil: Amapá: Aporema \[ 1.233N 50.78W \]. Distribution: Brazil. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.
