## Summary

***Microcerotermes bugnioni*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Seenigoda Estate, near Ambalangoda. Distribution: South Asia, Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bugnioni*: Named after E. Bugnion
