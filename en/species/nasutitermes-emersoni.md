## Summary

***Nasutitermes emersoni*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1934; the type locality is Bangladesh: Sundarbans. Distribution: India, Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *emersoni*: Named after the termite taxonomist A. E. Emerson
