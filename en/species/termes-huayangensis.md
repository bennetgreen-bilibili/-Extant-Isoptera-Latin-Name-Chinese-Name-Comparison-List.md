## Summary

***Termes huayangensis*** is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Huay Yang. Distribution: Guangdong, China, Huayang, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Termes*: From Latin termes (woodworm, termite), the type genus.

Specific epithet *huayangensis*: Named after the locality Huayang
