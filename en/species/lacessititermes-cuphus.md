## Summary

***Lacessititermes cuphus*** is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Bathellier in 1927; the type locality is Vietnam: Nhatrang, Cana region. Distribution: Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Lacessititermes*: From Latin lacessitus (provoked, attacked) + termes.

Specific epithet *cuphus*: From Greek kyphos ('bent, humped')
