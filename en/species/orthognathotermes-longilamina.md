## Summary

***Orthognathotermes longilamina*** is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Rocha & Cancello in 2009; the type locality is Brasil: Espírito Santo: Linhares \[ 19.3911S 40.0722W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Orthognathotermes*: From Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longilamina*: From the descriptive term 'long' (long)
