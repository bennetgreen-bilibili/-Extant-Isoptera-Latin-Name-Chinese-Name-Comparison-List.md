## Summary

***Promirotermes bechuana*** is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is South Africa: Greefdale. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *bechuana*: After Bechuanaland (Botswana) / the Tswana people
