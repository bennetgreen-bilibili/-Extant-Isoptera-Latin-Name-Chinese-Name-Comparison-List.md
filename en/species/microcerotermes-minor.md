## Summary

***Microcerotermes minor*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Maha-Iluppalama. Distribution: India, Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minor*: Latin minor (smaller)
