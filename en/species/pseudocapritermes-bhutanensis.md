## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes bhutanensis*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1977; the type locality is Bhutan: Thimpu, 2300–2500 m. Distribution: Bhutan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *bhutanensis*: Named after the locality Bhutan
