## Summary

***Odontotermes foveafrons*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Xia & Fan in 1982; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Yangwu. Distribution: Yunnan (Damenglong, Ganlanba, Simao), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *foveafrons*: From fovea + frons
