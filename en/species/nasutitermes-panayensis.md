## Summary

***Nasutitermes panayensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1920; the type locality is Philippines: Panay: Antique Province: Culasi. Distribution: Philippines.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *panayensis*: Named after the locality Panay
