## Summary

***Duplidentitermes jurioni*** is a species of the genus *Duplidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1959; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Yangambi. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Duplidentitermes*: From Latin duplex (double) + dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *jurioni*: Named after Jurion
