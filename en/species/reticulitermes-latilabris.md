## Summary

***Reticulitermes latilabris*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1986; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Jinxiu, Dayaoshan. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *latilabris*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
