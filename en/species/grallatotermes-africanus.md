## Summary

***Grallatotermes africanus*** is a species of the genus *Grallatotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga District: Ngomeni. Distribution: Mozambique, Tanzania, Kenya.

## Etymology

Genus name *Grallatotermes*: From Latin grallator (stilt-walker) + termes.

Specific epithet *africanus*: After Africa
