## Summary

***Odontotermes javanicus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1912; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Salak, Buitenzorg. Distribution: Southeast Asia, East Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *javanicus*: Named after the locality Javan
