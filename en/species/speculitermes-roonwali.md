## Summary

***Speculitermes roonwali*** is a species of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Maiti in 1983; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Midnapore: Keranitola. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Speculitermes*: From Latin speculum (mirror) + termes.

Specific epithet *roonwali*: Named after the termite taxonomist M. L. Roonwal
