## Summary

***Stylotermes latilabrum*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Cheli. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latilabrum*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
