## Summary

***Neotermes zuluensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Lake Sibayi. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zuluensis*: Named after the locality Zulu
