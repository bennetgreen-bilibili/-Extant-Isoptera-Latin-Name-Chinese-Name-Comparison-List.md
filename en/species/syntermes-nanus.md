## Summary

***Syntermes nanus*** is a species of the genus *Syntermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1995; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Pirassununga \[21.98S 47.42W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Syntermes*: From Greek syn- (together) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nanus*: Greek nanos (dwarf)
