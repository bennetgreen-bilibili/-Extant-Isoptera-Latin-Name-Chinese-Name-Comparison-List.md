## Summary

***Cryptotermes contognathus*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Constantino in 2000; the type locality is Brasil: Espírito Santo: Presidente Kennedy, Praia das Neves \[ 21.28S 40.97W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *contognathus*: Greek kontos (short) + gnathos (jaw), short-jawed
