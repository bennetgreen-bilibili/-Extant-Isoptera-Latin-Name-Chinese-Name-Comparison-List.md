## Summary

***Odontotermes adampurensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar in 1975; the type locality is Bangladesh: Adampur. Distribution: South Asia, India, Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *adampurensis*: Named after the locality Adampur
