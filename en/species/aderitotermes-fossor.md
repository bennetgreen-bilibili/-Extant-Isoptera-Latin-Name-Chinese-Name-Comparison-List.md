## Summary

***Aderitotermes fossor*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Aderitotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Uganda: Kampala: Makerere. Distribution: Cameroon, Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Aderitotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fossor*: Latin fossor (digger)
