## Summary

***Nasutitermes longirostris longirostris*** is a subspecies of *Nasutitermes longirostris*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), genus *Nasutitermes*. The subspecies was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Nasutitermes longirostris* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *longirostris*: From longi + rostris
