## Summary

***Nasutitermes eucalypti*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mjöberg in 1920; the type locality is Australia: Western Australia: Kimberley District. Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *eucalypti*: After the plant genus Eucalyptus
