## Summary

***Cryptotermes mangoldi*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Dominican Republic: La Altagracia Province, 0.5 km W Club Mediterraneo. Distribution: Dominican Republic, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *mangoldi*: Named after Mangold
