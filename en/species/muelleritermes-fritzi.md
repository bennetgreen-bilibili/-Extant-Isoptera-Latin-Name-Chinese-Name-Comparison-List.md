## Summary

***Muelleritermes fritzi*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Muelleritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cancello, Oliveira & Rocha in 2015; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Salesópolis, Estação Biológica de Boraceia \[23.63S 45.86W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Muelleritermes*: Named after a person (Müller); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fritzi*: Named after Fritz
