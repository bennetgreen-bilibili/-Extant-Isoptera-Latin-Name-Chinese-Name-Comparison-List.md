## Summary

***Neotermes mona*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Banks in 1919; the type locality is Puerto Rico: Mona Island. Distribution: Caribbean, Puerto Rico, Bahamas, Turks and Caicos Islands, Dominican Republic, Haiti, British Virgin Islands, U.S. Virgin Islands, Mexico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mona*: Named after Mona Island (Puerto Rico)
