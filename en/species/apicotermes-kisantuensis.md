## Summary

***Apicotermes kisantuensis*** is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Inkisi-Kisantu. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Apicotermes*: From Latin apex (tip, summit) + termes.

Specific epithet *kisantuensis*: Named after the locality Kisantu
