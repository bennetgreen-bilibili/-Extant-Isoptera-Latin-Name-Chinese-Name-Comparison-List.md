## Summary

***Glyptotermes adamsoni*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: Tunapuna-Piarco: Near Arima \[ 10.6667N 61.3333W \]. Distribution: Grenada, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *adamsoni*: Named after Adamson
