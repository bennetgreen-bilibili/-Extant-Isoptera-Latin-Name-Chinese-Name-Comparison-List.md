## Summary

***Microcerotermes propinquus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1910; the type locality is Madagascar: Europa Island. Distribution: Madagascar, Réunion, Europa Island (France).

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *propinquus*: Latin propinquus (near, neighbouring)
