## Summary

***Constrictotermes guantanamensis*** is a species of the genus *Constrictotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Krecek, Scheffrahn & Roisin in 1996; the type locality is Cuba: Guantanamo: Loma de Macambo. Distribution: Caribbean, Cuba.

## Etymology

Genus name *Constrictotermes*: From Latin constrictus (constricted) + termes.

Specific epithet *guantanamensis*: Named after the locality Guantanam
