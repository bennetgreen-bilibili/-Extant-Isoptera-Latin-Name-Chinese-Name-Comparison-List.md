## Summary

***Odontotermes ziyangensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Zhang & Xing in 1992; the type locality is China: Shaanxi Province: Ziyang County (Santai). Distribution: China (Shaanxi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ziyangensis*: Named after the locality Ziyang
