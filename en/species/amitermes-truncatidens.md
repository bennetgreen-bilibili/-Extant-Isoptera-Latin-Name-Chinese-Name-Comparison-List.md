## Summary

***Amitermes truncatidens*** is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1959; the type locality is Tanzania: Nachingwea. Distribution: West Africa, Nigeria, Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Amitermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *truncatidens*: From the descriptive term 'trunc' (truncated)
