## Summary

***Microcerotermes biswanathae*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1994; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: South Andaman Island, Port Blair, Radio Colony. Distribution: South Asia, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *biswanathae*: Named after the locality Biswanath
