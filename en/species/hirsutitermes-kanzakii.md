## Summary

***Hirsutitermes kanzakii*** is the only extant species of the genus *Hirsutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn, Carrijo & Castro in 2023; the type locality is Brasil: Rondonia: Porto Velho, Mutum-Paraná \[ 9.4513S 64.8439W \]. Distribution: Colombia, French Guiana, Bolivia, Brazil, Ecuador, Peru, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hirsutitermes*: From Latin hirsutus (hairy) + termes.

Specific epithet *kanzakii*: Named after Kanzaki
