## Summary

***Procryptotermes dioscurae*** is a species of the genus *Procryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Harris in 1954; the type locality is Yemen: Socotra; Bajin. Distribution: Socotra, Yemen.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procryptotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dioscurae*: Named after Dioscurides (ancient name of Socotra)
