## Summary

***Protermes minutus*** is a species of the genus *Protermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé in 1937; the type locality is Ivory Coast: near Abidjan; near Bingerville. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Protermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *minutus*: Latin minutus (tiny)
