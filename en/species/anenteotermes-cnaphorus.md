## Summary

***Anenteotermes cnaphorus*** is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribution: Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anenteotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cnaphorus*: Greek knaphos (wool) + -phoros (bearing)
