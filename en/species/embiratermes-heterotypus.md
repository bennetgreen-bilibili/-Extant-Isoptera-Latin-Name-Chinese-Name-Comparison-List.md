## Summary

***Embiratermes heterotypus*** is a species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Paraguay: Alto Paraná: Hernandarias (formerly Tacurú Pucú) \[25.3667S 54.6833W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Embiratermes*: From Tupi Embirá (a tree) + -termes.

Specific epithet *heterotypus*: From the descriptive term 'heter' (different)
