## Summary

***Odontotermes latericius*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Estcourt District. Distribution: Old World tropics (West Africa), East Africa, southern Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *latericius*: From the descriptive term 'lat' (broad)
