## Summary

***Neotermes viraktamathi*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ranjith & Kalleshwaraswamy in 2022; the type locality is India: Karnataka: Hebri-Karkala Rd \[ 13.4428N 75.0183E \]. Distribution: India, Karnataka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *viraktamathi*: Named after C. A. Viraktamath (Indian entomologist)
