## Summary

***Glyptotermes sensarmai*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti in 1976; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Koch Bihar Distric: Coochbehar Forest Range, Antiamochar Beat, Khagribari. Distribution: India, Bangladesh.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sensarmai*: Named after P.K. Sen-Sarma
