## Summary

***Trinervitermes rhodesiensis*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Zimbabwe: Harare (Salisbury). Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Angola, Botswana, Central Africa, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Namibia, South Africa, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 10 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *rhodesiensis*: Named after the locality Rhodes
