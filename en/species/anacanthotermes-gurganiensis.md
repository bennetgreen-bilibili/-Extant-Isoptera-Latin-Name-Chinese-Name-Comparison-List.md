## Summary

***Anacanthotermes gurganiensis*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ravan & Akhtar in 1999; the type locality is Iran: Gurgan, Tappay Mirza Biayl. Distribution: Iran.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *gurganiensis*: Named after Gurgan (Gorgan, Iran)
