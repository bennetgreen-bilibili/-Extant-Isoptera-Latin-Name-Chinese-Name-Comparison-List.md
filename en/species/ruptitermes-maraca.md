## Summary

***Ruptitermes maraca*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Acioli & Constantino in 2015; the type locality is Brasil: Roraima: Ilha de Maracá \[ 3.4N 61.65W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *maraca*: Tupi maracá (rattle)
