## Summary

***Embiratermes snyderi*** is a species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson & Banks in 1957; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Oko River \[6.46667N 58.8667W\]. Distribution: French Guiana, Guyana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Embiratermes*: From Tupi Embirá (a tree) + -termes.

Specific epithet *snyderi*: Named after the termite taxonomist T. E. Snyder
