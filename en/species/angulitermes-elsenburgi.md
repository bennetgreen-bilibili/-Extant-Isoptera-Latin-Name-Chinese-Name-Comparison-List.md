## Summary

***Angulitermes elsenburgi*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Cape Town: Elsenburg. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *elsenburgi*: Named after the locality Elsenburg
