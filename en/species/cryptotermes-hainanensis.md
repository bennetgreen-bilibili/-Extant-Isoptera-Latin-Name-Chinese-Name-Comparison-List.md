## Summary

***Cryptotermes hainanensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1987; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Ya. Distribution: China (Hainan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *hainanensis*: After Hainan (China)
