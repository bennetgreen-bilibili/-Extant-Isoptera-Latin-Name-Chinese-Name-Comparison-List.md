## Summary

***Basidentitermes malelaensis*** is a species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Malela. Distribution: Africa, Congo, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Ghana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Basidentitermes*: From Greek basis (base) + Latin dens (tooth) + termes.

Specific epithet *malelaensis*: Named after Malela, DR Congo
