## Summary

***Labiotermes brevilabius*** is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson & Banks in 1965; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Novo Horizonte \[21.4681S 49.2208W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Labiotermes*: From Latin labium (lip) + termes.

Specific epithet *brevilabius*: From the descriptive term 'brev' (short)
