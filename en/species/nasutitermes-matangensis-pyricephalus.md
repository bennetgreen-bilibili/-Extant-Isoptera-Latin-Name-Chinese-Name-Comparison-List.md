## Summary

***Nasutitermes matangensis pyricephalus*** is a subspecies of *Nasutitermes matangensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), genus *Nasutitermes*. The subspecies was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Depok; Java: Palabouhanratou; Krakatau; Timanjo; Wijnkoopsbaai. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Nasutitermes matangensis* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *pyricephalus*: From the descriptive term 'pyr' (fire)
