## Summary

***Glyptotermes simaoensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Li in 1987; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Simao. Distribution: China (Yunnan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *simaoensis*: Named after the locality Simao
