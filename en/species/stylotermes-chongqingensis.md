## Summary

***Stylotermes chongqingensis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Chen & Ping in 1983; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Jinyuan Shan, Chongqing Shi. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *chongqingensis*: Named after the locality Chongqing
