## Summary

***Angulitermes ceylonicus*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Maha-Iluppalama. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *ceylonicus*: After Ceylon (Sri Lanka)
