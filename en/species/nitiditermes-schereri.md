## Summary

***Nitiditermes schereri*** is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Rosen in 1912; the type locality is Liberia: Cape Mesurado. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Sierra Leone, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nitiditermes*: From Latin nitidus (shining) + termes.

Specific epithet *schereri*: Named after a person "Scherer" (identity unconfirmed)
