## Summary

***Odontotermes magdalenae*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé & Noirot in 1950; the type locality is Chad: Télémé, near Bongor. Distribution: West Africa, Nigeria, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Guinea, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *magdalenae*: Named after the Magdalena River / locality
