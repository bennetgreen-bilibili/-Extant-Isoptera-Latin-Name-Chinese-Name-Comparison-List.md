## Summary

***Coptotermes formosanus*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Shiraki in 1909; the type locality is Taiwan: Taipei. Distribution: southern China and Taiwan, native, widely distributed in the tropics and subtropics through the timber trade. This site records 17 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *formosanus*: Named after Formosa (Taiwan, former name)
