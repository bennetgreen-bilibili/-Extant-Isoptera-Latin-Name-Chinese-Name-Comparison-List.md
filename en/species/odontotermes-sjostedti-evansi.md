## Summary

***Odontotermes sjostedti evansi*** is a subspecies of *Odontotermes sjostedti*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Odontotermes*. The subspecies was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Cameroon: Senegambina. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Odontotermes sjostedti* below.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *evansi*: Named after Evans
