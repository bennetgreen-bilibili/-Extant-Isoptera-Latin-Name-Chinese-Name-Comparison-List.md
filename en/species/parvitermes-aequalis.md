## Summary

***Parvitermes aequalis*** is a species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Cuba: Camagüey. Distribution: Cuba.

## Etymology

Genus name *Parvitermes*: From Latin parvus (small) + termes.

Specific epithet *aequalis*: From aequ + alis
