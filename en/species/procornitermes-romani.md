## Summary

***Procornitermes romani*** is a species of the genus *Procornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1952; the type locality is Brasil: Bahia: Itaetê \[12.98S 40.97W\]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Procornitermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *romani*: Named in honor of Dr. A. Roman of the Naturhistoriska Riksmuseum, Stockholm, Sweden, a specialist on the Hymenoptera.
