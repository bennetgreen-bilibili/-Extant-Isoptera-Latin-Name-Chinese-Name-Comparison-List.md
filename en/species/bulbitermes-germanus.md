## Summary

***Bulbitermes germanus*** is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Singapore: Bukit Timah. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Bulbitermes*: From Latin bulbus (bulb) + termes.

Specific epithet *germanus*: Latin germanus (genuine, brotherly/of the same stock)
