## Summary

***Reticulitermes speratus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kolbe in 1885; the type locality is Japan: Hakodate, Kaga-Yashiki, Mohezi, Yedo. Distribution: Japan, Korean Peninsula, northeastern China, Taiwan, Russia, United States.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *speratus*: Latin speratus (hoped for)
