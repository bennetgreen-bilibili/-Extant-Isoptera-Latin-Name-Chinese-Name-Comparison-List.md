## Summary

***Pericapritermes silvestrianus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Liberia: Cape Mesurado. Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *silvestrianus*: Named after F. Silvestri
