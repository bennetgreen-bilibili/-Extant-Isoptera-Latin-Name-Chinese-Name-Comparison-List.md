## Summary

***Reticulitermes curvatus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hsia & Fan in 1965; the type locality is China: Chekiang: Ch’ing-yüan. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *curvatus*: From curv + atus
