## Summary

***Odontotermes gravelyi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Myanmar: east side of Dawna Hills, Sukli. Distribution: Yunnan, China, Myanmar, Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gravelyi*: Named after F. H. Gravely
