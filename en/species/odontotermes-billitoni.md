## Summary

***Odontotermes billitoni*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Belitung (Billiton). Distribution: Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Malaysia). This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *billitoni*: Named after the locality Billiton (Belitung)
