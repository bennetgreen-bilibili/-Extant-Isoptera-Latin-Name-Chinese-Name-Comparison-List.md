## Summary

***Microtermes osborni*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: St. Gabriel near Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *osborni*: Named after the entomologist H. Osborn
