## Summary

***Stylotermes mirabilis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by He & Qiu in 1990; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Tongren County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mirabilis*: Latin mirabilis (wonderful)
