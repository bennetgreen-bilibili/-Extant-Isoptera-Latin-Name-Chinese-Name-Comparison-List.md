## Summary

***Rhynchotermes diphyes*** is a species of the genus *Rhynchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rhynchotermes*: From Greek rhynchos (beak, snout) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *diphyes*: Greek di- (two) + phyē (nature), twofold
