## Summary

***Ruptitermes cangua*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Acioli & Constantino in 2015; the type locality is Brasil: Rondônia: Vilhena \[ 12.8S 60.1W \]. Distribution: South America, French Guiana, Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *cangua*: From the Tupi word cangua 'round-headed' (from canga 'head').
