## Summary

***Anoplotermes janus*** is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2010; the type locality is French Guiana: Nouragues Inselberg station \[ 4.0884N 52.6814W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Guiana Shield, Amazon region, Central America and Caribbean.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anoplotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.

Specific epithet *janus*: Janus, Roman god
