## Summary

***Kalotermes gracilignathus*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1924; the type locality is Chile: Juan Fernandez Islands, Robinson Crusoe \[ 33.646S 78.84W \]. Distribution: Chile, Juan Fernández Islands, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gracilignathus*: From gracili + gnathus
