## Summary

***Neotermes paraensis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Costa-Lima in 1942; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Ilha de Marajó \[ 1S 49.5W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paraensis*: Named after the locality Para
