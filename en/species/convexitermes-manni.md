## Summary

***Convexitermes manni*** is a species of the genus *Convexitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana, Paraguay, Suriname, Trinidad and Tobago.

## Etymology

Genus name *Convexitermes*: From Latin convexus (convex) + termes.

Specific epithet *manni*: Named after the entomologist W. M. Mann
