## Summary

***Rugitermes aridus*** is a species of the genus *Rugitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2020; the type locality is Peru: Huánuco: Huánuco city \[ 9.877S 76.1641W \]. Distribution: Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rugitermes*: From Latin ruga (wrinkle) + termes.

Specific epithet *aridus*: Latin aridus (arid, dry)
