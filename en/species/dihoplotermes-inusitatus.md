## Summary

***Dihoplotermes inusitatus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Dihoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Araujo in 1961; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: São Paulo \[23.53S 46.62W\]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, French Guiana, Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay, Uruguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dihoplotermes*: From Greek di- (two) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *inusitatus*: Latin inusitatus (unusual)
