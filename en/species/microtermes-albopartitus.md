## Summary

***Microtermes albopartitus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Zimbabwe: Harare (Salisbury). Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *albopartitus*: Latin albus (white) + partitus (divided), white-divided
