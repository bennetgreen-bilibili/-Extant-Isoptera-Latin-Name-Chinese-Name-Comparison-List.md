## Summary

***Nasutitermes brevirostris*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1917; the type locality is Palau: Palao Island; Ponapi Island. Distribution: Marshall Islands, Micronesia, Palau.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *brevirostris*: From brevi + rostris
