## Summary

***Glyptotermes kachongensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Ka-Chong. Distribution: Thailand, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *kachongensis*: Named after the locality Kachong (Thailand)
