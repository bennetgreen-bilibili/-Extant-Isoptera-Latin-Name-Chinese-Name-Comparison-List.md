## Summary

***Mucrotermes osborni*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Mucrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1960; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Pygmy Camp, 4 km N of Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Old World tropics, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Mucrotermes*: From Latin mucro (sharp point) + termes.

Specific epithet *osborni*: Named after the entomologist H. Osborn
