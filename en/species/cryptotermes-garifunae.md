## Summary

***Cryptotermes garifunae*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2018; the type locality is Honduras: Kerala, Laguna Guaimoreto. Distribution: Honduras.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *garifunae*: Named after the Garifuna people (Honduras)
