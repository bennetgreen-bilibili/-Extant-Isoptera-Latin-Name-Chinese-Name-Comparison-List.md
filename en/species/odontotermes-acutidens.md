## Summary

***Odontotermes acutidens*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Grassé in 1947; the type locality is Ivory Coast: Kouibli, 40 km east of Man \[approx. 07°24N, 07°34W\]. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *acutidens*: From the descriptive term 'acut' (sharp)
