## Summary

***Nasutitermes obscurus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]. Distribution: Peru, Brazil, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *obscurus*: From the descriptive term 'obscur' (obscure)
