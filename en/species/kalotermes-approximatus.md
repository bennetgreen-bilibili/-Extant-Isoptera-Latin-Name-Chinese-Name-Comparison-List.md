## Summary

***Kalotermes approximatus*** is a species of the genus *Kalotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1920; the type locality is United States: Florida: Ortega, near Jacksonville. Distribution: United States, Bermuda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Kalotermes*: From Greek kalos (beautiful) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *approximatus*: Latin approximatus (approaching)
