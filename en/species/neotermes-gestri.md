## Summary

***Neotermes gestri*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Sao Tome and Principe: Principe Island. Distribution: Equatorial Guinea, Cameroon, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *gestri*: Named after Raffaello Gestro
