## Summary

***Cubitermes pretorianus*** is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Pretoria. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *pretorianus*: Named after Pretoria, South Africa
