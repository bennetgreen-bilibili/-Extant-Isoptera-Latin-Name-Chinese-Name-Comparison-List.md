## Summary

***Odontotermes sudanensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Sudan: Wad Medani. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, East Africa, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Senegal, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sudanensis*: Named after the locality Sudan
