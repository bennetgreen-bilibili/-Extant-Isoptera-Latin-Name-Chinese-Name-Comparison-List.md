## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes nemorosus*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *nemorosus*: Latin nemorosus (wooded)
