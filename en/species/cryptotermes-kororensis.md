## Summary

***Cryptotermes kororensis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Bacchus in 1987; the type locality is Palau: Koror Island: Limestone Ridge. Distribution: Palau.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *kororensis*: Named after Koror (Palau)
