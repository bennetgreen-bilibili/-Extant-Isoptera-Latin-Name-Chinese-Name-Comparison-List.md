## Summary

***Odontotermes silvaticus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1956; the type locality is Cameroon: Nyong Reserve Forest, 16 km S of Makak. Distribution: West Africa, Burkina Faso, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *silvaticus*: Latin silvaticus (of the forest, silva = forest)
