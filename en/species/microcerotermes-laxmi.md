## Summary

***Microcerotermes laxmi*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1964; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Bikaner District, ca. 50 km w of Bikaner: Kolayat. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *laxmi*: Named after Laxmi (also the goddess Lakshmi)
