## Summary

***Stylotermes fontanellus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao, Zhu & Han in 1982; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Puwei. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fontanellus*: Latin fontana (spring) + -ellus (small), little spring
