## Summary

***Promirotermes holmgreni*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama. Distribution: Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *holmgreni*: Named after the termite taxonomist N. Holmgren
