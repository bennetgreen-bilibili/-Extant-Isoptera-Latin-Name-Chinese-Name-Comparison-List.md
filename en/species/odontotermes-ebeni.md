## Summary

***Odontotermes ebeni*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1912; the type locality is Ethiopia: Dolo; Mahal-Uonz (Scioa); Somalia: Brava. Distribution: East Africa, Ethiopia, Somalia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ebeni*: Named after Eben
