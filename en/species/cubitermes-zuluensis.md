## Summary

***Cubitermes zuluensis*** is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: East Zululand. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa (Zululand).

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *zuluensis*: Named after the locality Zulu
