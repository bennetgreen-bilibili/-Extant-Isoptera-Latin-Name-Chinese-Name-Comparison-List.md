## Summary

***Microcerotermes dolichognathus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama: Rio Cassine. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea-Bissau, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe, Sierra Leone.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dolichognathus*: From dolicho + gnathus
