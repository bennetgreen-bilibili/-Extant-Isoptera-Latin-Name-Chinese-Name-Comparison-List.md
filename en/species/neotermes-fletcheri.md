## Summary

***Neotermes fletcheri*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Coimbatore. Distribution: India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fletcheri*: Named after Fletcher
