## Summary

***Odontotermes matangensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Manzoor & Akhtar in 2005; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *matangensis*: Named after Matang, Sarawak, Malaysia
