## Summary

***Diversitermes tiapuan*** is a species of the genus *Diversitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oliveira & Constantino in 2016; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Catalão, Fazenda São Cipriano \[17.6392S 47.478W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Diversitermes*: From Latin diversus (diverse) + termes.

Specific epithet *tiapuan*: From Tupi itá 'stone' + puã 'standing', i.e. 'upright stone'.
