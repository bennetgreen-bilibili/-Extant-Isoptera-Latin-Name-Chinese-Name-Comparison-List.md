## Summary

***Microcerotermes progrediens*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Nigeria: Olokemeji. Distribution: Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *progrediens*: Latin progrediens (advancing)
