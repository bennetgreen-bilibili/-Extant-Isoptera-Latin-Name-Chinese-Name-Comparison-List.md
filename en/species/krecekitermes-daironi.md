## Summary

***Krecekitermes daironi*** is the only extant species of the genus *Krecekitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn, Carrijo & Castro in 2023; the type locality is Brasil: Rondonia: Porto Velho, Mutum-Paraná \[ 9.4513S 64.8439W \]. Distribution: South America, Colombia, French Guiana.

## Etymology

Genus name *Krecekitermes*: Named after a person (Krecek); -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *daironi*: Named after Dairon
