## Summary

***Stylotermes orthognathus*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1984; the type locality is China: Guizhou Province: Libo County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *orthognathus*: Greek orthos (straight) + gnathos (jaw), straight-jawed
