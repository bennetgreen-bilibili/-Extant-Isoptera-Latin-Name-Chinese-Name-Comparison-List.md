## Summary

***Ophiotermes mirandus*** is a species of the genus *Ophiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1905; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ophiotermes*: From Greek ophis (snake) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *mirandus*: Latin mirandus (wonderful)
