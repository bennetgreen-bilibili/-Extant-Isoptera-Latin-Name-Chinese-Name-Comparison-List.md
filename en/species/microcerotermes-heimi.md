## Summary

***Microcerotermes heimi*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Ahmednagar District: Wallon. Distribution: South Asia, India, Sri Lanka, Pakistan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *heimi*: Named after Heim
