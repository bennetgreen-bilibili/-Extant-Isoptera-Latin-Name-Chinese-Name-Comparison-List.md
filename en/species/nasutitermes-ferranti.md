## Summary

***Nasutitermes ferranti*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kasai: Sankuru. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Angola, Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Nigeria.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *ferranti*: Named after Ferrant
