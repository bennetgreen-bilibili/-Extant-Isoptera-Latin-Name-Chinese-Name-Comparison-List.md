## Summary

***Neotermes laticollis*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Seychelles: Longue Island; Silhouette; near Morne Blanc, Mahé. Distribution: Seychelles.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *laticollis*: From lati + collis
