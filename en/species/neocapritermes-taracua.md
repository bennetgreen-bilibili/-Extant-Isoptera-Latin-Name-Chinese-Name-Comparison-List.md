## Summary

***Neocapritermes taracua*** is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna & Araujo in 1968; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Taracuá \[ 0.1N 68.4667W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Ecuador, Venezuela, Peru.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neocapritermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *taracua*: From the Tupi vernacular name taracuá (also tracuá, tacuá), the local name for carpenter ants (genus Camponotus).
