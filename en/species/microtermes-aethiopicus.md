## Summary

***Microtermes aethiopicus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Barnett, Cowie & Wood in 1988; the type locality is Ethiopia: grounds of Sodo police station. Distribution: Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *aethiopicus*: After Ethiopia (Africa)
