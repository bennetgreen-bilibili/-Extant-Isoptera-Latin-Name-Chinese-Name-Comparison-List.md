## Summary

***Cryptotermes rotundiceps*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Dominican Republic: La Romana Province, 4 km N Guyamate. Distribution: Caribbean, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *rotundiceps*: Latin rotundus (round) + -ceps (head), round-headed
