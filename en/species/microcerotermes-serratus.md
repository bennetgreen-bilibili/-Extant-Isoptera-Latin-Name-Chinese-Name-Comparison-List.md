## Summary

***Microcerotermes serratus*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang. Distribution: Oceania, Australia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *serratus*: From serr + atus
