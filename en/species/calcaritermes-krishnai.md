## Summary

***Calcaritermes krishnai*** is a species of the genus *Calcaritermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1981; the type locality is India: Nicobar Islands: Great Nicobar Island: Champa Bay. Distribution: India (Nicobar Islands).

## Etymology

Genus name *Calcaritermes*: From Latin calcar (spur) + termes.

Specific epithet *krishnai*: Named after the termite taxonomist K. Krishna
