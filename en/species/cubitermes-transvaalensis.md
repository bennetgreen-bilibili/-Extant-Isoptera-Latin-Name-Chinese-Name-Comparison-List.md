## Summary

***Cubitermes transvaalensis*** is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: De Wildt. Distribution: southern Africa, Transvaal, South Africa, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cubitermes*: From Latin cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *transvaalensis*: Named after Transvaal, South Africa
