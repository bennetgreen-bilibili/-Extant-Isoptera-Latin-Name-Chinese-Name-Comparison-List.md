## Summary

***Odontotermes maesodensis*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: km 20 east of Mae Sod. Distribution: Thailand, Vietnam.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *maesodensis*: Named after Mae Sot, Thailand
