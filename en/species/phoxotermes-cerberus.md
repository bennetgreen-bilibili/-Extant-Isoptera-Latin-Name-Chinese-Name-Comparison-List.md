## Summary

***Phoxotermes cerberus*** is the only extant species of the genus *Phoxotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Collins in 1977; the type locality is Cameroon: Sanaga-Maritime: Edea-Marienberg Forest Reserve: Lac Tissongo. Distribution: Central Africa, Cameroon, Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Phoxotermes*: Etymology uncertain; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cerberus*: Greek Kerberos (Cerberus)
