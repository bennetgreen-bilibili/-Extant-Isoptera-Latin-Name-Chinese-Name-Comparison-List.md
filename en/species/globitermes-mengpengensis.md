## Summary

***Globitermes mengpengensis*** is a species of the genus *Globitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Huang in 1990; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Mengla County: Mengpeng Hill. Distribution: Yunnan (Mengla Mengbang 640 m), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Globitermes*: From Latin globus (ball, sphere) + termes.

Specific epithet *mengpengensis*: Named after Mengpeng, Yunnan, China
