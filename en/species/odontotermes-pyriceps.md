## Summary

***Odontotermes pyriceps*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Fan in 1985; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Haikou City. Distribution: Hainan (Haikou, Qionghai), China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *pyriceps*: From pyri + ceps
