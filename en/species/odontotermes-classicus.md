## Summary

***Odontotermes classicus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1912; the type locality is Eritrea: Bogos. Distribution: East Africa, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Somalia, Sudan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *classicus*: Latin classicus (classic, of the highest rank)
