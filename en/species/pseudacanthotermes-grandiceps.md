## Summary

***Pseudacanthotermes grandiceps*** is a species of the genus *Pseudacanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1915; the type locality is Kenya: Kimusu (Victoria Nyanzu, Golf Kavirondo). Distribution: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudacanthotermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *grandiceps*: From grandi + ceps
