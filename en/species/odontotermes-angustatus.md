## Summary

***Odontotermes angustatus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Rambur in 1842; the type locality is South Africa: Cape of Good Hope: Port Elizabeth. Distribution: southern Africa (South Africa).

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *angustatus*: From angust + atus
