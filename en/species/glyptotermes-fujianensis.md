## Summary

***Glyptotermes fujianensis*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Ping in 1983; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Pinghe County. Distribution: China (Fujian), Japan, China (Taiwan).

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fujianensis*: Named after the locality Fujian
