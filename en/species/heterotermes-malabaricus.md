## Summary

***Heterotermes malabaricus*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1933; the type locality is India: Madras \[sic, = Karnataka\], south Mangalore. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Heterotermes*: From Greek heteros (different) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *malabaricus*: Named after the locality Malabar
