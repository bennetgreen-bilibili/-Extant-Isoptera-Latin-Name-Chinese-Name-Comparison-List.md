## Summary

***Eremotermes fletcheri*** is a species of the genus *Eremotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Coimbatore. Distribution: India, Pakistan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Eremotermes*: From Greek erēmos (desert, solitary) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *fletcheri*: Named after Fletcher
