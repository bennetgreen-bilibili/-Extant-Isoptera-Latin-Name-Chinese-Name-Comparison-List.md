## Summary

***Ternicubitermes zavattarii*** is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Ghidini in 1937; the type locality is Ethiopia: Galla-Borana: Neghelli. Distribution: Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ternicubitermes*: From Latin terni (three each) + cubitus (elbow) + termes.

Specific epithet *zavattarii*: Named after Edoardo Zavattari (Italian entomologist)
