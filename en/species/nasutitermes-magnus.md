## Summary

***Nasutitermes magnus*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Froggatt in 1898; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Lolworth Station (W Chartern Towers). Distribution: Australia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *magnus*: Latin magnus (large)
