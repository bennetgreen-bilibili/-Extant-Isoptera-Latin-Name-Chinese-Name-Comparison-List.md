## Summary

***Thoracotermes lusingensis*** is a species of the genus *Thoracotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1958; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Parc National de l’ Upemba: Lusinga. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Thoracotermes*: From Greek thōrax (thorax) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lusingensis*: Named after the locality Lusing
