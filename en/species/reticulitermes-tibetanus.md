## Summary

***Reticulitermes tibetanus*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Huang & Han in 1988; the type locality is China: Tibet: Medog. Distribution: China (Tibet).

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *tibetanus*: Named after the locality Tibet
