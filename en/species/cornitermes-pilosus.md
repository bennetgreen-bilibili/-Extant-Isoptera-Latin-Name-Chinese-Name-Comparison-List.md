## Summary

***Cornitermes pilosus*** is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Peru, Colombia, Bolivia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cornitermes*: From Latin cornu (horn) + termes.

Specific epithet *pilosus*: Latin pilosus (hairy)
