## Summary

***Cavitermes parmae*** is a species of the genus *Cavitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Nova Xavantina \[14.6733S 52.3531W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Ecuador.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cavitermes*: From Latin cavus (hollow) + termes.

Specific epithet *parmae*: From Latin parma (small round shield)
