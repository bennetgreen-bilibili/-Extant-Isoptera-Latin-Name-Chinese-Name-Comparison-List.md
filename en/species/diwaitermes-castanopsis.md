## Summary

***Diwaitermes castanopsis*** is a species of the genus *Diwaitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roisin & Pasteels in 1996; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Manki Ridge: Bulolo. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Diwaitermes*: From the place name Diwai; -termes from Latin termes.

Specific epithet *castanopsis*: Latin castanea (chestnut) + Greek -opsis (appearance), chestnut-like
