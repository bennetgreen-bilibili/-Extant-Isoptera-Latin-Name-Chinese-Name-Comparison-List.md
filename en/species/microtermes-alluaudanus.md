## Summary

***Microtermes alluaudanus*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1915; the type locality is Kenya: Likoni or Cheteni, near Mombasa. Distribution: Cameroon, Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *alluaudanus*: Named after C. Alluaud
