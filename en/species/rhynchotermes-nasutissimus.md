## Summary

***Rhynchotermes nasutissimus*** is a species and the type species of the genus *Rhynchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá \[15.5961S 56.0967W\]; Paraguay: Central: Areguá \[25.3S 57.4167W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Paraguay, Argentina, Peru. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Rhynchotermes*: From Greek rhynchos (beak, snout) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *nasutissimus*: Latin nasutus (nasute) + -issimus (most), most nasute
