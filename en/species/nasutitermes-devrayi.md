## Summary

***Nasutitermes devrayi*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1994; the type locality is India: Great Nicobar Island: Campbell Bay. Distribution: India (Nicobar Islands).

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *devrayi*: Named after Devray
