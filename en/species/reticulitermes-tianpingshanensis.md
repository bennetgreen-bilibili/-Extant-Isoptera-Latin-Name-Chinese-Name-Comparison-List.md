## Summary

***Reticulitermes tianpingshanensis*** is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Zhu & Huang in 1993; the type locality is China: Hunan Province: Sangzhi County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

Specific epithet *tianpingshanensis*: Named after the locality Tianpingshan
