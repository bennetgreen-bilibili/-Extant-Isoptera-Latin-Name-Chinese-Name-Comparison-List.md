## Summary

***Trinervitermes trinervoides*** is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is South Africa: Cape Province: Laingsburg. Distribution: South Africa, Namibia, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Lesotho, Mozambique, Eswatini, Zimbabwe. This site records 10 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Trinervitermes*: From Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + termes.

Specific epithet *trinervoides*: Latin tri- (three) + nervus (vein) + -oides (like)
