## Summary

***Odontotermes robustus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Congo: Brazzaville. Distribution: Congo.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *robustus*: Latin robustus (robust)
