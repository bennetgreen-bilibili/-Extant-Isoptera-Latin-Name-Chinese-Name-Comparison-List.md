## Summary

***Isognathotermes ugandensis*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1923; the type locality is Uganda: "Nkulukuku.". Distribution: Uganda, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ugandensis*: Named after the locality Ugand
