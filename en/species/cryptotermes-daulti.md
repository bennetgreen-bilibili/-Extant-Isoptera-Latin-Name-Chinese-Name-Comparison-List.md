## Summary

***Cryptotermes daulti*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Rathore in 1994; the type locality is India: Gujarat: Taranga hills, 32 km. south of Danta and 60 km. NE of Mahesana. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *daulti*: Named after Dault
