## Summary

***Pericapritermes papuanus*** is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2008; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Western Province: Wipim. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pericapritermes*: From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *papuanus*: After Papua (New Guinea)
