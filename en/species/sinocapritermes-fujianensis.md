## Summary

***Sinocapritermes fujianensis*** is a species of the genus *Sinocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1986; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Jian'ou. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Sinocapritermes*: From Latin Sinae (China) + caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *fujianensis*: Named after the locality Fujian
