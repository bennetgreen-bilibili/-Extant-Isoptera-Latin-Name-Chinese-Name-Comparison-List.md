## Summary

***Nasutitermes globiceps*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Paraguay: Cordillera: San Bernardino \[25.27S 57.32W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Colombia, Paraguay.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *globiceps*: From globi + ceps
