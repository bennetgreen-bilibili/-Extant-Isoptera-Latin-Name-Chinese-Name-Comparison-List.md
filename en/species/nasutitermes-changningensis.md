## Summary

***Nasutitermes changningensis*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gao in 1988; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Changning County. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *changningensis*: Named after the locality Changning
