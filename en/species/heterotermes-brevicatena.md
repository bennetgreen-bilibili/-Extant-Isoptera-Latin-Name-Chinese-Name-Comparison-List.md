***Heterotermes brevicatena*** is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Watson & Miller in 1989; the type locality is Australia: New South Wales: Binya State Forest. Distribution: Australia.

Specific epithet *brevicatena*: From the descriptive term 'brev' (short)
