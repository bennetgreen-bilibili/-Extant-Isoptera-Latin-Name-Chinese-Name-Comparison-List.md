## Summary

***Microcerotermes cylindriceps*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Pankulam. Distribution: Sri Lanka.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cylindriceps*: From cylindri + ceps
