## Summary

***Promirotermes redundans*** is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: Cameroon, Togo, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, Senegal.

## Etymology

Genus name *Promirotermes*: From Greek pro- (before) + Latin mirus (wonderful) + termes.

Specific epithet *redundans*: Latin redundans (redundant)
