## Summary

***Stylotermes dunensis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Thakur in 1975; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: Dehra Dun: New Forest. Distribution: China, India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *dunensis*: From Latin duna (dune)
