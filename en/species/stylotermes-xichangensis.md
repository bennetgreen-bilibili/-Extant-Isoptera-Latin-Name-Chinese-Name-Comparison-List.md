## Summary

***Stylotermes xichangensis*** is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Huang & Zhu in 1986; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Q’onghai Lake, Xichang. Distribution: China.

## Etymology

Genus name *Stylotermes*: From Greek stylos (pillar, style) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *xichangensis*: Named after the locality Xichang
