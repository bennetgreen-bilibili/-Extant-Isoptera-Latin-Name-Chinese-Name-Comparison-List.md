## Summary

***Pseudocapritermes parasilvaticus*** is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Tung Sa-Lang Luang. Distribution: Indonesia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Pseudocapritermes*: From Greek pseudēs (false) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.

Specific epithet *parasilvaticus*: Greek para (beside) + Latin silvaticus (of the forest): near forests
