## Summary

***Cryptotermes dolei*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1932; the type locality is Polynesia (French): Marquesas: Eiao; Marquesas: Fatuhiva, Ouia Valley; Marquesas: Hivaoa, Anatuakina; Marquesas: Mohotani; Marquesas: Nukuhiva; Marquesas: Uahuka, Haahue Valley; Marquesas: Uahuka, Hanahoua Valley; Marquesas: Uapou. Distribution: French Polynesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *dolei*: Named after Dole
