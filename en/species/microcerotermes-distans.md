## Summary

***Microcerotermes distans*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Indonesia: Sulawesi: Menado. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *distans*: Latin distans (distant)
