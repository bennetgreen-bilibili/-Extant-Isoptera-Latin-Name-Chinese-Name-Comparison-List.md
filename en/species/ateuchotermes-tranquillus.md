## Summary

***Ateuchotermes tranquillus*** is a species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ateuchotermes*: From Greek a- (without) + teuchos (implement, weapon) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *tranquillus*: Latin tranquillus (tranquil)
