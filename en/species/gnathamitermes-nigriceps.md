## Summary

***Gnathamitermes nigriceps*** is a species of the genus *Gnathamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Light in 1930; the type locality is Mexico: Colima State: Jala, 20 km west of Colima. Distribution: Central America (Mexico).

## Etymology

Genus name *Gnathamitermes*: Contains Greek gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes; further derivation uncertain.

Specific epithet *nigriceps*: Latin niger (black) + -ceps (head), black-headed
