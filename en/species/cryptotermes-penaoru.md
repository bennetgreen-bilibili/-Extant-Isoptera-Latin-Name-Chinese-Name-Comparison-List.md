## Summary

***Cryptotermes penaoru*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roisin in 2011; the type locality is Vanuatu: Sanma, Espiritu Santo. Distribution: Vanuatu.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *penaoru*: Named after Penaoru village, Espiritu Santo, Vanuatu
