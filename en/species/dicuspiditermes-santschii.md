## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes santschii*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1922; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Padang-Pandjang. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia, Malaysia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *santschii*: Named after F. Santschi
