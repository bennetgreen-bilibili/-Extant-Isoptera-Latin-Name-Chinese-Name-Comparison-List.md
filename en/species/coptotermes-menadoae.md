## Summary

***Coptotermes menadoae*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oshima in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sulawesi: Menado. Distribution: Indonesia (Sulawesi).

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *menadoae*: Named after Menado (Manado), Sulawesi, Indonesia
