## Summary

***Cryptotermes crassicornis*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Durban. Distribution: South Africa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *crassicornis*: From crassi + cornis
