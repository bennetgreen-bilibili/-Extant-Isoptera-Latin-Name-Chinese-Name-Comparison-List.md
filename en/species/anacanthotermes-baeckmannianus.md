## Summary

***Anacanthotermes baeckmannianus*** is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Vasiljev in 1911; the type locality is Kazakhstan: Syr-dara-Geibet: Dzhulek. Distribution: Kazakhstan.

## Etymology

Genus name *Anacanthotermes*: From Greek an- (without) + akantha (thorn) + Latin termes, 'thornless termite'.

Specific epithet *baeckmannianus*: Named after Baeckmann
