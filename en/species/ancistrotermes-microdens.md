## Summary

***Ancistrotermes microdens*** is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1966; the type locality is Kenya: Kisumu. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Uganda.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ancistrotermes*: From Greek ankistron (hook) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *microdens*: Greek mikros (small) + Latin dens (tooth), small-toothed
