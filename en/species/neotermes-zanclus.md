## Summary

***Neotermes zanclus*** is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Oliveira in 1979; the type locality is Brasil: Minas Gerais: Belo Horizonte \[ 19.92S 43.93W \]. Distribution: Brazil.

## Etymology

Genus name *Neotermes*: From Greek neos (new) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *zanclus*: Greek zanklon (sickle)
