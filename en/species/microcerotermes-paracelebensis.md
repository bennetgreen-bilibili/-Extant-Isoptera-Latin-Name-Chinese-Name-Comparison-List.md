## Summary

***Microcerotermes paracelebensis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Ka-Chong. Distribution: Malaysia, Thailand.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *paracelebensis*: Greek para (near) + Celebes (Sulawesi): near Celebes
