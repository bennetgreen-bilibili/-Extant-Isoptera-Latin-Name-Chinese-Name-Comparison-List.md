## Summary

***Coptotermes cooloola*** is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Lee, Evans, Cameron, Ho, Namyatova & Lo in 2017; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Springfield. Distribution: southeastern Queensland, Australia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Coptotermes*: From Greek koptein (to cut) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *cooloola*: After Cooloola (Australia)
