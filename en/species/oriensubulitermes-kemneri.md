## Summary

***Oriensubulitermes kemneri*** is a species of the genus *Oriensubulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1968; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Tapang; Sarawak. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Oriensubulitermes*: From Latin Oriens (the East) + subula (awl) + termes.

Specific epithet *kemneri*: Named after the entomologist N. A. Kemner
