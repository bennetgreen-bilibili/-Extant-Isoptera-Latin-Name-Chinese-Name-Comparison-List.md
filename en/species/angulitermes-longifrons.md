## Summary

***Angulitermes longifrons*** is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Maiti in 1983; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Midnapore District: Gidhni. Distribution: India.

## Etymology

Genus name *Angulitermes*: From Latin angulus (angle, corner) + termes.

Specific epithet *longifrons*: From longi + frons
