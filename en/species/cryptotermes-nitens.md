## Summary

***Cryptotermes nitens*** is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Cayman Islands: Little Cayman Island: Pirates’ Point Resort. Distribution: Cayman Islands, Cuba, Jamaica.

## Etymology

Genus name *Cryptotermes*: From Greek kryptos (hidden) + Latin termes, for its concealed wood-dwelling habits.

Specific epithet *nitens*: Latin nitens (shining)
