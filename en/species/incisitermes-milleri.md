## Summary

***Incisitermes milleri*** is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Emerson in 1943; the type locality is United States: Florida: Elliot Key. Distribution: United States, Dominican Republic, Bahamas, Cayman Islands, Cuba, Jamaica, Turks and Caicos Islands.

## Etymology

Genus name *Incisitermes*: From Latin incisus (cut, notched) + termes.

Specific epithet *milleri*: Named after L.R. Miller
