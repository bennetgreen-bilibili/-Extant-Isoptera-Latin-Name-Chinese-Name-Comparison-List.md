## Summary

***Microcerotermes peraffinis*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1909; the type locality is Samoa: Ninafoon. Distribution: Oceania, Tonga, Samoa.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *peraffinis*: Latin per- (very) + affinis (related), very closely related
