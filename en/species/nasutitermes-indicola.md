## Summary

***Nasutitermes indicola*** is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu: Anamalai Hills. Distribution: India. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

Specific epithet *indicola*: Latin India + -cola (dweller): dwelling in India
