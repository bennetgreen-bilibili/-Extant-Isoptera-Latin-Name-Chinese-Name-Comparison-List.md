## Summary

***Odontotermes ostentans*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Sao Tome and Principe: Ribeira Palma, Aguã Izé; Ribeira Palma, Aguã Izé. Distribution: São Tomé and Príncipe.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *ostentans*: Latin ostentans (displaying)
