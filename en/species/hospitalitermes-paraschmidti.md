## Summary

***Hospitalitermes paraschmidti*** is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Akbar in 1986; the type locality is Malaysia: Johore: Gunong Arong Forest Reserve, 14 km ex Mersing-Endau. Distribution: Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Hospitalitermes*: From Latin hospes (host, guest) + termes.

Specific epithet *paraschmidti*: From Greek para (near) + schmidti, referring to affinity with H. schmidti
