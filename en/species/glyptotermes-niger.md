## Summary

***Glyptotermes niger*** is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: south coast, near Wijnkoopsbaai, Tjisolok. Distribution: Indonesia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Glyptotermes*: From Greek glyptos (carved, sculpted) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *niger*: Latin niger (black)
