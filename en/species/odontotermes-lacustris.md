## Summary

***Odontotermes lacustris*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1960; the type locality is Zambia: Abercorn. Distribution: Old World tropics, East Africa, southern Africa, Malawi, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *lacustris*: Latin lacustris (of lakes)
