## Summary

***Isognathotermes phallicaecalis*** is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Josens & Deligne in 2025; the type locality is Congo Republic: Loundoungou \[ 2.3804N 17.0704E \]. Distribution: Congo Republic, Cameroon.

## Etymology

Genus name *Isognathotermes*: From Greek isos (equal) + gnathos (jaw) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *phallicaecalis*: Greek phallos (phallus) + Latin caecum (caecum)
