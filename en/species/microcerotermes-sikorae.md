## Summary

***Microcerotermes sikorae*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1893; the type locality is Madagascar: Andrangoloaka. Distribution: Madagascar.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *sikorae*: Named after Franz Sikora (Austrian natural history collector)
