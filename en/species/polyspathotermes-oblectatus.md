## Summary

***Polyspathotermes oblectatus*** is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1958; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Parc National de l’ Upemba: Lusinga. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Polyspathotermes*: From Greek polys (many) + spathē (spatula) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *oblectatus*: Latin oblectatus (delighted)
