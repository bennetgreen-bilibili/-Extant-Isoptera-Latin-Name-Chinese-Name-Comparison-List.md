## Summary

***Globitermes sulphureus*** is a species of the genus *Globitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Selangor: Perak. Distribution: mainland Southeast Asia, Indochinese Peninsula, insular Southeast Asia, Malay Archipelago, China. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Globitermes*: From Latin globus (ball, sphere) + termes.

Specific epithet *sulphureus*: Latin sulphureus (sulphur-yellow)
