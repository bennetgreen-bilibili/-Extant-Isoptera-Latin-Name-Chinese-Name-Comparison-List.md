## Summary

***Ruptitermes franciscoi*** is a species of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1959; the type locality is Venezuela: Bolivar: El Pilar \[ 7.38333N 61.7333W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, French Guiana, Venezuela, Bolivia, Colombia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Ruptitermes*: From Latin ruptus (broken, burst) + termes.

Specific epithet *franciscoi*: Named after Francisco
