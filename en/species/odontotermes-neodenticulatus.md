## Summary

***Odontotermes neodenticulatus*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Keningau District: Biah Forest, mile 6. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *neodenticulatus*: From neodenticul + atus
