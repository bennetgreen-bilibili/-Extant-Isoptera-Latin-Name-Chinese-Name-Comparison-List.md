## Summary

***Macrotermes latignathus*** is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Kunak-Mostyn District: Kalumpang. Distribution: Nepal, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Macrotermes*: From Greek makros (large) + Latin termes, 'large termite'.

Specific epithet *latignathus*: Latin latus (broad) + Greek gnathos (jaw), broad-jawed
