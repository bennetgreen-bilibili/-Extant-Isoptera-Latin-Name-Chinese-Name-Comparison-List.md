## Summary

***Schedorhinotermes longirostris*** is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Brauer in 1866; the type locality is India: Nicobar Islands: Kondul. Distribution: India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Palau, Papua New Guinea; introduced: Guam (United States). This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Schedorhinotermes*: From Greek schedon (near) + rhis (nose) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *longirostris*: From longi + rostris
