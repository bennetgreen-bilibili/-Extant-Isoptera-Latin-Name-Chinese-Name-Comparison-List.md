## Summary

***Microcerotermes bouilloni*** is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Roisin & Pasteels in 2000; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Port Moresby: savanna next to University of Papua New Guinea campus. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microcerotermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + keras (horn) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *bouilloni*: Named after André Bouillon
