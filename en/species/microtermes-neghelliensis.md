## Summary

***Microtermes neghelliensis*** is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ghidini in 1937; the type locality is Ethiopia: Galla-Borana: Mega. Distribution: Ethiopia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Microtermes*: From Greek mikros (small) + Latin termes, 'small termite'.

Specific epithet *neghelliensis*: Named after Neghelli (Negele), Ethiopia
