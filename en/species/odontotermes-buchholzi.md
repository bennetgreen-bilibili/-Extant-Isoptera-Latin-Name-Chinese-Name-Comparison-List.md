## Summary

***Odontotermes buchholzi*** is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1897; the type locality is Cameroon: (Mapanja?); Equatorial Guinea: Bioko; Gabon: River Ogoué (Ogowe). Distribution: Central Africa, Gabon, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Liberia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Odontotermes*: From Greek odous (tooth) + Latin termes.

Specific epithet *buchholzi*: Named after Buchholz
