## Summary

***Leucopitermes thoi*** is a species of the genus *Leucopitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gathorne-Hardy in 2004; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Aceh Tenggara: Ketambe field station. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.

## Etymology

Genus name *Leucopitermes*: From Greek leukos (white) + Latin termes; further derivation uncertain.

Specific epithet *thoi*: Named after Y. P. Tho (Vietnamese termitologist)
