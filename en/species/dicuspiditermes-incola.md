## Summary

***Dicuspiditermes incola*** is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1893; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Colombo. Distribution: India, Sri Lanka. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

## Etymology

Genus name *Dicuspiditermes*: From Latin di- (two) + cuspis (point) + termes.

Specific epithet *incola*: Latin incola (inhabitant)
