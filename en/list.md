# Extant Isoptera (Termites) — Taxonomic Index (2026.10.30)

## Taxonomic framework

All taxa in this list belong to a single taxonomic framework, from top to bottom:

1. **Domain** — Eukaryota
2. **Kingdom** — Animalia
3. **Phylum** — Arthropoda
4. **Class** — Insecta
5. **Subclass** — Pterygota
6. **Infraclass** — Neoptera
7. **Superorder** — Dictyoptera
8. **Order** — Blattodea (cockroaches and termites)
9. **Infraorder** — Isoptera (epifamily Termitoidae)

Below follow **Family** → **Subfamily** → **Genus** → **Species**; the families, subfamilies, genera and species are listed below.

Above family level sit several unranked clades, nested one within another: Euisoptera is divided into Teletisoptera and Icoisoptera, Icoisoptera into Neoisoptera, and Neoisoptera into Geoisoptera; the sections below follow that nesting, indented by level.
