## How to use this page

- Stuck on a term in a description or a key → look it up on the morphology page, working head to abdomen.
- Taxa and scientific names link into the checklist; names and distributions follow the checklist, while morphological descriptions are only supporting material.

## Terms and classification

This site treats Isoptera as a branch within Blattodea, following current phylogenetic work. The 13 families used here do not always match older works such as <i>Fauna Sinica</i>: <i>Coptotermes</i> and <i>Reticulitermes</i>, long placed in Rhinotermitidae, are placed in Heterotermitidae here. Where a classification is at issue, the checklist and the family pages are authoritative; the page flags divergences where they arise.
