## 摘要

**卡尔须白蚁**（*Hospitalitermes kali*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）须白蚁属（*Hospitalitermes*）的一种。本种由 Maiti & Chakraborty 于 1994 年描述，模式产地为 India: Great Nicobar Island: Nancowry。分布记录包括印度尼科巴群岛。

EN — **卡尔须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes kali*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Maiti & Chakraborty in 1994; the type locality is India: Great Nicobar Island: Nancowry. Distribution: India (Nicobar Islands).
