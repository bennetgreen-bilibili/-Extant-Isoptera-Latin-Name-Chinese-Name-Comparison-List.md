EN — **细唇亚种** (*Reticulitermes speratus leptolabralis*) is a subspecies of *Reticulitermes speratus*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, genus *Reticulitermes*. The subspecies was described by Morimoto in 1968; the type locality is Japan: Aichi, Kasugai, Tokushima, Yami. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Reticulitermes speratus*.

## 词源 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Etymology</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Etimología</span>

属名 *Reticulitermes*：属名由拉丁文 reticulum（小网、网状）与 termes（白蚁）组合。

EN — *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

ES — *Reticulitermes*: Del latín reticulum (red diminuta) + termes.

种加词 *leptolabralis*：希腊文 leptos（细）＋拉丁文 labrum（唇），指细唇的

EN — epithet *leptolabralis*: Greek leptos (slender) + Latin labrum (lip): slender-lipped

ES — epithet *leptolabralis*: Del griego leptos (esbelto) + latín labrum (labio): de labio esbelto
