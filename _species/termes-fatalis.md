## 摘要

**祸根白蚁**（*Termes fatalis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）白蚁属（*Termes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Linnaeus 于 1758 年描述，模式产地为 Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]。分布记录包括新热带界、巴西、哥伦比亚、法属圭亚那等南美北部、玻利维亚、厄瓜多尔、巴拉圭、秘鲁、特立尼达和多巴哥、委内瑞拉。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **祸根白蚁** (*Termes fatalis*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Linnaeus in 1758; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Neotropics, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, etc., northern South America, Bolivia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
