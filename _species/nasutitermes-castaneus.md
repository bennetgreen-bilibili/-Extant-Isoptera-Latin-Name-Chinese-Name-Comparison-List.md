## 摘要

**栗色象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes castaneus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Oshima 于 1920 年描述，模式产地为 Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Sarai near Paete; Panay: Antique Province: Culasi。分布记录包括菲律宾。

EN — **栗色象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes castaneus*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Oshima in 1920; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Sarai near Paete; Panay: Antique Province: Culasi. Distribution: Philippines.
