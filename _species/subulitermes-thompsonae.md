## 摘要

**汤普森锥白蚁**（*Subulitermes thompsonae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）锥白蚁属（*Subulitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Emerson 于 1925 年描述，模式产地为 Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]。分布记录包括秘鲁、厄瓜多尔。

EN — **汤普森锥白蚁** (*Subulitermes thompsonae*) is a species of the genus *Subulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Peru: Puno: Chaquimayo \[13.45S 70.4W\]. Distribution: Peru, Ecuador.
