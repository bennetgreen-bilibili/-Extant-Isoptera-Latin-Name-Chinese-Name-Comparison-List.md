## 摘要

**森萨尔马树白蚁**（*Glyptotermes sensarmai*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）树白蚁属（*Glyptotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Maiti 于 1976 年描述，模式产地为 India: West Bengal: Koch Bihar Distric: Coochbehar Forest Range, Antiamochar Beat, Khagribari。分布记录包括印度、孟加拉国。

EN — **森萨尔马树白蚁** (*Glyptotermes sensarmai*) is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Maiti in 1976; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Koch Bihar Distric: Coochbehar Forest Range, Antiamochar Beat, Khagribari. Distribution: India, Bangladesh.
