## 摘要

**中间亚种**（*Odontotermes latericius media*）是似砖土白蚁（*Odontotermes latericius*）的一个亚种，隶属白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）。本亚种由 Wasmann 于 1912 年描述，模式产地为 Sudan: Mongalla。本页暂无单列的分布记录，母种 *Odontotermes latericius* 的分布见下文「分布」一节。

EN — **中间亚种** (*Odontotermes latericius media*) is a subspecies of *Odontotermes latericius*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Odontotermes*. The subspecies was described by Wasmann in 1912; the type locality is Sudan: Mongalla. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Odontotermes latericius*.

La subespecie fue descrita por Wasmann en 1912; la localidad tipo es Sudan: Mongalla.
