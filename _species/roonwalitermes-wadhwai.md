## 摘要

**沃德瓦鲁恩沃白蚁**（*Roonwalitermes wadhwai*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）鲁恩沃白蚁属（*Roonwalitermes*）的现存唯一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Bose 于 1997 年描述，模式产地为 India: Meghalaya: Mawphlang。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **沃德瓦鲁恩沃白蚁** (*Roonwalitermes wadhwai*) is the only extant species of the genus *Roonwalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Bose in 1997; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Mawphlang. Distribution: India.
