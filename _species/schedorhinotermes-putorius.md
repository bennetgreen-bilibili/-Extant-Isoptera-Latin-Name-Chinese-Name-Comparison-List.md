## 摘要

**臭鼬长鼻白蚁**（*Schedorhinotermes putorius*）是鼻白蚁科（RHINOTERMITIDAE）长鼻白蚁属（*Schedorhinotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1896 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Kitta。分布记录包括西非、喀麦隆、加蓬、赤道几内亚、加纳、几内亚、几内亚比绍、科特迪瓦、尼日利亚、圣多美和普林西比、塞拉利昂、安哥拉、中非共和国、刚果民主共和国、乌干达。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **臭鼬长鼻白蚁** (*Schedorhinotermes putorius*) is a species of the genus *Schedorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1896; the type locality is Cameroon: Kitta. Distribution: West Africa, Cameroon, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe, Sierra Leone, Angola, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda.
