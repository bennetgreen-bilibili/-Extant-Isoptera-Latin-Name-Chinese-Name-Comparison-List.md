## 摘要

**瓦里拉塔新几内亚白蚁**（*Niuginitermes variratae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）新几内亚白蚁属（*Niuginitermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Roisin & Pasteels 于 1996 年描述，模式产地为 Papua New Guinea: Central Province: Varirata National Park。分布记录包括巴布亚新几内亚。

EN — **瓦里拉塔新几内亚白蚁** (*Niuginitermes variratae*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Niuginitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roisin & Pasteels in 1996; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Central Province: Varirata National Park. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.
