EN — **小囟土白蚁** (*Odontotermes fontanellus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1925; the type locality is China: Jiangsu Province: Tschingkiang. Distribution: Jiangsu (Zhenjiang), Anhui, Yunnan, China.
