## 摘要

**巴特土白蚁**（*Odontotermes butteli*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Holmgren 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Sumatra: Bah Boelian; Sumatra: Lau Boentoe; Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat; Malaysia: Selangor: Gap。分布记录包括东南亚马来西亚、印度尼西亚。

EN — **巴特土白蚁** (*Odontotermes butteli*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Bah Boelian; Sumatra: Lau Boentoe; Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat; Malaysia: Selangor: Gap. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia.
