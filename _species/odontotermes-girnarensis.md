## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

**吉尔纳尔土白蚁**（*Odontotermes girnarensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Thakur 于 1989 年描述，模式产地为 India: Gujarat: Junagarh district: Bordevi forest, near Girnar Hills, about 8 km from Bhavnath Panchayat Rest House。分布记录包括南亚印度古吉拉特。

EN — **吉尔纳尔土白蚁** (*Odontotermes girnarensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Thakur in 1989; the type locality is India: Gujarat: Junagarh district: Bordevi forest, near Girnar Hills, about 8 km from Bhavnath Panchayat Rest House. Distribution: South Asia, Gujarat, India.

ES — **吉尔纳尔土白蚁** (*Odontotermes girnarensis*) es una especie del género *Odontotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Macrotermitinae).
