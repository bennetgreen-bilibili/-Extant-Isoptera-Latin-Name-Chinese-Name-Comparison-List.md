## 摘要

**萨赖怒白蚁**（*Lacessititermes saraiensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）怒白蚁属（*Lacessititermes*）的一种。本种由 Oshima 于 1916 年描述，模式产地为 Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Sarai near Paete。分布记录包括中国、印度尼西亚、菲律宾。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **萨赖怒白蚁** (*Lacessititermes saraiensis*) is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1916; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Laguna Province: Sarai near Paete. Distribution: China, Indonesia, Philippines. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
