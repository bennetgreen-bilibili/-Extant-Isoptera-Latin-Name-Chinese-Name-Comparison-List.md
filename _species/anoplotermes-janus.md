## 摘要

**门神解甲白蚁**（*Anoplotermes janus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）解甲白蚁属（*Anoplotermes*）的一种。本种由 Bourguignon & Roisin 于 2010 年描述，模式产地为 French Guiana: Nouragues Inselberg station \[ 4.0884N 52.6814W \]。分布记录包括巴西、圭亚那地盾、亚马孙地区、中美洲及加勒比。

EN — **门神解甲白蚁** (*Anoplotermes janus*) is a species of the genus *Anoplotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2010; the type locality is French Guiana: Nouragues Inselberg station \[ 4.0884N 52.6814W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Guiana Shield, Amazon region, Central America and Caribbean.
