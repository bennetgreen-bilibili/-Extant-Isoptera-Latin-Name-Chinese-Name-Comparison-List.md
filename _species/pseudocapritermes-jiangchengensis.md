## 摘要

**江城伪扭白蚁**（*Pseudocapritermes jiangchengensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）伪扭白蚁属（*Pseudocapritermes*）的一种。本种由 Yang, Zhu & Huang 于 1992 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: autonomous county of Jiangcheng Hani and Yi。分布记录包括中国云南。

EN — **江城伪扭白蚁** (*Pseudocapritermes jiangchengensis*) is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1992; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: autonomous county of Jiangcheng Hani and Yi. Distribution: China (Yunnan).
