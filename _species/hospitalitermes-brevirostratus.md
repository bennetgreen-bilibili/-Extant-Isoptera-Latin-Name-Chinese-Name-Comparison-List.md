## 摘要

**短鼻须白蚁**（*Hospitalitermes brevirostratus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）须白蚁属（*Hospitalitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Prashad & Sen-Sarma 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 Myanmar: Maymyo。分布记录包括缅甸。

EN — **短鼻须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes brevirostratus*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Prashad & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is Myanmar: Maymyo. Distribution: Myanmar.
