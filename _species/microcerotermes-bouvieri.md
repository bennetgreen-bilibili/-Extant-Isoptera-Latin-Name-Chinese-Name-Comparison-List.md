## 摘要

**布维尔锯白蚁**（*Microcerotermes bouvieri*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）锯白蚁亚科（Microcerotermitinae）锯白蚁属（*Microcerotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Desneux 于 1904 年描述，模式产地为 Colombia: Tolima: Mariquita \[ 5.2N 74.89W \]。分布记录包括新热带界、巴西、哥伦比亚、墨西哥。

EN — **布维尔锯白蚁** (*Microcerotermes bouvieri*) is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Desneux in 1904; the type locality is Colombia: Tolima: Mariquita \[ 5.2N 74.89W \]. Distribution: Neotropics, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico.
