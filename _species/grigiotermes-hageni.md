EN — **哈根灰白蚁** (*Grigiotermes hageni*) is a species and the type species of the genus *Grigiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder & Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Argentina: Chaco: Colonia Benítez \[ 27.3333S 58.9333W \]; Brasil: Mato Grosso do Sul: Ladário, Urucum \[ 19.155S 57.631W \]; Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[ 15.627S 56.058W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Venezuela, Bolivia, Paraguay, Argentina, Trinidad and Tobago. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

La especie fue descrita por Snyder & Emerson en 1949; la localidad tipo es Argentina: Chaco: Colonia Benítez \[ 27.3333S 58.9333W \]; Brasil: Mato Grosso do Sul: Ladário, Urucum \[ 19.155S 57.631W \]; Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[ 15.627S 56.058W \].
