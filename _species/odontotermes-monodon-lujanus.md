## 摘要

**卢扬亚种**（*Odontotermes monodon lujanus*）是单齿土白蚁（*Odontotermes monodon*）的一个亚种，隶属白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）。本亚种由 Wasmann 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 Mozambique: Morumballe。本页暂无单列的分布记录，母种 *Odontotermes monodon* 的分布见下文「分布」一节。

EN — **卢扬亚种** (*Odontotermes monodon lujanus*) is a subspecies of *Odontotermes monodon*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), genus *Odontotermes*. The subspecies was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Mozambique: Morumballe. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Odontotermes monodon*.
