## 摘要

**大唇棱角白蚁**（*Angularitermes clypeatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）棱角白蚁属（*Angularitermes*）的一种。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]。分布记录包括巴西、法属圭亚那、厄瓜多尔。

EN — **大唇棱角白蚁** (*Angularitermes clypeatus*) is a species of the genus *Angularitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Ecuador.
