## 摘要

EN — **云南土白蚁** (*Odontotermes yunnanensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Pu-erh. Distribution: Yunnan, China, Vietnam.
