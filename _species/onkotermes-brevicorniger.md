## 摘要

**短角倒钩白蚁**（*Onkotermes brevicorniger*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）锯白蚁亚科（Microcerotermitinae）倒钩白蚁属（*Onkotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1901 年描述，模式产地为 Argentina: Córdoba: Cosquin \[ 31.25S 64.4833W \]。分布记录包括阿根廷、巴西、智利。

EN — **短角倒钩白蚁** (*Onkotermes brevicorniger*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Onkotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Argentina: Córdoba: Cosquin \[ 31.25S 64.4833W \]. Distribution: Argentina, Brazil, Chile.
