## 摘要

**戴克夏娜前扭白蚁**（*Procapritermes dakshinae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）前扭白蚁属（*Procapritermes*）的一种。本种由 Chhotani & Ferry 于 1995 年描述，模式产地为 India: Kerala: on road from Kolattupuzha to Nedumangad, vicinity of village Arippal。分布记录包括印度喀拉拉。

EN — **戴克夏娜前扭白蚁** (*Procapritermes dakshinae*) is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani & Ferry in 1995; the type locality is India: Kerala: on road from Kolattupuzha to Nedumangad, vicinity of village Arippal. Distribution: India, Kerala.

ES — **戴克夏娜前扭白蚁** (*Procapritermes dakshinae*) es una especie del género *Procapritermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Mirocapritermitinae). La especie fue descrita por Chhotani & Ferry en 1995; la localidad tipo es India: Kerala: on road from Kolattupuzha to Nedumangad, vicinity of village Arippal. Distribución: India, Kerala.
