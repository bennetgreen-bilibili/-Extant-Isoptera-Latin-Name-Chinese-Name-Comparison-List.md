EN — **短肠虚白蚁** (*Acidnotermes praus*) is the only extant species of the genus *Acidnotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: tropical Africa.
