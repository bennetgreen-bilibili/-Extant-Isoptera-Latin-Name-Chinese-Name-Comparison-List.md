EN — **双裂方白蚁** (*Cubitermes bilobatus*) is a species and the type species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Estcourt, altitude 3500 ft. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nigeria, Sudan, Tanzania. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
