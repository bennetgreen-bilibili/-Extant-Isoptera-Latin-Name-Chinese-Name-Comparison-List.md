## 摘要

**显赫多铲白蚁**（*Polyspathotermes inclitus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）多铲白蚁属（*Polyspathotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Silvestri 于 1912 年描述，模式产地为 Zambia: Banguelo。分布记录包括刚果、刚果民主共和国、肯尼亚、马拉维、卢旺达、坦桑尼亚、乌干达、赞比亚。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **显赫多铲白蚁** (*Polyspathotermes inclitus*) is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Zambia: Banguelo. Distribution: Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
