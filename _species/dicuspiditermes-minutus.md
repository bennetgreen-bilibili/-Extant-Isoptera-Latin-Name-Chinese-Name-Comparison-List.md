## 摘要

**小突扭白蚁**（*Dicuspiditermes minutus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）突扭白蚁属（*Dicuspiditermes*）的一种。本种由 Akhtar & Riaz 于 1992 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Johore: Kluang Forest。分布记录包括文莱、印度尼西亚、马来西亚、新加坡。

EN — **小突扭白蚁** (*Dicuspiditermes minutus*) is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Riaz in 1992; the type locality is Malaysia: Johore: Kluang Forest. Distribution: Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore.
