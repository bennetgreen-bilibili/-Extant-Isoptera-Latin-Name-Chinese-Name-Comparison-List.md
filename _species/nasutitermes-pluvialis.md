## 摘要

**招雨象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes pluvialis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Mjöberg 于 1920 年描述，模式产地为 Australia: Queensland: Blackall (as Blackal) Range, Mt. Tambourine, Herbertone, Malanda, Ravenshoe (as Cedar Creek)。分布记录包括澳大利亚。

EN — **招雨象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes pluvialis*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mjöberg in 1920; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Blackall (as Blackal) Range, Mt. Tambourine, Herbertone, Malanda, Ravenshoe (as Cedar Creek). Distribution: Australia.
