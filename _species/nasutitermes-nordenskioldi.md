## 摘要

**努登舍尔德象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes nordenskioldi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Holmgren 于 1910 年描述，模式产地为 Argentina: Jujuy: Quinta, near Laguna la Brea \[23.8589S 64.4377W\]; Santiago del Estero: Icaño \[28.68S 62.885W\]。分布记录包括阿根廷、巴拉圭、玻利维亚。

EN — **努登舍尔德象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes nordenskioldi*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Argentina: Jujuy: Quinta, near Laguna la Brea \[23.8589S 64.4377W\]; Santiago del Estero: Icaño \[28.68S 62.885W\]. Distribution: Argentina, Paraguay, Bolivia.
