## 摘要

**可恨土白蚁**（*Odontotermes maledictus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Ghidini 于 1941 年描述，模式产地为 Ethiopia: Caschei; Elolo; Gongabaino。分布记录包括埃塞俄比亚。

EN — **可恨土白蚁** (*Odontotermes maledictus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Ghidini in 1941; the type locality is Ethiopia: Caschei; Elolo; Gongabaino. Distribution: Ethiopia.
