## 摘要

**连接瘤白蚁**（*Mirocapritermes connectens*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）瘤白蚁属（*Mirocapritermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Holmgren 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat。分布记录包括东南亚、马来西亚、新加坡。

EN — **连接瘤白蚁** (*Mirocapritermes connectens*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Mirocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Singapore.
