## 摘要

**饼头鸟嘴白蚁**（*Mycterotermes meringocephalus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）鸟嘴白蚁属（*Mycterotermes*）的现存唯一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Sands 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 Yemen: Burum, W of Mukalla。分布记录包括阿曼、也门。

EN — **饼头鸟嘴白蚁** (*Mycterotermes meringocephalus*) is the only extant species of the genus *Mycterotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Sands in 1965; the type locality is Yemen: Burum, W of Mukalla. Distribution: Oman, Yemen.
