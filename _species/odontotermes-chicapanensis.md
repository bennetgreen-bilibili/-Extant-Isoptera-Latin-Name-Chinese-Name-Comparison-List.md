## 摘要

**希卡帕土白蚁**（*Odontotermes chicapanensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Weidner 于 1956 年描述，模式产地为 Angola: Étang Shá-Mutuka: Alto Chicapa, 400 km SSE of Dundo。分布记录包括非洲南部安哥拉。

EN — **希卡帕土白蚁** (*Odontotermes chicapanensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1956; the type locality is Angola: Étang Shá-Mutuka: Alto Chicapa, 400 km SSE of Dundo. Distribution: southern Africa, Angola.
