## 摘要

**似砖土白蚁**（*Odontotermes latericius*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Haviland 于 1898 年描述，模式产地为 South Africa: Natal: Estcourt District。分布记录包括旧热带界西非、东非、非洲南部。

EN — **似砖土白蚁** (*Odontotermes latericius*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Estcourt District. Distribution: Old World tropics (West Africa), East Africa, southern Africa.
