## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

EN — **瓦克代特亚种** (*Microcerotermes labioangulatus wahkdaitensis*) is a subspecies of *Microcerotermes labioangulatus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae), genus *Microcerotermes*. The subspecies was described by Das & Choudhury in 2020; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Wahkdait area, East Khasi Hills \[ 25.208N 91.99E \]. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Microcerotermes labioangulatus*.
