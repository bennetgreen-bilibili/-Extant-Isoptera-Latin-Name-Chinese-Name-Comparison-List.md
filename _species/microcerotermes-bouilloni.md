## 摘要

**布永锯白蚁**（*Microcerotermes bouilloni*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）锯白蚁亚科（Microcerotermitinae）锯白蚁属（*Microcerotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Roisin & Pasteels 于 2000 年描述，模式产地为 Papua New Guinea: Port Moresby: savanna next to University of Papua New Guinea campus。分布记录包括澳大利亚、巴布亚新几内亚。

EN — **布永锯白蚁** (*Microcerotermes bouilloni*) is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Roisin & Pasteels in 2000; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Port Moresby: savanna next to University of Papua New Guinea campus. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.
