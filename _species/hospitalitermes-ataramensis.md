## 摘要

**阿特兰须白蚁**（*Hospitalitermes ataramensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）须白蚁属（*Hospitalitermes*）的一种。本种由 Prashad & Sen-Sarma 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 Myanmar: Pakabo Reserve: Ataram。分布记录包括柬埔寨、泰国、越南、印度、缅甸。

EN — **阿特兰须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes ataramensis*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Prashad & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is Myanmar: Pakabo Reserve: Ataram. Distribution: Cambodia, Thailand, Vietnam, India, Myanmar.
