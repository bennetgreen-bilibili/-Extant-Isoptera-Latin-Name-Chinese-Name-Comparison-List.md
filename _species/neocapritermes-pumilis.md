## 摘要

**侏儒新扭白蚁**（*Neocapritermes pumilis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）新扭白蚁亚科（Neocapritermitinae）新扭白蚁属（*Neocapritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Constantino 于 1991 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Pará: Belém, Reserva da APEG \[ 1.44S 48.42W \]。分布记录包括哥伦比亚、巴西、法属圭亚那、厄瓜多尔。

EN — **侏儒新扭白蚁** (*Neocapritermes pumilis*) is a species of the genus *Neocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Neocapritermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Constantino in 1991; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Belém, Reserva da APEG \[ 1.44S 48.42W \]. Distribution: Colombia, Brazil, French Guiana, Ecuador.
