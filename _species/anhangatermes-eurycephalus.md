## 摘要

**宽头魔白蚁**（*Anhangatermes eurycephalus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）魔白蚁属（*Anhangatermes*）的一种。本种由 Oliveira & Constantino 于 2014 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Amazonas: Rodovia ZF3 km 41 \[2.445S 59.7833W\]。分布记录包括巴西、哥伦比亚、法属圭亚那。

EN — **宽头魔白蚁** (*Anhangatermes eurycephalus*) is a species of the genus *Anhangatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oliveira & Constantino in 2014; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Rodovia ZF3 km 41 \[2.445S 59.7833W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana.
