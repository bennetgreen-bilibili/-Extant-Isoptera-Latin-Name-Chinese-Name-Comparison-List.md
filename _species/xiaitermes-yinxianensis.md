EN — **鄞县夏氏白蚁** (*Xiaitermes yinxianensis*) is the only extant species of the genus *Xiaitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gao & He in 1994; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Yinxia. Distribution: China. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
