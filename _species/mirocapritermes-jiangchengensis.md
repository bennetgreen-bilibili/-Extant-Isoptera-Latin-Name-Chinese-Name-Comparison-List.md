## 摘要

**江城瘤白蚁**（*Mirocapritermes jiangchengensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）瘤白蚁属（*Mirocapritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Yang, Zhu & Huang 于 1995 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province。分布记录包括中国、云南。

EN — **江城瘤白蚁** (*Mirocapritermes jiangchengensis*) is a species of the genus *Mirocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1995; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province. Distribution: China, Yunnan.
