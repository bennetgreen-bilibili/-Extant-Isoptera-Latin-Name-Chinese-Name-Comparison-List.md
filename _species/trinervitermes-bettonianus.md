## 摘要

**贝顿三脉白蚁**（*Trinervitermes bettonianus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）三脉白蚁属（*Trinervitermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1905 年描述，模式产地为 Kenya: Athi River。分布记录包括纳米比亚、刚果（布）、肯尼亚、刚果民主共和国、马拉维、莫桑比克、尼日利亚、坦桑尼亚、乌干达、赞比亚、津巴布韦。本站记录本种有 3 个次异名。

EN — **贝顿三脉白蚁** (*Trinervitermes bettonianus*) is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1905; the type locality is Kenya: Athi River. Distribution: Namibia, Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Kenya, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Malawi, Mozambique, Nigeria, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.
