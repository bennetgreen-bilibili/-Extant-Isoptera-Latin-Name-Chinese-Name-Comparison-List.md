## 摘要

**榴莲岛瓢白蚁**（*Bulbitermes durianensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）瓢白蚁属（*Bulbitermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Maiti 于 1966 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Riouw (Rhio) Archipelago: Durian。分布记录包括印度尼西亚。

EN — **榴莲岛瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes durianensis*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Maiti in 1966; the type locality is Indonesia: Riouw (Rhio) Archipelago: Durian. Distribution: Indonesia.
