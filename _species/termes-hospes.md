## 摘要

**宿主白蚁**（*Termes hospes*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）白蚁属（*Termes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1900 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Bonge。分布记录包括旧热带界、刚果民主共和国、塞内加尔、喀麦隆、加蓬、加纳、几内亚、科特迪瓦、肯尼亚、尼日利亚、乌干达。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **宿主白蚁** (*Termes hospes*) is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Cameroon: Bonge. Distribution: Old World tropics, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Senegal, Cameroon, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya, Nigeria, Uganda. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
