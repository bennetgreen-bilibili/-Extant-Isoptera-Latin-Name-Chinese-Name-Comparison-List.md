## 摘要

**小腔腔白蚁**（*Cavitermes parvicavus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）腔白蚁属（*Cavitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]。分布记录包括巴西、哥伦比亚、厄瓜多尔。

EN — **小腔腔白蚁** (*Cavitermes parvicavus*) is a species of the genus *Cavitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador.
