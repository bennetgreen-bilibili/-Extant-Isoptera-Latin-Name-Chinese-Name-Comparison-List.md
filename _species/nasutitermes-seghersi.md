## 摘要

**西格象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes seghersi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Roisin & Pasteels 于 1996 年描述，模式产地为 Papua New Guinea: Madang Province: Yoro。分布记录包括坦桑尼亚、巴布亚新几内亚。

EN — **西格象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes seghersi*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roisin & Pasteels in 1996; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Madang Province: Yoro. Distribution: Tanzania, Papua New Guinea.
