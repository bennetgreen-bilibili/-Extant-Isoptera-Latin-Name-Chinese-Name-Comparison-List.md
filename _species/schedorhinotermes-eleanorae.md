## 摘要

**埃莉诺长鼻白蚁**（*Schedorhinotermes eleanorae*）是鼻白蚁科（RHINOTERMITIDAE）长鼻白蚁属（*Schedorhinotermes*）的一种，隶属新等翅类（Neoisoptera）。本种由 Roonwal & Bose 于 1970 年描述，模式产地为 India: Andaman Islands: Little Andaman Island: 19 km south of Toikoibuea, Bedeabdulu。分布记录包括印度。

The species was described by Roonwal & Bose in 1970; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: Little Andaman Island: 19 km south of Toikoibuea, Bedeabdulu. Distribution: India.
