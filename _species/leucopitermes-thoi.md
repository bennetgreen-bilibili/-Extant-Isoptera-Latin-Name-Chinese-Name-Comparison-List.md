## 摘要

**索白脸白蚁**（*Leucopitermes thoi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）白脸白蚁属（*Leucopitermes*）的一种。本种由 Gathorne-Hardy 于 2004 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Sumatra: Aceh Tenggara: Ketambe field station。分布记录包括印度尼西亚、马来西亚。

EN — **索白脸白蚁** (*Leucopitermes thoi*) is a species of the genus *Leucopitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Gathorne-Hardy in 2004; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Aceh Tenggara: Ketambe field station. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.
