## 摘要

**朝比奈须白蚁**（*Hospitalitermes asahinai*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）须白蚁属（*Hospitalitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Morimoto 于 1973 年描述，模式产地为 Thailand: Khao Chong。分布记录包括泰国。

EN — **朝比奈须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes asahinai*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Morimoto in 1973; the type locality is Thailand: Khao Chong. Distribution: Thailand.
