## 摘要

**马查多发白蚁**（*Trichotermes machadoi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）发白蚁属（*Trichotermes*）的一种。本种由 Weidner 于 1974 年描述，模式产地为 Angola: Dundo: Caieno, "affl. du Canzololo, rive dr. du Luachimo."。分布记录包括旧热带界。

EN — **马查多发白蚁** (*Trichotermes machadoi*) is a species of the genus *Trichotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Weidner in 1974; the type locality is Angola: Dundo: Caieno, "affl. du Canzololo, rive dr. du Luachimo.". Distribution: Old World tropics.
