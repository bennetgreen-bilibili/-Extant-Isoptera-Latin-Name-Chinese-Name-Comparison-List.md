## 摘要

**凹额土白蚁**（*Odontotermes foveafrons*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Xia & Fan 于 1982 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Yangwu。分布记录包括云南（大勐龙、橄榄坝、思茅）、中国。

EN — **凹额土白蚁** (*Odontotermes foveafrons*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Xia & Fan in 1982; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Yangwu. Distribution: Yunnan (Damenglong, Ganlanba, Simao), China.
