## 摘要

**劳拉利纳异白蚁**（*Heterotermes lauralinearum*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）异白蚁属（*Heterotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Carrijo 于 2020 年描述，模式产地为 Paraguay: Presidente Hayes: NW Rio Verde \[ 22.8358S 59.5608W \]。分布记录包括阿根廷、玻利维亚、巴拉圭。

EN — **劳拉利纳异白蚁** (*Heterotermes lauralinearum*) is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Carrijo in 2020; the type locality is Paraguay: Presidente Hayes: NW Rio Verde \[ 22.8358S 59.5608W \]. Distribution: Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay.
