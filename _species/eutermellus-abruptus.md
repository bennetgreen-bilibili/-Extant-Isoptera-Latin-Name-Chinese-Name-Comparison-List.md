EN — **陡峭小真白蚁** (*Eutermellus abruptus*) is a species of the genus *Eutermellus*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1965; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Njili, 18 km from Kinshasa (Leopoldville). Distribution: tropical Africa.
