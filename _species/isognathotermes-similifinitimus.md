## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

EN — **似近邻等颚白蚁** (*Isognathotermes similifinitimus*) is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Josens & Deligne in 2025; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Yoko \[ 0.2937N 25.2962E \]. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.

ES — **似近邻等颚白蚁** (*Isognathotermes similifinitimus*) es una especie del género *Isognathotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Cubitermitinae).
