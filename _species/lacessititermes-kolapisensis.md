## 摘要

**戈拉皮斯怒白蚁**（*Lacessititermes kolapisensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）怒白蚁属（*Lacessititermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Thapa 于 1982 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Sabah: Sandakan North District: Kolapis Forest, mile 4。分布记录包括马来西亚沙巴。

EN — **戈拉皮斯怒白蚁** (*Lacessititermes kolapisensis*) is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Sandakan North District: Kolapis Forest, mile 4. Distribution: Malaysia (Sabah).
