## 摘要

**栖球土白蚁**（*Odontotermes globicola*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Wasmann 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 Sri Lanka: Anurhadhapura。分布记录包括南亚印度、巴基斯坦、斯里兰卡。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **栖球土白蚁** (*Odontotermes globicola*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Anurhadhapura. Distribution: South Asia, India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
