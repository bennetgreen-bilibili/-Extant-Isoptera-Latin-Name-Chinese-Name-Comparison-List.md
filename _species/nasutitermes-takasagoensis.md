## 摘要

**高砂象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes takasagoensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Nawa 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Japan: Loo Choo (Ryukyu) Islands: Ishigakijima。分布记录包括日本、关岛、中国台湾、圣诞岛。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **高砂象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes takasagoensis*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Nawa in 1911; the type locality is Japan: Loo Choo (Ryukyu) Islands: Ishigakijima. Distribution: Japan, Guam, Taiwan, China, Christmas Island. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
