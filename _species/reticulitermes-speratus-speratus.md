EN — **指名亚种** (*Reticulitermes speratus speratus*) is a subspecies of *Reticulitermes speratus*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, genus *Reticulitermes*. The subspecies was described by Kolbe in 1885; the type locality is Japan: Hakodate, Kaga-Yashiki, Mohezi, Yedo. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Reticulitermes speratus*.

EN — *Reticulitermes*: From Latin reticulum (little net) + termes.

ES — *Reticulitermes*: Del latín reticulum (red diminuta) + termes.

种加词 *speratus*：拉丁文 speratus（被期望的）

EN — epithet *speratus*: Latin speratus (hoped for)

ES — epithet *speratus*: Del latín speratus (esperado)
