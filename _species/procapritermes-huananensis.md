## 摘要

**华南前扭白蚁**（*Procapritermes huananensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）前扭白蚁属（*Procapritermes*）的一种。本种由 Yu & Ping 于 1966 年描述，模式产地为 China: Hainan Island: Tan-hsien。分布记录包括广西、云南（盈江870 m）、海南（琼中黎母岭350 m、五指山700 m）、中国。

EN — **华南前扭白蚁** (*Procapritermes huananensis*) is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yu & Ping in 1966; the type locality is China: Hainan Island: Tan-hsien. Distribution: Guangxi, Yunnan (Yingjiang 870 m), Hainan (Qiongzhong Limuling 350 m, Wuzhi Mountain 700 m), China.
