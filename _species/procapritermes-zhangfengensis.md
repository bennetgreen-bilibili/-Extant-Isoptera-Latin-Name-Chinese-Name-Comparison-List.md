## 摘要

**章凤前扭白蚁**（*Procapritermes zhangfengensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）前扭白蚁属（*Procapritermes*）的一种。本种由 Yang, Zhu & Huang 于 1995 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Longchuan County: Zhangfeng。分布记录包括云南（陇川890 m）、中国。

EN — **章凤前扭白蚁** (*Procapritermes zhangfengensis*) is a species of the genus *Procapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Yang, Zhu & Huang in 1995; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Longchuan County: Zhangfeng. Distribution: Yunnan (Longchuan890 m), China.
