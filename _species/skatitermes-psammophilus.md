## 摘要

**喜沙洁白蚁**（*Skatitermes psammophilus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）洁白蚁属（*Skatitermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Coaton 于 1971 年描述，模式产地为 Namibia: 64.3 km ex Gobabis-Epukiro。分布记录包括非洲南部南非。

EN — **喜沙洁白蚁** (*Skatitermes psammophilus*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Skatitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Coaton in 1971; the type locality is Namibia: 64.3 km ex Gobabis-Epukiro. Distribution: southern Africa (South Africa).
