## 摘要

**布永爪白蚁**（*Unguitermes bouilloni*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）爪白蚁属（*Unguitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Harris 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Takundi: River Kwando。分布记录包括刚果民主共和国。

EN — **布永爪白蚁** (*Unguitermes bouilloni*) is a species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Harris in 1965; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Takundi: River Kwando. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.
