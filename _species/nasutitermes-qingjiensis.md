## 摘要

**庆界象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes qingjiensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Li 于 1979 年描述，模式产地为 China: Zhejiang Province: Longquan County: Ruixiang Commune: Qingjie Brigade, Huangnikeng。分布记录包括中国。

EN — **庆界象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes qingjiensis*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Li in 1979; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Longquan County: Ruixiang Commune: Qingjie Brigade, Huangnikeng. Distribution: China.

## 词源

属名 *Nasutitermes*：属名由拉丁文 nasutus（大鼻的、具鼻突的）与 termes（白蚁）组合，指兵蚁具鼻状额突。

EN — *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

种加词 *qingjiensis*：以地名 清江（湖北） 命名

EN — epithet *qingjiensis*: Named after the locality Qingj
