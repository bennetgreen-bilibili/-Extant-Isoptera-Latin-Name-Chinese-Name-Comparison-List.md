## 摘要

**圣克鲁斯新白蚁**（*Neotermes sanctaecrucis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）新白蚁属（*Neotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Snyder 于 1925 年描述，模式产地为 Solomon Islands: Santa Cruz Archipelago: Graciosa Bay。分布记录包括所罗门群岛、瓦努阿图。

EN — **圣克鲁斯新白蚁** (*Neotermes sanctaecrucis*) is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Snyder in 1925; the type locality is Solomon Islands: Santa Cruz Archipelago: Graciosa Bay. Distribution: Solomon Islands, Vanuatu.
