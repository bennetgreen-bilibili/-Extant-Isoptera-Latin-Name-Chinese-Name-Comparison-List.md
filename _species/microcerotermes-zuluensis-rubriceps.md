EN — **红头亚种** (*Microcerotermes zuluensis rubriceps*) is a subspecies of *Microcerotermes zuluensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae), genus *Microcerotermes*. The subspecies was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is South Africa: Zululand: Mkosi. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Microcerotermes zuluensis*.

ES — **红头亚种** (*Microcerotermes zuluensis rubriceps*) es una subespecie de *Microcerotermes zuluensis*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Microcerotermitinae), género *Microcerotermes*.
