## 摘要

**高山土白蚁**（*Odontotermes montanus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Harris 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 Kenya: on the Kikuyu Escarpment, Nairobi-Naivasha road。分布记录包括东非肯尼亚、埃塞俄比亚。

EN — **高山土白蚁** (*Odontotermes montanus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Harris in 1960; the type locality is Kenya: on the Kikuyu Escarpment, Nairobi-Naivasha road. Distribution: East Africa, Kenya, Ethiopia.
