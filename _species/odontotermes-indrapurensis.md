## 摘要

**因德拉普拉土白蚁**（*Odontotermes indrapurensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Holmgren 于 1913 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong: Indrapura Estate。分布记录包括印度尼西亚。

EN — **因德拉普拉土白蚁** (*Odontotermes indrapurensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong: Indrapura Estate. Distribution: Indonesia.
