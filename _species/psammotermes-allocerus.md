## 摘要

**奇白沙白蚁**（*Psammotermes allocerus*）是沙白蚁科（PSAMMOTERMITIDAE）沙白蚁属（*Psammotermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1908 年描述，模式产地为 Namibia: Lüderitz (Namaland: Lüderitzbucht)。分布记录包括非洲南部、纳米比亚、南非、安哥拉、博茨瓦纳、埃及。

EN — **奇白沙白蚁** (*Psammotermes allocerus*) is a species of the genus *Psammotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1908; the type locality is Namibia: Lüderitz (Namaland: Lüderitzbucht). Distribution: southern Africa, Namibia, South Africa, Angola, Botswana, Egypt.
