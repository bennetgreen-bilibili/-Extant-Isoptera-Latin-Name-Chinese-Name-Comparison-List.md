## 摘要

**砂拉越土白蚁**（*Odontotermes sarawakensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Holmgren 于 1913 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Sarawak。分布记录包括东南亚马来西亚、印度尼西亚、泰国。

EN — **砂拉越土白蚁** (*Odontotermes sarawakensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand.
