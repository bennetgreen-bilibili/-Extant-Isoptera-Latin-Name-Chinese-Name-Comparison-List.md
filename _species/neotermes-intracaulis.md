## 摘要

**茎内新白蚁**（*Neotermes intracaulis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）新白蚁属（*Neotermes*）的一种。本种由 Scheffrahn & Krecek 于 2003 年描述，模式产地为 Virgin Islands (USA): St. Croix: Sion Hill。分布记录包括加勒比、圣巴泰勒米、瓜德罗普、阿鲁巴、巴巴多斯、博奈尔、法属圣马丁、美属维尔京群岛、哥伦比亚、委内瑞拉。

EN — **茎内新白蚁** (*Neotermes intracaulis*) is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 2003; the type locality is Virgin Islands (USA): St. Croix: Sion Hill. Distribution: Caribbean, Saint Barthélemy, Guadeloupe, Aruba, Barbados, Bonaire, French Saint Martin, U.S. Virgin Islands, Colombia, Venezuela.
