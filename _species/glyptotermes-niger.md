## 摘要

**黑色树白蚁**（*Glyptotermes niger*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）树白蚁属（*Glyptotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Kemner 于 1934 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Java: south coast, near Wijnkoopsbaai, Tjisolok。分布记录包括印度尼西亚。

EN — **黑色树白蚁** (*Glyptotermes niger*) is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: south coast, near Wijnkoopsbaai, Tjisolok. Distribution: Indonesia.
