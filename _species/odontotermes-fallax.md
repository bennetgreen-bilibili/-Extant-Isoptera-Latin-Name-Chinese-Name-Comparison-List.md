## 摘要

**欺诈土白蚁**（*Odontotermes fallax*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1924 年描述，模式产地为 Tanzania: Meru Mt。分布记录包括东非坦桑尼亚、肯尼亚、埃塞俄比亚。

EN — **欺诈土白蚁** (*Odontotermes fallax*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Tanzania: Meru Mt. Distribution: East Africa, Tanzania, Kenya, Ethiopia.
