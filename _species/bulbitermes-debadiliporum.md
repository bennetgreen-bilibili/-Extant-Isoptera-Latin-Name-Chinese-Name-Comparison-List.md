## 摘要

**德巴迪利普瓢白蚁**（*Bulbitermes debadiliporum*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）瓢白蚁属（*Bulbitermes*）的一种。本种由 Das & Choudhury 于 2023 年描述，模式产地为 India: Meghalaya: Mawlynnong, East Khasi Hills \[25.2047N 91.9112E\]。分布记录包括东南亚、印度尼西亚、马来西亚、新加坡、菲律宾、越南。

EN — **德巴迪利普瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes debadiliporum*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Das & Choudhury in 2023; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Mawlynnong, East Khasi Hills \[25.2047N 91.9112E\]. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines, Vietnam.

ES — **德巴迪利普瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes debadiliporum*) es una especie del género *Bulbitermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Nasutitermitinae).
