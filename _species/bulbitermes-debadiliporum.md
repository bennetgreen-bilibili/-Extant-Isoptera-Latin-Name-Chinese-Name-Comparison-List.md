EN — **德巴迪利普瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes debadiliporum*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Das & Choudhury in 2023; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Mawlynnong, East Khasi Hills \[25.2047N 91.9112E\]. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines, Vietnam.
