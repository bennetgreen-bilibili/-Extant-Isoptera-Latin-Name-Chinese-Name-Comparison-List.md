## 摘要

**德兰士瓦方白蚁**（*Cubitermes transvaalensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）方白蚁属（*Cubitermes*）的一种。本种由 Fuller 于 1925 年描述，模式产地为 South Africa: Transvaal: De Wildt。分布记录包括非洲南部、南非德兰士瓦、赞比亚。

EN — **德兰士瓦方白蚁** (*Cubitermes transvaalensis*) is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: De Wildt. Distribution: southern Africa, Transvaal, South Africa, Zambia.
