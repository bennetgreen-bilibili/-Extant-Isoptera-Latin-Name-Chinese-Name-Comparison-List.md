## 摘要

**尼韦迪塔锡兰白蚁**（*Ceylonitermes nivedita*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）锡兰白蚁属（*Ceylonitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Basak, Sengupta, Rajmohana & Baraik 于 2020 年描述，模式产地为 India: Manipur: Tamenglong, Zeilad Wildlife Sanctuary \[24.9109N 93.3842E\]。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **尼韦迪塔锡兰白蚁** (*Ceylonitermes nivedita*) is a species of the genus *Ceylonitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Basak, Sengupta, Rajmohana & Baraik in 2020; the type locality is India: Manipur: Tamenglong, Zeilad Wildlife Sanctuary \[24.9109N 93.3842E\]. Distribution: India.
