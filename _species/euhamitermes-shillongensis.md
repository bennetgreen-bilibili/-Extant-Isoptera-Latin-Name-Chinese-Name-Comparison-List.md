## 摘要

**西隆亮白蚁**（*Euhamitermes shillongensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）亮白蚁属（*Euhamitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Roonwal & Chhotani 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 India: Assam: Shillong。分布记录包括南亚、印度梅加拉亚。

EN — **西隆亮白蚁** (*Euhamitermes shillongensis*) is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1960; the type locality is India: Assam: Shillong. Distribution: South Asia, Meghalaya, India.
