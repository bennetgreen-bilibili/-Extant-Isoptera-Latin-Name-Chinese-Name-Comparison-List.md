## 摘要

**小额堆砂白蚁**（*Cryptotermes parvifrons*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）堆砂白蚁属（*Cryptotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Scheffrahn & Krecek 于 1999 年描述，模式产地为 Trinidad and Tobago: Diego Martin: William's Bay \[ 10.6833N 61.6167W \]。分布记录包括格林纳达、特立尼达和多巴哥。

EN — **小额堆砂白蚁** (*Cryptotermes parvifrons*) is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: Diego Martin: William's Bay \[ 10.6833N 61.6167W \]. Distribution: Grenada, Trinidad and Tobago.
