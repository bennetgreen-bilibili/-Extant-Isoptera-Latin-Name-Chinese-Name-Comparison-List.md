EN — **爪哇土白蚁** (*Odontotermes javanicus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1912; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Salak, Buitenzorg. Distribution: Southeast Asia, East Asia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, China.
