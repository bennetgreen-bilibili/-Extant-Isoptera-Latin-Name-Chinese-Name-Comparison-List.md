## 摘要

**毛多近扭白蚁**（*Pericapritermes pilosus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）近扭白蚁属（*Pericapritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Bourguignon & Roisin 于 2008 年描述，模式产地为 Papua New Guinea: Southern Highlands: Pimaga。分布记录包括巴布亚新几内亚。

EN — **毛多近扭白蚁** (*Pericapritermes pilosus*) is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2008; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Southern Highlands: Pimaga. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.
