## 摘要

**圣卢西亚方白蚁**（*Cubitermes sanctaeluciae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）方白蚁属（*Cubitermes*）的一种。本种由 Fuller 于 1925 年描述，模式产地为 South Africa: Natal: Nibele on the N extremity of St. Lucia。分布记录包括非洲南部、南非、赞比亚。

EN — **圣卢西亚方白蚁** (*Cubitermes sanctaeluciae*) is a species of the genus *Cubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1925; the type locality is South Africa: Natal: Nibele on the N extremity of St. Lucia. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa, Zambia.
