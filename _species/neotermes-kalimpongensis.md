**噶伦堡新白蚁**（*Neotermes kalimpongensis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）新白蚁属（*Neotermes*）的一种。本种由 Maiti 于 1975 年描述，模式产地为 India: West Bengal: Darjeeling District: Kalimpong: Bong Bosti。分布记录包括印度、西孟加拉。

EN — **噶伦堡新白蚁** (*Neotermes kalimpongensis*) is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Maiti in 1975; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Darjeeling District: Kalimpong: Bong Bosti. Distribution: India, West Bengal.
