## 摘要

**加丹加大颚白蚁**（*Megagnathotermes katangensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）大颚白蚁属（*Megagnathotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1927 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Lubumbashi。分布记录包括非洲、刚果民主共和国加丹加、赞比亚。

EN — **加丹加大颚白蚁** (*Megagnathotermes katangensis*) is a species of the genus *Megagnathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1927; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Lubumbashi. Distribution: Africa, Katanga, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia.
