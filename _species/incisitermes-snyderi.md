## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

**斯奈德楹白蚁**（*Incisitermes snyderi*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）楹白蚁属（*Incisitermes*）的一种。本种由 Light 于 1933 年描述，模式产地为 United States: Texas: Cameron County: Brownsville: Fort Brown。分布记录包括美国、墨西哥、巴哈马、百慕大、哥斯达黎加、古巴、巴拿马、波多黎各、特克斯和凯科斯群岛、英属维尔京群岛、美属维尔京群岛。

EN — **斯奈德楹白蚁** (*Incisitermes snyderi*) is a species of the genus *Incisitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Light in 1933; the type locality is United States: Texas: Cameron County: Brownsville: Fort Brown. Distribution: United States, Mexico, Bahamas, Bermuda, Costa Rica, Cuba, Panama, Puerto Rico, Turks and Caicos Islands, British Virgin Islands, U.S. Virgin Islands.

ES — **斯奈德楹白蚁** (*Incisitermes snyderi*) es una especie del género *Incisitermes*, en la familia KALOTERMITIDAE. La especie fue descrita por Light en 1933; la localidad tipo es United States: Texas: Cameron County: Brownsville: Fort Brown. Distribución: United States, Mexico, Bahamas, Bermuda, Costa Rica, Cuba, Panama, Puerto Rico, Turks and Caicos Islands, British Virgin Islands, U.S. Virgin Islands.
