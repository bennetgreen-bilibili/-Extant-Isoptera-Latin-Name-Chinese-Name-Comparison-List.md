## 摘要

**高兴无刃白蚁**（*Astratotermes hilarus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）无刃白蚁属（*Astratotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg。分布记录包括中非刚果盆地、东非、喀麦隆、刚果民主共和国、坦桑尼亚。

EN — **高兴无刃白蚁** (*Astratotermes hilarus*) is a species of the genus *Astratotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg. Distribution: Central African Congo Basin, East Africa, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tanzania.
