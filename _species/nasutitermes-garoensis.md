## 摘要

**山地象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes garoensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Chhotani 于 1962 年描述，模式产地为 India: Assam: Garo Hills District: Rongrengiri。分布记录包括不丹、中国、印度。

EN — **山地象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes garoensis*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Assam: Garo Hills District: Rongrengiri. Distribution: Bhutan, China, India.
