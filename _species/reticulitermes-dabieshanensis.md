## 摘要

EN — **大别山散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes dabieshanensis*) is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Wang & Li in 1984; the type locality is China: Henan Province: Shangcheng County: Borenao Mountain. Distribution: China.
