## 摘要

**比特铁克锯白蚁**（*Microcerotermes buettikeri*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）锯白蚁亚科（Microcerotermitinae）锯白蚁属（*Microcerotermes*）的一种。本种由 Chhotani & Bose 于 1979 年描述，模式产地为 Saudi Arabia: Wadi Tumair。分布记录包括中东、沙特阿拉伯、伊朗、也门。

EN — **比特铁克锯白蚁** (*Microcerotermes buettikeri*) is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Chhotani & Bose in 1979; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Wadi Tumair. Distribution: Middle East, Saudi Arabia, Iran, Yemen.
