## 摘要

**尼罗土白蚁**（*Odontotermes nilensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Emerson 于 1949 年描述，模式产地为 Sudan: Kaka。分布记录包括西非塞内加尔、刚果民主共和国、苏丹。

EN — **尼罗土白蚁** (*Odontotermes nilensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Sudan: Kaka. Distribution: West Africa, Senegal, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan.
