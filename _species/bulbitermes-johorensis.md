## 摘要

**柔佛瓢白蚁**（*Bulbitermes johorensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）瓢白蚁属（*Bulbitermes*）的一种。本种由 Akhtar & Pervez 于 1986 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Johore: 20 km N of Mersing: Guong Arong Forest Reserve。分布记录包括马来西亚。

EN — **柔佛瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes johorensis*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Pervez in 1986; the type locality is Malaysia: Johore: 20 km N of Mersing: Guong Arong Forest Reserve. Distribution: Malaysia.
