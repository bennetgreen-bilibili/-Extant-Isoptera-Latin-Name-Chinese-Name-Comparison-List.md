The species was described by Snyder in 1926; the type locality is Colombia: Magdalena: Río Frio \[ 11.1N 74.1833W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Venezuela.

ES — **长背新扭白蚁** (*Neocapritermes longinotus*) es una especie del género *Neocapritermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Neocapritermitinae).
