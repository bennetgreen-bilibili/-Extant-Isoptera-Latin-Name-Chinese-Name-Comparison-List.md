## 摘要

**等角堆砂白蚁**（*Cryptotermes aequacornis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）堆砂白蚁属（*Cryptotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Scheffrahn & Krecek 于 1999 年描述，模式产地为 Trinidad and Tobago: San Juan-Laventille: near La Vache Bay \[ 10.746N 61.488W \]。分布记录包括特立尼达和多巴哥、委内瑞拉。

EN — **等角堆砂白蚁** (*Cryptotermes aequacornis*) is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: San Juan-Laventille: near La Vache Bay \[ 10.746N 61.488W \]. Distribution: Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela.
