## 摘要

**粗壮拜拉白蚁**（*Biratermes robustus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）聚白蚁亚科（Syntermitinae）拜拉白蚁属（*Biratermes*）的现存唯一种。本种由 Constantino 于 1993 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Amapá: Serra do Navio \[0.8955N 52.0022W\]。分布记录包括法属圭亚那、巴西。

EN — **粗壮拜拉白蚁** (*Biratermes robustus*) is the only extant species of the genus *Biratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Constantino in 1993; the type locality is Brasil: Amapá: Serra do Navio \[0.8955N 52.0022W\]. Distribution: French Guiana, Brazil.
