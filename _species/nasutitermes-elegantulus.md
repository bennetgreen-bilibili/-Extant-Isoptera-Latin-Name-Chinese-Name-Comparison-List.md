EN — **小雅象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes elegantulus*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe, Uganda. ## 词源 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Etymology</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Etimología</span>

EN — *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

种加词 *elegantulus*：拉丁文 elegantulus（精致小巧的）

EN — epithet *elegantulus*: Latin elegantulus (rather elegant)
