## 摘要

**小雅象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes elegantulus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Victoria。分布记录包括刚果（布）、安哥拉、喀麦隆、刚果民主共和国、加蓬、加纳、几内亚、尼日利亚、圣多美和普林西比、乌干达。本站记录本种有 3 个次异名。

EN — **小雅象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes elegantulus*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe, Uganda.
