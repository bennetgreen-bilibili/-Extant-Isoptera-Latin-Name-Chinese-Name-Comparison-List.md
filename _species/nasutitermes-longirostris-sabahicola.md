The subspecies was described by Engel & Krishna in 2013; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Lamag District: Tenegang Besar, mile 8. ## 词源 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Etymology</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Etimología</span>

属名 *Nasutitermes*：属名由拉丁文 nasutus（大鼻的、具鼻突的）与 termes（白蚁）组合，指兵蚁具鼻状额突。

EN — *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

种加词 *sabahicola*：以地名 Sabah（沙巴，马来西亚）＋拉丁文 -cola（栖）命名，指沙巴栖

EN — epithet *sabahicola*: Named after Sabah (Malaysia) + Latin -cola (dweller)
