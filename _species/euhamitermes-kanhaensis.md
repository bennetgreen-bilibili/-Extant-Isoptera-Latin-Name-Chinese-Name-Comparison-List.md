## 摘要

**甘哈亮白蚁**（*Euhamitermes kanhaensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）亮白蚁属（*Euhamitermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Chhotani 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 India: Madhya Pradesh: Mandla District: Kanha National Park, ca. 1 km N of Forest Rest House, coll. sta. No. 6。分布记录包括南亚、印度。

EN — **甘哈亮白蚁** (*Euhamitermes kanhaensis*) is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1965; the type locality is India: Madhya Pradesh: Mandla District: Kanha National Park, ca. 1 km N of Forest Rest House, coll. sta. No. 6. Distribution: South Asia, India.
