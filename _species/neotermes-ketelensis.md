## 摘要

**凯特拉新白蚁**（*Neotermes ketelensis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）新白蚁属（*Neotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Kemner 于 1932 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Djampea Island, Poeloe Ketela, between Sulawesi and Flores。分布记录包括印尼。

EN — **凯特拉新白蚁** (*Neotermes ketelensis*) is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Kemner in 1932; the type locality is Indonesia: Djampea Island, Poeloe Ketela, between Sulawesi and Flores. Distribution: Indonesia.
