## 摘要

**哥伦比亚堆砂白蚁**（*Cryptotermes colombianus*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）堆砂白蚁属（*Cryptotermes*）的一种。本种由 Casalla, Scheffrahn & Korb 于 2016 年描述，模式产地为 Colombia: Magdalena: Tayrona National Natural Park, Gairaca Bay \[ 11.3152N 74.1032W \]。分布记录包括哥伦比亚。

EN — **哥伦比亚堆砂白蚁** (*Cryptotermes colombianus*) is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Casalla, Scheffrahn & Korb in 2016; the type locality is Colombia: Magdalena: Tayrona National Natural Park, Gairaca Bay \[ 11.3152N 74.1032W \]. Distribution: Colombia.
