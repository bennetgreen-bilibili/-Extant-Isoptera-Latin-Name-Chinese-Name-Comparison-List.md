## 摘要

**眉妙印白蚁**（*Indotermes maymensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）印白蚁属（*Indotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Roonwal & Sen-Sarma 于 1958 年描述，模式产地为 Myanmar: Maymyo: Botanical Garden。分布记录包括缅甸、中国。

EN — **眉妙印白蚁** (*Indotermes maymensis*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1958; the type locality is Myanmar: Maymyo: Botanical Garden. Distribution: Myanmar, China.
