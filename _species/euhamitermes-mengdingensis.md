## 摘要

**孟定亮白蚁**（*Euhamitermes mengdingensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）亮白蚁属（*Euhamitermes*）的一种。本种由 Zhu & Li 于 1987 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Autonomous County of Gengma, Dai-Va。分布记录包括中国、云南。

EN — **孟定亮白蚁** (*Euhamitermes mengdingensis*) is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Li in 1987; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Autonomous County of Gengma, Dai-Va. Distribution: China, Yunnan.
