## 摘要

**丰特斯林白蚁**（*Caetetermes fontesi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）林白蚁属（*Caetetermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Scheffrahn 于 2022 年描述，模式产地为 French Guiana: Petit Saut \[5.07N 53.032W\]。分布记录包括南美洲、法属圭亚那。

EN — **丰特斯林白蚁** (*Caetetermes fontesi*) is a species of the genus *Caetetermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2022; the type locality is French Guiana: Petit Saut \[5.07N 53.032W\]. Distribution: South America, French Guiana.
