## 摘要

**大近扭白蚁**（*Pericapritermes tetraphilus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）近扭白蚁属（*Pericapritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Silvestri 于 1922 年描述，模式产地为 Bangladesh: Chittagong Hill Tracts: Rangamati。分布记录包括广东（汕头、韶关），福建（平和），云南（允景洪500m、河口90m、屏边、金平猛喇370、420、450、680m、墨江1400m、三台山、思茅、蛮允）、印度、孟加拉国、缅甸。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **大近扭白蚁** (*Pericapritermes tetraphilus*) is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Silvestri in 1922; the type locality is Bangladesh: Chittagong Hill Tracts: Rangamati. Distribution: Guangdong (Shantou, Shaoguan), Fujian (Pinghe), Yunnan (Yunjinghong 500 m, Hekou 90 m, Pingbian, Jinping Mengla 370, 420, 450, 680 m, Mojiang 1400 m, Santaishan, Simao, Manyun); India; Bangladesh; Myanmar. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
