## 摘要

**乌干达等颚白蚁**（*Isognathotermes ugandensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）等颚白蚁属（*Isognathotermes*）的一种。本种由 Fuller 于 1923 年描述，模式产地为 Uganda: "Nkulukuku."。分布记录包括乌干达、刚果民主共和国、肯尼亚、马拉维、卢旺达、坦桑尼亚、赞比亚。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **乌干达等颚白蚁** (*Isognathotermes ugandensis*) is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Fuller in 1923; the type locality is Uganda: "Nkulukuku.". Distribution: Uganda, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
