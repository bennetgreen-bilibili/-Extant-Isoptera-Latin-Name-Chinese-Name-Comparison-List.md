## 摘要

**短齿壶头白蚁**（*Cyrilliotermes brevidens*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）聚白蚁亚科（Syntermitinae）壶头白蚁属（*Cyrilliotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Constantino & Carvalho 于 2012 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Minas Gerais: Lagoa Santa \[19.63S 43.88W\]。分布记录包括巴西。

EN — **短齿壶头白蚁** (*Cyrilliotermes brevidens*) is a species of the genus *Cyrilliotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Constantino & Carvalho in 2012; the type locality is Brasil: Minas Gerais: Lagoa Santa \[19.63S 43.88W\]. Distribution: Brazil.
