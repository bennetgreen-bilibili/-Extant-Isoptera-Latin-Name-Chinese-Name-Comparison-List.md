## 摘要

**常见前奇白蚁**（*Promirotermes redundans*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）前奇白蚁亚科（Promirotermitinae）前奇白蚁属（*Promirotermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Guinea: Mamou。分布记录包括喀麦隆、多哥、冈比亚、加纳、几内亚、尼日利亚、塞内加尔。

EN — **常见前奇白蚁** (*Promirotermes redundans*) is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Mamou. Distribution: Cameroon, Togo, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, Senegal.
