## 摘要

**乌彭巴小白蚁**（*Microtermes upembae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）小白蚁属（*Microtermes*）的一种。本种由 Harris 于 1958 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: "Parc National de l’Upemba, River Lupiala, affl. dr. Lufira."。分布记录包括中非、刚果民主共和国。

EN — **乌彭巴小白蚁** (*Microtermes upembae*) is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1958; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: "Parc National de l’Upemba, River Lupiala, affl. dr. Lufira.". Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo.
