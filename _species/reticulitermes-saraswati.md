## 摘要

**萨拉斯瓦特散白蚁**（*Reticulitermes saraswati*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）散白蚁属（*Reticulitermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Chhotani 于 1962 年描述，模式产地为 India: Meghalaya: Khasi-Jaintia Hills District: Shillong。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **萨拉斯瓦特散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes saraswati*) is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1962; the type locality is India: Meghalaya: Khasi-Jaintia Hills District: Shillong. Distribution: India.
