## 摘要

**摩洛哥小草白蚁**（*Microhodotermes maroccanus*）是草白蚁科（HODOTERMITIDAE）小草白蚁属（*Microhodotermes*）的一种，隶属远等翅类（Teletisoptera）。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1926 年描述，模式产地为 Morocco: Dar Kaïd; Dar Kaïd Enbarck Imi; M’tougui; N’takandout。分布记录包括摩洛哥、阿尔及利亚。

EN — **摩洛哥小草白蚁** (*Microhodotermes maroccanus*) is a species of the genus *Microhodotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Teletisoptera. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1926; the type locality is Morocco: Dar Kaïd; Dar Kaïd Enbarck Imi; M’tougui; N’takandout. Distribution: Morocco, Algeria.
