EN — **和平顺白蚁** (*Acholotermes imbellis*) is a species of the genus *Acholotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribution: tropical Africa.
