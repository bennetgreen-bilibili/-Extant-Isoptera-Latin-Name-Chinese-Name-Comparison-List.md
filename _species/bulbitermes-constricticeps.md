EN — **缩头瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes constricticeps*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Light & Wilson in 1936; the type locality is Philippines: Mindanao: Zamboanga Province: Caldera Bay Mountains. Distribution: Philippines.
