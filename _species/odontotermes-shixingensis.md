**始兴土白蚁**（*Odontotermes shixingensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Ping & Xu 于 1993 年描述，模式产地为 China: Guangdong Province: Shixing County: Wuzhishan of Chebaling。分布记录包括中国广东。

EN — **始兴土白蚁** (*Odontotermes shixingensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1993; the type locality is China: Guangdong Province: Shixing County: Wuzhishan of Chebaling. Distribution: China (Guangdong).

ES — **始兴土白蚁** (*Odontotermes shixingensis*) es una especie del género *Odontotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Macrotermitinae).
