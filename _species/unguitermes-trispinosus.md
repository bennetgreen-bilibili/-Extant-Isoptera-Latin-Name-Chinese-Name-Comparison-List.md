EN — **三针爪白蚁** (*Unguitermes trispinosus*) is a species of the genus *Unguitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Ruelle in 1973; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: tropical Africa.
