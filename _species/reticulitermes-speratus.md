## 摘要

**栖北散白蚁**（*Reticulitermes speratus*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）散白蚁属（*Reticulitermes*）的一种。本种由 Kolbe 于 1885 年描述，模式产地为 Japan: Hakodate, Kaga-Yashiki, Mohezi, Yedo。分布记录包括日本、朝鲜半岛、中国东北部、台湾、俄罗斯、美国。

EN — **栖北散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes speratus*) is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Kolbe in 1885; the type locality is Japan: Hakodate, Kaga-Yashiki, Mohezi, Yedo. Distribution: Japan, Korean Peninsula, northeastern China, Taiwan, Russia, United States.

ES — **栖北散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes speratus*) es una especie del género *Reticulitermes*, en la familia HETEROTERMITIDAE.
