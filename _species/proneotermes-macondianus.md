The species was described by Casalla, Scheffrahn & Korb in 2016; the type locality is Colombia: Magdalena: Tayrona National Natural Park, Gairaca Bay \[ 11.3152N 74.1032W \]. Distribution: Colombia.

ES — **马康多前新白蚁** (*Proneotermes macondianus*) es una especie del género *Proneotermes*, en la familia KALOTERMITIDAE.
