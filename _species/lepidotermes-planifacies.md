## 摘要

**扁形鳞白蚁**（*Lepidotermes planifacies*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）鳞白蚁属（*Lepidotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Williams 于 1954 年描述，模式产地为 Tanzania: Tanga District: Ngomeni。分布记录包括非洲南部、南非。

EN — **扁形鳞白蚁** (*Lepidotermes planifacies*) is a species of the genus *Lepidotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Williams in 1954; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga District: Ngomeni. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.
