## 摘要

**卡日普纳巨鼻白蚁**（*Cyranotermes karipuna*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）巨鼻白蚁属（*Cyranotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Rocha, Carrijo & Cancello 于 2012 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Rondônia: Porto Velho, Jaci Paraná \[8.76194S 63.9039W\]。分布记录包括巴西。

EN — **卡日普纳巨鼻白蚁** (*Cyranotermes karipuna*) is a species of the genus *Cyranotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Rocha, Carrijo & Cancello in 2012; the type locality is Brasil: Rondônia: Porto Velho, Jaci Paraná \[8.76194S 63.9039W\]. Distribution: Brazil.
