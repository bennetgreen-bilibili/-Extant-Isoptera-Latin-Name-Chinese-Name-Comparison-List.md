## 摘要

**弯曲原方白蚁**（*Procubitermes curvatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）原方白蚁属（*Procubitermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Guinea: Camayenne。分布记录包括加纳、几内亚、科特迪瓦、利比里亚。

EN — **弯曲原方白蚁** (*Procubitermes curvatus*) is a species of the genus *Procubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne. Distribution: Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia.
