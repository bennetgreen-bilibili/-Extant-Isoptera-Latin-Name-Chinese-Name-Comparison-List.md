## 摘要

**齿颚始扭白蚁**（*Protocapritermes odontomachus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）始扭白蚁属（*Protocapritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Desneux 于 1905 年描述，模式产地为 Papua New Guinea: Sattelberg。分布记录包括澳大利亚、巴布亚新几内亚、所罗门群岛。

EN — **齿颚始扭白蚁** (*Protocapritermes odontomachus*) is a species of the genus *Protocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Desneux in 1905; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Sattelberg. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands.
