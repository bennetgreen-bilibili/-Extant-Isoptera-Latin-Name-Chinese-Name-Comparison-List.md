EN — **具孔平白蚁** (*Homallotermes foraminifer*) is a species and the type species of the genus *Homallotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Perak. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
