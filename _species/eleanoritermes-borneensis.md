## 摘要

**婆罗埃莉诺白蚁**（*Eleanoritermes borneensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）埃莉诺白蚁属（*Eleanoritermes*）的现存唯一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Ahmad 于 1968 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang。分布记录包括婆罗洲。

EN — **婆罗埃莉诺白蚁** (*Eleanoritermes borneensis*) is the only extant species of the genus *Eleanoritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Ahmad in 1968; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Matang. Distribution: Borneo.
