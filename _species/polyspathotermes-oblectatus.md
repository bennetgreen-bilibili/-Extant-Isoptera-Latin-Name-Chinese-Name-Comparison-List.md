## 摘要

**愉悦多铲白蚁**（*Polyspathotermes oblectatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）多铲白蚁属（*Polyspathotermes*）的一种。本种由 Harris 于 1958 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Parc National de l’ Upemba: Lusinga。分布记录包括刚果民主共和国、马拉维、坦桑尼亚、赞比亚。

EN — **愉悦多铲白蚁** (*Polyspathotermes oblectatus*) is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1958; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Parc National de l’ Upemba: Lusinga. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia.
