## 摘要

**二型首白蚁**（*Caputitermes dimorphus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）首白蚁属（*Caputitermes*）的现存唯一种。本种由 Almeida-Azevedo, Acioli, Morais & Azevedo 于 2023 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Amazonas: Manaus, Fazenda Experimental da UFAM \[ 2.633S 60.1072W \]。分布记录包括巴西。

EN — **二型首白蚁** (*Caputitermes dimorphus*) is the only extant species of the genus *Caputitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Almeida-Azevedo, Acioli, Morais & Azevedo in 2023; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Manaus, Fazenda Experimental da UFAM \[ 2.633S 60.1072W \]. Distribution: Brazil.
