## 摘要

EN — **侏儒子民白蚁** (*Incolitermes pumilus*) is the only extant species of the genus *Incolitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: New South Wales: Unumgar State Forest, near Mt Lindesay (as Lindsay), near Queensland border. Distribution: Australia.
