## 摘要

**细瘦安的列斯白蚁**（*Antillitermes subtilis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）安的列斯白蚁属（*Antillitermes*）的现存唯一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Scheffrahn & Krecek 于 1993 年描述，模式产地为 Dominican Republic: Caracoles。分布记录包括古巴、多米尼加。

EN — **细瘦安的列斯白蚁** (*Antillitermes subtilis*) is the only extant species of the genus *Antillitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1993; the type locality is Dominican Republic: Caracoles. Distribution: Cuba, Dominican Republic.
