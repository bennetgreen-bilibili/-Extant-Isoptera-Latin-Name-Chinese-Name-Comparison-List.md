## 摘要

**高山多铲白蚁**（*Polyspathotermes montanus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）多铲白蚁属（*Polyspathotermes*）的一种。本种由 Williams 于 1966 年描述，模式产地为 Malawi: Nyika plateau, 22 m along access road from Rumpi-Chisenga road。分布记录包括马拉维、坦桑尼亚、赞比亚。

EN — **高山多铲白蚁** (*Polyspathotermes montanus*) is a species of the genus *Polyspathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Williams in 1966; the type locality is Malawi: Nyika plateau, 22 m along access road from Rumpi-Chisenga road. Distribution: Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia.
