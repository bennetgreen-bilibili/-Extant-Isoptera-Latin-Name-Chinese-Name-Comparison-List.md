## 摘要

**凯恩希拉木象白蚁**（*Diwaitermes kanehirai*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）木象白蚁属（*Diwaitermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Oshima 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Maluku: Aru Island: Dobo。分布记录包括印度尼西亚、所罗门群岛、澳大利亚、巴布亚新几内亚、瓦努阿图。本站记录本种有 6 个次异名。

EN — **凯恩希拉木象白蚁** (*Diwaitermes kanehirai*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Diwaitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Oshima in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Maluku: Aru Island: Dobo. Distribution: Indonesia, Solomon Islands, Australia, Papua New Guinea, Vanuatu. This site records 6 junior synonyms for the species.
