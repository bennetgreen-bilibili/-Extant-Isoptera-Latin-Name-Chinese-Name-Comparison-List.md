## 摘要

**驼背直颚白蚁**（*Orthognathotermes gibberorum*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）直颚白蚁属（*Orthognathotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]。分布记录包括巴西、圭亚那地盾。

EN — **驼背直颚白蚁** (*Orthognathotermes gibberorum*) is a species of the genus *Orthognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \].
