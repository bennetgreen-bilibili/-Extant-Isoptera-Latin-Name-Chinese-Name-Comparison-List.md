## 摘要

**埃默森钝颚白蚁**（*Ahmaditermes emersoni*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）钝颚白蚁属（*Ahmaditermes*）的一种。本种由 Maiti 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 India: West Bengal: Darjeeling District, ca. 5 km north-east of Teesta Bridge。分布记录包括不丹、印度。

EN — **埃默森钝颚白蚁** (*Ahmaditermes emersoni*) is a species of the genus *Ahmaditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Maiti in 1977; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Darjeeling District, ca. 5 km north-east of Teesta Bridge. Distribution: Bhutan, India.
