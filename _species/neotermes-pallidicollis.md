## 摘要

**灰颈新白蚁**（*Neotermes pallidicollis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）新白蚁属（*Neotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Johann-Albrechtshöhe。分布记录包括喀麦隆、尼日利亚、圣多美和普林西比。

EN — **灰颈新白蚁** (*Neotermes pallidicollis*) is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1902; the type locality is Cameroon: Johann-Albrechtshöhe. Distribution: Cameroon, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe.
