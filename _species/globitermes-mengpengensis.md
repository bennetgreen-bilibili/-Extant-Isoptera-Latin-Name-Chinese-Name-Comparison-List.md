## 摘要

**勐捧球白蚁**（*Globitermes mengpengensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）球白蚁属（*Globitermes*）的一种。本种由 Zhu & Huang 于 1990 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Mengla County: Mengpeng Hill。分布记录包括云南（勐腊勐棒 640 m）、中国。

EN — **勐捧球白蚁** (*Globitermes mengpengensis*) is a species of the genus *Globitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Zhu & Huang in 1990; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Mengla County: Mengpeng Hill. Distribution: Yunnan (Mengla Mengbang 640 m), China.
