## 摘要

**榕树双裂白蚁**（*Bifiditermes mutubae*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）双裂白蚁属（*Bifiditermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Harris 于 1948 年描述，模式产地为 Uganda: Kawanda Agricultural Research Station。分布记录包括肯尼亚、刚果民主共和国、苏丹、坦桑尼亚、乌干达。

EN — **榕树双裂白蚁** (*Bifiditermes mutubae*) is a species of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Harris in 1948; the type locality is Uganda: Kawanda Agricultural Research Station. Distribution: Kenya, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda.

La especie fue descrita por Harris en 1948; la localidad tipo es Uganda: Kawanda Agricultural Research Station. Distribución: Kenya, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda.
