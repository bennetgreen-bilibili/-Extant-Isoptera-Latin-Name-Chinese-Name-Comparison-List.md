EN — **土栖锐颚白蚁** (*Hoplognathotermes subterraneus*) is a species and the type species of the genus *Hoplognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Conakry; Kakoulima. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
