## 摘要

**好望角土白蚁**（*Odontotermes capensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 De Geer 于 1778 年描述，模式产地为 South Africa: Cape of Good Hope (Kapland): Near Humansdorp on Seekoei River。分布记录包括旧热带界西非、非洲南部、南非。

EN — **好望角土白蚁** (*Odontotermes capensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by De Geer in 1778; the type locality is South Africa: Cape of Good Hope (Kapland): Near Humansdorp on Seekoei River. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, southern Africa, South Africa.
