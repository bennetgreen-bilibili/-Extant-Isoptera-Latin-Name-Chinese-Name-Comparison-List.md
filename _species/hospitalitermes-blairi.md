## 摘要

**布莱尔须白蚁**（*Hospitalitermes blairi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）须白蚁属（*Hospitalitermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Sen-Sarma 于 1956 年描述，模式产地为 India: Andaman Islands: Port Blair, south of creek near Corbyn’s Cave。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **布莱尔须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes blairi*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1956; the type locality is India: Andaman Islands: Port Blair, south of creek near Corbyn’s Cave. Distribution: India.
