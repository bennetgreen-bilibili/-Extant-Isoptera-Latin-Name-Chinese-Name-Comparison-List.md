## 摘要

**职司三方白蚁**（*Ternicubitermes muneris*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）三方白蚁属（*Ternicubitermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1913 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Welgelegen。分布记录包括刚果民主共和国、肯尼亚、马拉维、坦桑尼亚、赞比亚。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **职司三方白蚁** (*Ternicubitermes muneris*) is a species of the genus *Ternicubitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Welgelegen. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
