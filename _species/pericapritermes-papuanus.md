The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2008; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Western Province: Wipim. Distribution: Papua New Guinea.

ES — **巴布亚近扭白蚁** (*Pericapritermes papuanus*) es una especie del género *Pericapritermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Mirocapritermitinae).
