## 摘要

**胖身土白蚁**（*Odontotermes obesus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Rambur 于 1842 年描述，模式产地为 India: Maharashtra: Mumbai。分布记录包括南亚印度、巴基斯坦、尼泊尔、孟加拉国、不丹、中国、缅甸。本站记录本种有 6 个次异名。

EN — **胖身土白蚁** (*Odontotermes obesus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Rambur in 1842; the type locality is India: Maharashtra: Mumbai. Distribution: South Asia, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, Myanmar. This site records 6 junior synonyms for the species.
