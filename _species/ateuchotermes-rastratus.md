## 摘要

**耙痕无锐白蚁**（*Ateuchotermes rastratus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）无锐白蚁属（*Ateuchotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Kenya: Nyambeni Hills, 22 mi from Meru。分布记录包括喀麦隆、埃塞俄比亚、肯尼亚、坦桑尼亚。

EN — **耙痕无锐白蚁** (*Ateuchotermes rastratus*) is a species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Kenya: Nyambeni Hills, 22 mi from Meru. Distribution: Cameroon, Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania.
