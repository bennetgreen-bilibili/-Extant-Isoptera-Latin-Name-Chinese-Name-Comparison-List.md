## 摘要

**短脊齿尖白蚁**（*Dentispicotermes brevicarinatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）齿尖白蚁属（*Dentispicotermes*）的一种。本种由 Emerson 于 1950 年描述，模式产地为 Guyana: East Berbice-Corentyne: Oronoque River \[ 2.7N 57.42W \]。分布记录包括圭亚那地盾、法属圭亚那/圭亚那/苏里南、巴西。

EN — **短脊齿尖白蚁** (*Dentispicotermes brevicarinatus*) is a species of the genus *Dentispicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1950; the type locality is Guyana: East Berbice-Corentyne: Oronoque River \[ 2.7N 57.42W \]. Distribution: Guiana Shield, French Guiana/Guyana/Suriname, Brazil.
