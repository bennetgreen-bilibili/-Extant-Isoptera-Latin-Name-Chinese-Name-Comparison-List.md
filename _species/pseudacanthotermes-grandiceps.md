## 摘要

**大头伪刺白蚁**（*Pseudacanthotermes grandiceps*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）伪刺白蚁属（*Pseudacanthotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1915 年描述，模式产地为 Kenya: Kimusu (Victoria Nyanzu, Golf Kavirondo)。分布记录包括肯尼亚、坦桑尼亚、乌干达。

EN — **大头伪刺白蚁** (*Pseudacanthotermes grandiceps*) is a species of the genus *Pseudacanthotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1915; the type locality is Kenya: Kimusu (Victoria Nyanzu, Golf Kavirondo). Distribution: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
