EN — **细瘦象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes gracilis*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Oshima in 1916; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon; Laguna; Sarai, near Paete. Distribution: Indonesia, Philippines.
