## 摘要

**卡西棒鼻白蚁**（*Parrhinotermes khasii*）是鼻白蚁科（RHINOTERMITIDAE）棒鼻白蚁属（*Parrhinotermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Sen-Sarma 于 1956 年描述，模式产地为 India: Assam: Khasi Hills, bank of river Nong-priang。分布记录包括西藏（墨脱：背崩800m、亚让1000m）、印度（阿萨姆：卡西山366m）。

EN — **卡西棒鼻白蚁** (*Parrhinotermes khasii*) is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1956; the type locality is India: Assam: Khasi Hills, bank of river Nong-priang. Distribution: Tibet (Mêdog: Beibeng 800 m, Yarang 1000 m); India (Assam: Khasi Hills 366 m).
