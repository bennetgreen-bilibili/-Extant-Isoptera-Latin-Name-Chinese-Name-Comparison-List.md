## 摘要

**班克斯象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes banksi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Emerson 于 1925 年描述，模式产地为 Guyana: Potaro-Siparuni: Tukeit \[5.2N 59.45W\]。分布记录包括巴西、法属圭亚那、哥伦比亚、圭亚那、玻利维亚、秘鲁。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **班克斯象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes banksi*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Potaro-Siparuni: Tukeit \[5.2N 59.45W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Guyana, Bolivia, Peru. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
