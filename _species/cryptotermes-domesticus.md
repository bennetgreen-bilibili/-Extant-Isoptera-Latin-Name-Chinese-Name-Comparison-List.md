## 摘要

**截头堆砂白蚁**（*Cryptotermes domesticus*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）堆砂白蚁属（*Cryptotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Haviland 于 1898 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Sarawak。分布记录包括东洋界至澳洲界、东南亚、中国南部至太平洋岛屿、厄瓜多尔、加拿大、哥斯达黎加、巴拿马、星期四岛（澳大利亚）、特立尼达和多巴哥、特罗梅林岛（法国）。本站记录本种有 11 个次异名。

EN — **截头堆砂白蚁** (*Cryptotermes domesticus*) is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. Distribution: Oriental region to Australian region, Southeast Asia, southern China to the Pacific islands, Ecuador, Canada, Costa Rica, Panama, Thursday Island (Australia), Trinidad and Tobago, Tromelin Island (France). This site records 11 junior synonyms for the species.
