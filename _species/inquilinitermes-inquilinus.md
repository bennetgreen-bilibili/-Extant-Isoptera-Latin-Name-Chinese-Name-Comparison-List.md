## 摘要

**旅居客白蚁**（*Inquilinitermes inquilinus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）客白蚁属（*Inquilinitermes*）的一种。本种由 Emerson 于 1925 年描述，模式产地为 Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]。分布记录包括巴西、法属圭亚那、哥伦比亚、圭亚那。

EN — **旅居客白蚁** (*Inquilinitermes inquilinus*) is a species of the genus *Inquilinitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1925; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[6.38N 58.7W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Guyana.
