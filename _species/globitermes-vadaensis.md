## 摘要

**瓦达球白蚁**（*Globitermes vadaensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）球白蚁属（*Globitermes*）的一种。本种由 Kemner 于 1934 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Java: Vada, near Tjandjoer。分布记录包括东南亚岛屿、南洋群岛。

EN — **瓦达球白蚁** (*Globitermes vadaensis*) is a species of the genus *Globitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Vada, near Tjandjoer. Distribution: Southeast Asian islands, Malay Archipelago.
