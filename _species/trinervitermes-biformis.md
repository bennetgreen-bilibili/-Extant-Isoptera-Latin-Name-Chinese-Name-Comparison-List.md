## 摘要

**二型三脉白蚁**（*Trinervitermes biformis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）三脉白蚁属（*Trinervitermes*）的一种。本种由 Wasmann 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 Sri Lanka: Bandarawela。分布记录包括印度、巴基斯坦、斯里兰卡。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **二型三脉白蚁** (*Trinervitermes biformis*) is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Bandarawela. Distribution: India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
