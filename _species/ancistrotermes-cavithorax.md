## 摘要

**凹胸钩白蚁**（*Ancistrotermes cavithorax*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）钩白蚁属（*Ancistrotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1899 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Victoria。分布记录包括西非、科特迪瓦、塞内加尔、贝宁、冈比亚、喀麦隆、中非、刚果民主共和国、加纳、几内亚、尼日利亚、塞拉利昂、乌干达。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **凹胸钩白蚁** (*Ancistrotermes cavithorax*) is a species of the genus *Ancistrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1899; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Benin, Gambia, Cameroon, Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Uganda. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
