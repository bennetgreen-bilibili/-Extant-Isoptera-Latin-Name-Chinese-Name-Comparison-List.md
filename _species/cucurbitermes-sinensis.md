## 摘要

**中华葫白蚁**（*Cucurbitermes sinensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）葫白蚁属（*Cucurbitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Li & Ping 于 1985 年描述，模式产地为 China: Guangxi Province: Longsheng County: Huaping forest。分布记录包括中国、广东。

EN — **中华葫白蚁** (*Cucurbitermes sinensis*) is a species of the genus *Cucurbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Li & Ping in 1985; the type locality is China: Guangxi Province: Longsheng County: Huaping forest. Distribution: China, Guangdong.
