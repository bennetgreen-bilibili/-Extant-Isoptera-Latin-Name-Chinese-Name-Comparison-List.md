## 摘要

**凹胸考登白蚁**（*Kaudernitermes salebrithorax*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）考登白蚁属（*Kaudernitermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1904 年描述，模式产地为 Madagascar: Tolanaro (Fort Dauphin); Seychelles: Silhouette。分布记录包括马达加斯加、塞舌尔。

EN — **凹胸考登白蚁** (*Kaudernitermes salebrithorax*) is a species of the genus *Kaudernitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Madagascar: Tolanaro (Fort Dauphin); Seychelles: Silhouette. Distribution: Madagascar, Seychelles.
