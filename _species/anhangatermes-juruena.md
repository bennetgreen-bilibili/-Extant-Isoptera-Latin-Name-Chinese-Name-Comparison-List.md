## 摘要

**茹鲁埃纳魔白蚁**（*Anhangatermes juruena*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）魔白蚁属（*Anhangatermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Oliveira & Constantino 于 2014 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Juruena \[10.3594S 58.4903W\]。分布记录包括巴西。

EN — **茹鲁埃纳魔白蚁** (*Anhangatermes juruena*) is a species of the genus *Anhangatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Oliveira & Constantino in 2014; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Juruena \[10.3594S 58.4903W\]. Distribution: Brazil.
