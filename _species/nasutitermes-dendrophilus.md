## 摘要

**喜树象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes dendrophilus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Desneux 于 1906 年描述，模式产地为 Ecuador: Tungurahua: Rosario \[1.4S 78.18W\]。分布记录包括厄瓜多尔、巴西、秘鲁。

EN — **喜树象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes dendrophilus*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1906; the type locality is Ecuador: Tungurahua: Rosario \[1.4S 78.18W\]. Distribution: Ecuador, Brazil, Peru.
