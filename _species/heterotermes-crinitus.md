## 摘要

**密毛异白蚁**（*Heterotermes crinitus*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）异白蚁属（*Heterotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Emerson 于 1924 年描述，模式产地为 Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]。分布记录包括南美洲、哥伦比亚、法属圭亚那、巴西。

EN — **密毛异白蚁** (*Heterotermes crinitus*) is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Emerson in 1924; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Kartabo \[ 6.38N 58.7W \]. Distribution: South America, Colombia, French Guiana, Brazil.
