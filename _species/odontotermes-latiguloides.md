## 摘要

**拟宽喉土白蚁**（*Odontotermes latiguloides*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Verma 于 1973 年描述，模式产地为 India: Rajasthan: Bhilwara District, ca. 48 km southwest of Bhilwara, Mandalgarh。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **拟宽喉土白蚁** (*Odontotermes latiguloides*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Verma in 1973; the type locality is India: Rajasthan: Bhilwara District, ca. 48 km southwest of Bhilwara, Mandalgarh. Distribution: India.
