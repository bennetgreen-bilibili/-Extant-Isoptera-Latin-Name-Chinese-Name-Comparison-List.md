EN — **黑色高跷白蚁** (*Grallatotermes niger*) is a species of the genus *Grallatotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Chatterjee & Thapa in 1964; the type locality is India: Tamil Nadu (Madras): Tunacadur Range, Topslip, Mt. Stuart Block. Distribution: India.
