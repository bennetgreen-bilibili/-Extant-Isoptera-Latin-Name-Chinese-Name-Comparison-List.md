The subspecies was described by Holmgren in 1913; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Nasutitermes longirostris*.

## 词源 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Etymology</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Etimología</span>

EN — *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

ES — *Nasutitermes*: Del latín nasutus (de nariz grande) + termes, por el soldado nasuto.

种加词 *longirostris*：拉丁文/希腊文 longi（长）+ rostris（喙），指长喙

EN — epithet *longirostris*: From longi (长) + rostris (喙)

ES — epithet *longirostris*: De longi (largo) + rostris (pico)
