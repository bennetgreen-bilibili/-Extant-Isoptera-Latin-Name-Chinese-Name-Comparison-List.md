## 摘要

**阿奎尔尖白蚁**（*Apicotermes arquieri*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）尖白蚁属（*Apicotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Grassé & Noirot 于 1955 年描述，模式产地为 Central African Republic: Bossembélé。分布记录包括中非、刚果盆地、尼日利亚。

EN — **阿奎尔尖白蚁** (*Apicotermes arquieri*) is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Grassé & Noirot in 1955; the type locality is Central African Republic: Bossembélé. Distribution: Central African Republic, Congo Basin, Nigeria.
