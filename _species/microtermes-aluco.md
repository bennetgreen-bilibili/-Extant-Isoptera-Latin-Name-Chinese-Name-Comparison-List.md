## 摘要

**林鸮小白蚁**（*Microtermes aluco*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）小白蚁属（*Microtermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1904 年描述，模式产地为 Mali: Sikasso。分布记录包括西非、尼日利亚、喀麦隆、刚果民主共和国、苏丹。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **林鸮小白蚁** (*Microtermes aluco*) is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Mali: Sikasso. Distribution: West Africa, Nigeria, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
