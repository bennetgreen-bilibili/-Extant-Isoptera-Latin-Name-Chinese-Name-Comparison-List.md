## 摘要

**巴舍尔白蚁**（*Termes baculi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）白蚁属（*Termes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1900 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Ekundu。分布记录包括旧热带界、喀麦隆、尼日利亚、刚果民主共和国、加蓬、几内亚、科特迪瓦、苏丹。

EN — **巴舍尔白蚁** (*Termes baculi*) is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Cameroon: Ekundu. Distribution: Old World tropics, Cameroon, Nigeria, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Sudan.
