## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

**冠毛无剑白蚁**（*Asagarotermes coronatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）无剑白蚁属（*Asagarotermes*）的现存唯一种。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville。分布记录包括刚果（金）加丹加、喀麦隆、刚果民主共和国。

EN — **冠毛无剑白蚁** (*Asagarotermes coronatus*) is the only extant species of the genus *Asagarotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribution: Katanga, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

ES — **冠毛无剑白蚁** (*Asagarotermes coronatus*) es la única especie viviente del género *Asagarotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Apicotermitinae). La especie fue descrita por Sands en 1972; la localidad tipo es Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribución: Katanga, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo.
