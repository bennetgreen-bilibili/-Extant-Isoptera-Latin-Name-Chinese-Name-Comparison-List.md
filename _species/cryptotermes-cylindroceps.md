## 摘要

**筒头堆砂白蚁**（*Cryptotermes cylindroceps*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）堆砂白蚁属（*Cryptotermes*）的一种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Scheffrahn & Krecek 于 1999 年描述，模式产地为 Curaçao: Santa Barbara。分布记录包括南美洲、委内瑞拉、哥伦比亚、阿鲁巴、博奈尔、库拉索。

EN — **筒头堆砂白蚁** (*Cryptotermes cylindroceps*) is a species of the genus *Cryptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Scheffrahn & Krecek in 1999; the type locality is Curaçao: Santa Barbara. Distribution: South America, Venezuela, Colombia, Aruba, Bonaire, Curaçao.

ES — **筒头堆砂白蚁** (*Cryptotermes cylindroceps*) es una especie del género *Cryptotermes*, en la familia KALOTERMITIDAE, perteneciente al clado Icoisoptera.
