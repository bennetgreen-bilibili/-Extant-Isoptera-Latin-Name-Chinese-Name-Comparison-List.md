## 摘要

**巨人稀白蚁**（*Speculitermes cyclops*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）稀白蚁属（*Speculitermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Wasmann 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 India: Maharashtra, near Bombay: Khandala。分布记录包括南亚、印度。

EN — **巨人稀白蚁** (*Speculitermes cyclops*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Speculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is India: Maharashtra, near Bombay: Khandala. Distribution: South Asia, India.
