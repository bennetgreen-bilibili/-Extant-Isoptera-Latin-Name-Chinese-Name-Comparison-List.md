## 摘要

**细小锯白蚁**（*Microcerotermes parvulus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）锯白蚁亚科（Microcerotermitinae）锯白蚁属（*Microcerotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu。分布记录包括旧热带界、西非、中非、乍得、刚果、刚果民主共和国、埃塞俄比亚、加纳、几内亚、科特迪瓦、尼日利亚、塞内加尔、坦桑尼亚、阿曼、沙特阿拉伯、也门。

EN — **细小锯白蚁** (*Microcerotermes parvulus*) is a species of the genus *Microcerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Microcerotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, Chad, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Senegal, Tanzania, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Yemen.

ES — **细小锯白蚁** (*Microcerotermes parvulus*) es una especie del género *Microcerotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Microcerotermitinae).
