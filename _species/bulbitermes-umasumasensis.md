## 摘要

**乌马斯乌马斯瓢白蚁**（*Bulbitermes umasumasensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）瓢白蚁属（*Bulbitermes*）的一种。本种由 Thapa 于 1982 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Sabah: Kalabakan District: Umas Umas。分布记录包括新加坡、马来西亚。

EN — **乌马斯乌马斯瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes umasumasensis*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Thapa in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sabah: Kalabakan District: Umas Umas. Distribution: Singapore, Malaysia.
