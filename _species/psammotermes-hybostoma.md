## 摘要

**弯嘴沙白蚁**（*Psammotermes hybostoma*）是沙白蚁科（PSAMMOTERMITIDAE）沙白蚁属（*Psammotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种，隶属新等翅类（Neoisoptera）。本种由 Desneux 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 Algeria: Biskra; Touggourt。分布记录包括西非、塞内加尔至东北非、阿尔及利亚、巴林、埃及、厄立特里亚、埃塞俄比亚、以色列、科威特、利比亚、毛里塔尼亚、尼日尔、阿曼、沙特阿拉伯、南非、苏丹、也门。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **弯嘴沙白蚁** (*Psammotermes hybostoma*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Psammotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Neoisoptera. The species was described by Desneux in 1902; the type locality is Algeria: Biskra; Touggourt. Distribution: West Africa, Senegal to Northeast Africa, Algeria, Bahrain, Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Israel, Kuwait, Libya, Mauritania, Niger, Oman, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Sudan, Yemen.
