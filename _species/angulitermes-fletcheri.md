## 摘要

**弗莱彻角白蚁**（*Angulitermes fletcheri*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）前奇白蚁亚科（Promirotermitinae）角白蚁属（*Angulitermes*）的一种。本种由 Holmgren & Holmgren 于 1917 年描述，模式产地为 India: Karnataka; Bellary District; Hadagalli \[see Chhotani, 1997: 246\]。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **弗莱彻角白蚁** (*Angulitermes fletcheri*) is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Karnataka; Bellary District; Hadagalli \[see Chhotani, 1997: 246\]. Distribution: India.
