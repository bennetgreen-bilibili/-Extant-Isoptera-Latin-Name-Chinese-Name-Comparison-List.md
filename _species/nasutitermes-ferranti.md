## 摘要

**费朗象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes ferranti*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Wasmann 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kasai: Sankuru。分布记录包括刚果（金）、安哥拉、刚果（布）、刚果民主共和国、尼日利亚。

EN — **费朗象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes ferranti*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kasai: Sankuru. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Angola, Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Nigeria.

ES — **费朗象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes ferranti*) es una especie del género *Nasutitermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Nasutitermitinae).
