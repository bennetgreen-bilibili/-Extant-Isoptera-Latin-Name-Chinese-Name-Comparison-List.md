## 摘要

**窄翅土白蚁**（*Odontotermes angustipennis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1900 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kinshasa (Leopoldville)。分布记录包括旧热带界中非、东非、刚果民主共和国。

EN — **窄翅土白蚁** (*Odontotermes angustipennis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1900; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kinshasa (Leopoldville). Distribution: Old World tropics, Central Africa, East Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

ES — **窄翅土白蚁** (*Odontotermes angustipennis*) es una especie del género *Odontotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Macrotermitinae).
