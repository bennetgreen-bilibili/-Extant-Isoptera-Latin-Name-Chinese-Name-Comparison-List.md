## 摘要

**原黑翅土白蚁**（*Odontotermes proformosanus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Ahmad 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 Thailand: Ka-Chong。分布记录包括东南亚柬埔寨、越南、孟加拉国、印度、泰国。

EN — **原黑翅土白蚁** (*Odontotermes proformosanus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Ka-Chong. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Cambodia, Vietnam, Bangladesh, India, Thailand.
