## 摘要

EN — **黄触角须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes flavoantennaris*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Oshima in 1923; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Simeulue, Sinabang. Distribution: Indonesia.
