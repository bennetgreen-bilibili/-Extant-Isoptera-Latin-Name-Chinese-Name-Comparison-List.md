**卢哈象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes lujae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Wasmann 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai)。分布记录包括喀麦隆、尼日利亚、加蓬、乌干达、安哥拉、刚果（布）、刚果民主共和国、加纳、科特迪瓦。本站记录本种有 5 个次异名。

EN — **卢哈象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes lujae*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai). Distribution: Cameroon, Nigeria, Gabon, Uganda, Angola, Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire. This site records 5 junior synonyms for the species.

## 词源 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Etymology</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Etimología</span>

属名 *Nasutitermes*：属名由拉丁文 nasutus（大鼻的、具鼻突的）与 termes（白蚁）组合，指兵蚁具鼻状额突。

EN — *Nasutitermes*: From Latin nasutus (large-nosed) + termes, for the nasute soldier.

种加词 *lujae*：以人名 Luja（卢雅）命名（卢森堡博物学家 Édouard Luja，在刚果采集）

EN — epithet *lujae*: Named after Luja (Luxembourg naturalist Édouard Luja)
