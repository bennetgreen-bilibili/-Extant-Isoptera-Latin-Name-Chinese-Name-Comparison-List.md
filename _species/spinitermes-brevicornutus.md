## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

**短角针刺白蚁**（*Spinitermes brevicornutus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）针刺白蚁属（*Spinitermes*）的一种。本种由 Desneux 于 1904 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]。分布记录包括法属圭亚那、哥伦比亚、阿根廷、玻利维亚、巴西、巴拉圭。

EN — **短角针刺白蚁** (*Spinitermes brevicornutus*) is a species of the genus *Spinitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae). The species was described by Desneux in 1904; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: French Guiana, Colombia, Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay.

ES — **短角针刺白蚁** (*Spinitermes brevicornutus*) es una especie del género *Spinitermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Termitinae).
