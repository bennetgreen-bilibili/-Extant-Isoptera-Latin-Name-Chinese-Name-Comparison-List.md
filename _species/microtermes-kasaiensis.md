## 摘要

**开赛小白蚁**（*Microtermes kasaiensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）小白蚁属（*Microtermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1913 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kasai。分布记录包括中非、刚果民主共和国、肯尼亚、马拉维、乌干达。

EN — **开赛小白蚁** (*Microtermes kasaiensis*) is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kasai. Distribution: Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Uganda.
