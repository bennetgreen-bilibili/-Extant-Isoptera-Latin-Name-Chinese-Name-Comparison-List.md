## 摘要

EN — **黑胸散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes chinensis*) is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Snyder in 1923; the type locality is China: Sichuan Province: Suifu. Distribution: China, India, Vietnam.
