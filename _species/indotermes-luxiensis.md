## 摘要

**潞西印白蚁**（*Indotermes luxiensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）印白蚁属（*Indotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Huang & Zhu 于 1984 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Luxi County。分布记录包括中国、云南。

EN — **潞西印白蚁** (*Indotermes luxiensis*) is a species of the genus *Indotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Huang & Zhu in 1984; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Luxi County. Distribution: China, Yunnan.
