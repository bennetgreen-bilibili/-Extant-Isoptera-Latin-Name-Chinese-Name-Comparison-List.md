## 摘要

**贝拉胡尼斯土白蚁**（*Odontotermes bellahunisensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Holmgren & Holmgren 于 1917 年描述，模式产地为 India: Karnataka \[Mysore\], Bellary District: Bellahunisi。分布记录包括南亚印度。

EN — **贝拉胡尼斯土白蚁** (*Odontotermes bellahunisensis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Karnataka \[Mysore\], Bellary District: Bellahunisi. Distribution: South Asia (India).
