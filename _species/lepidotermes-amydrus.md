## 摘要

**幽暗鳞白蚁**（*Lepidotermes amydrus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）鳞白蚁属（*Lepidotermes*）的一种。本种由 Uys 于 1994 年描述，模式产地为 Namibia: 64 km ex Windhoek-Gallschau crossroads。分布记录包括非洲南部、南非。

EN — **幽暗鳞白蚁** (*Lepidotermes amydrus*) is a species of the genus *Lepidotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Uys in 1994; the type locality is Namibia: 64 km ex Windhoek-Gallschau crossroads. Distribution: southern Africa, South Africa.
