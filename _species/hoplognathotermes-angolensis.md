## 摘要

**安哥拉锐颚白蚁**（*Hoplognathotermes angolensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）锐颚白蚁属（*Hoplognathotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Weidner 于 1974 年描述，模式产地为 Angola: source of River Cuílo, 400 km SSE of Dundo。分布记录包括非洲、安哥拉。

EN — **安哥拉锐颚白蚁** (*Hoplognathotermes angolensis*) is a species of the genus *Hoplognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Weidner in 1974; the type locality is Angola: source of River Cuílo, 400 km SSE of Dundo. Distribution: Africa, Angola.
