## 摘要

EN — **似黄腹须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes proflaviventris*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Akhtar & Akbar in 1986; the type locality is Malaysia: Penang: Pantai Acheh Forest Reserve. Distribution: Malaysia.
