## 摘要

**佩雷斯前新白蚁**（*Proneotermes perezi*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）前新白蚁属（*Proneotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种，隶属近等翅类（Icoisoptera）。本种由 Holmgren 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Costa Rica: San José。分布记录包括哥斯达黎加、危地马拉、洪都拉斯、巴拿马。

EN — **佩雷斯前新白蚁** (*Proneotermes perezi*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Proneotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Icoisoptera. The species was described by Holmgren in 1911; the type locality is Costa Rica: San José. Distribution: Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Panama.
