## 摘要

**光背快白蚁**（*Velocitermes glabrinotus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）快白蚁属（*Velocitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]。分布记录包括巴西、哥伦比亚。

EN — **光背快白蚁** (*Velocitermes glabrinotus*) is a species of the genus *Velocitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia.
