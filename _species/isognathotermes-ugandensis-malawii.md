## 摘要

**马拉维亚种**（*Isognathotermes ugandensis malawii*）是乌干达等颚白蚁（*Isognathotermes ugandensis*）的一个亚种，隶属白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）等颚白蚁属（*Isognathotermes*）。本亚种由 Josens & Deligne 于 2025 年描述，模式产地为 Malawi: Dowa-Lilongwe road \[ 13.65S 33.85E \]。本页暂无单列的分布记录，母种 *Isognathotermes ugandensis* 的分布见下文「分布」一节。

EN — **马拉维亚种** (*Isognathotermes ugandensis malawii*) is a subspecies of *Isognathotermes ugandensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), genus *Isognathotermes*. The subspecies was described by Josens & Deligne in 2025; the type locality is Malawi: Dowa-Lilongwe road \[ 13.65S 33.85E \]. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Isognathotermes ugandensis*.
