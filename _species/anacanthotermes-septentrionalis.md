## 摘要

**北方无刺白蚁**（*Anacanthotermes septentrionalis*）是草白蚁科（HODOTERMITIDAE）无刺白蚁属（*Anacanthotermes*）的一种，隶属远等翅类（Teletisoptera）。本种由 Jacobson 于 1905 年描述，模式产地为 Iran: Chosassan, between Kerat and Sengun; Turkmenistan: AkarTscheschmè (on River Tedshen); Geok-Tepe; Kopet-dagh; Mulla-Kara, station Perwal; Serachs。分布记录包括中东、伊朗、阿富汗、土库曼斯坦。

EN — **北方无刺白蚁** (*Anacanthotermes septentrionalis*) is a species of the genus *Anacanthotermes*, in the family HODOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Teletisoptera. The species was described by Jacobson in 1905; the type locality is Iran: Chosassan, between Kerat and Sengun; Turkmenistan: AkarTscheschmè (on River Tedshen); Geok-Tepe; Kopet-dagh; Mulla-Kara, station Perwal; Serachs. Distribution: Middle East, Iran, Afghanistan, Turkmenistan.
