## 摘要

**东方伪扭白蚁**（*Pseudocapritermes orientalis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）伪扭白蚁属（*Pseudocapritermes*）的一种。本种由 Ahmad & Akhtar 于 1981 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Johore: Kluang Forest。分布记录包括巴基斯坦、印度、印度尼西亚、马来西亚。

EN — **东方伪扭白蚁** (*Pseudocapritermes orientalis*) is a species of the genus *Pseudocapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad & Akhtar in 1981; the type locality is Malaysia: Johore: Kluang Forest. Distribution: Pakistan, India, Indonesia, Malaysia.
