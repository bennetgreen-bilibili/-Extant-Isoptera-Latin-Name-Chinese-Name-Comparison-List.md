EN — **霍姆格伦怒白蚁** (*Lacessititermes holmgreni*) is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Light & Wilson in 1936; the type locality is Philippines: Mindanao: Surigao Province: Dinagat Island: Dinagat. Distribution: Philippines.
