## 摘要

**马拉巴尔土白蚁**（*Odontotermes malabaricus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Holmgren & Holmgren 于 1917 年描述，模式产地为 India: Karnataka: Mysore: Bababudin Hills; Kerala: Malabar: Poovanur; Tamil Nadu: Shevaroy Hills: Yercaud。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **马拉巴尔土白蚁** (*Odontotermes malabaricus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren & Holmgren in 1917; the type locality is India: Karnataka: Mysore: Bababudin Hills; Kerala: Malabar: Poovanur; Tamil Nadu: Shevaroy Hills: Yercaud. Distribution: India.
