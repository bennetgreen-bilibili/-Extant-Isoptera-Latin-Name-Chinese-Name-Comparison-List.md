## 摘要

**布拉干提纳圆白蚁**（*Rotunditermes bragantinus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）圆白蚁属（*Rotunditermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Rathore 于 1976 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Pará: Região Bragantina \[1.25S 47.75W\]。分布记录包括巴西、哥伦比亚、法属圭亚那、苏里南、厄瓜多尔、秘鲁。

EN — **布拉干提纳圆白蚁** (*Rotunditermes bragantinus*) is a species of the genus *Rotunditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Rathore in 1976; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Região Bragantina \[1.25S 47.75W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Suriname, Ecuador, Peru.
