## 摘要

**小刺无锐白蚁**（*Ateuchotermes spinulatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）无锐白蚁属（*Ateuchotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville。分布记录包括刚果民主共和国。

EN — **小刺无锐白蚁** (*Ateuchotermes spinulatus*) is a species of the genus *Ateuchotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Katanga: Keyberg, near Elizabethville. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo.
