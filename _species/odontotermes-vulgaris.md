## 摘要

**常见土白蚁**（*Odontotermes vulgaris*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Haviland 于 1898 年描述，模式产地为 South Africa: Natal。分布记录包括旧热带界西非、东非、非洲南部、安哥拉、纳米比亚、南非、斯威士兰、津巴布韦。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **常见土白蚁** (*Odontotermes vulgaris*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Haviland in 1898; the type locality is South Africa: Natal. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, East Africa, southern Africa, Angola, Namibia, South Africa, Eswatini, Zimbabwe. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
