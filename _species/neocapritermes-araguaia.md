## 摘要

**阿拉瓜亚新扭白蚁**（*Neocapritermes araguaia*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）新扭白蚁亚科（Neocapritermitinae）新扭白蚁属（*Neocapritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Krishna & Araujo 于 1968 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Goiás: Goiás, Fazenda Piedade \[ 15.645S 50.5813W \]。分布记录包括巴西、法属圭亚那。

The species was described by Krishna & Araujo in 1968; the type locality is Brasil: Goiás: Goiás, Fazenda Piedade \[ 15.645S 50.5813W \]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana.
