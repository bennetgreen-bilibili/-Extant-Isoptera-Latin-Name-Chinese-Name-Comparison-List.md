## 摘要

**森林土白蚁**（*Odontotermes silvaticus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Harris 于 1956 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Nyong Reserve Forest, 16 km S of Makak。分布记录包括西非布基纳法索、喀麦隆。

EN — **森林土白蚁** (*Odontotermes silvaticus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1956; the type locality is Cameroon: Nyong Reserve Forest, 16 km S of Makak. Distribution: West Africa, Burkina Faso, Cameroon.
