## 摘要

**奇丽原鼻白蚁**（*Prorhinotermes spectabilis*）是沙白蚁科（PSAMMOTERMITIDAE）原鼻白蚁属（*Prorhinotermes*）的一种，隶属新等翅类（Neoisoptera）。本种由 Ping & Xu 于 1989 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Hekou Yaozu Zizhixian。分布记录包括中国、云南。

The species was described by Ping & Xu in 1989; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Hekou Yaozu Zizhixian. Distribution: China, Yunnan.
