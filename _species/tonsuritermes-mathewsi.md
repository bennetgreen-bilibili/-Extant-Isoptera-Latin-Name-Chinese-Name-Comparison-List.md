## 摘要

EN — **马修斯奇囟白蚁** (*Tonsuritermes mathewsi*) is a species of the genus *Tonsuritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Constantini & Cancello in 2018; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Chapada dos Guimarães \[ 15.4606S 55.7497W \]. Distribution: Brazil.
