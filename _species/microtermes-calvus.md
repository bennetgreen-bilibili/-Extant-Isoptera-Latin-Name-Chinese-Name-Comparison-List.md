## 摘要

**无毛小白蚁**（*Microtermes calvus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）小白蚁属（*Microtermes*）的一种。本种由 Emerson 于 1928 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: St. Gabriel near Kisangani (Stanleyville)。分布记录包括中非、刚果民主共和国。

EN — **无毛小白蚁** (*Microtermes calvus*) is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: St. Gabriel near Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo.
