## 摘要

**刺棘弯肠白蚁**（*Aparatermes thornatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）弯肠白蚁属（*Aparatermes*）的一种。本种由 Pinzon & Scheffrahn 于 2019 年描述，模式产地为 Ecuador: Orellana: Yasuni National Park \[ 0.6717S 76.3979W \]。分布记录包括巴西、圭亚那地盾、法属圭亚那/圭亚那/苏里南、亚马孙地区、哥伦比亚/秘鲁/玻利维亚/厄瓜多尔、中美洲及加勒比。

EN — **刺棘弯肠白蚁** (*Aparatermes thornatus*) is a species of the genus *Aparatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Pinzon & Scheffrahn in 2019; the type locality is Ecuador: Orellana: Yasuni National Park \[ 0.6717S 76.3979W \]. Distribution: Brazil, Guiana Shield, French Guiana/Guyana/Suriname, Amazon region, Colombia/Peru/Bolivia/Ecuador, Central America and Caribbean.
