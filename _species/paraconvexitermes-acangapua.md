## 摘要

**圆头近凸白蚁**（*Paraconvexitermes acangapua*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）近凸白蚁属（*Paraconvexitermes*）的一种。本种由 Cancello & Noirot 于 2003 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Pará: Benevides, Fazenda Morelândia \[1.22S 48.28W\]。分布记录包括巴西、哥伦比亚、法属圭亚那、委内瑞拉。

EN — **圆头近凸白蚁** (*Paraconvexitermes acangapua*) is a species of the genus *Paraconvexitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cancello & Noirot in 2003; the type locality is Brasil: Pará: Benevides, Fazenda Morelândia \[1.22S 48.28W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Venezuela.

ES — **圆头近凸白蚁** (*Paraconvexitermes acangapua*) es una especie del género *Paraconvexitermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Nasutitermitinae).
