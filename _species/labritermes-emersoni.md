## 摘要

**埃默森唇白蚁**（*Labritermes emersoni*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）孔白蚁亚科（Foraminitermitinae）唇白蚁属（*Labritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Krishna & Adams 于 1982 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Sarawak: Kapit District, Third Division: Batang Balleh: Sungei Mengiong: Nanga Tekalit Camp。分布记录包括马来西亚、印度尼西亚、新加坡。

EN — **埃默森唇白蚁** (*Labritermes emersoni*) is a species of the genus *Labritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Krishna & Adams in 1982; the type locality is Malaysia: Sarawak: Kapit District, Third Division: Batang Balleh: Sungei Mengiong: Nanga Tekalit Camp. Distribution: Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore.

ES — **埃默森唇白蚁** (*Labritermes emersoni*) es una especie del género *Labritermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Foraminitermitinae), perteneciente al clado Geoisoptera.
