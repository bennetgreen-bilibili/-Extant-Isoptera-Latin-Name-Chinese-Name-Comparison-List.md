## 摘要

**细小树白蚁**（*Glyptotermes parvulus*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）树白蚁属（*Glyptotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1907 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu。分布记录包括刚果民主共和国、加纳、科特迪瓦、乌干达。

EN — **细小树白蚁** (*Glyptotermes parvulus*) is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1907; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Uganda.
