## 摘要

**沙特三脉白蚁**（*Trinervitermes saudiensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）三脉白蚁属（*Trinervitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Sands 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 Saudi Arabia: Jiddah。分布记录包括沙特阿拉伯、也门。

EN — **沙特三脉白蚁** (*Trinervitermes saudiensis*) is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Sands in 1965; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Jiddah. Distribution: Saudi Arabia, Yemen.
