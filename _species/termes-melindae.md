## 摘要

**梅林达白蚁**（*Termes melindae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）白蚁亚科（Termitinae）白蚁属（*Termes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Harris 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 Belize: Melinda Forest Station, Stann Creek Valley。分布记录包括中美洲、墨西哥、伯利兹、开曼群岛。

EN — **梅林达白蚁** (*Termes melindae*) is a species of the genus *Termes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Termitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Harris in 1960; the type locality is Belize: Melinda Forest Station, Stann Creek Valley. Distribution: Central America, Mexico, Belize, Cayman Islands.
