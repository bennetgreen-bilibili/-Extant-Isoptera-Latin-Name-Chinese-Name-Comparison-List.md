## 摘要

EN — **凋零冢白蚁** (*Tumulitermes marcidus*) is a species of the genus *Tumulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Hill in 1942; the type locality is Australia: Queensland: Torres Strait: Moa (Banks) Island. Distribution: Australia, Papua New Guinea.
