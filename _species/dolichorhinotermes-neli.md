## 摘要

**内尔狭鼻白蚁**（*Dolichorhinotermes neli*）是鼻白蚁科（RHINOTERMITIDAE）狭鼻白蚁属（*Dolichorhinotermes*）的一种，隶属新等翅类（Neoisoptera）。本种由 Ensaf & Betsch 于 2002 年描述，模式产地为 French Guiana: Route de Cacao, 1.2 km avant Bellevue \[ 4.548N 52.4915W \]。分布记录包括法属圭亚那。

EN — **内尔狭鼻白蚁** (*Dolichorhinotermes neli*) is a species of the genus *Dolichorhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Neoisoptera. The species was described by Ensaf & Betsch in 2002; the type locality is French Guiana: Route de Cacao, 1.2 km avant Bellevue \[ 4.548N 52.4915W \]. Distribution: French Guiana.
