## 摘要

EN — **大型无刃白蚁** (*Astratotermes prosenus*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Astratotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Nigeria: Northern Region: 30 miles from Lokoja on Okene Road. Distribution: tropical Africa.
