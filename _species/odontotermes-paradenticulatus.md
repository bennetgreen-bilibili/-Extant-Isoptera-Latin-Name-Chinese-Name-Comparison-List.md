EN — **似齿小土白蚁** (*Odontotermes paradenticulatus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Ahmad in 1947; the type locality is Philippines: Leyte, west of Tarragona, east shore of Lake Bito. Distribution: Philippines.
