## 摘要

**具沟异白蚁**（*Heterotermes sulcatus*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）异白蚁属（*Heterotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]。分布记录包括南美洲、巴西、委内瑞拉、苏里南。

EN — **具沟异白蚁** (*Heterotermes sulcatus*) is a species of the genus *Heterotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Venezuela, Suriname.
