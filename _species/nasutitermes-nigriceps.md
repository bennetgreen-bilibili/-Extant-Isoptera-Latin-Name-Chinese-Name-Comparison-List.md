## 摘要

**黑头象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes nigriceps*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Haldeman 于 1854 年描述，模式产地为 Mexico: Western Mexico。分布记录包括墨西哥、哥伦比亚、巴西、巴拿马、伯利兹、法属圭亚那、安提瓜和巴布达、阿鲁巴、巴哈马、博奈尔、开曼群岛、哥斯达黎加、库拉索、厄瓜多尔、萨尔瓦多、危地马拉、洪都拉斯、牙买加、尼加拉瓜、秘鲁、波多黎各、特立尼达和多巴哥、特克斯和凯科斯群岛、委内瑞拉、美属维尔京群岛。本站记录本种有 3 个次异名。

EN — **黑头象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes nigriceps*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Haldeman in 1854; the type locality is Mexico: Western Mexico. Distribution: Mexico, Colombia, Brazil, Panama, Belize, French Guiana, Antigua and Barbuda, Aruba, Bahamas, Bonaire, Cayman Islands, Costa Rica, Curaçao, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Jamaica, Nicaragua, Peru, Puerto Rico, Trinidad and Tobago, Turks and Caicos Islands, Venezuela, United States Virgin Islands. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.
