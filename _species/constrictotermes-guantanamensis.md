## 摘要

**关塔那摩缩白蚁**（*Constrictotermes guantanamensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）缩白蚁属（*Constrictotermes*）的一种。本种由 Krecek, Scheffrahn & Roisin 于 1996 年描述，模式产地为 Cuba: Guantanamo: Loma de Macambo。分布记录包括加勒比古巴。

EN — **关塔那摩缩白蚁** (*Constrictotermes guantanamensis*) is a species of the genus *Constrictotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Krecek, Scheffrahn & Roisin in 1996; the type locality is Cuba: Guantanamo: Loma de Macambo. Distribution: Caribbean, Cuba.
