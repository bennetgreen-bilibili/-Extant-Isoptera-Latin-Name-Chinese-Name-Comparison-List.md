## 摘要

**双角双角白蚁**（*Bicornitermes bicornis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）双角白蚁属（*Bicornitermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Krishna 于 1961 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River。分布记录包括中非、刚果民主共和国、乌干达。

EN — **双角双角白蚁** (*Bicornitermes bicornis*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Bicornitermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna in 1961; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda.
