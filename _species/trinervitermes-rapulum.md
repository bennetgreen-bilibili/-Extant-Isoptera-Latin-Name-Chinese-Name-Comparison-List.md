## 摘要

**蔓菁三脉白蚁**（*Trinervitermes rapulum*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）三脉白蚁属（*Trinervitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1904 年描述，模式产地为 Tanzania: Tanga。分布记录包括南非、埃塞俄比亚、马拉维、坦桑尼亚、津巴布韦。本站记录本种有 3 个次异名。

EN — **蔓菁三脉白蚁** (*Trinervitermes rapulum*) is a species of the genus *Trinervitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1904; the type locality is Tanzania: Tanga. Distribution: South Africa, Ethiopia, Malawi, Tanzania, Zimbabwe. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.
