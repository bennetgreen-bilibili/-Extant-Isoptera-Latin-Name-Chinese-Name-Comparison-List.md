## 摘要

**多产土白蚁**（*Odontotermes dives*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Hagen 于 1858 年描述，模式产地为 Philippines: Luzon: Manila。分布记录包括东南亚印度尼西亚、菲律宾、马来西亚。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **多产土白蚁** (*Odontotermes dives*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Philippines: Luzon: Manila. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
