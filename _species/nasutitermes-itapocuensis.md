## 摘要

**伊塔波库象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes itapocuensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Holmgren 于 1910 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Santa Catarina: Blumenau \[26.91S 49.044W\]; Santa Catarina: Jaraguá do Sul \[26.4905S 49.0763W\]; São Paulo: São Paulo \[23.53S 46.62W\]。分布记录包括巴西、阿根廷。

EN — **伊塔波库象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes itapocuensis*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1910; the type locality is Brasil: Santa Catarina: Blumenau \[26.91S 49.044W\]; Santa Catarina: Jaraguá do Sul \[26.4905S 49.0763W\]; São Paulo: São Paulo \[23.53S 46.62W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Argentina.
