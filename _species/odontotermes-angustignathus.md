## 摘要

**细颚土白蚁**（*Odontotermes angustignathus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Tsai & Chen 于 1963 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Cheli。分布记录包括东南亚越南、中国。

EN — **细颚土白蚁** (*Odontotermes angustignathus*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Tsai & Chen in 1963; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Cheli. Distribution: Southeast Asia, Vietnam, China.
