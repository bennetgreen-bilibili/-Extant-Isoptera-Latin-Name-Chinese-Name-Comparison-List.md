## 摘要

**贝奎特土白蚁**（*Odontotermes bequaerti*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Emerson 于 1928 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Thysville。分布记录包括旧热带界西非、中非、刚果民主共和国、加纳。

EN — **贝奎特土白蚁** (*Odontotermes bequaerti*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Emerson in 1928; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Thysville. Distribution: Old World tropics, West Africa, Central Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana.
