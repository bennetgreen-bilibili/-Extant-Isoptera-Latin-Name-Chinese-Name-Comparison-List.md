## 摘要

**圆胸圆球白蚁**（*Sphaerotermes sphaerothorax*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）圆球白蚁亚科（Sphaerotermitinae）圆球白蚁属（*Sphaerotermes*）的现存唯一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu。分布记录包括刚果（布）、喀麦隆、中非共和国、安哥拉、刚果民主共和国、加蓬、几内亚、尼日利亚。

EN — **圆胸圆球白蚁** (*Sphaerotermes sphaerothorax*) is the only extant species of the genus *Sphaerotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Sphaerotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), Cameroon, Central African Republic, Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon, Guinea, Nigeria.
