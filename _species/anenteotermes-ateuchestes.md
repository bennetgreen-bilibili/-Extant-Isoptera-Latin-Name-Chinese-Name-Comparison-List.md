## 摘要

**无锐无甲白蚁**（*Anenteotermes ateuchestes*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）无甲白蚁属（*Anenteotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Gambia: 11 m. from Bathurst on Yundum Road。分布记录包括喀麦隆、冈比亚、坦桑尼亚。

EN — **无锐无甲白蚁** (*Anenteotermes ateuchestes*) is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Gambia: 11 m. from Bathurst on Yundum Road. Distribution: Cameroon, Gambia, Tanzania.
