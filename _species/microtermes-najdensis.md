## 摘要

**内志小白蚁**（*Microtermes najdensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）小白蚁属（*Microtermes*）的一种。本种由 Harris 于 1964 年描述，模式产地为 Saudi Arabia: Riyadh。分布记录包括阿拉伯半岛、沙特阿拉伯、也门、苏丹。

EN — **内志小白蚁** (*Microtermes najdensis*) is a species of the genus *Microtermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1964; the type locality is Saudi Arabia: Riyadh. Distribution: Arabian Peninsula, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Sudan.
