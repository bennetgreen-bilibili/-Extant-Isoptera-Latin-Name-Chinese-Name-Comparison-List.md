## 摘要

**短小无名白蚁**（*Ereymatermes piquira*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）无名白蚁属（*Ereymatermes*）的一种。本种由 Cancello & Cuezzo 于 2007 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Bahia: Ilhéus \[14.7972S 39.073W\]。分布记录包括南美洲、巴西、秘鲁。

EN — **短小无名白蚁** (*Ereymatermes piquira*) is a species of the genus *Ereymatermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Cancello & Cuezzo in 2007; the type locality is Brasil: Bahia: Ilhéus \[14.7972S 39.073W\]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Peru.
