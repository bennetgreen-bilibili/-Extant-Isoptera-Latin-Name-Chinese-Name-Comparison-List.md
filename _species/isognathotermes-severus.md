## 摘要

**严肃等颚白蚁**（*Isognathotermes severus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）等颚白蚁属（*Isognathotermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Guinea: Camayenne; Kakoulima。分布记录包括西非科特迪瓦、喀麦隆、刚果民主共和国、冈比亚、几内亚、尼日利亚、苏丹、坦桑尼亚。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **严肃等颚白蚁** (*Isognathotermes severus*) is a species of the genus *Isognathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne; Kakoulima. Distribution: West Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gambia, Guinea, Nigeria, Sudan, Tanzania. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
