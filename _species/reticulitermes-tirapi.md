## 摘要

**特拉普散白蚁**（*Reticulitermes tirapi*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）散白蚁属（*Reticulitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Chhotani & Das 于 1983 年描述，模式产地为 India: Arunachal Pradesh: Tirap District: Nampong。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **特拉普散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes tirapi*) is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Chhotani & Das in 1983; the type locality is India: Arunachal Pradesh: Tirap District: Nampong. Distribution: India.
