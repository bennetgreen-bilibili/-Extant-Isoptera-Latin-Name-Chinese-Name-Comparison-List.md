## 摘要

**毛颏棒鼻白蚁**（*Parrhinotermes barbatus*）是鼻白蚁科（RHINOTERMITIDAE）棒鼻白蚁属（*Parrhinotermes*）的一种，隶属新等翅类（Neoisoptera）。本种由 Bourguignon & Roisin 于 2011 年描述，模式产地为 Papua New Guinea: Southern Highlands: Lake Kutubu。分布记录包括印度尼西亚、巴布亚新几内亚。

EN — **毛颏棒鼻白蚁** (*Parrhinotermes barbatus*) is a species of the genus *Parrhinotermes*, in the family RHINOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Neoisoptera. The species was described by Bourguignon & Roisin in 2011; the type locality is Papua New Guinea: Southern Highlands: Lake Kutubu. Distribution: Indonesia, Papua New Guinea.
