## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1913; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Limumbashi. Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Côte d'Ivoire.

ES — **贝奎特锯白蚁** (*Microcerotermes bequaertianus*) es una especie del género *Microcerotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Microcerotermitinae).
