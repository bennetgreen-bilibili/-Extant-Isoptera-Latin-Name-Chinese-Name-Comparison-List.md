**体大顺白蚁**（*Acholotermes tithasus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）顺白蚁属（*Acholotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Yangambi。分布记录包括非洲热带。

EN — **体大顺白蚁** (*Acholotermes tithasus*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Acholotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Yangambi. Distribution: tropical Africa.
