EN — **姚氏土白蚁** (*Odontotermes yaoi*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Huang & Li in 1988; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Longxi County. Distribution: China (Fujian).
