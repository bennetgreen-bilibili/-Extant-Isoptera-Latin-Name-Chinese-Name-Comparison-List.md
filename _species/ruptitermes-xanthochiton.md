## 摘要

**黄袍腹爆白蚁**（*Ruptitermes xanthochiton*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）腹爆白蚁属（*Ruptitermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]。分布记录包括南美洲、巴西、巴拉圭、法属圭亚那、玻利维亚。

EN — **黄袍腹爆白蚁** (*Ruptitermes xanthochiton*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Ruptitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Paraguay, French Guiana, Bolivia.

La especie fue descrita por Mathews en 1977; la localidad tipo es Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[ 12.8145S 51.7745W \].
