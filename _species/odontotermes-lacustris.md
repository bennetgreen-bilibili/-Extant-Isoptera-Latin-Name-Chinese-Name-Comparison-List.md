## 摘要

**湖泊土白蚁**（*Odontotermes lacustris*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Harris 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 Zambia: Abercorn。分布记录包括旧热带界东非、非洲南部、马拉维、赞比亚。

EN — **湖泊土白蚁** (*Odontotermes lacustris*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1960; the type locality is Zambia: Abercorn. Distribution: Old World tropics, East Africa, southern Africa, Malawi, Zambia.
