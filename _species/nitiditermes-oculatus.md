## 摘要

**眼显净白蚁**（*Nitiditermes oculatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）净白蚁属（*Nitiditermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Guinea: Camayenne。分布记录包括加纳、几内亚、尼日利亚、塞内加尔、苏丹、坦桑尼亚。

EN — **眼显净白蚁** (*Nitiditermes oculatus*) is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Guinea: Camayenne. Distribution: Ghana, Guinea, Nigeria, Senegal, Sudan, Tanzania.
