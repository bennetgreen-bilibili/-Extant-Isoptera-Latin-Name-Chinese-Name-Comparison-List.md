EN — **浙江亮白蚁** (*Euhamitermes zhejianensis*) is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by He & Xia in 1983; the type locality is China: Zhejiang Province: Quxian. Distribution: China, Zhejiang.
