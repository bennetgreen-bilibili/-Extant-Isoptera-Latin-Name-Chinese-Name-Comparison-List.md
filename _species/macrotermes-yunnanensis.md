## 摘要

**云南大白蚁**（*Macrotermes yunnanensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）大白蚁属（*Macrotermes*）的一种。本种由 Han 于 1986 年描述，模式产地为 China: Yunnan Province: Damenglong, Jinghong。分布记录包括中国云南、景洪大勐龙。

EN — **云南大白蚁** (*Macrotermes yunnanensis*) is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Han in 1986; the type locality is China: Yunnan Province: Damenglong, Jinghong. Distribution: China (Yunnan), Jinghong (Damenglong).
