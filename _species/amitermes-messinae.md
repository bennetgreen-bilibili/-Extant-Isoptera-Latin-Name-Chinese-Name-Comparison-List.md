## 摘要

**墨西拿弓白蚁**（*Amitermes messinae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）弓白蚁属（*Amitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Fuller 于 1922 年描述，模式产地为 South Africa: Transvaal: Messina。分布记录包括南非、埃及、埃塞俄比亚、伊朗、肯尼亚、马拉维、沙特阿拉伯、苏丹、坦桑尼亚、也门、赞比亚。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **墨西拿弓白蚁** (*Amitermes messinae*) is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Fuller in 1922; the type locality is South Africa: Transvaal: Messina. Distribution: South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Kenya, Malawi, Saudi Arabia, Sudan, Tanzania, Yemen, Zambia. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
