## 摘要

**萨拉山瓢白蚁**（*Bulbitermes salakensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）瓢白蚁属（*Bulbitermes*）的一种。本种由 Kemner 于 1934 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Java: Depok, near Bogor; Kali Bata, near Bogor; Tjandjoer, Vada; near Tjidaho。分布记录包括印度尼西亚。

EN — **萨拉山瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes salakensis*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Kemner in 1934; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Depok, near Bogor; Kali Bata, near Bogor; Tjandjoer, Vada; near Tjidaho. Distribution: Indonesia.
