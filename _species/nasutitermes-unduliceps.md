## 摘要

**浪头象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes unduliceps*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]。分布记录包括巴西、法属圭亚那。

EN — **浪头象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes unduliceps*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana.
