## 摘要

**长鼻怒白蚁**（*Lacessititermes longinasus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）怒白蚁属（*Lacessititermes*）的一种。本种由 Syaukani 于 2008 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Sumatra: Mentawai Islands: Siberut Island: Simabuggei: Siberut National Park。分布记录包括印度尼西亚、苏门答腊。

EN — **长鼻怒白蚁** (*Lacessititermes longinasus*) is a species of the genus *Lacessititermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Syaukani in 2008; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Mentawai Islands: Siberut Island: Simabuggei: Siberut National Park. Distribution: Indonesia, Sumatra.
