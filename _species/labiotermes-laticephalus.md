## 摘要

**宽头厚唇白蚁**（*Labiotermes laticephalus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）聚白蚁亚科（Syntermitinae）厚唇白蚁属（*Labiotermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1901 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso do Sul: Ladário, Urucum \[19.155S 57.631W\]; Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]。分布记录包括巴西、玻利维亚、巴拉圭、阿根廷。

EN — **宽头厚唇白蚁** (*Labiotermes laticephalus*) is a species of the genus *Labiotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1901; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso do Sul: Ladário, Urucum \[19.155S 57.631W\]; Mato Grosso: Cuiabá, Coxipó (district) \[15.627S 56.058W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Paraguay, Argentina.
