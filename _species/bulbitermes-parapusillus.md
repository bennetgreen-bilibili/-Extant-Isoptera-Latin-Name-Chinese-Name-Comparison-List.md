## 摘要

**似极小瓢白蚁**（*Bulbitermes parapusillus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）瓢白蚁属（*Bulbitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Ahmad 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 Thailand: Prew。分布记录包括孟加拉国、印度、马来西亚、泰国。

EN — **似极小瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes parapusillus*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Ahmad in 1965; the type locality is Thailand: Prew. Distribution: Bangladesh, India, Malaysia, Thailand.
