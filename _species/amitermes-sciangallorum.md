## 摘要

**夏安盖洛姆弓白蚁**（*Amitermes sciangallorum*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）弓白蚁属（*Amitermes*）的一种。本种由 Ghidini 于 1941 年描述，模式产地为 Ethiopia: Murlè。分布记录包括埃塞俄比亚、肯尼亚、苏丹、坦桑尼亚。

EN — **夏安盖洛姆弓白蚁** (*Amitermes sciangallorum*) is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Ghidini in 1941; the type locality is Ethiopia: Murlè. Distribution: Ethiopia, Kenya, Sudan, Tanzania.
