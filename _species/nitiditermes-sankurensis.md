## 摘要

**桑库鲁净白蚁**（*Nitiditermes sankurensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）净白蚁属（*Nitiditermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Wasmann 于 1911 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai)。分布记录包括安哥拉、刚果共和国、刚果民主共和国、几内亚、肯尼亚、马拉维、马里、尼日利亚、塞内加尔、坦桑尼亚、赞比亚、津巴布韦。本站记录本种有 3 个次异名。

EN — **桑库鲁净白蚁** (*Nitiditermes sankurensis*) is a species of the genus *Nitiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Wasmann in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Sankuru (Kassai). Distribution: Angola, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea, Kenya, Malawi, Mali, Nigeria, Senegal, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe. This site records 3 junior synonyms for the species.
