## 摘要

**贝奎特角象白蚁**（*Cornitermes bequaerti*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）聚白蚁亚科（Syntermitinae）角象白蚁属（*Cornitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Emerson 于 1952 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: São Paulo: Tatuí \[23.35S 47.85W\]。分布记录包括巴西、玻利维亚、巴拉圭。

EN — **贝奎特角象白蚁** (*Cornitermes bequaerti*) is a species of the genus *Cornitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Emerson in 1952; the type locality is Brasil: São Paulo: Tatuí \[23.35S 47.85W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Paraguay.
