## 摘要

**暗头地白蚁**（*Hypotermes obscuriceps*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）地白蚁属（*Hypotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Wasmann 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 Sri Lanka: Trincomali。分布记录包括印度、斯里兰卡、越南。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **暗头地白蚁** (*Hypotermes obscuriceps*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Hypotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Trincomali. Distribution: India, Sri Lanka, Vietnam. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
