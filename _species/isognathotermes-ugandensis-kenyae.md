## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

**肯尼亚亚种**（*Isognathotermes ugandensis kenyae*）是乌干达等颚白蚁（*Isognathotermes ugandensis*）的一个亚种，隶属白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）等颚白蚁属（*Isognathotermes*）。本亚种由 Josens & Deligne 于 2025 年描述，模式产地为 Kenya: Mukuyuni \[ 0.0269N 36.0051E \]。本页暂无单列的分布记录，母种 *Isognathotermes ugandensis* 的分布见下文「分布」一节。

EN — **肯尼亚亚种** (*Isognathotermes ugandensis kenyae*) is a subspecies of *Isognathotermes ugandensis*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), genus *Isognathotermes*. The subspecies was described by Josens & Deligne in 2025; the type locality is Kenya: Mukuyuni \[ 0.0269N 36.0051E \]. No separate distribution record is listed on this page; see the parent species *Isognathotermes ugandensis*.

ES — **肯尼亚亚种** (*Isognathotermes ugandensis kenyae*) es una subespecie de *Isognathotermes ugandensis*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Cubitermitinae), género *Isognathotermes*.
