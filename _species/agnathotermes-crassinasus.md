## 摘要

**粗鼻无颚白蚁**（*Agnathotermes crassinasus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）无颚白蚁属（*Agnathotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Constantino 于 1990 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[1.848S 65.523W\]。分布记录包括巴西、哥伦比亚、厄瓜多尔。

EN — **粗鼻无颚白蚁** (*Agnathotermes crassinasus*) is a species of the genus *Agnathotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Constantino in 1990; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Maraã \[1.848S 65.523W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador.
