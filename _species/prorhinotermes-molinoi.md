## 摘要

**莫利诺原鼻白蚁**（*Prorhinotermes molinoi*）是沙白蚁科（PSAMMOTERMITIDAE）原鼻白蚁属（*Prorhinotermes*）的一种，隶属新等翅类（Neoisoptera）。本种由 Snyder 于 1924 年描述，模式产地为 Panama: Largo Remo Island。分布记录包括巴拿马、哥斯达黎加、危地马拉、洪都拉斯。

EN — **莫利诺原鼻白蚁** (*Prorhinotermes molinoi*) is a species of the genus *Prorhinotermes*, in the family PSAMMOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Neoisoptera. The species was described by Snyder in 1924; the type locality is Panama: Largo Remo Island. Distribution: Panama, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras.
