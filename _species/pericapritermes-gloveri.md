## 摘要

**格洛弗近扭白蚁**（*Pericapritermes gloveri*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）近扭白蚁属（*Pericapritermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Harris 于 1951 年描述，模式产地为 Tanzania: Kakoma, 50 miles south of Tabora; Kapondwa, near Kasanga; Zambia: Kalambo Falls, south bank river。分布记录包括坦桑尼亚、赞比亚。

EN — **格洛弗近扭白蚁** (*Pericapritermes gloveri*) is a species of the genus *Pericapritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Harris in 1951; the type locality is Tanzania: Kakoma, 50 miles south of Tabora; Kapondwa, near Kasanga; Zambia: Kalambo Falls, south bank river. Distribution: Tanzania, Zambia.
