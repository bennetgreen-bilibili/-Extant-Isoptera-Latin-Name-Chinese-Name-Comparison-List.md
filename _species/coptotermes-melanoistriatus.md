EN — **黑带乳白蚁** (*Coptotermes melanoistriatus*) is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Gao, Lau & He in 1995; the type locality is China: Hong Kong: New Territories: Fanling, Kam Tsin Chuen—Fung Shui Ground. Distribution: China (Hong Kong).
