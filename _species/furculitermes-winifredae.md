## 摘要

**威尼弗雷德叉白蚁**（*Furculitermes winifredae*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）叉白蚁属（*Furculitermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Emerson 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River。分布记录包括旧热带界。

EN — **威尼弗雷德叉白蚁** (*Furculitermes winifredae*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Furculitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Emerson in 1960; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Camp Putnam, Epulu River. Distribution: Old World tropics.
