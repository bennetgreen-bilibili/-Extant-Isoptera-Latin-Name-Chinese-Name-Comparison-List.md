## 摘要

**克什米尔角白蚁**（*Angulitermes kashmirensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）前奇白蚁亚科（Promirotermitinae）角白蚁属（*Angulitermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Chhotani 于 1971 年描述，模式产地为 India: Jammu and Kashmir, ca. 10 km east of Chineni near confluence of River Tawi and Binisangh Nullah。分布记录包括印度。

EN — **克什米尔角白蚁** (*Angulitermes kashmirensis*) is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1971; the type locality is India: Jammu and Kashmir, ca. 10 km east of Chineni near confluence of River Tawi and Binisangh Nullah. Distribution: India.

La especie fue descrita por Roonwal & Chhotani en 1971; la localidad tipo es India: Jammu and Kashmir, ca. 10 km east of Chineni near confluence of River Tawi and Binisangh Nullah.
