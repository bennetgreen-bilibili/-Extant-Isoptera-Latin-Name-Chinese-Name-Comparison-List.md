## 摘要

**斯奈德生白蚁**（*Embiratermes snyderi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）聚白蚁亚科（Syntermitinae）生白蚁属（*Embiratermes*）的一种。本种由 Emerson & Banks 于 1957 年描述，模式产地为 Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Oko River \[6.46667N 58.8667W\]。分布记录包括法属圭亚那、圭亚那。

EN — **斯奈德生白蚁** (*Embiratermes snyderi*) is a species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson & Banks in 1957; the type locality is Guyana: Cuyuni-Mazaruni: Oko River \[6.46667N 58.8667W\]. Distribution: French Guiana, Guyana.
