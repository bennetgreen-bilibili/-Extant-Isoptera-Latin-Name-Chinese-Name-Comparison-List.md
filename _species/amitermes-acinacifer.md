## 摘要

**短刀弓白蚁**（*Amitermes acinacifer*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）弓白蚁属（*Amitermes*）的一种。本种由 Sands 于 1959 年描述，模式产地为 Kenya: Masabit district, 43 km from Masabit township on the Isiolo Road。分布记录包括埃塞俄比亚、肯尼亚。

The species was described by Sands in 1959; the type locality is Kenya: Masabit district, 43 km from Masabit township on the Isiolo Road. Distribution: Ethiopia, Kenya.
