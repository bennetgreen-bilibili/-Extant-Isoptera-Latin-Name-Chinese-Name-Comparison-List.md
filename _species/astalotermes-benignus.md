## 摘要

**仁慈无利器白蚁**（*Astalotermes benignus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）无利器白蚁属（*Astalotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Guinea: Mt. Nimba: Pierré Richaud。分布记录包括非洲热带、几内亚、科特迪瓦、尼日利亚。

EN — **仁慈无利器白蚁** (*Astalotermes benignus*) is a species of the genus *Astalotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Guinea: Mt. Nimba: Pierré Richaud. Distribution: tropical Africa, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Nigeria.
