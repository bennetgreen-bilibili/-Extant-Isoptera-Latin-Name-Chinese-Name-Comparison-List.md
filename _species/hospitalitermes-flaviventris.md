## 摘要

**黄腹须白蚁**（*Hospitalitermes flaviventris*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）须白蚁属（*Hospitalitermes*）的一种。本种由 Wasmann 于 1902 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Negeri Sembilan: Berhentian Tingi。分布记录包括印度尼西亚、马来西亚。

EN — **黄腹须白蚁** (*Hospitalitermes flaviventris*) is a species of the genus *Hospitalitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Wasmann in 1902; the type locality is Malaysia: Negeri Sembilan: Berhentian Tingi. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.
