**霍姆格伦前奇白蚁**（*Promirotermes holmgreni*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）前奇白蚁亚科（Promirotermitinae）前奇白蚁属（*Promirotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1912 年描述，模式产地为 Guinea Bissau: Bolama。分布记录包括塞内加尔、几内亚比绍、加纳、科特迪瓦、多哥、尼日利亚、喀麦隆。本站记录本种有 1 个次异名。

EN — **霍姆格伦前奇白蚁** (*Promirotermes holmgreni*) is a species and the type species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama. Distribution: Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.

La especie fue descrita por Silvestri en 1912; la localidad tipo es Guinea Bissau: Bolama. Distribución: Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon.
