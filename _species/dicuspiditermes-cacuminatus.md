## 摘要

**尖齿突扭白蚁**（*Dicuspiditermes cacuminatus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）瘤白蚁亚科（Mirocapritermitinae）突扭白蚁属（*Dicuspiditermes*）的一种。本种由 Krishna 于 2001 年描述，模式产地为 Malaysia: Selangor: Sungei Buloh Forest Reserve, 150 ft. altitude。分布记录包括马来西亚。

EN — **尖齿突扭白蚁** (*Dicuspiditermes cacuminatus*) is a species of the genus *Dicuspiditermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Mirocapritermitinae). The species was described by Krishna in 2001; the type locality is Malaysia: Selangor: Sungei Buloh Forest Reserve, 150 ft. altitude. Distribution: Malaysia.
