## 摘要

**宽齿生白蚁**（*Embiratermes latidens*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）聚白蚁亚科（Syntermitinae）生白蚁属（*Embiratermes*）的一种。本种由 Emerson & Banks 于 1957 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Amazonas: Autazes, Rio Autaz \[3.58S 59.125W\]。分布记录包括巴西、玻利维亚、哥伦比亚、巴拉圭、秘鲁、苏里南、委内瑞拉。

EN — **宽齿生白蚁** (*Embiratermes latidens*) is a species of the genus *Embiratermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Emerson & Banks in 1957; the type locality is Brasil: Amazonas: Autazes, Rio Autaz \[3.58S 59.125W\]. Distribution: Brazil, Bolivia, Colombia, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Venezuela.

ES — **宽齿生白蚁** (*Embiratermes latidens*) es una especie del género *Embiratermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Syntermitinae).
