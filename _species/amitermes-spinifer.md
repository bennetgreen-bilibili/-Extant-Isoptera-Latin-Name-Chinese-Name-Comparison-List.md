## 摘要

**具刺弓白蚁**（*Amitermes spinifer*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）弓白蚁亚科（Amitermitinae）弓白蚁属（*Amitermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Senegal: Dakar; Hann; Thiès。分布记录包括西非、东非、肯尼亚、坦桑尼亚、乌干达。

EN — **具刺弓白蚁** (*Amitermes spinifer*) is a species of the genus *Amitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Amitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Senegal: Dakar; Hann; Thiès. Distribution: West Africa, East Africa, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
