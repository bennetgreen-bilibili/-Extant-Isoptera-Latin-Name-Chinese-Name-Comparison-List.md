## 摘要

**拉克什曼瓢白蚁**（*Bulbitermes lakshmani*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）瓢白蚁属（*Bulbitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Roonwal & Maiti 于 1966 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Peutjang Island, near western tip of Java。分布记录包括印度尼西亚。

EN — **拉克什曼瓢白蚁** (*Bulbitermes lakshmani*) is a species of the genus *Bulbitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Roonwal & Maiti in 1966; the type locality is Indonesia: Peutjang Island, near western tip of Java. Distribution: Indonesia.
