## 摘要

**多魁无甲白蚁**（*Anenteotermes polyscolus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）无甲白蚁属（*Anenteotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sands 于 1972 年描述，模式产地为 Ghana: 10 mi N Wenchi on Bamboi Road。分布记录包括西非、中非、刚果盆地。

EN — **多魁无甲白蚁** (*Anenteotermes polyscolus*) is a species of the genus *Anenteotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Sands in 1972; the type locality is Ghana: 10 mi N Wenchi on Bamboi Road. Distribution: West Africa, Central Africa, Congo Basin.
