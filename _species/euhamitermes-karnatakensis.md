## 摘要

**卡纳塔克亮白蚁**（*Euhamitermes karnatakensis*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）亮白蚁属（*Euhamitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Roonwal & Chhotani 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 India: Karnataka (Mysore): Dharwar, open ground opposite Karnataka University Compound, coll. sta. No. 5。分布记录包括印度卡纳塔克。

EN — **卡纳塔克亮白蚁** (*Euhamitermes karnatakensis*) is a species of the genus *Euhamitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Roonwal & Chhotani in 1965; the type locality is India: Karnataka (Mysore): Dharwar, open ground opposite Karnataka University Compound, coll. sta. No. 5. Distribution: Karnataka, India.
