## 摘要

**细颚散白蚁**（*Reticulitermes leptomandibularis*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）散白蚁属（*Reticulitermes*）的一种。本种由 Hsia & Fan 于 1965 年描述，模式产地为 China: Fujian Province: Yung-an。分布记录包括东亚、台湾、中国。

EN — **细颚散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes leptomandibularis*) is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Hsia & Fan in 1965; the type locality is China: Fujian Province: Yung-an. Distribution: East Asia, Taiwan, China.
