## 摘要

**方头角白蚁**（*Angulitermes quadriceps*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）前奇白蚁亚科（Promirotermitinae）角白蚁属（*Angulitermes*）的一种。本种由 Harris 于 1964 年描述，模式产地为 Israel: Sdom area, on Sdom-Ilat road along west slope of rift valley。分布记录包括以色列、沙特阿拉伯、也门。

EN — **方头角白蚁** (*Angulitermes quadriceps*) is a species of the genus *Angulitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Harris in 1964; the type locality is Israel: Sdom area, on Sdom-Ilat road along west slope of rift valley. Distribution: Israel, Saudi Arabia, Yemen.
