## 摘要

**格斯瓦尔德尖白蚁**（*Apicotermes goesswaldi*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）尖白蚁亚科（Apicotermitinae）尖白蚁属（*Apicotermes*）的一种。本种由 Bouillon 于 1966 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mayumbe region, Kakongo, near Lemba River on the side of Pemo village。分布记录包括安哥拉、刚果民主共和国。

EN — **格斯瓦尔德尖白蚁** (*Apicotermes goesswaldi*) is a species of the genus *Apicotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Bouillon in 1966; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mayumbe region, Kakongo, near Lemba River on the side of Pemo village. Distribution: Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

ES — **格斯瓦尔德尖白蚁** (*Apicotermes goesswaldi*) es una especie del género *Apicotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Apicotermitinae).
