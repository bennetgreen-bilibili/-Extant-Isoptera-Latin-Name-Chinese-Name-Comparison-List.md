## 摘要

**光背象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes glabritergus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Snyder & Emerson 于 1949 年描述，模式产地为 Costa Rica: Colombiana。分布记录包括哥斯达黎加、巴拿马、危地马拉、厄瓜多尔。

EN — **光背象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes glabritergus*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Snyder & Emerson in 1949; the type locality is Costa Rica: Colombiana. Distribution: Costa Rica, Panama, Guatemala, Ecuador.
