The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1911; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Mukimbungu. Distribution: Cameroon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

ES — **侏儒无甲白蚁** (*Anenteotermes nanus*) es una especie del género *Anenteotermes*, en la familia TERMITIDAE (subfamilia Apicotermitinae).
