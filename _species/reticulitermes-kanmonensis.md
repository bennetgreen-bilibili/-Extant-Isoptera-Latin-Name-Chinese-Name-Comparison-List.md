## 摘要

**关门散白蚁**（*Reticulitermes kanmonensis*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）散白蚁属（*Reticulitermes*）的一种。本种由 Takematsu 于 1999 年描述，模式产地为 Japan: Yamaguchi Province: Atsugi。分布记录包括日本、朝鲜半岛、中国、台湾、韩国。

EN — **关门散白蚁** (*Reticulitermes kanmonensis*) is a species of the genus *Reticulitermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Takematsu in 1999; the type locality is Japan: Yamaguchi Province: Atsugi. Distribution: Japan, Korean Peninsula, China, Taiwan, South Korea.
