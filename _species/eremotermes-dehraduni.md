## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is India: Uttarakhand: about 12 miles west of Dehra Dun: Malhan Forest Range: Bainkhala Block, in Ocimum plantation. Distribution: India.
