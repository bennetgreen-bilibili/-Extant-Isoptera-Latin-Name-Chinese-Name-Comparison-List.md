## 摘要 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Summary</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Resumen</span>

**角头壶头白蚁**（*Cyrilliotermes angulariceps*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）聚白蚁亚科（Syntermitinae）壶头白蚁属（*Cyrilliotermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Mathews 于 1977 年描述，模式产地为 Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]。分布记录包括南美洲、巴西、哥伦比亚、法属圭亚那、苏里南、秘鲁、厄瓜多尔。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **角头壶头白蚁** (*Cyrilliotermes angulariceps*) is a species and the type species of the genus *Cyrilliotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Syntermitinae). The species was described by Mathews in 1977; the type locality is Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\]. Distribution: South America, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Suriname, Peru, Ecuador. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.

La especie fue descrita por Mathews en 1977; la localidad tipo es Brasil: Mato Grosso: Ribeirão Cascalheira, Base Camp, Xavantina-Cachimbo Expedition \[12.8145S 51.7745W\].
