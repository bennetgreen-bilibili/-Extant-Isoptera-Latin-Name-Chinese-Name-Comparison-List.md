## 摘要

**暗黄大白蚁**（*Macrotermes gilvus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）大白蚁属（*Macrotermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Hagen 于 1858 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Java: Bogor。分布记录包括旧热带界、印度尼西亚、马来西亚、菲律宾、新加坡、泰国、柬埔寨、东帝汶、缅甸、越南。本站记录本种有 12 个次异名。

EN — **暗黄大白蚁** (*Macrotermes gilvus*) is a species of the genus *Macrotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Hagen in 1858; the type locality is Indonesia: Java: Bogor. Distribution: Old World tropics, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Cambodia, Timor-Leste, Myanmar, Vietnam. This site records 12 junior synonyms for the species.
