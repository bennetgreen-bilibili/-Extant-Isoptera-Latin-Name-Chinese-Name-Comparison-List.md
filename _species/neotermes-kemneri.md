## 摘要

**凯姆勒新白蚁**（*Neotermes kemneri*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）新白蚁属（*Neotermes*）的一种。本种由 Roonwal & Sen-Sarma 于 1960 年描述，模式产地为 Sri Lanka: Peradeniya: Gannoruwa。分布记录包括南亚、印度、斯里兰卡。

EN — **凯姆勒新白蚁** (*Neotermes kemneri*) is a species of the genus *Neotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Roonwal & Sen-Sarma in 1960; the type locality is Sri Lanka: Peradeniya: Gannoruwa. Distribution: South Asia, India, Sri Lanka.
