EN — **曼长工白蚁** (*Longustitermes manni*) is the only extant species of the genus *Longustitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Apicotermitinae). The species was described by Snyder in 1922; the type locality is Honduras: Lombardia. Distribution: Brazil, French Guiana, Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, Costa Rica, Honduras, Panama. This site records 1 junior synonym for the species.
