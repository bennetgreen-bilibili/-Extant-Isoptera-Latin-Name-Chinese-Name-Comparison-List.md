**细足前奇白蚁**（*Promirotermes gracilipes*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）前奇白蚁亚科（Promirotermitinae）前奇白蚁属（*Promirotermes*）的一种。本种由 Schmitz 于 1917 年描述，模式产地为 Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville)。分布记录包括刚果民主共和国、刚果共和国、肯尼亚、乌干达。

EN — **细足前奇白蚁** (*Promirotermes gracilipes*) is a species of the genus *Promirotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Promirotermitinae). The species was described by Schmitz in 1917; the type locality is Democratic Republic of the Congo: Kisangani (Stanleyville). Distribution: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Uganda.
