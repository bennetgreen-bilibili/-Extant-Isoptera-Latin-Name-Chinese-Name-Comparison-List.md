## 摘要

**巴特里彭唇白蚁**（*Labritermes buttelreepeni*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）孔白蚁亚科（Foraminitermitinae）唇白蚁属（*Labritermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Holmgren 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat。分布记录包括印度尼西亚、马来西亚。

EN — **巴特里彭唇白蚁** (*Labritermes buttelreepeni*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Labritermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Foraminitermitinae). The species was described by Holmgren in 1914; the type locality is Indonesia: Sumatra: Tandjong Slamat. Distribution: Indonesia, Malaysia.
