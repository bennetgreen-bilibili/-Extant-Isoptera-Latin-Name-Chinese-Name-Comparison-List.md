## 摘要

**小型象白蚁**（*Nasutitermes minor*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）象白蚁属（*Nasutitermes*）的一种，隶属土栖等翅类（Geoisoptera）。本种由 Holmgren 于 1906 年描述，模式产地为 Bolivia: La Paz: Río Tuichi \[14.28S 68.54W\]。分布记录包括玻利维亚、巴西。

EN — **小型象白蚁** (*Nasutitermes minor*) is a species of the genus *Nasutitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Holmgren in 1906; the type locality is Bolivia: La Paz: Río Tuichi \[14.28S 68.54W\]. Distribution: Bolivia, Brazil.
