## 摘要

**亚当森树白蚁**（*Glyptotermes adamsoni*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）树白蚁属（*Glyptotermes*）的一种。本种由 Krishna & Emerson 于 1962 年描述，模式产地为 Trinidad and Tobago: Tunapuna-Piarco: Near Arima \[ 10.6667N 61.3333W \]。分布记录包括格林纳达、特立尼达和多巴哥、委内瑞拉。

EN — **亚当森树白蚁** (*Glyptotermes adamsoni*) is a species of the genus *Glyptotermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Krishna & Emerson in 1962; the type locality is Trinidad and Tobago: Tunapuna-Piarco: Near Arima \[ 10.6667N 61.3333W \]. Distribution: Grenada, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela.
