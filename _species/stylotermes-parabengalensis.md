## 摘要

**似西孟加拉杆白蚁**（*Stylotermes parabengalensis*）是杆白蚁科（STYLOTERMITIDAE）杆白蚁属（*Stylotermes*）的一种，隶属新等翅类（Neoisoptera）。本种由 Maiti 于 1975 年描述，模式产地为 India: West Bengal: Jalpaiguri District, near Nagrakata railway station, alt. 270 m, 85 km NE of Jalpaiguri。分布记录包括中国、印度。

EN — **似西孟加拉杆白蚁** (*Stylotermes parabengalensis*) is a species of the genus *Stylotermes*, in the family STYLOTERMITIDAE, belonging to the clade Neoisoptera. The species was described by Maiti in 1975; the type locality is India: West Bengal: Jalpaiguri District, near Nagrakata railway station, alt. 270 m, 85 km NE of Jalpaiguri. Distribution: China, India.
