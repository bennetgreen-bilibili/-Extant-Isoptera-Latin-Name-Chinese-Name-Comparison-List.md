## 摘要

EN — **锥鼻棱角白蚁** (*Angularitermes coninasus*) is a species of the genus *Angularitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Carrijo, Rocha, Cuezzo & Cancello in 2011; the type locality is Brasil: Rondônia: Porto Velho, Jaci Paraná, Ilha da Pedra \[9.15S 64.5833W\]. Distribution: Brazil.
