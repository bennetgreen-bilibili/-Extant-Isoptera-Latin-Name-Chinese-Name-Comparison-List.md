EN — **南方土白蚁** (*Odontotermes meridionalis*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae), belonging to the clade Geoisoptera. The species was described by Gao & Yang in 1987; the type locality is China: Guangxi autonomous region: Shanglin. Distribution: China.
