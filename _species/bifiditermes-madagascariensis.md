## 摘要

**马达加斯加双裂白蚁**（*Bifiditermes madagascariensis*）是木白蚁科（KALOTERMITIDAE）双裂白蚁属（*Bifiditermes*）的一种，也是本属的模式种。本种由 Wasmann 于 1897 年描述，模式产地为 Madagascar: Nosy Be。分布记录包括马达加斯加、科摩罗。

EN — **马达加斯加双裂白蚁** (*Bifiditermes madagascariensis*) is a species and the type species of its genus of the genus *Bifiditermes*, in the family KALOTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Wasmann in 1897; the type locality is Madagascar: Nosy Be. Distribution: Madagascar, Comoros.
