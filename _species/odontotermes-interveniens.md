## 摘要

**中间土白蚁**（*Odontotermes interveniens*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）大白蚁亚科（Macrotermitinae）土白蚁属（*Odontotermes*）的一种。本种由 Sjöstedt 于 1924 年描述，模式产地为 Namibia: Okahandja。分布记录包括非洲南部纳米比亚、安哥拉。

EN — **中间土白蚁** (*Odontotermes interveniens*) is a species of the genus *Odontotermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Macrotermitinae). The species was described by Sjöstedt in 1924; the type locality is Namibia: Okahandja. Distribution: southern Africa, Namibia, Angola.
