## 摘要

**裂额基齿白蚁**（*Basidentitermes diversifrons*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）方白蚁亚科（Cubitermitinae）基齿白蚁属（*Basidentitermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1914 年描述，模式产地为 Cameroon: Victoria。分布记录包括旧热带界、喀麦隆、刚果共和国。

EN — **裂额基齿白蚁** (*Basidentitermes diversifrons*) is a species of the genus *Basidentitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Cubitermitinae). The species was described by Silvestri in 1914; the type locality is Cameroon: Victoria. Distribution: Old World tropics, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo.
