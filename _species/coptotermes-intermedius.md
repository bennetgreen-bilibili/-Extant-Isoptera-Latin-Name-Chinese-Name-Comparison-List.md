## 摘要

**中间乳白蚁**（*Coptotermes intermedius*）是异白蚁科（HETEROTERMITIDAE）乳白蚁属（*Coptotermes*）的一种。本种由 Silvestri 于 1912 年描述，模式产地为 Guinea Bissau: Bolama: Rio Cassine。分布记录包括西非、科特迪瓦、塞内加尔、塞拉利昂、布基纳法索、加纳、几内亚比绍、尼日利亚、阿曼。本站记录本种有 2 个次异名。

EN — **中间乳白蚁** (*Coptotermes intermedius*) is a species of the genus *Coptotermes*, in the family HETEROTERMITIDAE. The species was described by Silvestri in 1912; the type locality is Guinea Bissau: Bolama: Rio Cassine. Distribution: West Africa, Ivory Coast, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Nigeria, Oman. This site records 2 junior synonyms for the species.
