## 摘要

**中美洲微白蚁**（*Parvitermes mesoamericanus*）是白蚁科（TERMITIDAE）象白蚁亚科（Nasutitermitinae）微白蚁属（*Parvitermes*）的一种。本种由 Scheffrahn 于 2016 年描述，模式产地为 Honduras: S. Pinalillo。分布记录包括危地马拉、尼加拉瓜、洪都拉斯。

EN — **中美洲微白蚁** (*Parvitermes mesoamericanus*) is a species of the genus *Parvitermes*, in the family TERMITIDAE (subfamily Nasutitermitinae). The species was described by Scheffrahn in 2016; the type locality is Honduras: S. Pinalillo. Distribution: Guatemala, Nicaragua, Honduras.
