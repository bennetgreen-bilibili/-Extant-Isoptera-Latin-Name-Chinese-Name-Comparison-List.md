  14| ## Taxonomic framework
  15| 
  16| All taxa in this list belong to a single taxonomic framework, from top to bottom:
  17| 
  18| 1. **Domain** — Eukaryota
  19| 2. **Kingdom** — Animalia
  20| 3. **Phylum** — Arthropoda
  21| 4. **Class** — Insecta
  22| 5. **Subclass** — Pterygota
  23| 6. **Infraclass** — Neoptera
  24| 7. **Superorder** — Dictyoptera
  25| 8. **Order** — Blattodea (cockroaches and termites)
  26| 9. **Infraorder** — Isoptera (epifamily Termitoidae)
