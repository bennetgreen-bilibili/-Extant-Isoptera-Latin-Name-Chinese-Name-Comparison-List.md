<h2>词源 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Etymology</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Etimología</span></h2>
<p>属名由希腊文 an-（无）+ hoplon（武器、甲）与拉丁文 termes（白蚁）组合，意为「无武装白蚁」。</p>
<p class="etymology-en fact-en" lang="en">From Greek an- (without) + hoplon (weapon) + Latin termes, 'unarmed termite'.</p>
