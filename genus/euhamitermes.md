<section class="taxon-section taxon-etymology" data-pagefind-ignore>
<h2>词源 <span class="fact-en" lang="en">Etymology</span> <span class="fact-es" lang="es">Etimología</span></h2>
<p>属名由希腊文 eu-（良好、真）+（hami，或源自拉丁文 hamus「钩」）与 termes（白蚁）组合。</p>
<p class="etymology-en fact-en" lang="en">From Greek eu- (well) + (hami, perhaps Latin hamus 'hook') + termes.</p>
<p class="etymology-es fact-es" lang="es">Del griego eu- (bien) + (hami, quizá el latín hamus «anzuelo») + termes.</p>
