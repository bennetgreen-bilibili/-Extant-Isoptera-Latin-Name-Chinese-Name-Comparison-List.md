<section class="taxon-section taxon-etymology" data-pagefind-ignore>
<h2>词源</h2>
<p>属名由希腊文 peri-（周围）+ 拉丁文 caper（山羊）与 termes（白蚁）组合。</p>
<p class="etymology-en" lang="en">From Greek peri- (around) + Latin caper (goat) + termes.</p>
