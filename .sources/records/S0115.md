## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 2**：

  > Head small, cylindrical, height almost equal to width, frons sloping toward clypeus at about 45-degree angle. Frontal gland very small; fontanelle round, very small, about 0.014-0.019 mm. in diameter, situated just above frontal slope.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头、额面、囟门形态

- **p. 2**：

  > Labrum very large and wide, wider than long, asymmetrical, tilted upward; anterior region with wide hyaline tip; underside with posterior median sclerotized protrusion pointing downward.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上唇形态

- **p. 2**：

  > Mandibles rather strongly curved, asymmetrical. Left mandible slightly longer than right; inner cutting edge beginning at tip with distinct and regular serrations up to one-third or one-half the length, ending with four progressively larger teeth; posterior margin of third and fourth tooth long; small pointed projection at base of fourth tooth.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁左颚锯齿与齿列

- **p. 2**：

  > Right mandible more sharply curved inward than left; inner margin smooth dorsally, narrow groove running from near base to tip, ventral margin of groove finely serrated; one or two very tiny thornlike points at base. Pronotum saddle-shaped, narrower than head. Antennae with 12 articles. Tibial spurs 3:2:2.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁右颚、前胸背板、触角、胫距

- **p. 2**：

  > Left mandible (fig. 1) with short apical tooth; posterior margin of fused first plus second marginal tooth longer than apical tooth, with almost straight cutting edge, joining evenly (without a distinct angle) with anterior margin of third marginal tooth; mandible index LA/Li .28;

  说明：支撑：工蚁左颚齿列与颚指数 LA/Li 0.28

- **p. 2**：

  > fourth marginal tooth small, faintly visible from above, almost hidden behind molar prominence; molar plate with a few (4-5) grinding ridges.

  说明：支撑：工蚁左颚第4缘齿与臼齿板

- **pp. 3–4**：

  > Our study confirms that Labritermes is most closely related to Foraminitermes, as the two genera share several synapomorphic characters.

  说明：支撑：与 Foraminitermes 的近缘关系

- **pp. 3–4**：

  > In the soldier of each of these genera, the labrum has a hyaline tip; the upper inner cutting edge of the left mandible is finely serrated, with blunt teeth appearing as crenulations below the serrations; and the inner margin of the right mandible is smooth and without teeth, the base of its molar region with one or two very tiny, pointed, thornlike spines or projections.

  说明：支撑：与 Foraminitermes 的兵蚁共衍征（上唇透明端、左颚锯齿、右颚基部棘状突）
