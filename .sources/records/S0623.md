## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 63**：

  > Head capsule hemispherical. Nasus projecting well beyond mandibles; basal one-third narrow, gradually increasing in diameter beyond 1/3 length to a maximum at about 2/3 length, then gradually decreasing at tip to less than basal diameter. Diameter of nasus at 2/3 length about 50% greater than narrowest basal diameter. Nasus hollow, thickness of outer wall even. Nasus circular in cross-section throughout.
