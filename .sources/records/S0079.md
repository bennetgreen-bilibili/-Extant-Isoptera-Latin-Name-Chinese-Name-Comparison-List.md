# S0079 Redescription of the enigmatic genus Genuotermes Emerson (Isoptera, Termitidae, Termitinae)

access: ZooKeys OA 全文（Plazi treatment 同源）。license: CC BY（Pensoft 常规，未逐条核）。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Description › Soldier（p. 109）**：

  > Soldier. Mandibles elongated, elbowed at proximal one-third, distal two-thirds with serrated blade on inner surface and salient tooth at base

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚延长、近端 1/3 处肘弯，远端 2/3 内缘为锯齿刃并具基部突齿。

- **Description › Soldier（p. 109）**：

  > Head capsule subrectangular in dorsal view, with a characteristic frontal projection, best viewed in profile

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头囊背面观近长方形，具特征性额突（侧面观最清楚）。

- **Description › Soldier（p. 109）**：

  > Frontal gland aperture at tip of head projection, oriented anteriorly.

  说明：支撑：额腺开口位于头突末端、朝前（与 Orthognathotermes 的开口不明显相对）。

- **Description › Worker（p. 109）**：

  > Worker. Head capsule rounded in dorsal view. Fontanelle inconspicuous. Antenna with 14-15 articles.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头囊背面观圆，囟不明显，触角 14–15 节。

- **Description › Digestive tube（p. 109）**：

  > P1 dilated, located on left side of body, with the final portion forming an elongated loop that reaches the dorsal region and down to insert on P3

  说明：支撑：P1 膨大、位于体左侧，末段成延长环上达背区再下行接入 P3。

- **Description › Imago（p. 108）**：

  > Imago. Eyes semispherical, oval in profile, close to but not touching lower margin of head and ocellus

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫复眼半球形、侧观椭圆，接近但不触头下缘与单眼。
