## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 89（article 40 前，属级订立段）**：

  > Ahamitermes n. g. Body small, of Hamitermes-type, head small, longer than broad, rounded behind; clypeus small, labrum long and broad, tapering to the tip, rounded; jaws short and stout, standing apart from each other, without median tooth; antennae very short, 12-jointed; prothorax broad, only very slightly ridged in front.

  说明：支撑：无钩白蚁属原始属征（体小、头长大于宽、上唇长而宽、上颚无中齿、触角 12 节）

- **p. 90（A. nidicola 兵蚁）**：

  > jaws very short and stout, broad at the base, curved, without any dentition whatsoever (on the innerside of the left jaw there are two very minute, hardly visible »teeth»)

  说明：支撑：无钩白蚁属兵蚁上颚完全无齿，仅左颚内侧有两枚极微小齿
