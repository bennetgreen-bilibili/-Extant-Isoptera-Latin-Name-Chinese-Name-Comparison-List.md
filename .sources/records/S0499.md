- **Diagnosis**：

  > Anenteotermescherubimi is the smallest soldierless termite worker in Africa (head width, HW), followed by Acidnotermespraus (0.52 mm HW) and An.nanus (0.56 mm HW). The EVA of A.cherubimi is very diagnostic and unlike that of any other termite species in having two broad and symmetrical pectinate paddles
