  > Genus  allodontermes  5ilvestri. Allodontermes   schulizei  (Silv.) The  type  localities  for  this  species  are  Severella  and  Kang- Kgokong which  lie  in  the  heart  of  the  Kalahari  some  300  miles west  of  the  Transvaal  boroer.
