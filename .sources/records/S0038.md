# S0038 Revision of the termite family Rhinotermitidae (Isoptera) in New Guinea

作者顺序以 Crossref DOI 元数据为准（Roisin 在前）；Europe PMC XML 的 contrib 顺序相反。本机 roisin-bourguignon-2011-zookeys148.pdf 实为另一篇论文，正文据 Europe PMC JATS（PMC3264403）。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Genus Heterotermes, 「Diagnosis.」节（Imago）**：

  > Imago head roughly oval, narrower anteriorly than posteriorly. Fontanelle small, located in the middle of the head. Eyes small and flat. Ocelli situated in front of the head, before eyes. Antennae generally with 15 to 19 articles.

  说明：Heterotermes（异白蚁属）— 有翅成虫节：头近卵形前窄后宽、fontanelle 小位于中部、眼小而平、单眼在眼前方、触角 15–19 节

- **同节（Soldier）**：

  > Soldier head long and narrow, rectangular-shaped. Fontanelle small, circular, situated forward. Labrum short to medium-sized, about half as long as mandibles. Mandibles sabre-shaped, slightly curved at tips.

  说明：Heterotermes（异白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：头长窄矩形、fontanelle 小圆形靠前、上唇短、上颚剑形端微弯

- **Genus Termitogeton, 「Diagnosis.」节（Imago）**：

  > Imagoes densely hairy. Head larger posteriorly than anteriorly. Fontanelle very narrow, placed in the middle of the head. Eyes small. Antennae with 10 to 15 articles. Pronotum very small, half as broad as head.

  说明：Termitogeton（寡脉白蚁属）— 有翅成虫节：密毛、头后大前小、fontanelle 极窄、眼小、触角 10–15 节、前胸背板为头宽之半

- **同节（Soldier）**：

  > Soldiers densely hairy, with characteristic heart-shaped, dorso-ventrally flattened head. Antennae generally with 13 to 15 articles. Labrum roughly triangular-shaped. Mandibles elongated, without marginal teeth.

  说明：Termitogeton（寡脉白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：密毛、头心形背腹扁、触角 13–15 节、上唇近三角形、上颚伸长无缘齿
