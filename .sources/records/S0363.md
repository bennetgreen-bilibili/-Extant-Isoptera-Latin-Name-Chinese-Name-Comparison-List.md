- **摘要 / full.txt**：

  > Soil-feeding termite species, such as Patawatermes turricola (Silvestri, 1901), construct mounds that significantly alter soil properties; this species is an abundant mound-builder in the Amazon region.

  说明：支撑：土栖帕塔瓦白蚁为亚马逊常见筑丘种并显著改变土壤性质（页面 /genus/patawatermes/）

- **Results / full.txt**：

  > Within each transect, all encountered mounds were identified, totaling 63 mounds in all study areas; 54 were determined to be P. turricola and were subsequently sampled.

  说明：支撑：全区共见 63 个丘体、54 个鉴定为该种并取样（页面 /genus/patawatermes/、/species/patawatermes-turricola/）

- **Results / full.txt**：

  > Our results showed that termites significantly improved soil aggregate stability, as reflected in a higher weighted mean diameter value in the mound (3.88 mm) than in unmodified topsoil (3.57 mm). Macro-porosity was also higher in the mound (18.49% vs. 11.47%).

  说明：支撑：团聚体平均重量直径与大孔隙度对比值（页面 /species/patawatermes-turricola/）

- **Results / full.txt**：

  > The data obtained for the chemical attributes revealed significant increases in the content of Ca2+, Mg2+, K+, Pav, SOC, and BS were found in the mound soil compared to the surrounding soil.

  说明：支撑：丘体土壤交换性盐基、有效磷与有机碳升高（页面 /species/patawatermes-turricola/）
