- **pp. 422—423**：

  > TERMES FUSCIPENNIS, n. sp. Male 8 mm. long, castaneous, abdomen beneath umbrinous, legs subisabelline. Head ovate. Ocelli not large, separated from the eyes by twice their diameter. Fenestra minute. Antennae of 15 segments, the 3rd somewhat longer than the 2nd. ... Wing 13·5 mm. long, fuliginous; the subcostal very broad and with a yellow stain along its posterior border; the median nerve runs near and parallel to the submedian, it reaches the apex of the wing and gives 4 offsets in the apical half. Soldier 4·5 mm. long, slender. Head 1·2 mm. broad, red-testaceous. ... Gula half as long as broad.
