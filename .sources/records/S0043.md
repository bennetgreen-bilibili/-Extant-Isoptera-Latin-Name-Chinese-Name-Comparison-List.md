# S0043 Australian Rhinotermes (Isoptera)

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 1（引 Holmgren 的属征，Imago）**：

  > Imago.—Head almost circular; clypeus more or less strongly inflated, with median furrow; fontanelle open, rather broad, placed towards the front; antennae of 20-22 segments, third segment longer than second.

  说明：Rhinotermes（鼻白蚁属）— 有翅成虫节：头近圆形、唇基隆起具中沟、fontanelle 开放靠前、触角 20–22 节

- **p. 1 同段（Soldier）**：

  > Soldier.—Of two distinct forms, either large or small, with bifid labrum. Mandibles of the large soldier stout and very strong, left mandible with two and the right with one strong tooth, basal part not finely toothed.

  说明：Rhinotermes（鼻白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：大、小两型，上唇双叉，大兵蚁上颚粗壮、左二齿右一齿

- **p. 1 同段（Worker）**：

  > Worker.—Antennae of 16-18 segments, 3rd segment longer than 2nd. Pronotum saddle-shaped.

  说明：Rhinotermes（鼻白蚁属）— 工蚁节：触角 16–18 节、第 3 节长于第 2 节、前胸背板鞍形
