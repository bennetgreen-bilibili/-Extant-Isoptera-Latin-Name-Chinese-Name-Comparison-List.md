## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **摘要**：

  > The rates of consumption of soil and organic matter by individual workers (the dominant caste) were estimated in the first instance.

  说明：支撑：先估测单个工蚁（优势品级）的土壤与有机质消耗速率。

- **摘要**：

  > Insects were taken from the mound and were fed in the laboratory on known amounts of soil whose organic matter content had previously been determined.

  说明：支撑：实验方法：从蚁丘取虫，在实验室饲以已知量、有机质含量预先测定的土壤。

- **摘要**：

  > Consumption of soil varied between 0.72 mg and 0.911 mg/termite per day and of organic matter between 0.65 mg and 0.84 mg/termite per day.

  说明：支撑：每头每天消耗土壤 0.72–0.911 mg、有机质 0.65–0.84 mg。
