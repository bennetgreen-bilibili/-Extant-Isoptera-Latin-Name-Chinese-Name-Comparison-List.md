## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **摘要**：

  > Damage caused by Porotermes adamsoni and its effect on log quality were investigated in coastal lowland eucalypt forests in eastern Tasmania.

  说明：支撑：在塔斯马尼亚东部沿海低地桉树林中调查 Porotermes adamsoni 的危害及其对原木质量的影响。

- **摘要**：

  > In two study areas, 60 per cent and 43 per cent of trees sampled had been damaged by P. adamsoni.

  说明：支撑：两个研究区中分别有 60% 与 43% 的抽样树木受 P. adamsoni 危害。

- **摘要**：

  > All eucalypt species (6) were attacked and there was no significant difference between species in the proportion of trees so affected.

  说明：支撑：6 种桉树全部受害，各树种之间受害株比例无显著差异。
