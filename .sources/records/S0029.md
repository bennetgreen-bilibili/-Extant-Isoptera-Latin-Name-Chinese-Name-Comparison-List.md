## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 111（Morphological diagnosis）**：

  > Morphological diagnosis. — Soldier. Soldiers with antennae 15 segmented, labrum with substraight anterior margin equipped with broad-based, robust and long antero-lateral points. Frons sharply inclining in front, fontanelle transverse and frontal gland large, postmentum club-shaped, pronotum saddle-shaped, anterior margin convex, without notch.

  说明：兵蚁鉴别要点（触角 15 节、上唇前缘与前侧突、额面、囟、后颏、前胸背板）

- **p. 112（Remarks）**：

  > The soldiers of Rinacapritermes Amina & Rajmohana, n. gen. have antennae with 15 segments vs 14 segments in Indocapritermes; anterior margin of labrum is substraight with broad based, robust and long, antero-lateral points in the new genus vs labrum with narrow based, short, thin and minute lateral points in the latter

  说明：鉴别要点（与 Indocapritermes 的逐条对比）

- **p. 112（Description, Worker, Head capsule）**：

  > Worker. Head capsule. Monomorphic; head and body densely hairy. In dorsal view head subcircular, maximum width of head capsule at base of antennae. Fontanelle plate translucent and round. Antennae with 14-15 segments. Postclypeus swollen.

  说明：工蚁（单型、头壳近圆形、囟板、触角 14–15 节、后唇基膨隆）

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 4 (Homallotermes 分支)**：

  > Antennae with 13 segments; mandibles thin, slender and weakly bent at middle

  说明：支撑：兵蚁触角 13 节，上颚细弱、中部弱弯。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 3（本属落在该分支）**：

  > Left mandible moderately twisted at middle, sharply pointed at tip or forming a beak or bent like a hook

  说明：支撑：左颚中部中度扭转、末端锐尖（检索路径上位特征）。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 9**：

  > Head moderately hairy; frons sharply inclining in front

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头被毛中等，额面前倾明显。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 10 (Indocapritermes 分支)**：

  > Antero-lateral points of labrum minute, thin and with a narrow base

  说明：支撑：上唇前侧角微小、细、基部窄。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 10 (Indocapritermes 分支)**：

  > Antennae 14 segmented

  说明：支撑：兵蚁触角 14 节。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 3（本属落在该分支）**：

  > Left mandible strongly twisted at middle, blunt at tip

  说明：支撑：左颚中部强扭转、末端钝（检索路径上位特征）。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 10（Indocapritermes 分支）**：

  > anterior lobe of pronotum slightly convex and weakly notched

  说明：支撑：前胸背板前叶微凸、具弱缺刻。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 1 (Kemneritermes 分支)**：

  > Head with highly sclerotized frontal ridge, head somewhat phragmotic

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头具高度骨化额脊，头略呈“堵塞型”（phragmotic）。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 11 (Krishnacapritermes 分支)**：

  > Fontanelle transverse, fontanelle gland large

  说明：支撑：兵蚁囟横宽、囟腺大。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 9（本属落在该分支）**：

  > Head sparsely hairy; frons gradually inclining in front

  说明：支撑：头被毛稀疏，额面缓倾（检索路径上位特征）。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 5 (Mirocapritermes 分支)**：

  > Head with frontal projection, left mandible moderately bent at middle

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头具额突，左颚中部中度弯曲。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 4（本属落在该分支）**：

  > Antennae with 14 segments, mandibles thick, moderately to strongly bent at middle

  说明：支撑：触角 14 节，上颚粗、中部中度至强度弯曲。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 13 (Oriencapritermes 分支)**：

  > Labrum anteriorly deeply concave, as long as wide, antero-lateral processes with points at bases

  说明：支撑：上唇前缘深凹、长宽约相等，前侧突基部具尖。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 11（本属落在该分支）**：

  > Fontanelle small, circular, fontanelle gland small

  说明：支撑：囟小、圆形，囟腺小（检索路径上位特征）。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 12（本属落在该分支）**：

  > Left mandible with well developed indented lower basal projection. Labrum long, as long as or longer than wide

  说明：支撑：左颚具发达、具齿的下基突；上唇长，长≥宽。

- **Key to the soldiers of genera under Pericapritermes-group found in the Oriental region, couplet 13 (Syncapritermes 分支)**：

  > Labrum anteriorly shallowly concave, longer than wide, antero-lateral processes without points at bases

  说明：支撑：上唇前缘浅凹、长大于宽，前侧突基部无尖。
