# S0042 New Dolichorhinotermes from Ecuador and in Mexican amber (Isoptera: Rhinotermitidae)

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 2「DIAGNOSIS」右栏**：

  > Major soldier with labrum distinctly elongate, apex of labrum frequently extending to mandibular apex (in Rhinotermes the labrum is subquadrate and much shorter).

  说明：Dolichorhinotermes（狭鼻白蚁属）— 鉴别要点/有翅成虫：第 3 flagellar 节短于第 1 节；大兵蚁上唇伸长伸达颚端（对立于 Rhinotermes）

- **p. 2「DIAGNOSIS」右栏**：

  > Minor soldier with opening of frontal gland at front of head but not on conspicuous prolongation of head capsule; mandibles vestigial, with rounded margins; sides of head in dorsal aspect straight or convex.

  说明：Dolichorhinotermes（狭鼻白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：小兵蚁额腺开口位于头前但无头突、上颚退化、头侧缘直或凸
