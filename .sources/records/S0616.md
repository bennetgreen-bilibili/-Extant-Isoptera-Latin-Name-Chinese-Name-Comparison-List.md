Species of Mirotermes Wasmann are widely distributed throughout the southern hemisphere and also occur in Central America. At Panama the species M. hispaniolae Banks also occurs. Species of the genus Orthognathotermes Holmgren are re- stricted to America. Only 3 species have been described and they are all from South America. The new species from Panama is the first record of this genus from Central America and I have taken pleasure in naming it after Dr. W. M. Wheeler.
