# S0027 Diminishing the taxonomic gap in the neotropical soldierless termites: descriptions of four new genera and a new Anoplotermes species (Isoptera, Termitidae, Apicotermitinae)

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **章节 Krecekitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis（文章 pp. 317–352）**：

  > Dehiscent organs fill the hemocoel anterior to the crop. The EVS forms three large lobes. The Mesenteron/P1 junction is well marked, but the mixed segment is short. The EVA armature of Krecekitermes is composed of cushions with six sclerotized and crown-shaped termini adorned with thorns.

  说明：鉴别要点（破裂器官、肠瓣座三叶、混合段短、6 个冠状硬化末端具棘刺的瓣垫）

- **章节 Mangolditermes gen. nov., Diagnosis**：

  > In the left view, P1 makes a curve dorsally. The enteric valve seating (EVS) is weakly trilobed. The EVA is unarmed and composed of six elongated and inflated cushions, with well-marked fringed pentagonal scales in the distal half of the cushions. On the proximal ends, each cushion has about 10–20 tiny triangulate spines.

  说明：鉴别要点与工蚁肠瓣、P1 走行

- **章节 Ourissotermes gen. nov., Diagnosis**：

  > Enteric valve armature composed of six pectinate bunches with 4–6 rows of spines, distal portion (proximal to P3) with longer spines, decreasing posteriorly; smaller spines tooth-like. Proximal portion of cushions with a small spine tooth-like (near P1). Enteric valve (EV) inserted directly into the paunch (P3), without an enteric valve seating (EVS) differentiated.

  说明：鉴别要点（6 组栉状刺束、远端刺长、近端小齿刺、肠瓣直插 P3 无分化肠瓣座）

- **Hirsutitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis (ZooKeys 1167: 317–352)**：

  > embellished with hundreds of thin spines on the posterior rim

  说明：属级鉴别：肠瓣后缘细刺；原文此句前为 Enteric valve armature ( EVA )

- **Hirsutitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis**：

  > The EVA seating is tubular, trilobed, and long.

  说明：EVS 形态

- **Hirsutitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis**：

  > The mesenteric tongue is long but not inflated.

  说明：mesenteric tongue

- **Key to neotropical Apicotermitinae genera based on the worker caste, couplet 14 (Aparatermes 分支)**：

  > EVA Cuticle between cushions without scales; EVS trilobed [see Pinzón et al. (2019)]

  说明：支撑：工蚁肠瓣（EVA）垫间角质无鳞片、肠瓣座（EVS）三叶，为检索表中界定 Aparatermes 的组合特征。

- **Key to neotropical Apicotermitinae genera based on the worker caste, couplet 12**：

  > Mesenteric tongue short (drop shaped), not spheroidal (Fig. 6B, C)

  说明：支撑：中肠舌短、滴状，而非球形或延长。

- **Comparison and remarks (Mangolditermes)**：

  > the mesenteric tongue (MT, mixed segment) morphology, that is elongated in the new genus and short in Aparatermes

  说明：支撑：中肠舌（混合段）短（Mangolditermes 为延长）。

- **Comparison and remarks (Mangolditermes)**：

  > EVA with the pointed scales in the proximal portion of the cushions in Mangolditermes and towards the central portion of the cushion in Aparatermes

  说明：支撑：肠瓣垫上的尖鳞位于垫的中央部。

- **Key to neotropical Apicotermitinae genera based on the worker caste, couplet 19 (Grigiotermes 分支)**：

  > Distal end of EVA armature pads ringed by unevenly arranged spines; EVS short

  说明：支撑：肠瓣垫末端刺排列不齐、肠瓣座短。

- **Key to neotropical Apicotermitinae genera based on the worker caste, couplet 15**：

  > EVA armature resembling a continuous (spines on cuticle between cushions) wreath of hundreds of tiny and thin spines (Figs 9A–C, 11H)

  说明：支撑：肠瓣武装为覆盖垫间角质的连续花环状、数百枚细小薄刺。

- **Key to neotropical Apicotermitinae genera based on the worker caste, couplet 16 (Longustitermes 分支)**：

  > Enteric valve seating trilobed (Fig. 6C; see Bourguignon et al. 2010)

  说明：支撑：肠瓣座三叶，与 Hirsutitermes（管状）区分。

- **Comparison and remarks (Hirsutitermes)**：

  > Longustitermes is smaller in size and has a trilobed EVS, while Hirsutitermes has a tubular

  说明：支撑：体型较小、肠瓣座三叶（Hirsutitermes 为管状）。

- **Comparison and remarks (Hirsutitermes)**：

  > the anterior portion of the cushions is more rounded in the latter than pyriform in the former

  说明：支撑：肠瓣垫前部较圆（Hirsutitermes 为梨形）。

- **Comparison and remarks (Dissimulitermes)**：

  > Ruptitermes does not present armature in the EVA, except for R.bandeirai

  说明：支撑：肠瓣（EVA）无武装，仅 R. bandeirai 例外。

- **Key to neotropical Apicotermitinae genera based on the worker caste, couplet 2 (Tetimatermes 分支)**：

  > Fore tibia with long “scooped out” excavation [see Fontes (1986)]

  说明：支撑：工蚁前胫节具长勺状凹槽，为检索表中识别本属的唯一特征。
