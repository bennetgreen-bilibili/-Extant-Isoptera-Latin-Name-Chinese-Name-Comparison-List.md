## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 7**：

  > SOLDIER (Figs. 1-2) - Head yellow; nasus yellow-brown; antenna yellow; pronotum, mesonotum and metanotum yellowish-white; sclerites transparent. Antenna: third segment greater than second, fourth greater than third, fifth equal fourth. Top of head with sparse medium to long bristles and numerous microscopic hairs. Nasus covered with dense microscopic hairs, becoming longer toward apex.
