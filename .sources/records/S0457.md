# S0457 Favicon issues in Bing search results and how to fix them – SiteLint

SEO 服务商博客（二手经验总结），非官方；其中「Bing 曾建议把 favicon 放进 sitemap，现为过时建议」一条与其他来源互相印证。仅用于本轮答复（m00002）。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **§Troubleshooting tips → Ensure the favicon has a valid format and size**：

  > The website must have a valid favicon file in `.ico`, `.png`, or `.svg` format, and it must be at least 16×16 pixels. Bing prefers **32×32 or larger** for high-resolution displays.

  说明：支撑：Bing 偏好 ≥32×32 的图标

- **§Ensure the favicon file is not blocked by robots.txt or server config → Bing’s crawler may not have updated its index yet**：

  > Bing can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks to refresh favicons.

  说明：支撑：Bing 图标缓存刷新周期

- **§Do favicons belong in sitemaps?**：

  > **Best practice:** Keep your sitemap focused on indexable content pages (HTML, PDF, etc.). Do not include favicon URLs.

  说明：支撑：favicon 不该塞进 sitemap（Bing 早期建议已过时）

- **§Be patient after making changes**：

  > it may take **1 to 4 weeks** for Bing to fully update its favicon across all regions.

  说明：支撑：给出 1–4 周的预期窗口
