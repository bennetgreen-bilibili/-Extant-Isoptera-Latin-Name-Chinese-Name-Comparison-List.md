# S0069 Muelleritermes: A new termite genus with two species from the Brazilian Atlantic Forest (Isoptera: Termitidae: Nasutitermitinae)

为 1 个属（米勒白蚁属）提供属级形态文本；本记录逐字摘录合计 747 字符。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **pp. 259–263，「Comparisons with other Nasutitermitinae genera」段**：

  > can be easily distinguished from other members of Nasutitermitinae , except Velocitermes and Diversitermes , by the presence of three types of soldiers and a pair of vestigial ocelli (described below) on the soldier's head

  说明：支撑：三型兵蚁 + 一对退化单眼，仅本属与 Velocitermes、Diversitermes 具——鉴别要点。

- **pp. 259–263，「Soldier」段**：

  > Trimorphic. Head capsule of major soldier broad, rounded, almost square or slightly oval, without constriction behind antennae ( M. globiceps , sp. n. ), occasionally very slightly constricted (some individuals in M. fritzi , sp. n. ), oval or slightly oval in intermediate soldier.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁三型；大型兵头近方形或略卵形、触角后无收缩，中型兵头卵形——兵蚁节。

- **pp. 259–263，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker : Dimorphic, the minor worker occurs rarely. Major worker has two types of mandibles (termed narrow- and broad-gap by Fontes, 1987 ). Head rounded ( Fig. 3 ), antennae with 14 articles, postclypeus not inflated, fontanelle inconspicuous

  说明：支撑：工蚁二型、上颚两式、头圆、触角 14 节、后唇基不膨大——工蚁节。
