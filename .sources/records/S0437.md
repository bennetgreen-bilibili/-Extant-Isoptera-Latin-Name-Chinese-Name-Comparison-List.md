# S0437 Data quality recommendations

checklist 表（Table 1）：taxonID = Required；scientificName、taxonRank、kingdom、parentNameUsageID、acceptedNameUsageID = Strongly recommended（GBIF 管理项目下 scientificName/taxonRank 升为 Required）；vernacularName = Share if available。另：scientificName 不许工作名 / 俗名 / 带 cf. 之类的限定词；vernacularName 与 higherClassification 要附 ISO 639-1 语言码。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **§Status › Required**：

  > The terms constitute the minimum formal requirements for publishing an occurrence dataset. GBIF will not accept a dataset without these terms and will not index the records.

  说明：支撑：缺 Required 项 GBIF 直接不受理、不建索引 —— 决定 checklist 的 taxonID 不可为空

- **§Terms › taxonRank**：

  > For practical purposes, the ranks used have to be (major) Linnean ranks: kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, and species. Both Latin and English terms are accepted.

  说明：支撑：subfamily / subspecies 不在 GBIF 推荐的 7 个主要阶元内，发布树形结构时要显式决定怎么处理（这是本站 18 亚科 + 68 亚种的现实冲突点）

- **§Terms › parentNameUsageID**：

  > The taxonID of the next available higher-ranked (parent) entry within the checklist dataset, if higher taxon names are supplied as separate entries in the list.

  说明：支撑：要发布层级树，上层阶元必须作为独立条目出现，parentNameUsageID 指向的是同表内条目的 taxonID

- **§Terms › acceptedNameUsageID**：

  > Within the record of a synonym, the taxonID of the accepted taxon name entry within the checklist dataset, if both synonyms and accepted names are supplied.

  说明：支撑：异名要进同一张表并带 acceptedNameUsageID，才能让 GBIF 把旧名套到接受名上
