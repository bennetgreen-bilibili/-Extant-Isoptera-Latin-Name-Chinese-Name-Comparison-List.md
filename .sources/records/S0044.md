# S0044 The termite family Serritermitidae (Isoptera)

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 2「Diagnostic Characters」**：

  > The imago, worker, and nymph differ from all other termite families by the proportionally elongated sharp apical tooth of each mandible separated from a single marginal tooth near the ridged molar plate by a widely curved inner margin

  说明：Serritermes（齿白蚁属）— 鉴别要点/有翅成虫、工蚁：端齿比例伸长而尖，以宽弯内缘与磨板旁的一枚缘齿相隔

- **p. 2 同节（Soldier）**：

  > The soldier has long, relatively straight apical portions of both mandibles, each with numerous proportionally coarse, large serrations on the inner apical margins behind the tips

  说明：Serritermes（齿白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：两颚端段长而直，端部后方内缘多粗锯齿

- **p. 2 同节**：

  > The pronotum is proportionally narrow, elongated, and both front and hind margins are conspicuously bilobed

  说明：Serritermes（齿白蚁属）— 鉴别要点/兵蚁：前胸背板窄长、前后缘双叶、中央深凹
