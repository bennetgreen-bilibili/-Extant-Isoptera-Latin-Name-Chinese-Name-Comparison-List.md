## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 544**：

  > Head quite broad, eye situate before the middle, ocelli present. Prothorax much broader than long, scarcely narrower than the head, rounded behind, with a median pale line above.

  说明：支撑：Incisitermes galapagoensis 有翅成虫头与前胸背板特征

- **p. 545**：

  > Head castaneous, darker in front than behind; mandibles black. Head about once and one-third longer than broad, sides slightly convex, equally broad in front and behind, somewhat flattened above.

  说明：支撑：Incisitermes pacificus 兵蚁头囊形态
