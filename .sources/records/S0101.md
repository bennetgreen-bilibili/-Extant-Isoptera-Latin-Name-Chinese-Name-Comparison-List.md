## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **couplet 2, pp. 4–11**：

  > Large mandibulate soldier; antennae with more than 20 articles (Fig. 4A); prominent cerci in all castes (Fig. 4B)

  说明：支撑：兵蚁大型具颚、触角>20节、各品级尾须显著

- **couplet 23, pp. 10–11**：

  > Base of the mandibles originate near anterolateral corners of the head, with inner edge of mandibles smooth (Figs. 2A, 5B, 17B)

  说明：支撑：上颚基部位置与内缘光滑

- **couplet 25, pp. 10–11**：

  > Mandibles curved, usually shorter than head length (Fig. 2A)

  说明：支撑：上颚弯曲、短于头长

- **couplet 25, pp. 10–11**：

  > Mandibles straight, curved only at the tip and as long or longer than head length (Fig. 5B)

  说明：支撑：上颚直、仅端部弯曲、与头等长或更长

- **couplet 24, pp. 10–11**：

  > Head width less than 1.4 mm; mandibles narrower, usually longer than head width

  说明：支撑：头宽<1.4mm、上颚窄而长

- **couplet 24, pp. 10–11**：

  > Head width greater than 1.8 mm, with mandibles robust, shorter than head width (Fig. 17B)

  说明：支撑：头宽>1.8mm、上颚粗壮且短于头宽

- **couplet 23, pp. 10–11**：

  > Base of the mandibles originate near anterolateral corners of the head, with inner edge of mandibles smooth (Figs. 2A, 5B, 17B)

  说明：支撑：上颚基部位置与内缘光滑
