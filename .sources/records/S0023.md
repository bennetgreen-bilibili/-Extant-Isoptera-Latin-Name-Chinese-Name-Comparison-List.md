## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 127, Soldier**：

  > Head capsule sub-quadrangular with almost parallel lateral margins and two very discrete saliencies on the latero-posterior corners … Surface of head capsule covered with numerous minute and closely set points of about equal diameter, forming a conspicuous and characteristic microsculpture

  说明：支撑：Acangaobitermes 兵蚁头壳形状与微刻纹

- **p. 127, Soldier**：

  > Frontal tube conical and upturned, in profile, apex with a relatively wide aperture surrounded by a white membrane. Antennae with 14 articles. Piercing slender mandibles; blade strongly curved inwards and upturned; a very small tooth at the base of the blade

  说明：支撑：额管、触角与刺吸式上颚

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae**：

  > Soldiers of Cahuallitermes have straighter cutting mandibles, with well-developed marginal teeth; a well-developed hyaline tip to the labrum; and a larger body size.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁切缘上颚较直、缘齿发达、上唇具发达透明端，体型较大。
