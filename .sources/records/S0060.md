# S0060 Rounditermes dechambrei, a new genus and species of Nasutitermitinae from French Guiana (Isopt., Termitidae)

法属圭亚那新属新种，全文只描述兵蚁（正模为兵蚁；副模 29 兵蚁 + 3 工蚁，但工蚁未描述），故 04-7.json 只有「鉴别要点 / 兵蚁」两节。法语刊物的英文文章；DOI 前缀 10.3406 为 Persée 的注册号，出版社原始 DOI 未另设。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 53，Diagnosis**：

  > Rounditermes n. gen. mainly differs from the closely related genus Cortari¬ termes in the presence of 14 antennal segments in soldier, its longer and broader soldier head, its relatively longer hind tibia of soldier, the absence of microscopic pilosity of the soldier head, its nasus more conical and shorter than in the Cortaritermes spp.

  说明：支撑：Rounditermes 与 Cortaritermes 的四项兵蚁区别（触角 14 节、头更长更宽、后足胫节相对更长、头面无微毛、鼻更锥形且较短）

- **p. 54，Descriptions — Soldiers (11)**：

  > mean dimensions (based on ten specimens) : length of entire soldier, 5.20mm ; length of head with nasus, 1.99mm ; width of head, 1.55 mm ; length of nasus, 0.72 mm ; length of pronotum, 0.4 mm ; length of antennae, 1.97 mm

  说明：支撑：Rounditermes 兵蚁 10 头标本的均值测量
