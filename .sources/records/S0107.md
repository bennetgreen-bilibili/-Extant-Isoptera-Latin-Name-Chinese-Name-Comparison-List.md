## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 11（Lophotermes 属级 Diagnosis；Plazi treatment 6230356）**：

  > Soldiers of Lophotermes are usually characterized by well-developed frontal and lateral tubercles (Figs 17 –18, 21– 22); mandibles generally stout and longer than head capsule; antennae of 14 or sometimes 15 segments; labrum usually large; tibial spurs 3: 2: 2 or 2: 2: 2.

  说明：支撑：Lophotermes 兵蚁的额突/侧瘤、上颚、触角节数、胫距式

- **p. 11（同上，工蚁段）**：

  > Monomorphic workers. Worker left mandible index> 1.5, often> 2.0 (Figs 20, 24). Worker digestive tract with P 3 subdivided into a small posterior section and a larger anterior one (Fig. 62).

  说明：支撑：Lophotermes 工蚁单态、左颚指数与后肠 P3 分节

- **p. 13（Macrognathotermes 属级 Diagnosis；Plazi treatment 6230362）**：

  > Soldiers of this genus are characterized by the following characters (Figs 25–26): frontal projection well developed; lateral tubercles usually present, sometimes reduced or absent; mandibles very long and slender; postmentum projecting only slightly below the head capsule; antennae 14 -segmented; tibial spurs 2: 2: 2.

  说明：支撑：Macrognathotermes 兵蚁的长而细上颚、后颏突出程度、触角 14 节、胫距 2:2:2

- **p. 13（同上，工蚁段）**：

  > Monomorphic workers. Worker left mandible index> 1.5, usually> 2.0 (Figs 28). Enteric valve cushions of alternating size, slightly asymmetrical, with numerous small spines. Worker digestive tract with P 1 enlarged, P 3 subdivided into a posterior and an anterior section of the same size (Fig. 63).

  说明：支撑：Macrognathotermes 工蚁颚指数、肠瓣垫与 P1/P3 形态
