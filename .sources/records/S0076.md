# S0076 A taxonomic revision of the neotropical termite genus Ruptitermes (Isoptera, Termitidae, Apicotermitinae)

access: Plazi treatment（分页抽取文本），属级描述位于 p. 454；摘录按 1900 字符/作品预算截取，未收全。license: 未核实。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Description › Imago（p. 454）**：

  > Imago: Head capsule rounded in dorsal view, short and broad, densely covered with short hairs and scattered bristles. Fontanelle variable, elliptical to rounded, usually conspicuous. Eyes relatively small; ocelli rounded or elliptical, distant from eyes by at least its diameter

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫头短宽而圆，囟多变，复眼较小、单眼距复眼至少其直径。

- **Description › Imago（p. 454）**：

  > Postclypeus relatively small and moderately inflated to large and inflated; depressed at both ends of the midline; midline conspicuous, depressed, and incomplete, vanishing near anterior and posterior margins.

  说明：支撑：后唇基中线两端凹陷，中线明显、不完整。

- **Description › Imago（p. 454）**：

  > Antenna with 15 articles. Each tibia with two apical spurs. Wings uniformly covered with irregularly shaped micrasters, with 2–4 arms

  说明：支撑：触角 15 节，各胫节 2 端距，翅面覆 2–4 臂微鳞。

- **Description › Worker（p. 454）**：

  > Head broad and rounded, usually well sclerotized and dark colored (pale colored in a few species); chaetotaxy variable. Fontanelle elliptical to rounded, small to large, of variable color, usually conspicuous. Postclypeus inflated or flattened.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头宽圆、通常骨化深色深；囟多变；后唇基膨大或扁平。

- **Description › Worker（p. 454）**：

  > A pair of rounded, well-developed dehiscent glands present in the anterior part of abdomen, filling up to 1/3 of its volume.

  说明：支撑：腹部前部具一对球形发达破裂腺，可达腹部体积 1/3。

- **Description › Gut morphology（p. 454–455）**：

  > Enteric valve with bilateral symmetry, bearing 6 unsclerotized, reticulated ridges (3 longer ones on one side and 3 shorter ones on the other side); spines absent in most species.

  说明：支撑：肠瓣两侧对称、6 条无骨化网纹脊，多数种无刺。

- **Comparisons（p. 455）**：

  > The workers of Ruptitermes are easily recognized by the presence of the dehiscent glands, which are usually conspicuous as a whitish mass in the anterior part of the abdomen.

  说明：支撑：工蚁以腹部前部白色团块状的破裂腺易于识别。

- **Comparisons（p. 455）**：

  > Imagoes are larger than most other neotropical Apicotermitinae. The imagoes of Aparatermes Fontes 1985 are the most similar and can be distinguished by the shape of the postclypeus, which is not depressed at the ends of the midline.

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫大于多数新热带 Apicotermitinae；与 Aparatermes 以后唇基中线两端不凹陷区分。

- **Description › Worker（p. 454）**：

  > Left mandible: A shorter than M1 (except in R. bandeirai , sp.n, where they are about the same length), M3 usually very short (except in R. arboreus and R. bandeirai , sp.n); notch between M1 and M3 always present

  说明：支撑：工蚁左上颚端齿（A）短于第一缘齿（M1），M1–M3 之间恒有缺刻。

- **Description › Worker, gut morphology（p. 454）**：

  > Crop very small in most species (large only in R. arboreus ); gizzard unarmed

  说明：支撑：多数种嗉囊很小，砂囊无武装。
