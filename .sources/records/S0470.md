## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Abstract**：

  > Caetetermes fontesi sp. nov. is described from soldiers, workers, and winged imagos collected in French Guiana. Soldiers of C. fontesi are distinguished from Caetetermes taquarussu Fontes 1981, until now a monotypic genus, by their larger size, deeper coloration, and shorter and thicker nasus.

  说明：支撑：新种采于法属圭亚那；属此前为单型属（仅 C. taquarussu）；鉴别特征为体型更大、色更深、鼻更短粗
