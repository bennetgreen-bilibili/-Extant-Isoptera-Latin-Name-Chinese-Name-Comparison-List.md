## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **摘要**：

  > The subterranean nests of the African genus of termites, Apicotermes, present unique material for the study of the evolution of behavior.

  说明：支撑：非洲 Apicotermes 属的地下巢为行为演化研究提供了独特材料。

- **摘要**：

  > The nests are species-specific and show phylogenetic relationships much more clearly than do the morphological characteristics of the termites building them.

  说明：支撑：Apicotermes 的巢具有种特异性，比筑巢白蚁自身的形态特征更清晰地反映系统发育关系。

- **摘要**：

  > Circular galleries seem to have evolved independently four times within the genus.

  说明：支撑：环形廊道（circular galleries）在 Apicotermes 属内似独立演化了 4 次。
