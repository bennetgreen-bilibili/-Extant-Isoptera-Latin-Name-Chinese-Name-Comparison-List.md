## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Humutermes gen. nov., Diagnosis (pp. 20–22)**：

  > Humutermes workers have an elongated mixed segment with a whitish, spherical mesenteric diverticulum.

  说明：属级鉴别：伸长的 mixed segment 与球形 diverticulum

- **Humutermes gen. nov., Diagnosis / Comparisons (pp. 20–22)**：

  > The genus mainly differs from Anoplotermes sensu stricto by the shape of its enteric valve, composed of six pads, each with ten to 20 long spines protruding into the paunch.

  说明：与 Anoplotermes s.s. 的区别

- **Humutermes gen. nov., Description (pp. 20–22)**：

  > Imago: Eyes and ocelli of medium size. Fontanelle of medium size, appearing as an elongated white dot located between eyes.

  说明：有翅成虫头部

- **Patawatermes gen. nov., Diagnosis / Comparisons**：

  > Patawatermes is very similar to Grigiotermes but can easily be distinguished from it by the shape of the armature of its worker enteric valve.

  说明：属级鉴别：与 Grigiotermes 的区别

- **Patawatermes gen. nov., Description: Worker**：

  > Enteric valve inserted into a tubular extension of the paunch, with well-developed armature composed of six rounded spiny pads.

  说明：肠瓣插入位置与 armature

- **Patawatermes gen. nov., Description: Imago**：

  > Imago: Sclerotized body parts dark brown, covered by thousands of tiny setae.

  说明：有翅成虫体色与刚毛

- **Rubeotermes gen. nov., Diagnosis (pp. 27–30)**：

  > Rubeotermes workers are distinctive from all other South American soldierless termites in that they turn red in alcohol and have some long and thick spines on the forecoxa.

  说明：属级鉴别（唯一性）

- **Rubeotermes gen. nov., Description: Worker**：

  > Mesenteron and proctodeum connection truncated, devoid of mixed segment.

  说明：无 mixed segment

- **Rubeotermes gen. nov., Description: Worker**：

  > Worker: Large, with head width of about 1 mm.

  说明：属级体型

- **p. 20, Description (Worker)**：

  > Description Worker: Medium-sized (see Table 3). Head pale yellow, rounded to slightly ovoid, fully covered by about 60 setae. Fontanelle indistinct. Mandibles as in Figure 2D. Fore tibia strongly inﬂated, about three times as long as wide.

  说明：支撑：工蚁中型、头淡黄被约 60 根刚毛、前胫节强烈膨大约 3 倍于宽。

- **p. 20, Description (Worker, digestive tube)**：

  > Enteric valve inserted into an extension of the paunch, consisting of six elongated pads, each composed of about 100 scales and endowed posteriorly with about 15 weakly sclerotized spines.

  说明：支撑：肠瓣着生于 paunch 延伸部，6 个细长垫、每垫约 100 枚鳞片、后缘约 15 根弱硬化刺。

- **p. 20, Diagnosis**：

  > Amplucrutermes is clearly distinct from all other described South American soldierless termite genera. It can be recognized by its enlarged fore tibia, which are similar to Tetimatermes but less concave and lacking proximal glands. The enteric valves of Amplucrutermes, composed of about 100 scales and 15 weakly sclerotized apical spines, are also diagnostic (Fig. 2C).

  说明：支撑：与所有已知南美无兵蚁白蚁属区分；前胫节似 Tetimatermes 但凹陷较浅、近端无腺体；肠瓣约 100 鳞片 + 15 根弱硬化端刺。

- **p. 24, Diagnosis**：

  > Hydrecotermes is morphologically closest to Grigiotermes and Patawatermes but can easily be distinguished from these two genera by its smaller workers devoid of enteric valve armatures. Its workers differ from other Neotropical soldierless termites by their enlarged P1 and overall squat abdomen, and by their oval enteric valve cushions without armature.

  说明：支撑：最接近 Grigiotermes 与 Patawatermes，工蚁更小且肠瓣无硬化 armature；P1 膨大、腹部粗短、肠瓣垫卵形无刺。

- **p. 23, Description (Worker)**：

  > Medium-sized, with head width between 0.6 and 0.8 mm. Abdomen with slightly enlarged digestive tract. Head pale yellow, rounded, covered by 50 to 200 bristles and devoid of tiny ones.

  说明：支撑：工蚁中型、头宽 0.6–0.8 mm、腹部消化道略膨大、头淡黄圆形被 50–200 根刚毛而无微毛。

- **p. 24, Description (Imago)**：

  > Sclerotized body dark brown, covered by thousands of tiny setae. Head covered by a few long bristles. Eyes and ocelli large. Fontanelle fairly large, situated at the posterior level of eyes and appearing as a spot devoid of setae.

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫体部深褐密被微毛、头仅数根长刚毛、复眼与单眼大、fontanelle 大而位于复眼后缘水平且无毛。

- **Description › Worker（p. 18）**：

  > Worker: Large, head width over 0.9 mm. Abdomen with subconical distal half and elongated digestive tract.

  说明：支撑：工蚁体型大（头宽 > 0.9 mm）、腹末半亚圆锥形、消化道延长。

- **Description › Worker（p. 18）**：

  > Head pale yellow, rounded, fully covered with various numbers of tiny setae and between 50 and 100 long bristles. Fontanelle small, almost indistinct. Fore tibia long and slender.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头淡黄、圆形，密被小毛与 50–100 根长刚毛；囟小几不可见；前胫节细长。

- **Description › Imago（p. 18）**：

  > Imago: Sclerotized body parts dark brown, covered by thousands of tiny setae. Head covered by about 100 scattered long bristles. Eyes and ocelli large.

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫硬化部深褐、密被小毛；头具约 100 根散生长刚毛；复眼与单眼大。

- **Diagnosis（p. 19）**：

  > Grigiotermes is distinguishable from other genera of South American soldierless termites by its workers with widened P 1 in which the food bolus has a visible serpentine path.

  说明：支撑：以工蚁 P1 极宽、食物团呈可见蛇形路径区别于南美其他无兵蚁属。

- **Description › Worker, digestive tract（p. 18）**：

  > Enteric valve inserted into a short extension of the paunch, with welldeveloped armature composed of six spiny plates.

  说明：支撑：肠瓣位于胃（paunch）的短延伸部内，武装为 6 块具刺骨板。

- **Description › Worker, digestive tract（p. 18）**：

  > Mesenteric junction forming a small ball overlapping the proctodeum, slightly elongated to form a short, distinct mixed segment.

  说明：支撑：中肠接点成小球状叠于后肠之上，略延长形成短而明显的混合段。

- **Description › Imago（p. 18）**：

  > Fontanelle fairly small to medium size, situated at the level of eyes. Pronotum elongated, covered by dozens of long bristles.

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫囟小至中等、位于复眼水平；前胸背板延长。
