# S0087 Morphology of the alate and worker mandibles of the soil-feeding nasute termites (Isoptera, Termitidae, Nasutitermitinae) from Neotropical region

属级比较（非单属原始描述）：用于 Agnathotermes、Araujotermes、Coatitermes 的工蚁颚齿与工蚁宽隙/窄隙形态。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 506**：

  > not sinuate in Agnathotermes (fig. 49)

  说明：无颚白蚁属（agnathotermes）：支撑：「工蚁」「鉴别要点」左颚切缘不呈波状。

- **p. 506**：

  > cutting edge between the first + second marginal tooth and the third marginal tooth sinuate in Convexitermes, Atlantitermes, Araujotermes and Subulitermes

  说明：阿劳若白蚁属（araujotermes）：支撑：「工蚁」左颚 M1+2 与 M3 之间切缘呈波状。

- **p. 506**：

  > markedly wider in Atlantitermes and Araujotermes

  说明：阿劳若白蚁属（araujotermes）：支撑：「工蚁」「鉴别要点」宽隙型裂隙明显更宽。

- **p. 506**：

  > weakly or not sinuate in Coatitermes (fig. 31)

  说明：凸鼻白蚁属（coatitermes）：支撑：「工蚁」窄隙型切缘弱波状或不波状。

- **V.1.b Group 2（第 56-60 行）**：

  > Angularitermes.  Left  mandible  index  ( unknown  in  the  alate  ) of  worker  1.46,  third 
  > marginal  tooth  of  left  mandible  and  second  marginal  tooth  of  right  mandible 
  > reduced  but  distinct,  and  molar  regions  without  ridges.  Presence  of  a monomorphic 
  > worker  caste.

  说明：支撑：工蚁左颚指数 1.46、左上颚第三缘齿与右上颚第二缘齿退化、臼齿区无脊、单型工蚁品级

- **V.1.b Group 2 齿列描述（figs. 55、57）**：

  > From  the  description  and  figure  of  Mathews  ( 1977  : 233-234,  fig.  166  ),  it 
  > seems  that  the  dentition  of  the  mandibles  of  the  alate  of  Angularitermes  orestes  is 
  > similar  to  that  of  the  worker. 
  > 
  > Left  mandible  (fig. 55)  : apical  tooth  with  posterior  margin  strongly  concave; 
  > cutting  edge  between  the  first  + second  marginal  tooth  and  the  third  marginal  tooth 
  > sinuate;  third  marginal  tooth  very  reduced  and  visible  as  a pointed  projection  in 
  > front  of  the  molar  tooth;  gap  between  the  third  marginal  tooth  and  the  molar  promi- 
  > nence narrow;  molar  tooth  developed  and  mostly  hidden  beneath  the  molar  promi- 
  > nence. 
  > 
  > Right  mandible  (fig.  57)  . apical  tooth  with  posterior  margin  strongly  conca- 
  > ve; second  marginal  tooth  visible  as  a rounded  projection  at  about  half  distance 
  > between  the  point  of  the  first  marginal  tooth  and  the  molar  plate;  posterior  margin  of 
  > the  second  marginal  tooth  concave.

  说明：支撑：左上颚与右上颚齿列的中文改写（端齿、第三缘齿、第二缘齿、臼齿突）

- **V.2 Development of the Apical Tooth（第 217-227 行）**：

  > The  genus  Angularitermes  still  deserves  a word  of  explanation,  as  the  index 
  > of  the  alate  left  mandible  is  unknown.  Mathews  ( 1977  : 233  ) gives  a value  of  about 
  > 0.77  for  the  left  mandible  index  of  the  alate  of  A.  orestes,  but  this  was  obtained  by 
  > dividing  the  distance  between  the  apical  tooth  and  first  + second  marginal  tooth  by 
  > the  distance  between  the  first  + second  marginal  tooth  and  the  molar  prominence 
  > ( this  was  called  “modified  left  mandible  index”  by  Mathews  ),  instead  of  dividing 
  > the  first  measurement  above  by  the  distance  between  the  first  + second  marginal 
  > tooth  and  the  third  marginal  tooth  ( index  of  Emerson  ),  as  adopted  in  this  paper. 
  > The  “modified  left  mandible  index”  for  the  worker  of  A.  orestes,  the  sole  caste  stu- 
  > died in  this  paper,  is  approximately  1.15  and  therefore  considerably  higher  than  in 
  > the  alate.

  说明：支撑：改良左颚指数工蚁约 1.15、Mathews 记有翅成虫约 0.77、仅工蚁被研究
