# S0090 New records for Coatitermes pallidus (Snyder, 1926) and Coatitermes clevelandi (Snyder, 1926) (Isoptera, Termitidae, Nasutitermitinae) from Amazonia

OA。非属级原始描述：仅提供 Coatitermes 兵蚁鼻形的属级比较（宽锥状鼻与头宽比例），其余性状据 Constantino 1999、Fontes 1987；Coatitermes 原始描述（Fontes 1982）未取得可读全文，已在正文 note 说明。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 700**：

  > The Coatitermes nasus is very broadly conical in dorsal view with a basal width more than one-third of the soldier maximum head width. The nearest Neotropical nasutitermitines with wide conical nasi are the larger soldiers of Convexitermes Holmgren, 1910 and Paraconvexitermes Cancello & Noirot, 2003.

  说明：凸鼻白蚁属（coatitermes）：支撑：「鉴别要点」「兵蚁」鼻形与基部宽比例，以及所比较的近缘类群。
