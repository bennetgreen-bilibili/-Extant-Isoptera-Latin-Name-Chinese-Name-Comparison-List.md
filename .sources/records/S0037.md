# S0037 The Kashmir termite, Termopsis wroughtoni

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 293「The genus Termopsis (Heer) is characterized as follows」段内（Imago）**：

  > ocelli totally absent ; epistoma not prominent ; pronotum nearly flat, narrower than the head

  说明：Archotermopsis（古白蚁属）— 有翅成虫节：单眼完全缺失、额板不突出、前胸背板近平而窄于头

- **p. 293 同段（Soldier）**：

  > Soldier, very robust, with large, rectangular, flat head. Eyes present and small, or rudimentary.

  说明：Archotermopsis（古白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：体粗壮、头大而呈矩形扁平、眼小或退化

- **p. 297**：

  > I have recently established for him a distinct subgenus, Archotermopsis

  说明：Archotermopsis（古白蚁属）— note：作者自述已另立亚属 Archotermopsis，说明属征原属广义 Termopsis
