  > LONGIPENNIS, nov.sp. Winged form.— U pper surface of head castaneous; mouth-parts ochreous; antennæ dark ochreous, the apex of each segment paler; thorax and scapular shields ferruginous; dorsal plates of abdomen dark brown, ventral plates with a light brown spot at each end; the rest of insect ochreous. Length, to tip of wings, 20 mm.; to tip of body, 10mm.
