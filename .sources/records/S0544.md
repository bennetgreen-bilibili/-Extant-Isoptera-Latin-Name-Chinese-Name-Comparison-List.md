- **pp. 303–305**：

  > Rugitermes unicolor, new species. Winged adult—Head yellow-brown, smooth, shining, longer than broad … Antennae light yellow-brown, whitish towards apex, 16-17 segments … Wings dark brown to blackish, coarsely punctate … Soldier—Mandibles black, left mandible with two marginal teeth on apical third … Antenna yellow, 13 segments … Measurements in mm.: Length of entire soldier 7.60-8.00; head with mandibles 3.50-3.65; … to anterior margin (average) 2.43; hind tibia 0.95; Width of head 1.80; pronotum 1.80.
