# S0059 New genera on the Subulitermes branch of the Nasutitermitinae from the Ethiopian region (Isoptera, Termitidae)

1960 年 3 月 1 日出版（No. 1987），建立 Verrucositermes、Afrosubulitermes、Postsubulitermes、Tarditermes 四新属；属级描述明确分 IMAGO / SOLDIER / Worker（OCR 把 IMAGO 印成 Imaco、FIG. 印成 Fic.），并附 Table 4–7 测量。Postsubulitermes、Tarditermes 两属的体色与测量值取自其后的种记述（P. parviconstrictus、T. contracolor），在 04-7.json 中已标明。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 10–11，Genus Postsubulitermes, Imago**：

  > Imaco (Fic. 6): Head and postclypeus covered rather thinly with very short hairs and also with several long bristles. Pronotum with nu- merous long bristles near the margins of the sides and rear; surface with a few short hairs. Tergites and sternites with many long bristles or hairs about equal in length on both the tergites and sternites.

  说明：支撑：Postsubulitermes 有翅成虫头与后唇基被稀疏短毛并有长刚毛、前胸背板多长刚毛

- **p. 16，Genus Tarditermes, Imago（上颚）**：

  > Mandibles as in figure 10. Each mandible with a very long apical tooth and a reduced cutting edge behind the first marginal tooth. The left mandible index is 2.91, indicating a proportionately longer apical

  说明：支撑：Tarditermes 有翅成虫上颚端齿极长、左颚指数 2.91

- **p. 14，Table 5（10 头兵蚁测量）**：

  > Length of head 1.39-1.53 Width of head 0.55-0.60 Thickness of head with postmentum 0.50-0.56 Width of pronotum 0.34-0.39 Length of hind tibia 0.71-0.79

  说明：支撑：Postsubulitermes 兵蚁头长 1.39–1.53 mm、头宽 0.55–0.60 mm、后足胫节 0.71–0.79 mm
