# S0456 How do I update my favicon logo on Bing search results - Microsoft Q&A

Microsoft Q&A 上由 Independent Advisor（非微软员工）给出的答复，属社区回答而非官方文档；与 sitelint 博客说法一致，故按 secondary 收录。仅用于本轮答复（m00002 的「为什么图标还是灰色地球」）。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **1 answer（Arlene D，2025-08-29）**：

  > Save the favicon in your site’s root directory.\n\n• Use .ico, .png, .gif, or .jpg format, at least 16x16 pixels.\n\n• Check that robots.txt is not blocking it.\n\n• Resubmit or refresh your sitemap in Bing Webmaster Tools.\n\n• Use “Request indexing” in Bing Webmaster Tools to speed up the update.

  说明：支撑：Bing 侧刷新站点图标的标准步骤（根目录放置、≥16×16、robots.txt 不挡、BWT 重交 sitemap、Request indexing）

- **1 answer 末段**：

  > Bing takes time to refresh icons. It might take several days or weeks. If the old favicon still shows after these steps, submit a support request through Bing Webmaster Support.

  说明：支撑：Bing 图标刷新需数天到数周，仍不生效再走 Bing Webmaster Support
