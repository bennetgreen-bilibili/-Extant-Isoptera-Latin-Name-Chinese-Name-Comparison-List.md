# S0040 Five new genera of termites from South America and Madagascar (Isoptera, Rhinotermitidae, Termitidae)

AMNH Novitates 单篇扫描，djvu OCR；页码按页眉还原（Novitates no. 1444）。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 4（Glossotermes 的属级对比段）**：

  > Psammotermes hybostoma (generitype) soldier has the following differences: head and body with many long hairs; mandibles more curved and both mandibles coarsely serrate in the middle

  说明：Psammotermes（沙白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：全体长毛、两颚中部粗锯齿

- **p. 4 同段**：

  > eye spot not so conspicuous; antenna with more articles; pronotum without lobed front margin; femora thicker; tibial spurs 3: 2: 2.

  说明：Psammotermes（沙白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：眼点不明显、触角节数多、前胸背板前缘无叶突、腿节粗、胫距 3:2:2

- **p. 4 同段末**：

  > The soldier of Psammotermes is more primitive with regard to the mandibles, antenna, and tibial spur formula.

  说明：Psammotermes（沙白蚁属）— 鉴别要点：作者判本属兵蚁在上颚、触角、胫距式上更原始

- **p. 2（SOLDIER, FIG. 1 起首）**：

  > Glossotermes oculatus, new genus, new species SOLDIER (FIG. 1): Head, pronotum, and tergites brownish yellow. Labrum with a wide, light, transverse band in the middle.

  说明：Glossotermes（舌白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：头、前胸背板与背板褐黄，上唇中部有宽浅色横带（其余为种级描述）

- **p. 4（Comparison with other genera）**：

  > The closest genus seems to be Psammotermes (Rhinotermitidae), but the relationship is rather remote.

  说明：Glossotermes（舌白蚁属）— 鉴别要点：与 Psammotermes 最近但关系甚远

- **p. 13（Cornicapritermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Head thick and elongate with slightly curved sides; frontal conical projection pointed forward and extended well beyond the base of the mandibles, slightly upturned at the tip, with a small bump in front and below the tip that seems to be the opening of the frontal gland.

  说明：支撑：角扭白蚁属（/genus/cornicapritermes/）

- **p. 14（Cornicapritermes 兵蚁）**：

  > tip shallowly forked with two lateral points each making an angle of about 60° and the angle between the points about 150°.

  说明：支撑：角扭白蚁属（/genus/cornicapritermes/）

- **p. 14（Cornicapritermes 工蚁）**：

  > WoRKER: Mandibles with the apical tooth distinctly larger than the first marginal tooth; the right mandible with the second marginal tooth reduced or absent; the left mandible with the third marginal tooth separated from the fused first and second marginal teeth by a small indentation.

  说明：支撑：角扭白蚁属（/genus/cornicapritermes/）

- **p. 10（Quasitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Head elongate, quadrangular, sides parallel and slightly convex; frontal projection pointed forward, not very acute

  说明：支撑：相似白蚁属（/genus/quasitermes/）

- **p. 11（Quasitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Labrum hardly asymmetrical, longer than wide, tip shallowly and slightly concave or angulate in the middle (turned up in figure and shows the under side), sometimes with a weak lobe between the lateral point and the middle; lateral points sharp but short.

  说明：支撑：相似白蚁属（/genus/quasitermes/）

- **p. 11（Quasitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Mandibles asymmetrical and adapted for snapping; left mandible somewhat sinuate; right mandible somewhat bowed outward

  说明：支撑：相似白蚁属（/genus/quasitermes/）
