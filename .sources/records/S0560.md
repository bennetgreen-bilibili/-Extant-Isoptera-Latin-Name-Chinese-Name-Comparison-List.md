## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 34, alate**：

  > AMITERMES BEAUMONT I Banks. Winged adult.—Brown-black, shining, slightly longer than broad, rounded posteriorly, with dense, long, light yellow hairs. Fontanelle indistinct, a linear slit in a depression between the eyes. Antennae dark yellow-brown, 15 segments

  说明：支撑：体色、囟、触角

- **p. 34, alate**：

  > Eye black, large, nearly round, prominent and projecting; very close to lateral margin of head. Ocellus elongate, fairly deeply set in head (with prominent overhanging rim), separated from the com- pound eye by a distance equal to its short diameter.

  说明：支撑：复眼与单眼

- **p. 35, alate**：

  > Wings dusky gray iridescent, costal veims darker brown, tissue punctate, margins ciliate; agen vein runs straight to apex, close to cubitus, which is nearly in the center of the wing, cubitus parallel to the median vein, with 10 to 11 branches or sub-branches, cubitus reaches apex of wing.

  说明：支撑：翅色与脉序

- **p. 15-16, alate**：

  > LEUCOTERMES CONVEXINOTATUS, new species. Winged adult (pl. 1, fig. 7) —Head yellow-brown, darker between the eyes, paler posteriorly, not so dark as in L. tenuis Hagen; sub- elliptical, broadest at eyes, narrower than in tenuis, rounded pos- teriorly

  说明：支撑：新种与体色比较

- **p. 16, alate**：

  > Fontanelle not so large as in tenuis, a pale, slightly raised, round spot on a line just back of the eyes. Antennae light yellow-brown, with 17 segments, pubescent

  说明：支撑：囟与触角节数

- **p. 16, alate**：

  > Post-clypeus light yellow, prominent and bulging as in tenwis, over three times as wide as long; posterior margin markedly convex, border of head concave—more so than in tenuis; post-clypeus longer than in tenuis but not as broad; not strongly bilobed as in tenuis. Pronotum same color as head, subcordate, wider than long, but. not as wide as head, not emarginate posteriorly, but anterior margin indented in middle

  说明：支撑：后唇基与前胸背板

- **p. 17, alate**：

  > LEUCOTERMES CARDINI, new species. Winged adult.—Head pale yellow-brown, darker between the eyes, paler posteriorly; subelliptical, widest at the eyes, rounded poste- riorly; with dense short and longer hairs.

  说明：支撑：体色与头形

- **pp. 17-18, alate**：

  > Antennae light yellow, with 17 segments, pubescent; segments become wider toward the apex, vary in size and shape; first segment elongate, cylindrical; second shorter than first, third ringlike, about half the length of the second segment

  说明：支撑：触角

- **p. 18, alate**：

  > Pronotum same color as head, subcordate, indented in middle anteriorly, but slightly emarginate posteriorly, with both long and short hairs. Meso- and metanotum not emarginate posteriorly; mesonotum covered by the wing scale.

  说明：支撑：前胸背板与翅鳞

- **p. 20, soldier**：

  > LEUCOTERMES LONGICEPS, new species. Soldier.—Head light yellowish-brown, slender, very elongate, over twice as long as broad, sides slightly concave at center, with but few scattered long hairs.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头形

- **p. 21, soldier**：

  > Antennae white with tinge of yellow, 17 segments, segments become broader toward apex, pubescent; third segment subclavate, shorter than second or fourth segments. Labrum light yellow, pointed at apex, where long hairs, broadest in middle. Mandibles dark castaneous brown to black, slender, curved in at tips.

  说明：支撑：触角、上唇、上颚

- **p. 21, measurements**：

  > Measurements—Length of entire soldier, 6.75 mm.; length of head with mandibles, 3.2 mm.; length of head without mandibles (to anterior), 2.2 mm.; length of head (to tip of labrum), 2.775 mm.; length of pronotum, 0.5 mm.; length of left mandible, 1.1 mm.; length of hind tibia, 0.708 mm.; width of head, 1 mm.; width of pro- notum, 0.8 mm.

  说明：支撑：量度
