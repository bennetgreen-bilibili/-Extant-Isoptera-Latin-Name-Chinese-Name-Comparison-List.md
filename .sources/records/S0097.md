# S0097 Obtusitermes monomorphus, a new termite species (Blattodea: Termitidae: Nasutitermitinae) from Panama

Zenodo 记录 14862136（cc-zero）。与 Constantino & Acioli (2009) 就兵蚁上颚「without points / with small points」存在分歧，正文采本记录原文并已注明。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 572（Etymology）**：

  > Named for the monomorphic soldier caste which is exclusive to Obtusitermes .

  说明：支撑：兵蚁单型为该属属征与命名由来

- **p. 572（Soldier）**：

  > Monomorphic, small size. Head capsule reddish brown, nasus darker. […] Head capsule pyriform from above, constricted at anterior one-third. […] Nasus broadly conical, upturned about 10°. Antennae with 11 articles; antennal article formula 2<3>4≤5. Mandibles with small points.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁单型、体型小、头壳梨形、象鼻宽锥形上翘 10°、触角 11 节、上颚具小齿突

- **pp. 573–574（Worker）**：

  > Antennae with 12 articles […] Mesenteron and P1 tubular, mixed segment lacking; P1 divided near middle by slight darkened constriction.

  说明：支撑：工蚁单型、触角 12 节、混合段缺如、P1 近中部收缩

- **p. 574（Comparisons）**：

  > The worker hindgut of Obtusitermes spp. is unique among termites in having the P1 joined to the P3 by a very long and tubular P2 with a curvature of 180° and weak armature at both ends

  说明：支撑：本属后肠 P1–P2–P3 构造在等翅目中独一无二

- **p. 573（Worker）**：

  > Enteric valve armature composed of two rings separated by nearly 1 mm of smooth cuticle.

  说明：支撑：肠瓣两圈之间隔以近 1 mm 平滑角质
