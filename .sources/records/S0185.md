- **Abstract / full.txt**：

  > The harvester termite, Baucaliotermes hainesi (Fuller) (Termitidae: Nasutitermitinae), is an endemic in southern Namibia, where it collects and eats dry grass.

  说明：支撑：纳米比亚南部特有种、取食干草

- **Distribution / full.txt**：

  > The range of this species is limited to southern Namibia and the northwestern Cape Province of South Africa (Coaton and Sheasby 1973).

  说明：支撑：分布限纳米比亚南部与南非西北开普省

- **Tunnel excavation and mapping / full.txt**：

  > Nests of B. hainesi were regularly visible at the surface as small mounds of cemented material 10 to 15 cm high.

  说明：支撑：巢在地表露出 10–15 cm 高胶结砂小丘

- **Results, tunnel architecture / full.txt**：

  > At 50 to 75 cm intervals, the tunnels are connected to the surface by vertical risers that can be opened to gain foraging access to the surrounding area.

  说明：支撑：每 50–75 cm 一条通往地面的竖井

- **Results, tunnel architecture / full.txt**：

  > Riser openings were usually closed during the day, as this termite species forages mostly at night.

  说明：支撑：竖井白天关闭、主要夜间觅食

- **Results, tunnel architecture / full.txt**：

  > The distance between risers averaged 50 to 75 cm among tunnels (SD 14 to 40 cm), suggesting that the termites rarely needed to travel more than one half to one meter on the surface.

  说明：支撑：竖井间距 50–75 cm（SD 14–40），地面行走很少超过 0.5–1 m

- **Results, excavation / full.txt**：

  > The total length of tunnels exposed was 76 m within an area of roughly 170 m2 in the first excavation and 110 m of tunnel in an area of about 300 m2 in the second excavation.

  说明：支撑：两次发掘分别为 76 m/170 m² 与 110 m/300 m²

- **Results, tunnel cross-section / full.txt**：

  > In the center runs a smooth, raised walkway along which termites travel, and along the sides lie pockets that act as depots where foragers deposit grass pieces harvested from the surface.

  说明：支撑：隧道中央走道、两侧凹囊为草片段暂存点

- **Results, nest census / full.txt**：

  > The nest was home to about 45,000 termites, of which about 6,000 (13%) were immature (but very small immatures, and eggs were not counted), 32,300 (71%; 36 g, dry) were workers and 4,100 (9.1%; 3 g, dry) were soldiers.

  说明：支撑：巢内约 45,000 头；未成熟 13%、工蚁 71%、兵蚁 9.1%

- **Results, nest census / full.txt**：

  > In addition, there were about 2,800 (6.2%; 5 g, dry) immature reproductives with wing buds.

  说明：支撑：具翅芽未成熟生殖蚁约 2,800 头（6.2%）

- **Results, nest census / full.txt**：

  > No primary reproductives and no “royal cell” were found, suggesting this may have been a subsidiary nest (or calie).

  说明：支撑：未见原始生殖蚁与王台，疑为副巢

  说明：支撑：巢内草片段干重约 15 g
