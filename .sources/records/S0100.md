  说明：支撑：峰白蚁属（/genus/fastigitermes/）

- **p. 41（Profastigitermes 兵蚁）**：

  说明：支撑：前峰白蚁属（/genus/profastigitermes/）

- **p. 41（Profastigitermes 兵蚁）**：

  说明：支撑：前峰白蚁属（/genus/profastigitermes/）

- **p. 41（Profastigitermes 兵蚁）**：

  说明：支撑：前峰白蚁属（/genus/profastigitermes/）

- **pp. 3–4（Nitiditermes 有翅成虫）**：

  > Head widely oval, smooth, without much irregularity in the surface; with fine microscopic sculpturing or roughness around the fontanelle. Fontanelle elongate; in a short narrow depression in the head. Top of head between the ocelli flatly and evenly convex. Eye rather flat, of about medium size, a little less than one-third of its diameter from the lower margin of the head.

  说明：支撑：叉白蚁属（/genus/furculitermes/）

- **p. 20（Furculitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Labrum (figs. 7A, 9) large, with two forked lateral lobes that are somewhat narrow and end in rounded or slightly truncated tips reaching beyond the marginal teeth of the extended mandibles; front margin deeply concave.

  说明：支撑：叉白蚁属（/genus/furculitermes/）

- **p. 20（Furculitermes 工蚁）**：

  > Worker (FIG. 8B): The apical tooth of each mandible comparatively large. The second marginal tooth of the right mandible absent.

  说明：支撑：叉白蚁属（/genus/furculitermes/）

- **pp. 12–13（Mucrotermes 兵蚁）**：

  > A tuft of bristles near the opening of the frontal gland is lacking in Mucrotermes but is characteristic of several related genera. The head is comparatively short, wide, and thick.

  说明：支撑：锐尖白蚁属（/genus/mucrotermes/）

- **pp. 12–13（Mucrotermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Vertex (fig. 4B) rather sharply humped in profile, with a steeply sloping front and vertex above and below the frontal gland opening that lies in the middle of the slope a little closer to the postclypeus than to the top.

  说明：支撑：锐尖白蚁属（/genus/mucrotermes/）

- **p. 14（Mucrotermes 工蚁）**：

  > Both mandibles with proportionately large apical teeth. There is no visible angular notch in front of the third marginal tooth of the left mandible. The right mandible has a small but distinct second marginal tooth.

  说明：支撑：锐尖白蚁属（/genus/mucrotermes/）

- **pp. 3–4（Nitiditermes 有翅成虫）**：

Eye rather flat, of about medium size, a little less than one-third of its diameter from the lower margin of the head.

  说明：支撑：净白蚁属（/genus/nitiditermes/）

- **p. 4（Nitiditermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Profile of head with a bulbous front above the depressed gland opening, front not reaching over the base of the mandibles.

  说明：支撑：净白蚁属（/genus/nitiditermes/）

- **pp. 5–6（Nitiditermes 工蚁）**：

  > In Nitidi- termes, the apical tooth is markedly larger than the first marginal tooth in each mandible, the distance from the apex to the tip of the first marginal tooth of the left mandible is longer than the distance from the tip of the first marginal tooth to the tip of the third marginal tooth

  说明：支撑：净白蚁属（/genus/nitiditermes/）

- **p. 45, Soldier**：

  > Head from above (fig. 16A) subquadrangular, with somewhat straight or slightly convex sides. Front and vertex relatively flat compared to those of related genera. Frontal gland in a small depression, with two flat longitudinal ridges in front of the opening on each side

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头形、额/头顶扁平、额腺凹陷与纵脊

- **pp. 45–46, Soldier**：

  > Labrum with two lateral sharp points in front and with a conspicuous convex median lobe in the middle of the front margin . . . Mandibles of the biting type . . . comparatively straight and slender . . . tips not hooked . . . base of left mandible with a small, fairly sharp tooth . . . behind the tooth there is a deep and small, somewhat angular curve ending in a sharp spine-like projection

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上唇尖角与中叶、咬切式上颚、左上颚基齿与棘突

- **pp. 46–47, Worker & Comparisons**：

  > The mandibles resemble the more generalized genera near Basidentitermes. The apical tooth is large compared to the first marginal tooth . . . The right mandible possesses a distinct second marginal tooth... Forficulitermes is related to Basidentitermes, Proboscitermes, Orthotermes, Profastigitermes, and Fastigitermes in the shape of the soldier labrum with its median lobe and sharp lateral points.

  说明：支撑：工蚁上颚形态、属级亲缘关系

- **p. 16（种级兵蚁描述起首）**：

  > Mucrotermes osborni, new species SOLDIER (FIG. 4): Color of head brownish yellow. Pronotum generally paler than the head. Legs and abdomen paler than pronotum. Head with a few scattered bristles and short hairs on the top and sides and a very few short hairs near the opening of the frontal gland. A single bristle is usually on the lateral hump below and behind the base of the antenna. Postmentum without hairs or bristles. Labrum with a few bristles near the anterior border at the sides.

  说明：支撑条目「兵蚁」节的体色与毛被描述；后续句（前胸背板刚毛、腹节缘刚毛、触角 14 或 15 节）在同一段续至 p. 17。

- **p. 17（LOCALITIES AND MATERIAL）**：

  > LOCALITIES AND MATERIAL: The species is described from six soldiers (holotype, paratypes) and one worker from the type colony collected by A. Emerson in the ground at a Pygmy camp 4 kilometers north of Camp Putnam (latitude 1° 24' N., longitude 28° 36' E.) on the Epulu River, Belgian Congo, on May 20, 1948; and five soldiers and two workers from the paratype colony collected by A. Emerson in the outside layers of a mound at the base of a tree occupied by Protermes prorepens (Sjostedt) at Yangambi (latitude 0° 47' N., longitude 24° 23' E.), Belgian Congo, on May 29, 1948.

  说明：支撑条目「其他」节的模式材料与产地；原文 Sjöstedt 的 ö 在 OCR 中作 o。

- **p. 17（Comparisons）**：

  > A cotype soldier of M. heterochilus from Segboroué (latitude 6° 20' N., longitude 2° 0' E.), Dahomey, differs from M. osborni in its smaller size (width of head, 1.10 mm.); in the more pronounced hump at the rear portion of the postmentum; in the more concave front margin of the labrum; in the sharper lateral points of the labrum and the more concave side behind the points; in the fact that the elbow of each mandible is proportionately farther from the base; and in the more shallow indentation of the front margin of the pronotum.

  说明：支撑条目「鉴别要点」中与 M. heterochilus（Silvestri）的六项区别（由「heterochilus 较小/更…」反推 osborni 的对应状态）。
