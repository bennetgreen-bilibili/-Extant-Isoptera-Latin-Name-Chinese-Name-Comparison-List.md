# S0100 Six new genera of Termitinae from the Belgian Congo (Isoptera, Termitidae)

AMNH Novitates 单篇扫描，djvu OCR；页码按页眉还原（Novitates no. 1988）。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 21（Nitiditermes 工蚁）**：

  > The imago-worker mandibles, with the absence of the second marginal tooth in the right mandible, are similar only to those of Euchilotermes, Ophiotermes, Tuberculitermes, and Spini- termes

  说明：支撑：真唇白蚁属（/genus/euchilotermes/）

- **p. 42（Profastigitermes 兵蚁诊断）**：

  > Fastigitermes has a more forward-produced hump on the vertex above the opening of the frontal gland

  说明：支撑：峰白蚁属（/genus/fastigitermes/）

- **p. 42（Profastigitermes 兵蚁诊断）**：

  > The projec- tion of the postmentum is proportionately much greater in Fastigiter- mes and ends in a fairly sharp, single, backward-pointing spine.

  说明：支撑：峰白蚁属（/genus/fastigitermes/）

- **p. 41（Profastigitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Profile of head (fig. 15B) with a somewhat rounded hump on the vertex above and somewhat posterior to the opening of the frontal gland.

  说明：支撑：前峰白蚁属（/genus/profastigitermes/）

- **p. 41（Profastigitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Postmentum (fig. 15C) constricted from below; side margins behind the anterior portion concave; narrowest region in the area of a conspicuous double- pointed projection about one-fourth of the length of the postmentum from the middle hind margin

  说明：支撑：前峰白蚁属（/genus/profastigitermes/）

- **p. 41（Profastigitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > SOLDIER (FIG. 15): Head (figs. 15A, 15B) with numerous scattered bristles and a large bunch of long hairs around the frontal gland open- ing; a few bristles near the front margin of the labrum; and two bristles near the anterior margin of the postmentum (fig. 15C).

  说明：支撑：前峰白蚁属（/genus/profastigitermes/）

- **pp. 3–4（Nitiditermes 有翅成虫）**：

  > Head widely oval, smooth, without much irregularity in the surface; with fine microscopic sculpturing or roughness around the fontanelle. Fontanelle elongate; in a short narrow depression in the head. Top of head between the ocelli flatly and evenly convex. Eye rather flat, of about medium size, a little less than one-third of its diameter from the lower margin of the head.

  说明：支撑：叉白蚁属（/genus/furculitermes/）

- **p. 20（Furculitermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Labrum (figs. 7A, 9) large, with two forked lateral lobes that are somewhat narrow and end in rounded or slightly truncated tips reaching beyond the marginal teeth of the extended mandibles; front margin deeply concave.

  说明：支撑：叉白蚁属（/genus/furculitermes/）

- **p. 20（Furculitermes 工蚁）**：

  > Worker (FIG. 8B): The apical tooth of each mandible comparatively large. The second marginal tooth of the right mandible absent.

  说明：支撑：叉白蚁属（/genus/furculitermes/）

- **pp. 12–13（Mucrotermes 兵蚁）**：

  > A tuft of bristles near the opening of the frontal gland is lacking in Mucrotermes but is characteristic of several related genera. The head is comparatively short, wide, and thick.

  说明：支撑：锐尖白蚁属（/genus/mucrotermes/）

- **pp. 12–13（Mucrotermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Vertex (fig. 4B) rather sharply humped in profile, with a steeply sloping front and vertex above and below the frontal gland opening that lies in the middle of the slope a little closer to the postclypeus than to the top.

  说明：支撑：锐尖白蚁属（/genus/mucrotermes/）

- **p. 14（Mucrotermes 工蚁）**：

  > Both mandibles with proportionately large apical teeth. There is no visible angular notch in front of the third marginal tooth of the left mandible. The right mandible has a small but distinct second marginal tooth.

  说明：支撑：锐尖白蚁属（/genus/mucrotermes/）

- **pp. 3–4（Nitiditermes 有翅成虫）**：

  > Head widely oval, smooth, without much irregularity in the surface; with fine microscopic sculpturing or roughness around the fontanelle. Fontanelle elongate; in a short narrow depression in the head. Top of head between the ocelli flatly and evenly convex. Eye rather flat, of about medium size, a little less than one-third of its diameter from the lower margin of the head.

  说明：支撑：净白蚁属（/genus/nitiditermes/）

- **p. 4（Nitiditermes 兵蚁）**：

  > Profile of head with a bulbous front above the depressed gland opening, front not reaching over the base of the mandibles.

  说明：支撑：净白蚁属（/genus/nitiditermes/）

- **pp. 5–6（Nitiditermes 工蚁）**：

  > In Nitidi- termes, the apical tooth is markedly larger than the first marginal tooth in each mandible, the distance from the apex to the tip of the first marginal tooth of the left mandible is longer than the distance from the tip of the first marginal tooth to the tip of the third marginal tooth

  说明：支撑：净白蚁属（/genus/nitiditermes/）
