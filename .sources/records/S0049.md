# S0049 The nasute termites (Isoptera: Nasutitermitinae) of Papua New Guinea

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 583（Diwaitermes gen. nov., soldier）**：

  > Fully nasute, related to Nasutitermes. Head not constricted behind antennal sockets. Soldier caste monomorphic, small to medium sized. Antennae of 11 or 12 segments; if of 12 segments, third one very short and incompletely separated from the fourth (Fig. 111). Head capsule hairy, with many setae of various lengths. Points on mandibles small, sometimes missing.

  说明：支撑：Diwaitermes 兵蚁单型、触角 11–12 节、头壳多毛

- **p. 584（Diwaitermes, worker）**：

  > Upper ring of spiny areas of enteric valve slightly asymmetrical, lower ring with spines penetrating into the paunch. Worker caste dimorphic. Size distribution of large workers unimodal.

  说明：支撑：Diwaitermes 工蚁二型与肠瓣刺区

- **pp. 572–573（Genus Niuginitermes gen. nov.，Soldier）**：

  > Soldier Small to medium-sized nasutes, closely allied to Nasutitermes. Soldier caste monomorphic. Head capsule without constriction behind antennae. Antennae 12-segmented. Mandibles with points.

  说明：支撑：新几内亚白蚁属兵蚁单型、头壳触角后无缢缩、触角 12 节、上颚具尖

- **p. 579**：

  > Although originally thought to be related to Subulitermes (Ahmad 1968), the monotypic genus Eleanoritermes Ahmad (Figs 94-96), from Borneo, also resembles Niuginitermes, but differs from it by the enteric valve positioned more anteriorly, weakly armed with few small spines on 3 swellings (Fig. 93), and malpighian tubules inserted contiguously.

  说明：Eleanoritermes 属级比较性诊断；双栏 pdftotext 图号 '9 3' 已校正为 93。

- **p. 579**：

  > In addition, the soldier of E. borneensis Ahmad has 11-segmented antennae and mandibles without points, the worker 12-segmented antennae.

  说明：兵蚁与工蚁触角；OCR 漏字 'bomeensis'/'1 1-segmented' 已校正为 borneensis/11-segmented。
