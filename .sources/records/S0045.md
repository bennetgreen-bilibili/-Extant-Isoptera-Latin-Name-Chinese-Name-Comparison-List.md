# S0045 The genus Porotermes (Isoptera)

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 144「Key to the genera of Calotermitinae Holmgr.」**：

  > Imago : i. Ocelli wanting. Empodia wanting. Cerci 5-jointed Porotermes.

  说明：Porotermes（洞白蚁属）— 鉴别要点：无单眼、无 empodium、尾须 5 节（兵蚁尾须长 5 节）

- **p. 144（与 Stolotermes 对比段）**：

  > It may be noted that soldiers of Stolotermes bear a superficial resemblance to Porotermes, but are easily distinguished by their small size, more flattened head and body, small heart-shaped pronotum and facetted eyes.

  说明：Porotermes（洞白蚁属）— 鉴别要点：与胄白蚁属的区别（体型小、头体更扁、前胸背板心形、眼具小眼面）

- **p. 144（工蚁品级说明段）**：

  > that caste devolve upon the immature stages of the winged imago

  说明：Porotermes（洞白蚁属）— 工蚁节：本亚科无真工蚁，职能由未成熟阶段承担
