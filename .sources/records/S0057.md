# S0057 A new genus and species of termites (Isoptera, Termitidae, Nasutitermitinae) from the rainforest of northern Peru

属级 Diagnosis 仅列肠部特征；兵蚁与工蚁体壁特征在属级 Description 内。imago 记为 Unknown。页内 locator 用章节名，未逐页核对页码。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Sinqasapatermes gen. n., Diagnosis (ZooKeys 159: 1–9)**：

  > distal margin of the enteric valve (P2) not everted into the paunch (P3) but bending towards the ileum (P1), that is, directed against the flow of food

  说明：属级鉴别核心：肠瓣远端逆流弯折

- **Sinqasapatermes gen. n., Diagnosis**：

  > armed with one ring of six equal subtriangularly-shaped ridges, each ridge with short spines on entire surface

  说明：armature 一环六脊

- **Sinqasapatermes gen. n., Etymology**：

  > sinqa = nose and sapa = big, and Latin termes = termite, meaning termite with a big nose

  说明：属名词源（兵蚁 nasus 大）

- **兵蚁比较段**：

  > Agnathotermes Snyder and Paraconvexitermes Cancello & Noirot also have a conical nasus and antenna with 11 articles, but they differ from Sinqasapatermes by their chaetotaxy and head shape of the soldier.

  说明：支撑：Paraconvexitermes 兵蚁象鼻锥形、触角 11 节

- **工蚁比较段**：

  > Reduced ridges on molar areas are found in Araujotermes , Atlantitermes , Coatitermes , Convexitermes , Ereymatermes , Paraconvexitermes , and Subulitermes , but none of these genera have lost the marginal teeth.

  说明：支撑：Ereymatermes 与 Paraconvexitermes 工蚁臼齿区脊减弱但保留缘齿
