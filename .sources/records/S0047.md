# S0047 A revision of the termite subfamily Nasutitermitinae (Isoptera, Termitidae) from the Ethiopian Region

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 141（Eutermellus, imago）**：

  > Left mandible with apical tooth distinctly longer than first marginal, but distance between them slightly shorter than that from first to third marginals (left mandible index 96-98), cutting edge between the latter sinuate and indented in front of third marginal, which is approximately equilateral, small subsidiary tooth just visible in gap between third marginal and molar prominence

  说明：支撑：Eutermellus 有翅成虫左上颚端齿、缘齿与左颚指数

- **p. 141（Eutermellus, soldier）**：

  > Monomorphic. Nose conical to cylindrical, tapering to fine point, distinctly rugose. Vestigial mandibles with minute points or without, labrum with tri-lobed anterior margin. Head capsule very weakly to moderately constricted behind antennae. Antennae 13 segmented.

  说明：支撑：Eutermellus 兵蚁单型、鼻锥形至圆柱形、触角 13 节

- **p. 158（Mimeutermes, worker）**：

  > Mandibles as imago except apical tooth longer in proportion (left mandible index 3.32-3.59), and right molar plate larger with more pronounced flanges.

  说明：支撑：Mimeutermes 工蚁上颚指数与右臼板
