  > The swarming imagoes ofB. beesonirepresent both the sexes in equal proportion. The phenomenon of parthenogenesis is of rare occurrence. Although pairing of a female with the opposite sex exhibited a strong influence on development of the ovarioles and consequently the egg‐laying, dealation was of the primary concern. The process of oogenesis appears to be an important need for the normal survival of the reproductives. The females which initiated oviposition earlier laid more eggs and survived longer as compared to the ones started later or those laid no eggs at all. To initiate egg‐laying the females ofB. beesoniprefer a higher relative humidity than for their normal survival.
