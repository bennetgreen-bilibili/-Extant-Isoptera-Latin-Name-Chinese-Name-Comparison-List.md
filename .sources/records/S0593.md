  > Termes (Termes) culturarum (Sjostedt) Text Figure 21 Imago (Queen).—Head oval, brown; a large lighter spot surrounding the rather inconspicuous fontanelle; head covered with bristles and hairs; back of head with median dark line. Ocelli of medium size, .267 mm. wide, .32 mm. long, .28 mm. removed from the eyes. Postclypeus rather short, lighter than the head, median line present.
