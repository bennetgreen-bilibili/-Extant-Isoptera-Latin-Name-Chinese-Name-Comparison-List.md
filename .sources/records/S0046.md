# S0046 The relations of a relict South African termite (Isoptera, Hodotermitidae, Stolotermes)

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 7（IMAGO）**：

  > IMAGO.-Head, pronotum, wing scales and abdomen clothed with fairly long hairs, fewer on head. Fontanelle absent; epicranial suture present; ocelli reduced

  说明：Stolotermes（胄白蚁属）— 有翅成虫节：无 fontanelle、单眼退化、复眼中等大、后唇基极短、触角 15–17 节、前胸背板 Y 形凹陷

- **p. 8（SOLDIER）**：

  > SOLDIER.-Head, thorax and abdomen sparsely covered with hairs and longer bristles.

  说明：Stolotermes（胄白蚁属）— 兵蚁节：头胸腹疏被毛与长刚毛（该段其余特征见正文）
