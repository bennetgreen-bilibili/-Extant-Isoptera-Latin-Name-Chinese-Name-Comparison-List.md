## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **摘要**：

  > Work carried out in five forests in southern Cameroon indicates that termitaries of Cubitermes subarquatus, whether active or abandoned, provide shelter for large numbers of invasive invertebrates, particularly ants and other termite species

  说明：支撑：在喀麦隆南部 5 片森林的工作表明，Cubitermes subarquatus 的蚁丘无论活跃或废弃，都为大量入侵性无…

- **摘要**：

  > Dissections of 90 termitaries (65 active, 25 abandoned) produced a great diversity of invasive fauna, including 354 ant colonies representing 75 species in 31 genera.

  说明：支撑：解剖 90 座蚁丘（65 座活跃、25 座废弃），共得 354 个蚂蚁群落，隶属 31 属 75 种。

- **摘要**：

  > Ants occurred in all abandoned termitaries but 6·1% of active termitaries had no ants present.

  说明：支撑：所有废弃蚁丘中都有蚂蚁，而 6.1% 的活跃蚁丘内没有蚂蚁。
