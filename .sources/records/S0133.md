# S0133 New species of the genus Glyptotermes Froggatt from the Papuan, Oriental, Ethiopian, and Neotropical regions (Isoptera, Kalotermitidae)

agent-collected; quote verbatim from the archived full text.

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **American Museum Novitates No.2089, schmidti 词源段**：

  > The species is named in honor of the late Dr. K. P. Schmidt, Chief Curator of Zoology, Chicago Natural History Museum, Chicago, Illinois.

  说明：支撑：schmidti 纪念 Karl Patterson Schmidt

- **pp. 3—4**：

  > Glyptotermes guamensis is close to G. xantholabrum Hill, but can be distinguished from it in the following respects: the sides of the head are almost parallel, the postmentum is more constricted in the posterior half, and the sides of the pronotum are convex.

  说明：支撑：与 G. xantholabrum 的鉴别（头侧近平行、后颏后半收缩、前胸背板侧缘凸）

- **pp. 4—8**：

  > Glyptotermes lighti closely resembles G. schmidti, new species, and G. palauensis, new species. In Glyptotermes schmidti the head is smaller, the forehead is steeper, the mandibles are shorter, with their outer bases distinctly humped, and the postmentum is less constricted.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁与 G. schmidti、G. palauensis 的鉴别

- **pp. 16—20**：

  > Glyptotermes concavifrons closely resembles G. dilatatus (Bugnion and Popoff), but differs as follows: the postmentum is less constricted in the middle, and the anterolateral corners of the pronotum are less broadly rounded.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁与 G. dilatatus 的鉴别（后颏中部收缩较弱、前胸背板前侧角圆度较小）

- **pp. 20—23**：

  > It is distinguishable from G. dilatatus as follows: it has a depressed and concave forehead and shorter mandibles, the first marginal tooth of the left mandible is closer to the tip of the apical tooth, the postmentum is less constricted in the middle, and the pronotum is broader, with its posterolateral margins slightly concave.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁与 G. dilatatus 的鉴别（额凹、上颚短、左颚第 1 缘齿更近端齿、后颏收缩弱、前胸背板更宽）

- **pp. 23—25**：

  > Glyptotermes parki, new species, is smaller throughout, and the anterodorsal prominences on its forehead are not so prominent. Glyptotermes longuisculus, new species, is smaller in all respects, and the anterodorsal prominences on its forehead are more feeble.

  说明：支撑：与 G. parki、G. longuisculus 的鉴别

- **pp. 25—28**：

  > Glyptotermes ignotus Wilkinson closely resembles G. jurioni, but can be distinguished from it as follows: it has a longer and broader head, the mandible has a prominent basal hump, and the pronotum is broader, with its anterior margin concave and its posterolateral margins more broadly rounded.

  说明：支撑：与 G. ignotus 的鉴别（头、上颚基瘤、前胸背板形状）

- **pp. 28—31**：

  > Glyptotermes jurioni, new species, G. sinomalatus, new species, and G. ueleensis Coaton are smaller in all measurements. In G. insulanus Silvestri the forewing is shorter and narrower, the eye shape is different, the ocellus is larger, the anterolateral corners of the pronotum are narrower, and the antenna has 12 articles.

  说明：支撑：与 G. jurioni、G. sinomalatus、G. ueleensis、G. insulanus 的量度与形态鉴别

- **pp. 44—45**：

  > In the Neotropical Region the imago of Glyptotermes longipennis has the longest wings. It differs from G. hospitalis Emerson in having larger and more prominent eyes, more broadly rounded anterolateral corners of the pronotum, and a longer tibia. Glyptotermes contracticornis (Snyder) has shorter and narrower wings and a broader pronotum, with its posterolateral margins rounded.

  说明：支撑：新翅地区有翅成虫以本种翅最长，及与 G. hospitalis、G. contracticornis 的鉴别

- **pp. 39—42**：

  > Head elongate; sides parallel, slightly narrow in front; forehead in profile nearly vertical, in some specimens sloping at an angle; bilobed, with a deep and narrow incision in middle.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头部形状与额部双叶、中部深窄切口
