## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **章节 Rustitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis（文章 pp. 35–49）**：

  > Enteric valve with six slightly asymmetrical cushions. Each cushion forming a central pouch made of scales smaller than those between the cushions. Each cushion composed of about 60–80 scales, wider at the base and narrower at the apex. Posterior portion of pads truncated, with 35 to 50 rectangular scales arranged from the middle to the apex of the cushion […]

  说明：鉴别要点（6 个不对称瓣垫、中央囊袋、60–80 枚鳞片、后部 35–50 枚长方形鳞片）

- **同上, Comparison and remarks**：

  > The digestive tube coiling of the new genus is similar to Hydrecotermes, but R. boteroi sp. nov. can be differentiated by the worker, which has thick bristles along the inner margin of the fore tibia, absent in Hydrecotermes.

  说明：鉴别要点（与 Hydrecotermes 的区分：前胫节内缘粗刚毛）

- **同上, Description, Worker**：

  > Worker (Figs 2–4). Monomorphic. Small and rounded fontanelle; postclypeus rather slightly inflated; head capsule covered with medium and long bristles.

  说明：工蚁（单型、囟小而圆、后唇基稍膨隆、头壳毛被）

- **Comparison and remarks**：

  > In Aparatermes the insertion of P1 in P3 occurs in dorsal view with a trilobate EV setting

  说明：支撑：P1 与 P3 的连接在背面观呈三叶状肠瓣座。

- **Comparison and remarks**：

  > the enteric valve of Aparatermes has small spines or pointy scales, which are absent in R. boteroi sp. nov.; in addition, the EV in Aparatermes has the posterior portion of the pads without scales

  说明：支撑：肠瓣具小刺或尖鳞，且垫的后部无鳞片。
