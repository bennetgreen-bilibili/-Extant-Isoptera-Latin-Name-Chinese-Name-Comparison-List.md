- **p. 1043（newrecordsfourt17sche）**：

  > Identification. The illustration of the E. /atidens sol- 
  > dier in the original description by Emerson and Banks 
  > (1957) show the broad base of the marginal teeth, espe- 
  > cially in the left mandible as in Figure 2A. Soldiers are 
  > also adorned with “short hairs profusely scattered over 
  > head, nasus, and postmentum; dorsal surface of distal 
  > one-third of nasus and posterior portion of head almost 
  > bare” (Emerson and Banks 1957: 4, not visible in Fig. 2). 
  > The original measurements also agree with my material.

  说明：本种 Identification 段：上颚边缘齿基部宽、兵蚁毛被（引 Emerson & Banks 1957: 4 逐字）；支撑条目「鉴别要点」。

- **p. 1044（newrecordsfourt17sche）**：

  > The EVAs in E. /atidens (Fig. 2D) and E. spissus (Fig. 
  > AE) are composed of three finger-like pads of the three 
  > lengths covered with 80-100 minute spines interspersed 
  > by three shorter and wider fields covered with even more 
  > minute spines. The wider fields in FE. /atidens are com- 
  > posed of groups (double, triple, quadruple) spines, while 
  > the wider fields in E. spissus are covered with singleton 
  > spines. These two species are among the largest in the 
  > genus (soldier head widths 1.55—1.77 mm).

  说明：肠瓣（EVA）比较段：三个指状垫、80-100 枚刺、刺成组；两物种合计兵蚁头宽 1.55-1.77 mm；支撑条目「其他」。
