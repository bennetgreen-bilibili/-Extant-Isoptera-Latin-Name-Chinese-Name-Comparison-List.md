  > The new genus is distinguished from other morphologically related genera by having an extremely elongated first proctodeal segment, forming a spiral in the distal region of the abdomen, completing a full turn along the body axis before connecting to P3 on the right side, in ventral view (Figure 2C).

  说明：支撑：属级诊断——P1 极长螺旋。

  > Furthermore, the new genus has an unarmed enteric valve (Figure 2G,H), a characteristic also observed in genera such as Mangolditermes and Tucupitermes. However, none of these genera have spines directed toward P1 (Figure 2G,H).

  说明：支撑：鉴别要点——无刺肠瓣 + 棘朝向 P1。

- **p. 4**：

  > Head capsule light yellow with many short and sparse setae distributed throughout the length of the head capsule (Figure 1A,B). Fontanelle conspicuous, oval in shape, and white (Figure 1B). Postclypeus appears slightly inflated, with only a few short setae (Figure 1A,B).

  说明：支撑：工蚁头部外部形态。

- **p. 4**：

  > Labrum convex, with some setae that are thicker than those of the head capsule (Figure 1B). Antenna with 13 antennomeres, with antennomere I>II, III < others (Figure 1A,B).

  说明：支撑：上唇、触角 13 节。

- **p. 4**：

  > Left mandible with apical tooth slightly shorter than M1+2 (Figure 1C); typical Neotropical Apicotermitinae incision pronounced between M1+2 and M3; tooth M3 not very prominent; molar process completely occluded by the molar prominence, which projects to the same length as M1 + 2 (Figure 1C).

  说明：支撑：左颚齿列与臼齿突。

- **p. 5**：

  > P1 extremely elongated and visible in all four perspectives (Figure 2A-D), starting on the left side of the body and forming a pronounced S-shape in the left lateral view (Figure 2D). P1 very elongated, sinuous in left lateral view (Figure 2D), forming a complete loop ventrally.

  说明：支撑：消化管 P1 走向。

- **p. 5**：

  > It is inserted into P3 on a well-marked enteric valve seating (Figure 2F). Enteric valve seating unlobed and funnel-shaped (Figure 2C,F).

  说明：支撑：肠瓣座不分叶、漏斗状。

- **p. 5**：

  > Enteric valve unarmed, with six symmetrical lobes, each ornamented with spines in the proximal median region and oriented toward P1 (Figure 2G,H). The size of the spines gradually decreases as they extend away from the apex of the enteric valve (Figure 2G,H).

  说明：支撑：肠瓣无刺、六瓣、棘朝向 P1。
