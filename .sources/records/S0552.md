- **p. 155, soldier**：

  > Labrum large, wide at base, slightly rounded on the sides to the broadly rounded apex, which hardly covers the mandibular teeth, bearing about 14 long reddish setae. Mandibles very long, falciform, broad at the base, each with a large tooth in line with the apex of the labrum. Gula at narrowest part about one fourth the width of head.
