# S0078 A new termite (Isoptera, Termitidae, Syntermitinae, Macuxitermes) from Colombia

access: Europe PMC JATS 全文；属级描述明言系改写自 Cancello & Bandeira (1992) 原描述。license: CC BY 4.0。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description**：

  > The genus Macuxitermes was erected for a single species, Macuxitermes triceratops Cancello & Bandeira, 1992.

  说明：支撑：属为单种属建立，后增补 M. colombicus。

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description**：

  > Soldier. Dimorphic or monomorphic.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁二型或不二型。

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description**：

  > Major soldier. Head capsule evenly rounded or with a dorsal elevation and anterior protuberance either side of the midline, entire surface covered with minute granulations.

  说明：支撑：主兵头囊均匀圆或具背隆起及中线两侧前突，表面满布微颗粒。

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description**：

  > Anterior of head capsule narrows to form a robust conical nasus whose apex extends far beyond tips of mandibles

  说明：支撑：头前部收狭为粗壮锥形象鼻，鼻端远超上颚末端。

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description**：

  > mandibles curved strongly inwards, apices directed laterally at rest and not upturned, each mandible with a pointed marginal tooth half-way along inner surface

  说明：支撑：上颚强内弯、静止时末端指向两侧且不上翘，各具一半位尖缘齿。

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description › Worker, Addenda**：

  > enteric valve narrow, on the left posterior side of the abdomen, with three finger-like cushions each bearing two rows of regularly spaced long, narrow, straight or slightly curved spines of equal length throughout

  说明：支撑：工蚁肠瓣窄、位于腹部左后侧，三指状垫各具两列等长规则长刺。

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description › Major soldier**：

  > antennae yellow with 15 articles, I largest and III shortest; rows of dark short, tooth-like spines along the margins of the thoracic nota

  说明：支撑：兵蚁触角黄色、15 节（第 I 节最长、第 III 节最短），胸部背板边缘具成列深色短齿状刺。

- **Taxonomic treatment › Macuxitermes › Description › Worker, Addenda**：

  > digestive tube showing complete dorsal torsion and with small crop, long and inflated mixed segment that is proximally narrow and broadly oval distally, very large and voluminous P1

  说明：支撑：工蚁消化道完全背向扭转，嗉囊小、混合段长而膨大，P1 极大而饱满。
