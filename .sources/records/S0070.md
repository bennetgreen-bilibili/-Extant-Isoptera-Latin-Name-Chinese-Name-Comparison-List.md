# S0070 Tonsuritermes, a new soldierless termite genus and two new species from South America (Blattaria: Isoptera: Termitidae: Apicotermitinae)

为 1 个属（奇囟白蚁属）提供属级形态文本；本记录逐字摘录合计 880 字符。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **pp. 384–387，「Diagnosis」段**：

  > Diagnosis. Worker and imago. Fontanelle very large in dorsal view, ranging from 1/4 to 3/4 the diameter of the head capsule ( Figs. 1B ; 2A, C, E ); two rows of spine-like bristles on the inner face of the protibia

  说明：支撑：囟极大（1/4–3/4）+ 前足胫节两列刺状刚毛——鉴别要点。

- **pp. 384–387，「Worker」段**：

  > Mono- or dimorphic ( Figs. 2 ; 3 B–D, H, 4, 5). Head capsule flattened dorsoventrally in profile, rounded in dorsal view. Fontanelle margins well delineated, depressed within vertex, occupying 1/4 to 3/4 of the head capsule ( Fig. 2A,C, E , see histological discussion below). Antenna with 14 articles. Postclypeus moderately to highly inflated.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头形、囟缘与大小、触角 14、后唇基膨大——工蚁节。

- **pp. 384–387，「Imago」段**：

  > Head capsule trapezoidal in dorsal view, flattened dorsoventrally in profile ( Fig. 1A, B ). Two frontal marks located in dorsal view between the postclypeus and ocellus, above the antenna insertion ( Fig. 1B , arrows), larger than the ocelli, and two smaller triangular marks between the frontal marks and the fontanelle

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫头背视梯形；额斑位于后唇基与单眼之间、触角着生处上方，大于单眼；额斑与囟之间另有较小的三角斑——有翅成虫节。

- **Etymology 段（Plazi 存档）**：

  > In reference to the character Friar Tuck, the supposed Franciscan monk of the legend of Robin Hood.

  说明：支撑：tucki 纪念罗宾汉传说人物 Friar Tuck
