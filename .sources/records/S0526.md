- **pp. 26–27**：

  > NASUTITERMES (NASUTITERMES) ADUNCUS, new species
  > 
  > Soldier (pl. 2, figs. 1-2)—Head light castaneous-brown, smooth, shining, in prefile nearly a straight line, slightly convex at base of nasus and depression on top of head, flat, dividing into two bulging cheeks or lobes at median line, sides of head slightly asymmetrical, two rows of long hairs, one on raised area at base of nasus, the other near posterior margin, head broadly pear-shaped. Mandibles with elongate sharp points.
  > 
  > Nasus dark castaneous-brown at apex, elongate, conical and robust, apex turned down, aquiline, with long hairs.
