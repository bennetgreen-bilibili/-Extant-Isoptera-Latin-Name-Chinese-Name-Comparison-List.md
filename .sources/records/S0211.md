- **Abstract / full.txt**：

  > Under the term of sociotomy, (or fission of the society) we describe a curious social behaviour of two African Termites: Anoplotermes sp. and Trinervitermes bettonianus.

  说明：支撑草稿：把两种非洲白蚁（Anoplotermes sp. 与 T. bettonianus）的群体分裂称为 sociotomy。

- **Abstract / full.txt**：

  > Crowded columns, one including the royal couple, emerge in broad daylight and crawl away to various distances. They represent only a small part of the society to which they belong.

  说明：支撑草稿：队伍在白天离巢、含王室配偶，且只占原群体的一小部分。

- **Abstract / full.txt**：

  > After having covered a greater or lesser distance, each column breaks into several portions, each of which remains alone, scoops out (Anoplotermes) or builds (Trinervitermes) a nest which becomes the foundation of a new society.

  说明：支撑草稿：每列分裂为若干小群，各自挖出（Anoplotermes）或建造（Trinervitermes）新巢作为新群体起点。

- **Abstract / full.txt**：

  > Substitution reproductives are obtained by means of neoteny which is very frequent in the two species in which sociotomy has been observed. All the castes are represented in the migration, including the functional reproductives and the winged individuals.

  说明：支撑草稿：补充生殖蚁通过幼态持续频繁产生；迁徙队伍涵盖所有品级包括具功能生殖蚁与有翅个体。

- **Abstract / full.txt**：

  > The winged individuals, which normally only issue from the nest to undertake their swarming flight have their genetic behaviour abolished and can be seen creeping on the surface of the ground carrying larvae. The Anoplotermes workers can even acquire the behaviour of soldiers.

  说明：支撑草稿：有翅个体在地面爬行并搬运幼虫；解甲白蚁属工蚁甚至表现出兵蚁行为。

- **Abstract / full.txt**：

  > For the first time, the usefulness of the nasuti in defending the nest is firmly established, and so is the importance of the organization of the group in the defence against the assailants.

  说明：支撑草稿：作者首次以确凿观察确认象鼻兵蚁（nasuti）在防御中的作用。
