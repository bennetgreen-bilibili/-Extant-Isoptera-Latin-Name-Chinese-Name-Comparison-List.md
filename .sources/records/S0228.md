  > This is a taxonomic revision of the Neotropical genus Orthognathotermes Holmgren, 1910 (Termitidae, Termitinae), previously with nine species: O. aduncus, O. brevipilosus, O. gibberorum, O. heberi, O. humilis, O. insignis, O. macrocephalus, O. orthognathus and O. wheeleri. We redescribe these species and describe six new species: O. longilamina sp. nov., O. mirim sp. nov., O. okeyma sp. nov., O.

  说明：支撑：属页：新热带区、修订前 9 种；种页：6 个新种与 wheeleri/heberi 有翅成虫的首次描述

- **p. 6 / treatment 6213679**：

  > Soldier. Head sides parallel in dorsal view. In profile dorsal lump salient, behind the flanges. Mandibles of the first pattern, tooth-like projection at proximal half.

  说明：Zootaxa 2280 修订的 Zenodo/Plazi treatment 含 Imago 与 Soldier 描述

- **p. 6 / treatment 6213681**：

  > O. brevipilosus soldier has mandibles of the second pattern, a head capsule shorter than O. gibberorum, O. heberi, O. insignis, O. orthognathus and O. uncimandibularis sp. nov., and differs from all other species by the much denser short hairs only on the ventral surface of head, without bristles.

  说明：treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 7 / treatment 6213683**：

  > O. gibberorum soldier differs from all other species (of the same mandibles pattern) by a denser layer of short hairs on entire head capsule.

  说明：treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 8 / treatment 6213685**：

  > O. heberi soldier is larger than O. brevipilosus, O. longilamina sp. nov., O. macrocephalus, O. okeyma sp. nov. and has parallel head capsule sides (slightly convergent in front in O. insignis and O. uncimandibularis sp. nov., both most similar species).

  说明：treatment 含 Imago 与 Soldier 描述

- **p. 9 / treatment 6213687**：

  > O. humilis soldier has a head capsule larger than all other species of the same mandible pattern.

  说明：treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 9 / treatment 6213689**：

  > The imago of O. insignis differs from those of the other species by the combination of subtriangular ocelli in lateral view and large fontanelle.

  说明：treatment 含 Imago 与 Soldier 描述

- **p. 10 / treatment 6213691**：

  > O. longilamina sp. nov. soldier has mandibles of the second pattern, a head capsule smaller than O. gibberorum, O. heberi, O. insignis, O. okeyma sp. nov., O. orthognathus, O. uncimandibularis sp. nov. and differs form all other species by the distal long slender cutting edge on inner margin of mandible.

  说明：treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 11 / treatment 6213695**：

  > O. macrocephalus soldier has a head capsule smaller than all other species with the same mandible pattern.

  说明：treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 12 / treatment 6213697**：

  > O. mirim sp. nov. soldier has a head capsule smaller than all other species of the same mandible pattern and differs from all by the labrum without lobes.

  说明：新种，treatment 含 Imago 与 Soldier 描述

- **p. 12 / treatment 6213699**：

  > O. okeyma sp. nov. soldier has robust mandibles, a head capsule smaller than O. heberi, O. insignis, O. orthognathus, O. gibberorum, O. uncimandibularis and parallel head capsule sides (slightly convergent in front in O. brevipilosus and O. longilamina sp. nov.).

  说明：新种，treatment 含 Imago 与 Soldier 描述

- **p. 13 / treatment 6213701**：

  > O. orthognathus soldier has robust mandibles, a head capsule larger than O. brevipilosus, O. longilamina sp. nov., O. macrocephalus, O. okeyma sp. nov., and parallel head capsule sides (slightly convergent in front in O. insignis and O. uncimandibularis sp. nov.).

  说明：treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 14 / treatment 6213703**：

  > O. pilosus sp. nov. differs from all other species with the same mandible patterns by the head capsule covered with denser short hairs.

  说明：新种，treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 14 / treatment 6213705**：

  > O. tubesauassu sp. nov. soldier has a head capsule larger than O. mirim sp. nov., O. pilosus sp. nov., and smaller than O. humilis. The most similar species is O. aduncus whose flanges do not have a slight constriction at the base.

  说明：新种，treatment 含 Imago 与 Soldier 描述

- **p. 14 / treatment 6213707**：

  > O. uncimandibularis sp. nov. soldier differs from all other species by the pronounced terminally curved tip of left mandible, like a hook.

  说明：新种，treatment 含 Soldier 描述，Imago 原文记为 Unknown

- **p. 15 / treatment 6213709**：

  > O. wheeleri soldier differs from all other Orthognathotermes species by the visible ocellar spots.

  说明：treatment 含 Imago 与 Soldier 描述
