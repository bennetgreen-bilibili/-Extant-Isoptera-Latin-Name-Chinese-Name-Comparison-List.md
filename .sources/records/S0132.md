# S0132 The neotropical genera Procornitermes and Cornitermes (Isoptera, Termitidae)

agent-collected; quote verbatim from the archived full text.

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Bull. AMNH 99:479-539, romani 词源段**：

  > This species is named in honor of Dr. A. Roman of the Naturhistoriska Riksmuseum, Stockholm, Sweden, a specialist on the Hymenoptera, who took time on his expedition to Amazonas and Bahia to make an important collection of the termites

  说明：支撑：romani 纪念 Dr. A. Roman

- **p. 492**：

  > Soldier (figs. 3, 7, 8): Tergites a little paler yellow than the head and pronotum; abdomen often gray from the contents of the intestines. Head, pronotum, and tergites with scattered bristles, rather sparse on the head; a few short, almost invisible, hairs on the head and postmentum.

  说明：支撑：Procornitermes araujoi 兵蚁体色与毛被的原始描述

- **p. 494**：

  > Comparisons: Differs from all other described species of this genus in the three sharply pointed projections of the labrum (fig. 1A). Closest to P. araujoi, from which it differs in the sharper side points of the labrum, the straighter and more parallel sides of the head, the somewhat more concave sides of the postmentum, and the proportionately shorter hind tibia.

  说明：支撑：Procornitermes triacifer 兵蚁的鉴别特征

- **p. 496**：

  > Comparisons: This species seems to be the only one (except for one specimen of P. araujoi) with three apical spurs on the fore tibia of both imago and soldier. This is a primitive character compared to the 2:2:2 arrangement in all the other species (one exception already noted). P. striatus is also the only species with 15 articles in the antenna of the imago and 14 articles in the antenna of the soldier.

  说明：支撑：Procornitermes striatus 胫距式与触角节数的鉴别特征

- **p. 498**：

  > Head somewhat rectangular, the sides fairly straight and parallel. Frontal tube proportionately long, reaching the front of the postclypeus from above. Postmentum with the narrow portion about half the width of the widest portion (0.28-0.30:0.58-0.63 mm.).

  说明：支撑：Procornitermes romani 兵蚁头部与后颏的原始描述与量度

- **p. 500**：

  > Comparisons: Procornitermes cornutus has the angle of the notch of the right mandible greater than a right angle (fig. 2C), and the sides of the head are more angular. P. romani has the sides of the head straighter and more parallel (fig. 10), and the head is thicker (1.62-1.66 mm.).

  说明：支撑：Procornitermes lespesii 兵蚁与近缘种的鉴别

- **pp. 511—515**：

  > Soldier (figs. 3, 19-22): Head yellowish, the pronotum a little lighter than the head; tergites a little lighter than the pronotum; intestinal contents often producing a dirt color. Head sparsely covered with bristles and numerous fairly long hairs about half of the length of the bristles not, however, forming a contrasting mat of short hair;

  说明：支撑：兵蚁体色、毛被与头形描述

- **pp. 519—521**：

  > Comparisons: Cornitermes snyderi differs from other described imagoes in the genus in the vertical position of the ocellus and the small white portion of the ocellus visible from above.

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫的鉴别特征（单眼位置）

- **pp. 521—523**：

  > Comparisons: The soldiers from the type colony from Lago Rogagua have a more bulging postmentum in profile, and the frontal tube is not so curved on the under side as in the paratype soldiers from Cachuela Esperanza.

  说明：支撑：两产地兵蚁后颏与额管的形态差异

- **pp. 523—524**：

  > Imago: Described by Silvestri, but not available for study by the author.

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫描述缺失（Silvestri 描述过，本文作者未获标本）

- **pp. 524—525**：

  > Soldier: Head, pronotum, and abdomen yellowish; pronotum a little paler than head; abdomen with grayish appearance from gut contents. Head sparsely covered with bristles, no short hair visible;

  说明：支撑：兵蚁体色与毛被

- **pp. 525—526**：

  > Comparisons: Although the cotype soldiers of C. acignathus, subsp. costaricensis Snyder, from Hamburg Farm, Costa Rica, are somewhat larger than the soldiers from numerous colonies of C. walkeri from Panama and one colony from Estrella, Costa Rica, the differences are insufficient in the author’s opinion to warrant the separation of two subspecies.

  说明：支撑：costaricensis 亚种并入本种的处理

- **pp. 526—529**：

  > Comparisons: There is a considerable variation in the mean size of the soldiers from different colonies. For example, the head width of 35 specimens from various Guiana localities (except Itabu Creek) averaged 2.59 mm., 23 specimens from Itabu Creek, British Guiana, averaged 3.07 mm., six specimens from Lago Poraquequare, Manaos, Brazil, averaged 3.12 mm., five specimens from Cachuela Esperanza, Bolivia, averaged 2.85 mm.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头宽的居群间变异（2.59—3.12 mm）

- **p. 530**：

  > Comparisons: The arrangement of the bristles and hairs on the head distinguishes this species from all others in the genus.

  说明：支撑：毛被排列为本种唯一鉴别特征

- **pp. 530—531**：

  > Comparisons: The lateral angles of the labrum are sharper in C. weberi than in any other described soldier with a dense mat of short hair on the head.

  说明：支撑：上唇侧角为本种鉴别特征

- **pp. 531—532**：

  > Comparisons: All other species of the genus with a mat of short hair covering the head have straighter and more parallel sides of the head, with a sharper curve joining the sides and the hind margin.

  说明：支撑：头形与其他具短毛毯种的对比

- **pp. 532—534**：

  > Comparisons: Close to C. bequaerti (fig. 32), but C. villosus has a more dense mat of short hair on the head, the sides of the head converge more towards the front, and the front margin of the pronotum is more distinctly emarginate.

  说明：支撑：与 C. bequaerti 的鉴别

- **pp. 534—535**：

  > Comparisons: Comitermes incisus (fig. 29) is close, but the sides and hind angles of the head are rounder, the pronotum is more deeply emarginate in front, the frontal tube is thicker at the base, and the notch of the left mandible is closer to the tip of the mandible.

  说明：支撑：与 C. incisus 的鉴别（原文印作 Comitermes）

- **pp. 535—536**：

  > Comparisons: A single colony from Piraputanga, Mato Grosso, Brazil, contained one soldier of normal size and a number of markedly smaller soldiers. About a third of the measurements in table 28 were taken from these small soldiers, so that the averages are less than would otherwise have been the case.

  说明：支撑：同巢大、小兵蚁并存及对量度均值的影响

- **pp. 536—537**：

  > Comparisons: Closest to C. villosus from which it differs in the wider angle of the notch of the right mandible, and in the smaller size.

  说明：支撑：与 C. villosus 的鉴别
