# S0012 Laboratory culturing of some Indian wood destroying termites

只读到摘要页（IndianJournals 记录页），正文未读；原文期刊为 Journal of Entomological Research 20(1): 73–82。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **摘要**：

  > Mass culturing of wood inhabiting termites, such as Bifiditermes beesoni (Gardner), Neotetmes bosei Snyder (Kalotermitidae) and Stylotermes spp. (Stylotermitidae) is easier as compared to subterranean species. The attacked portions with live colonies and sufficient portion of sound wood were cut, and kept in iron or cement containers under controlled laboratory conditions (28±1°C, 80–90% R.H.).
