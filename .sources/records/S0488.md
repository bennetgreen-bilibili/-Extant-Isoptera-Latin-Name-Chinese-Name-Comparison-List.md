# S0488 Australian Termitidae. Part III

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 745**：

  > Eutermes Triodiae, n.sp. (Plate XXXV., fig. 8.) Upper surface of the head, thorax, scapular, and the abdominal plates castaneous; the rest pale ochreous; wings fuscous, the nervures darker.

  说明：支撑本种有翅成虫体色与翅色

- **p. 746**：

  > Soldier. — Head dark castaneous-brown, darkest on the sides and the base of the snout, the tip reddish-brown

  说明：支撑兵蚁头壳与鼻突颜色

- **p. 746**：

  > Worker. — Head pale yellowish-brown, with the centre of the upper surface dark brown, divided into two rounded patches by a pale yellow suture down the centre

  说明：支撑工蚁头部色斑
