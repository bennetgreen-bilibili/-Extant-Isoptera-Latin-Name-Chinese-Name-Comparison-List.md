# S0068 Updated taxonomy of Syntermitinae (Blattodea: Isoptera, Termitidae), with the description of three new genera

为 3 个属（拜拉白蚁属、班代拉白蚁属、瓦宁白蚁属）提供属级形态文本；本记录逐字摘录合计 1434 字符。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 447–449，属级 Description「Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae」段**：

  > All three genera workers have pulvilli ornamented with conspicuous spines ( Fig. 2a ), a character absent in all other members of Syntermitinae

  说明：支撑：Syntermitinae 中仅 Biratermes、Mapinguaritermes、Ibitermes 三属工蚁垫具显著刺——本属鉴别要点。

- **p. 447–449，属级 Description「Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae」段**：

  > In the new genera the soldier mandibles are bladelike and robust, and the forecoxae have a smooth surface

  说明：支撑：原文对三个新属的共同兵蚁特征：上颚刀状粗壮、前基节表面光滑，而 Embiratermes s.s. 为具尖端穿刺型上颚、前基节腹缘有突起——兵蚁节（本属兵蚁外部形态本身沿用 Constantino 1992）。

- **p. 447–449，「Digestive tube」段**：

  > Cuticular armature of gizzard with 24 visible folds, six of these first-order, six second-order and 12 third-order; ratio between columnar and pulvillar belts approximately equal to one; pulvillus I (major) ornamented with conspicuous spines

  说明：支撑：砂囊 24 褶（6/6/12）、柱状带与垫状带近等、第一级垫具显著刺——工蚁节。

- **p. 456–458，「Digestive tube」段**：

  > Enteric valve (P2) composed of three nearly equal conical ridges, covered with curved spines ( Fig. 14b ), [ Constantino (1990) fig. 12)]. Dorsal torsion well developed. Third proctodeal segment joined P4 by an isthmus is inserted subapically

  说明：支撑：P2 三锥形脊覆弯刺、峡部近端插入——鉴别要点。

- **p. 456–458，「Soldier」段**：

  > Head elongate, rectangular or sub-rectangular, narrowing slightly towards front with lateral and posterior margins slightly convex and posterolateral angles broadly rounded

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头长方形、后侧角宽圆——兵蚁节。

- **p. 456–458，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker . Head capsule rounded. Postclypeus moderately inflated or more inflated. Antenna with 14 articles.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头圆、后唇基中等至较强膨大、触角 14 节——工蚁节。

- **p. 450–451，「Digestive tube」段**：

  > Enteric valve (P2) composed of three nearly equal ridges covered with thin curved spines ( Figs 6b , 11b ). Dorsal torsion well-developed. P3 joined to P4 by an isthmus is inserted subapically

  说明：支撑：P2 三脊覆细弯刺、峡部近端插入——鉴别要点。

- **p. 450–451，「Soldier」段**：

  > Head sub-rectangular, with lateral and posterior margins of varying convexity, rounded posterolateral angles; moderate-sized conical nasus

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头近长方形、鼻圆锥形中等——兵蚁节。

- **p. 450–451，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker . Head capsule rounded. Postclypeus short and inflated. Antennae with 13 or 14 articles.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头圆、后唇基短而膨大、触角 13 或 14 节——工蚁节。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > Soldiers of Cahuallitermes Constantino have a rounded head, a well-developed labrum tip, and a postmentum with convex lateral margins

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头圆、上唇端部发达、后颏侧缘凸。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > In the genera Cyrilliotermes Fontes and Curvitermes Holmgren the soldiers have peculiar mandibles, with a molar plate, molar prominence, and marginal teeth quite similar to the mandibles of their corresponding workers.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚“异常”，具磨板、磨突与缘齿，形似同名工蚁的上颚。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > In addition, the apical tooth is hook-shaped in Curvitermes

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚端齿呈鱼钩状。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > and cylindrical and elongated frontal tube in the Cyrilliotermes soldier

  说明：支撑：兵蚁额管圆柱状且延长。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > Embiratermes s.s. species have piercing mandibles with a sharp point, and a projection in the ventral margin of the forecoxae.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚穿刺型、末端锐尖；前足基节腹缘具突起。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > the mesenteric tongue is straight and does not reach the dilated portion of the P1

  说明：支撑：工蚁中肠舌直、不达 P1 膨大部。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Bandeiratermes 属征比较）**：

  > short and broad, at the middle of the abdomen length in Embiratermes s.s.

  说明：支撑：肠瓣段短而宽，位于腹部中点。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Bandeiratermes 属征比较）**：

  > The soldiers of Embiratermes s.s. have piercing mandibles with marginal teeth at the proximal third of the mandible length

  说明：支撑：兵蚁缘齿位于上颚长度近端 1/3 处。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Bandeiratermes 属征比较）**：

  > The soldiers of Ibitermes s.s. have a markedly inflated postclypeus (absent in the new genus).

  说明：支撑：兵蚁后唇基显著膨大。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > Ibitermes s.s. has a markedly inflated postclypeus and the mandibles lack marginal teeth

  说明：支撑：兵蚁后唇基显著膨大、上颚无缘齿。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Bandeiratermes 属征比较）**：

  > The worker gut of Ibitermes s.s. have a long and twisted mesenteric tongue (short and rounded in Bandeiratermes gen. n.), and the enteric valve segment is dorsally oriented

  说明：支撑：工蚁中肠舌长而扭曲，肠瓣段背向。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > Noirotitermes Cancello & Myles are smaller with piercing mandibles

  说明：支撑：兵蚁体型较小、上颚穿刺型。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > that of Noirotitermes with a remarkably distinctive shape to the head capsule. In both of the latter genera, there is a characteristic microsculpture present on the surface of the head capsule.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头囊形状极为独特，且头表具特征性微 sculpturing。

- **Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae（Biratermes 属征比较）**：

  > Soldiers of Paracurvitermes Constantino & Carvalho are smaller, and have a well-developed conical and shorter frontal tube than Biratermes ; and the mandibles are distinctly curved.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁体型较小，额管发达、圆锥形且较短，上颚明显弯曲。
