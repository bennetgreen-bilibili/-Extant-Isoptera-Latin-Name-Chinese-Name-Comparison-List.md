# S0068 Updated taxonomy of Syntermitinae (Blattodea: Isoptera, Termitidae), with the description of three new genera

为 3 个属（拜拉白蚁属、班代拉白蚁属、瓦宁白蚁属）提供属级形态文本；本记录逐字摘录合计 1434 字符。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 447–449，属级 Description「Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae」段**：

  > All three genera workers have pulvilli ornamented with conspicuous spines ( Fig. 2a ), a character absent in all other members of Syntermitinae

  说明：支撑：Syntermitinae 中仅 Biratermes、Mapinguaritermes、Ibitermes 三属工蚁垫具显著刺——本属鉴别要点。

- **p. 447–449，属级 Description「Comparisons with other genera of Syntermitinae」段**：

  > In the new genera the soldier mandibles are bladelike and robust, and the forecoxae have a smooth surface

  说明：支撑：原文对三个新属的共同兵蚁特征：上颚刀状粗壮、前基节表面光滑，而 Embiratermes s.s. 为具尖端穿刺型上颚、前基节腹缘有突起——兵蚁节（本属兵蚁外部形态本身沿用 Constantino 1992）。

- **p. 447–449，「Digestive tube」段**：

  > Cuticular armature of gizzard with 24 visible folds, six of these first-order, six second-order and 12 third-order; ratio between columnar and pulvillar belts approximately equal to one; pulvillus I (major) ornamented with conspicuous spines

  说明：支撑：砂囊 24 褶（6/6/12）、柱状带与垫状带近等、第一级垫具显著刺——工蚁节。

- **p. 456–458，「Digestive tube」段**：

  > Enteric valve (P2) composed of three nearly equal conical ridges, covered with curved spines ( Fig. 14b ), [ Constantino (1990) fig. 12)]. Dorsal torsion well developed. Third proctodeal segment joined P4 by an isthmus is inserted subapically

  说明：支撑：P2 三锥形脊覆弯刺、峡部近端插入——鉴别要点。

- **p. 456–458，「Soldier」段**：

  > Head elongate, rectangular or sub-rectangular, narrowing slightly towards front with lateral and posterior margins slightly convex and posterolateral angles broadly rounded

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头长方形、后侧角宽圆——兵蚁节。

- **p. 456–458，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker . Head capsule rounded. Postclypeus moderately inflated or more inflated. Antenna with 14 articles.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头圆、后唇基中等至较强膨大、触角 14 节——工蚁节。

- **p. 450–451，「Digestive tube」段**：

  > Enteric valve (P2) composed of three nearly equal ridges covered with thin curved spines ( Figs 6b , 11b ). Dorsal torsion well-developed. P3 joined to P4 by an isthmus is inserted subapically

  说明：支撑：P2 三脊覆细弯刺、峡部近端插入——鉴别要点。

- **p. 450–451，「Soldier」段**：

  > Head sub-rectangular, with lateral and posterior margins of varying convexity, rounded posterolateral angles; moderate-sized conical nasus

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头近长方形、鼻圆锥形中等——兵蚁节。

- **p. 450–451，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker . Head capsule rounded. Postclypeus short and inflated. Antennae with 13 or 14 articles.

  说明：支撑：工蚁头圆、后唇基短而膨大、触角 13 或 14 节——工蚁节。
