## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 359（Genus Tiunatermes gen. nov., Description, Soldier）**：

  > Soldier (Figs 1–7). Monomorphic. In dorsal view, head capsule broad, not constricted. Nasus conical, with a broad base. Mandibles with a conspicuous outer margin, not forming a well defined 'point'. Antenna with 13 articles.

  说明：兵蚁（单型、头壳宽阔、鼻锥形、内颚外缘明显、触角 13 节）

- **p. 359（Comparisons）**：

  > Tiunatermes differs from Anhangatermes by the former's conical nasus; procoxa without a hump on the anterior surface; well sclerotized abdominal sclerites; and imbricate tergites.

  说明：鉴别要点（与 Anhangatermes 的四条区别）

- **p. 359（Description, Worker）**：

  > Head capsule in dorsal view subtrapezoidal with posterior margin rounded; fontanelle oval, situated at posterior third of the head capsule in dorsal view. Surface of the head capsule not depressed at the fontanelle level in profile view. Postclypeus well curved in profile view. Antenna with 14 articles.

  说明：工蚁（头壳近梯形、囟位置、后唇基、触角 14 节）
