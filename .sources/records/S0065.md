# S0065 Gastrotermes spinatus gen. n. sp. n., an African soil-feeding termite described from the worker caste (Isoptera, Termitidae, Apicotermitidae)

PDF 自 mapress.com 公开下载；许可未标注。属级描述与 Diagnosis 同在 p. 291。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 291, Description**：

  > Description. Imago. Unknown. Soldier. Unknown, but may exist. Worker. Head, pronotum, and abdomen covered with hundreds of long or very long hairs

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫未知、兵蚁未知但可能存在；工蚁头、前胸背板与腹部密被数百根长至很长的毛。

- **p. 291, Diagnosis**：

  > An absence of a mixed segment and the presence of both a very short P1 and a voluminous trilobite enteric valve seating places Gastrotermes in the Apicotermes group (sensu Noirot 2001).

  说明：支撑：无 mixed segment、P1 很短、EVS 大而三叶，归入 Apicotermes 群。

- **p. 291, Diagnosis（与 Labidotermes 亚群对比）**：

  > Also, genera within the Labidotermes subgroup lack P3a spines (as in L. celisi Deligne and Pasteels, Fig. 4C) while in Gastrotermes, the EVS is lined with small erect spines and the opening of the P3a is lined with many robust spines.

  说明：支撑：该亚群其他属 P3a 无刺，而本属 EVS 内壁具小直立刺、P3a 开口列粗壮刺。
