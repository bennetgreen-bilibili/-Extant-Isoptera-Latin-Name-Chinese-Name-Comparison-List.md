- **Diagnosis**：

  > The soldier of R. tinto is the only non-phragmotic kalotermitid worldwide with dark coloration of the anterior head capsule. Aside from head color, it differs from congeneric soldiers in that the anterolateral corners of the frontal ridges project to form acute angles.
