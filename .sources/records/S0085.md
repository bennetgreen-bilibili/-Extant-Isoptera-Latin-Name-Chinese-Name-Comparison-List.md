# S0085 Caetetermes taquarussu, a new genus and species of Ecuadorian nasute (Isoptera, Termitidae, Nasutitermitinae)

Caetetermes 原始属级描述（Imago. Unknown）；工蚁一节据原文对大型工蚁（major worker）的描述。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **pp. 135-136**：

  > Head capsule sharply constricted (figs. 1-4) behind antennae, posterior region bulbous. Nose long, cylindrical and not specially enlarged at base. Postclypeus oblique, clearly visible in dorsal view (figs. 1-2). Mandibles (figs. 5-6) without points, with a distinct but not sharply defined thinner patch of cuticle at the apex.

  说明：林白蚁属（caetetermes）：支撑：「兵蚁」头壳急剧收缩与后部球状、鼻形、后唇基与上颚端部构造。

- **p. 136**：

  > Width of postclypeus/length of postclypeus 3.66-4.89. Left mandible (figs. 9, 12): index 0.49; A shorter than Ml + 2; posterior margin of A almost straight and shorter than the anterior margin of Ml+2; angle, between A and Ml+2 acute; cutting edge of Ml+2 sinuate, separated from M3 by a sharp notch; M3 developed, posterior margin convex;

  说明：林白蚁属（caetetermes）：支撑：「工蚁」后唇基比例、左颚指数、端齿与 M1+2、M3 的关系。

- **p. 136**：

  > Legs long but shorter than the corresponding soldier legs; anterior coxae with a weak hump outside corresponding to the prominent soldier hump

  说明：林白蚁属（caetetermes）：支撑：「工蚁」「鉴别要点」足长与前足基节外侧隆起。
