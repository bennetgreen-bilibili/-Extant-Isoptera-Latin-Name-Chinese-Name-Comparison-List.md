# S0053 Ekueitermes alerosinus, a new genus and species of soldierless termite from Colombia (Isoptera, Termitidae, Apicotermitinae)

soldierless。卷期行为 2026 年出版（收稿 2026-04-16，接受 2026-08-10）。imago 记为 Unknown。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Ekueitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis (ZooKeys 1292: 275–292)**：

  > Mixed segment with a short, but not inflated, mesenteric tongue

  说明：属级鉴别第一条

- **Ekueitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis**：

  > The cushions are armed at the distal end with 7–17 sclerotised spines arranged in a palmate shape

  说明：肠瓣刺数与排列

- **Ekueitermes gen. nov., Diagnosis**：

  > The enteric valve (EV) has a well-differentiated, tubular enteric valve seating

  说明：肠瓣 seating 形态

- **Etymology (species)**：

  > Etymology. The specific epithet alerosinus is after Alejandro Duran, Leofrey Duran, and Rosario Bautista (son and parents of the first author), whose names are combined in the root Alero, in recognition of their unconditional support and affection throughout his training as a researcher.

  说明：支撑：Ekueitermes alerosinus 种加词词源 = 人名合成 Alero

- **Etymology (genus)**：

  > Etymology. From the Uitoto word eeko (phonetically eekuei), meaning spine, refers to the spines located on the distal portion of the EV cushions in workers and honours the Uitoto indigenous community inhabiting the Colombian Amazon.

  说明：支撑：Ekueitermes 属名词源 = Uitoto 语 eeko 刺
