# S0485 Guide to the termites (Isoptera) from the extreme south-west of Western Australia

该指南只给检索表与简明属注，未给完整属征段；Hesperotermes 的属级信息仅此两条。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 82**：

  > Hesperotermes infrequens, the only species of its genus, is entirely south-western, extending north to Eneabba, east to Woorooloo and to near W.lliams, and south to the coast. The soldier is distinguished not only by its reduced frontal tubercle (Fig. 5a), but also by its reddish brown head capsule.

  说明：支撑：Hesperotermes 为单型属，兵蚁以额突退化与红褐色头壳鉴别

- **p. 74, Table 3 (soldier key to the Termes complex)**：

  > Frontal tubercle: reduced (5a) Hesperotermes infrequens (5a)

  说明：支撑：检索表中以额突退化直接检索到 Hesperotermes infrequens
