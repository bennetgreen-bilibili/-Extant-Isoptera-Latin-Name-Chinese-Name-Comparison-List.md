# S0081 New Genera of Termites Related to Subulitermes from the Oriental, Malagasy, and Australian Regions (Isoptera, Termitidae, Nasutitermitinae)

Aciculitermes、Ceylonitermellus、Australitermes 的原始属级描述。archive.org 扫描 OCR；引文照 OCR 文本，未作拼接。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 11**：

  > The soldier of Aciculitermes lacks points on the mandibles.

  说明：针白蚁属（aciculitermes）：支撑：「兵蚁」「鉴别要点」上颚无端尖这一建属性状。

- **p. 12**：

  > Head and frontal projection with many short microscopical hairs. Vertex and base of head projection with a few bristles. Head proportionately wider than that of Subulitermes microsoma. Profile of head from tip of projection to the vertex almost straight or slightly concave, with a slight hump at the base of the projection.

  说明：针白蚁属（aciculitermes）：支撑：「兵蚁」头与额突的微毛、与 Subulitermes microsoma 的头宽比较、头部侧轮廓。

- **p. 12**：

  > The left mandibular index is 0.73, while it is 0.56 for Leucopitermes and 1.33 for Subulitermes. The apical teeth of both mandibles are proportionately larger than in Leucopitermes (fig. 2), there is no notch immediately posterior to the first marginal tooth of the left mandible, and the third marginal tooth is narrower.

  说明：针白蚁属（aciculitermes）：支撑：「工蚁」左颚指数 0.73 及其与 Leucopitermes、Subulitermes 的对比，端齿比例、缺刻与第 3 缘齿宽度。

- **p. 24**：

  > Eye and ocellus large, the ocellus less than its width from the ocular suture and longer (0.15 mm.) than the diameter of the basal socket of the antenna (0.12 mm.). Fontanelle long and narrowly oval, without a distinctly forked tip. Postclypeus much longer than half of its width (length, 0.32 mm.; width, 0.50 mm.).

  说明：澳大利亚白蚁属（australitermes）：支撑：「有翅成虫」眼与单眼、囟、后唇基的性状与量度。

- **p. 24**：

  > Antenna with 15 articles, the third article longer than the fourth and the fourth longer than the second. Mandibles (fig. 11) with the highest left mandible index (1.54) of any of the Australian genera of the Subulitermes branch of the Nasutitermitinae. The closest Australian genus is Macrosubulitermes (fig. 9), with an index of 1.07.

  说明：澳大利亚白蚁属（australitermes）：支撑：「有翅成虫」触角节数与比例、左颚指数及其在澳大利亚支系中的位置。

- **p. 26**：

  > Head with a small constriction visible from both the top and side. The profile of the pronotum has a sharp angle close to a right angle between the front lobe and the basal portion.

  说明：澳大利亚白蚁属（australitermes）：支撑：「兵蚁」单型、头部小收缩与前胸背板侧轮廓的近直角。

- **p. 12**：

  > SOLDIER: The mandibles lack apical points.

  说明：小锡兰白蚁属（ceylonitermellus）：支撑：「兵蚁」唯一可引性状。

- **p. 13**：

  > The left mandible has a characteristically long first plus second marginal tooth. The left mandible index is 0.62.

  说明：小锡兰白蚁属（ceylonitermellus）：支撑：「工蚁」左颚第 1+2 缘齿特别长与指数 0.62。

- **p. 13**：

  > The third marginal tooth is proportionately small compared to that of Leucopitermes. The right mandible has a concave outline of the molar plate. The angles between the apical teeth and the first marginal teeth of both mandibles are wider than those of any other related genera in the Oriental region.

  说明：小锡兰白蚁属（ceylonitermellus）：支撑：「工蚁」「鉴别要点」第 3 缘齿小、右颚磨板凹、端齿与第 1 缘齿夹角最宽。
