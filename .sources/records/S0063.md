# S0063 Echinotermes biriba, a new genus and species of soldierless termite from the Colombian and Peruvian Amazon (Termitidae, Apicotermitinae)

JATS 全文（_tmp/lit/fulltext/07-noentry-a/echinotermes.xml）与出版社 PDF 互校；属级 Diagnosis 位于 p. 24。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 24, Diagnosis**：

  > Diagnosis. The crop of E. biriba is unusually large and the enteric valve armature, consisting of six spherical pectinate pads, is unique among all apicotermitine genera.

  说明：支撑：嗉囊异常大、肠瓣为 6 个球形栉状垫，在 Apicotermitinae 中独有。

- **pp. 23–24, Description of worker**：

  > Monomorphic. Head capsule and antennae a light yellowish colour; pronotum pale yellow; legs hyaline.

  说明：支撑：单型、头与触角淡黄、前胸背板淡黄、足透明。

- **p. 24, Description of worker (gut)**：

  > Enteric valve seating trilobed, with smaller lobe not visible in intact gut. Enteric valve with six cushions, terminating at the opening to the P3 as spiny spheroids (Fig. 2).

  说明：支撑：EVS 三叶且较小一叶在完整肠道中不可见；肠瓣 6 垫，末端呈带刺球状。
