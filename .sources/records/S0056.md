# S0056 Sandsitermes gen. nov., a new nasute termite genus from South America (Isoptera, Termitidae, Nasutitermitinae)

正文经 Plazi treatment（pp. 563–569）读取。属级 Description 含 Imago / Soldier / Worker 三节，工蚁二型在属级节内区分。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Genus Sandsitermes, Diagnosis (Zootaxa 4221: 563–569)**：

  > Imagoes with dorsal surface of head capsule with slight elevation anterior to fontanelle, where the ocelli are situated

  说明：有翅成虫头部隆起

- **Genus Sandsitermes, Diagnosis**：

  > Soldiers with long conical nasus, but no apparent uniquely distinct morphological character.

  说明：兵蚁无独有特征

- **Genus Sandsitermes, Diagnosis**：

  > Workers showing distinct set of morphological characters involving mandibles with concave molar area with no well-developed ridges

  说明：工蚁上颚与肠
