  > Termes (Eutermes) gracilirostris n. sp. Soldier (nasute). Length to the tip of the rostrum 3—3.5 mm. Head ochraceous, light yellowish-brown, about 1.5 mm. long, less than 0.9 mm. broad, rounded behind, narrowed in front, with long, slender rostrum which is in a plane with the front surface of the head. Antennæ of 12 segments … Worker. 3.5—4 mm. long. Head ochroleucous, rounded.
