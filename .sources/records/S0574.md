## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **Trinervitermes 属修订，T. saudiensis 一节**：

  > Posterior margin of head capsule behind eyes less than semi-circular, slightly sinuate immediately behind eyes; fontanelle distinct, Y-shaped, lateral arms long, reaching almost half-way to ocelli

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫头后缘与囟的 Y 形侧臂

- **Trinervitermes 属修订，T. togoensis 一节**：

  > fontanelle small, paler than head, short Y- or inverted A-shaped, sometimes with small depression in front or behind, or partly occluded posteriorly by darker cuticle, lateral arms often dilated terminally

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫囟的形状与侧臂端部

- **Trinervitermes 属修订，T. oeconomus 一节**：

  > Head capsule, central part of frons and vertex brown to dark chestnut brown; vertex with three diverging yellow-brown streaks; postclypeus, and area surrounding eyes, extending to a point behind eyes and to mandible base in front, yellow-brown

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫体色与头顶纵纹
