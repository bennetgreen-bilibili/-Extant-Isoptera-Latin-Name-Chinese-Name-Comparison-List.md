  > Left mandible strongly bent in middle, with base thick, its outer region with ridge and concavity; anterior region spatulate; apex blunt; and inner basal region with toothlike projection, fitting into corresponding excavation in right mandible.   说明：支撑：兵蚁左上颚形态、右上颚平直、触角 14 节。

  说明：支撑：工蚁左右上颚齿式。
