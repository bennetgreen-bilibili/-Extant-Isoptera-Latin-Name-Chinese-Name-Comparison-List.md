## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 317（Soldier，头与囟）**：

  > Head very flat, with sides converging toward front; posterior margin three-lobed, middle lobe not so prominent; fontanelle small and circular; and frontal gland absent.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头极扁、两侧前收、后缘三叶、囟小圆、额腺缺。

- **p. 318（Soldier，上颚与触角）**：

  > Left mandible strongly bent in middle, with base thick, its outer region with ridge and concavity; anterior region spatulate; apex blunt; and inner basal region with toothlike projection, fitting into corresponding excavation in right mandible. Right mandible flat and straight. Antennae with 14 articles.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁左上颚形态、右上颚平直、触角 14 节。

- **p. 318（Worker，上颚）**：

  > Left mandible with apical tooth small, posterior margin of fused first plus second marginal tooth long, third marginal tooth well developed. Right mandible with posterior margin of second marginal tooth straight and acute angle between first and second marginal teeth.

  说明：支撑：工蚁左右上颚齿式。

- **p. 274**：

  > Imago (fig. 2): Small, dark brown. Head densely hairy, with numerous stiff bristles interspersed with short hairs. Fontanelle distinct, narrow, elliptical. Eyes small.

  说明：支撑：Paracapritermes 有翅成虫形态

- **p. 274**：

  > Labrum with anterior margin almost straight or faintly angular, in some individuals faintly indented in middle, and anterolateral corners slightly pointed.

  说明：支撑：Paracapritermes 兵蚁上唇形态（与 Protocapritermes 共衍征）

- **p. 274**：

  > Tibial spurs 3:2:2. Middle tibia with one spine in primus

  说明：支撑：Paracapritermes 胫节端距式与中胫刺数（原文下接 two in kraepelinii，因跨行连字符截断未引入；转述见描述）
