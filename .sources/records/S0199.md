- **Materials and methods, caste percentages in foraging territories / full.txt**：

  > Foraging termites of H. tenuis were captured in underground cardboard traps over 12 months from three field colonies (A, B, and C). The colonies were located in Rio Claro (22° 23′ 43″ S, 47° 32′ 39″ W), Sao Paulo, Brazil.

  说明：支撑草稿：里奥克拉罗（22°23′43″S，47°32′39″W）3 个野外群体、历时 12 个月的诱捕监测。

- **Results / full.txt**：

  > The percentages of each caste found in the foraging territories of the field colonies of H. tenuis were not significantly different for the three colonies (ANOVA, F= 0.168, df= 2, P= 0.846) (Table 1). Workers were the most abundant caste in all colonies (ANOVA, F= 2489.81, df= 2, P= 0.0001)

  说明：支撑草稿：群体间品级比例无显著差异（F=0.168，df=2，P=0.846），且工蚁在各群体中都最多（F=2489.81）。

- **Materials and methods, monitoring foraging behavior / full.txt**：

  > Bioassays 1 and 2 were based on the same proportion of workers (95%) and soldiers (2% of major soldiers and 3% minor soldiers) found in the field colonies.

  说明：支撑草稿：野外取食个体中工蚁 95%、小型兵蚁 3%、大型兵蚁 2%。

- **Results / full.txt**：

  > Minor soldiers were the individuals that initiated foraging behavior most frequently in all bioassays (Table 2).

  说明：支撑草稿：实验室中小兵蚁是最常启动觅食的个体。

- **Results / full.txt**：

  > The results from bioassays 1, 2 and 3 show that the frequency of the castes in the foraging activity was dependent on the number of soldiers inside the test chambers (Test of independence; df= 6, G= 20.5092, P< 0.01)

  说明：支撑草稿：各品级参与觅食的频率取决于兵蚁数量（G=20.5092，df=6）。

- **Results / full.txt**：

  > On the other hand, the frequency of each caste in foraging activities was independent from the total number of termites inside the test chambers (Test of Independence, df= 2, G= 0.14619, P> 0.01).

  说明：支撑草稿：觅食频率与个体总数无关（G=0.14619，df=2）。
