## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 308**：

  > The genus Ahamitermes was established for a species the soldier caste of which was described as not having mandibular teeth. The characters of the winged adult were defined later from an allied species. An examination of yet another related form indicates that Ahamitermes is best regarded as a subgenus of Hamitermes.

  说明：支撑：无钩白蚁属的建立依据与 Hill 的亚属处置

- **p. 14（兵蚁分属检索表，第 7 对）**：

  > Mandibles not serrate .. Hamitermes subgenus Ahamitermes

  说明：支撑：与 Microcerotermes（上颚内缘细锯齿）的对立鉴别

- **p. 310（有翅成虫亚属检索表，第 2 对）**：

  > Apical segment of maxillary palp inflated .. Ahamitermes

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫上颚须末节膨大

- **p. 310（兵蚁亚属检索表，第 2 对）**：

  > Mandibular tooth wanting or vestigial ..... Ahamitermes

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚齿缺或退化

- **p. 309**：

  > Species of the subgenus Ahamitermes live exclusively in the nests of Coptotermes.

  说明：支撑：全部种类栖于 Coptotermes 巢内
