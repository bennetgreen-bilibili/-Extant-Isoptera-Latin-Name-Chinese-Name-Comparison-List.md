## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 47, Imago**：

  > Head capsule yellowish-brown to reddish-brown posteriorly dull yellow anteriorly, middle region with a dark V-shaped band across eyes behind frons

  说明：支撑：C. darwini 有翅成虫头部体色与 V 形带

- **p. 48, Soldier**：

  > dorsal and lateral surfaces very rugose, tuberculated. Frontal ridge prominent, rough, produced forward

  说明：支撑：C. darwini 兵蚁头背粗糙具瘤突、额脊前突

- **p. 54, Comparisons**：

  > In C. fatulus the frontal horn is a very short knob and the genal horn is scarcely developed

  说明：支撑：C. fatulus 兵蚁额角短小、颊角不发达

- **p. 54, Soldier**：

  > Head squarish narrowing slightly posteriorly, weakly to moderately rugose in black region

  说明：支撑：C. fatulus 兵蚁头形与黑区粗糙度

- **p. 54, 首行异名**：

  > Kalotermes (Cryptotermes) occidentalis Light, 1933: 103. Holotype imago, MAR/A MADRE ISLAND (Keifer) [not examined].

  说明：支撑：fatulus 原名 occidentalis Light 1933（ preoccupied），Light 1935 给新名；正模未检视
