- **Diagnosis（R. aridus）**：

  > The R. aridus soldier is much smaller than that of R. laticollis. The antennal carinae and anterolateral ridges of the R. aridus soldier are smoothly rounded, modestly sclerotized, and appear polished; while these structures in R. rufus and R. volcanensis are more angular, darkly sclerotized, and rugose.
