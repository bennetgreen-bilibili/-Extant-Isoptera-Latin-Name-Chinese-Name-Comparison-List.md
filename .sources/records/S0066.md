# S0066 Ebogotermes raphaeli, new genus and new species, an African soldierless termite described from the worker caste (Isoptera, Termitidae, Apicotermitinae)

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 279, Diagnosis**：

  > Diagnosis. Ebogotermes is the largest (Figs. 1, 2) of the soldierless apicotermitine workers in equatorial Africa.

  说明：支撑：为赤道非洲无兵蚁 apicotermitine 工蚁中体型最大者。

- **p. 279, Diagnosis**：

  > The circular mesenteron, well-developed mixed segment, moderately long P1, P3/P4 isthmus, and long tubular P4 place Ebogotermes in the Anoplotermes-group (Noirot, 2001).

  说明：支撑：中肠成环、mixed segment 发达、P1 中等长、P3/P4 缢部、P4 长管状，归入 Anoplotermes 群。

- **p. 279, Worker**：

  > Worker. Head, thorax, abdomen, and legs covered with setae of varying lengths. Antennae with 14 articles. Postclypeus moderately inflated. Fore tibia slightly inflated. Tibial spurs 3, 2, 2.

  说明：支撑：全身被长短不一刚毛、触角 14 节、后唇基中度膨大、前胫节轻微膨大、胫节距 3:2:2。

- **Etymology 段（Plazi XML）**：

  > Named for Raphael Awoumou Onana, the chief of Ebogo village, a resilient guide, and expert on Cameroonian termites.

  说明：支撑：raphaeli 纪念 Raphael Awoumou Onana
