# S0083 The Termites of Kartabo, Bartica District, British Guiana

Angularitermes 作为 Nasutitermes 亚属的原始描述（p. 419）。OCR 把 elevation 断作 eleva tion，引文止于该词之前。

## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 419**：

  > Head yellow brown, constricted behind the antennae; thickly covered with bristles. Antennae same color as the head, with 14 segments, the 3d one and one- half times the length of the 2d, the 4th equals the 3d or is very slightly longer. Mandibles with long points, the left with a very small microscopical tooth near the base, the right without a tooth near the base.

  说明：棱角白蚁属（angularitermes）：支撑：「兵蚁」头色、触角后收缩、刚毛、触角节数与比例、上颚长端尖及基部齿的有无。

- **p. 419**：

  > Nose darker than the head, very thickly covered with short hairs from the constriction in the head to the tip; very large, long, and thick at the base. This is the most extreme case of the development of the nose among all the nasuate soldiers known to me.

  说明：棱角白蚁属（angularitermes）：支撑：「鉴别要点」「兵蚁」鼻的大小与毛被，以及原作者对其发达程度的判断。

- **p. 419**：

  > Head dark brown, sparsely clothed with long hairs; oval, posterior margin behind the eyes angular from above

  说明：棱角白蚁属（angularitermes）：支撑：「有翅成虫」头色、毛被、卵形轮廓与眼后棱角状后缘。

- **p. 442（Subgenus Cavitermes 一节）**：

  > Cavitermes seems to be nearest to Mirotermes, s. str. than to any other subgenus but it differs from the typical Mirotermes, s. str. in the ridges along the forehead of the soldier and the hollowed front which suggested the name.

  说明：支撑：腔白蚁属（/genus/cavitermes/）

- **p. 442（Subgenus Cavitermes 一节）**：

  > The mandibles of the soldier are similar in shape and adaptation to those of Mirotermes, s. str.

  说明：支撑：腔白蚁属（/genus/cavitermes/）

- **p. 433, Subgenus Crepititermes diagnosis**：

  > The soldier is quite distinct from all the other groups of the genus Mirotermes in that it has a flat forehead. With this exception, I believe it comes closest to the subgenus Mirotermes, s. str. The mandibles are adapted for snapping and have the same form as in Mirotermes, s. str.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁额部平坦、弹射式上颚

- **pp. 433–434, Imago**：

  > Head brown, widely oval, covered with short hair and bristles . . . Fontanelle a little darker than the rest of the head, inconspicuous. Left mandible with 2 large teeth, one sharply pointed, the other rounded; right mandible with only 1 pointed tooth. The 3d segment of the antennae very short, much shorter than the 4th

  说明：支撑：有翅成虫头形、颚齿、触角第3节

- **p. 434, Soldier**：

  > Head pale yellow, covered with short hairs; long, subrectangular, sides nearly parallel, converging sharply in front of the base of the antennae. Antennae paler than the head, with 13 segments . . . Labrum with 2 points . . . Mandibles brown, slender, extreme end sharply curved, base broad with a tooth on the inner edge . . . Pronotum paler than the head, saddle-shaped

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头形、触角、上唇、上颚、前胸背板

- **Zoologica (N.Y.) 6(4):291-459, teevani 词源段**：

  > I take pleasure in naming the species in honor of Mr. John Tee-Van, who collected the only specimens of winged imagos and who helped me in many other ways in my work on termites at Kartabo.

  说明：支撑：teevani 纪念 John Tee-Van
