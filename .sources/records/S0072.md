## 摘录（逐字，不得改写）

- **p. 812，「Comparisons」段**：

  > The soldier of Silvestritermes gen.n. can be distinguished from Armitermes because the former lacks a row of short thick spines on the margins of the pro-, meso- and metanotum

  说明：支撑：前、中、后胸背板边缘无一行短粗刺，可与 Armitermes 区分——鉴别要点。

- **p. 811–812，「Soldier」段**：

  > Soldier. Monomorphic. Head capsule, in dorsal view, rounded or with lateral margins rounded with angular lateral posterior corners. Frontal tube conical, in dorsal view extend- ing beyond the labrum

  说明：支撑：兵蚁单型、头形、额管背视伸出上唇——兵蚁节。

- **p. 812，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker. Monomorphic. Head capsule rounded, with incon- spicuous epicranial suture; fontanelle oval, but very inconspic- uous (except in Silvestritermes lanei). Postclypeus and labrum as in imago. Antennae with 13 or 14 articles

  说明：支撑：工蚁单型、头圆、囟极不显（lanei 除外）、触角 13/14 节——工蚁节。

- **p. 822，「Comparisons」段**：

  > The soldier of Uncitermes gen.n. is easily distinguished from Armitermes by the hooked mandibles, the absence of a conspicuous row of short thick spines on the margins of the pro-, meso- and metanotum, and the lack of keel-shaped projections and thick bristles on the forecoxae.

  说明：支撑：钩状上颚、无背板短粗刺列、前基节无龙骨突起与粗刚毛——鉴别要点。

- **p. 821，「Soldier」段**：

  > Soldier. Monomorphic. Head capsule, in dorsal view, rounded. Frontal tube conical, same length as base of head capsule; forming a nearly 45◦angle with base of head, in pro- ﬁle. Antennae with 15 articles. Labrum rounded, anterior mar- gin hyaline, without distinct tip. Postmentum sub-rectangular

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头圆、额管与头基等长、触角 15、上唇圆、后颏近长方形——兵蚁节。

- **p. 821，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker. Monomorphic. Head capsule rounded; epicranial suture inconspicuous; fontanelle oval, little visible. Antennae with 15 articles.

  说明：支撑：工蚁单型、头圆、囟不明显、触角 15 节——工蚁节。

- **p. 825，「Comparisons」段**：

  > Also, the gizzard pulvilli in Mapinguaritermes gen.n. is ornamented with well-developed spines, which is also a synapomorphy of the new genus in our analysis.

  说明：支撑：砂囊一级垫具发达刺，为本属共衍征——鉴别要点。

- **p. 825，「Soldier」段**：

  > Head capsule oval, in dorsal view. Frontal tube conical, almost parallel to base of head capsule, and approximately four-ﬁfths of its length in proﬁle. Antennae with 14 articles. Labrum almost pentagonal, anterior margin hyaline, without distinct tip. Postmentum with concave sides. Piercing mandibles symmetrical; only one marginal tooth, acute and inward-orientated

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头卵形、额管约为头基长的 4/5、触角 14 节、上唇近五边形、后颏两侧凹、上颚仅一枚内向锐齿——兵蚁节。

- **p. 825，「Worker」段**：

  > Worker. Monomorphic. Head capsule rounded; epicranial suture not evident; fontanelle oval, but very discreet. Post- clypeus little inﬂated. Antennae with 14 articles.

  说明：支撑：工蚁单型、头圆、囟很不明显、后唇基稍膨大、触角 14 节——工蚁节。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 11**：

  > Laminar mandibles, with an incision anterior to the first marginal teeth; postmentum finely rugose; Mexico, Belize and Honduras

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚片状、第一缘齿前具切刻；后颏细皱；分布于墨西哥至洪都拉斯。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 7**：

  > Mandibles with the molar region enlarged and a posteriorly pointed subapical tooth

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚磨区扩大、具向后指的亚端齿，据此与无此特征的类群（9 支）分开。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 8**：

  > Frontal tube with a smooth surface; broad and conical.

  说明：支撑：额管表面光滑、宽锥形（与 Cyrilliotermes 的皱褶环状端相对）。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 8**：

  > Frontal tube wrinkled with irregular transverse ridges and with a doughnut-shaped tip

  说明：支撑：额管具不规则横脊而皱褶、端部环状（doughnut-shaped）。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 12**：

  > Mandibles with a recognisable marginal tooth, near the middle or near the base; clypeus not inflated.

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚具可辨缘齿（近中部或基部），唇基不膨大。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 10**：

  > Head capsule elongate and sub-rectangular in dorsal view; length of base of head capsule normally more than the width

  说明：支撑：头囊背面观延长、近长方形，头基部长大于宽。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 12**：

  > Mandibles without marginal teeth; clypeus strongly inflated (except in I. tellustris Constantino)

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚无缘齿、唇基强烈膨大（I. tellustris 除外）。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 6**：

  > marginal tooth on the middle of mandibles, dimorphic soldiers

  说明：支撑：上颚缘齿位于中部，兵蚁二型。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 14**：

  > Posterior region of head with four conspicuous protuberances resembling horns; sides convex, with a slight constriction at the anterior half

  说明：支撑：兵蚁头后部具四个角状显著突起，两侧凸、前半略收缢。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 11**：

  > Piercing mandibles, with a conspicuous marginal tooth near the base; South America

  说明：支撑：兵蚁上颚穿刺型、基部附近具显著缘齿。

- **Key to genera of Syntermitinae, couplet 10**：

  > Head capsule rounded or sub-quadrate; length of the base of head capsule almost equal to width

  说明：支撑：头囊圆或近方形，头基部长约等于宽。
